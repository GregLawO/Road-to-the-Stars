# ROAD TO THE STARS — KNOWLEDGE TRACKER

**Purpose:** Track what Kenji knows, what he can reasonably infer, what remains uncertain, and what he must not know yet. This prevents foreknowledge from becoming omniscience and protects the story's chronology.

**Scope:** Kenji's personal knowledge, independently acquired information, ASID intelligence, and important changes in the understanding of the Mass Effect universe.

---

## 1. Knowledge Principles

- Kenji retains knowledge from his previous life, including familiarity with selected fictional technologies and the Mass Effect setting.
    
- His knowledge is incomplete, particularly concerning events, people, and details he did not encounter in his previous experience.
    
- Knowing that something exists is not the same as understanding how to build it.
    
- Remembered information must be distinguished from experimentally verified results.
    
- Events in this timeline can diverge from the original Mass Effect storyline.
    
- Kenji cannot assume that remembered future events will occur exactly as he expects.
    
- His private Knowledge Management AI can organize and preserve information, but cannot independently verify remembered claims without evidence.
    
- AI analysis and external research can improve his understanding, but cannot eliminate uncertainty.
    
- Kenji's foreknowledge provides strategic direction, not automatic solutions.
    
- ASID must acquire its own intelligence through investigation, sources, analysis, and verification. It must not simply inherit everything Kenji remembers.
    

### Information Classification

All significant information should be understood according to its source and reliability:

1. **Remembered:** Information retained from Kenji's previous life.
    
2. **Reported:** Information obtained from another person or organization.
    
3. **Observed:** Information directly observed or detected.
    
4. **Investigated:** Information developed through research or intelligence operations.
    
5. **Tested:** Information supported by experiments or practical trials.
    
6. **Verified:** Conclusions supported by sufficient independent evidence.
    
7. **Unresolved:** Information that remains uncertain, disputed, or incomplete.
    

These categories should not be treated as interchangeable.

## 2. Kenji's Knowledge Before 2148

### Personal and Technical Knowledge

Kenji knows the underlying principles of selected technologies from fictional settings:

|Source|Relevant knowledge|
|---|---|
|Elysium|Advanced medical technology and medical-pod concepts|
|Halo|Specialized, non-sapient AI concepts|
|Terminator|Advanced humanoid robotics concepts|
|StarCraft|Gravitic technology concepts|

These are private authoring references. Their franchise-specific names and terminology must not appear in manuscript prose, dialogue, narration, or in-universe documents.

This knowledge gives Kenji a development direction. It does not provide complete schematics, manufacturing processes, materials, or an existing supply chain.

Kenji must establish technical feasibility through research, testing, and industrial development.

### Knowledge of Mass Effect

Kenji retains memories of the Mass Effect setting from his previous life, but before 2148 he does not consciously recognize that his present world is that universe.

He does not actively interpret his circumstances through the setting's chronology or prepare for its major events.

Do not portray him as secretly preparing for the Reapers, Citadel politics, or first contact before the Charon Relay discovery.

## 3. The 2148 Realization

The discovery of the Charon Relay causes Kenji to recognize the Mass Effect setting.

This is the major knowledge transition in the early story.

### What He Understands

From the discovery and his incomplete memories, Kenji recognizes that:

- Humanity is not alone.
    
- The structure beneath Charon is artificial and part of a larger relay network.
    
- The relays connect distant regions of the galaxy.
    
- Advanced alien civilizations exist.
    
- A galactic political institution exists, associated with the Citadel.
    
- Humanity is approaching contact with an established galactic community.
    
- The setting contains dangers extending far beyond ordinary interstellar politics and warfare.
    

He also begins recognizing the broad existential threat represented by the Reapers.

### What He Does Not Know

His recognition does not provide:

- A complete history of the galaxy.
    
- Reliable intelligence on every alien civilization.
    
- Exact dates for future events.
    
- Complete knowledge of the relay network.
    
- A comprehensive understanding of Prothean technology.
    
- The precise capabilities and vulnerabilities of the Reapers.
    
- Certainty that the original storyline will unfold as remembered.
    

Chapter 10 establishes that Kenji's memories are incomplete. His recollections of the relay network, the galactic council, war, and greater dangers are fragmented rather than a complete historical record.

His Knowledge Management AI records significant recollections as **"Remembered. Unverified."**

### Immediate Response

Kenji's first response is verification and reassessment.

He does not immediately disclose everything he remembers or assume that his recollections are sufficient for strategic decisions.

He begins comparing remembered information against actual evidence and reconsidering Akatsuki's long-term priorities.

## 4. Knowledge of the Mass Effect Setting

### General Galactic Knowledge

After 2148, Kenji can use remembered setting information as an initial reference for:

- The Citadel and its political institutions.
    
- Major known species and civilizations.
    
- The Systems Alliance and humanity's likely place in galactic politics.
    
- Mass relay infrastructure.
    
- Element zero and conventional mass-effect technology.
    
- The historical background of the First Contact War.
    
- The Reapers and the broad pattern of the cycles.
    
- The threat of indoctrination.
    
- Major organizations and individuals from the original setting.
    

This information is a starting reference, not a complete intelligence database.

### Limitations

Kenji may not remember:

- Exact dates for events outside his previous experience.
    
- Every individual involved in historical events.
    
- Details of obscure missions, records, or classified programs.
    
- Every technological specification.
    
- The precise motivations and private decisions of other characters.
    
- The actual state of organizations at a particular date.
    
- How the timeline will change after Akatsuki's intervention.
    

His memory should be treated as a useful but imperfect reference.

### Canon Versus Actual History

The original Mass Effect storyline is not a guaranteed future.

As events diverge, Kenji must increasingly rely on current intelligence, observation, and professional judgment.

He may recognize familiar situations, but must not automatically assume that their outcomes will be identical.

## 5. Knowledge Boundaries by Period

### 2137–2148: Before Recognition

Kenji knows:

- His previous-life memories.
    
- The selected fictional technology concepts he intends to reproduce.
    
- His own research, experimental results, and corporate developments.
    

Kenji does not consciously know:

- That the Charon Relay exists in this world.
    
- That his present circumstances correspond to the Mass Effect setting.
    
- The impending First Contact War as an immediate strategic event.
    
- The Reaper threat as an immediate strategic concern.
    

The narrative must not insert deliberate Reaper preparations or explicit Mass Effect recognition into this period.

### 2148–2150: Initial Reassessment

Kenji begins:

- Comparing remembered Mass Effect information against newly available evidence.
    
- Assessing humanity's technological and political position.
    
- Reviewing Akatsuki's strategic priorities.
    
- Expanding ASID's intelligence responsibilities.
    
- Planning a legitimate commercial presence in the Shanxi system.
    
- Establishing a dedicated effort to investigate the Charon structure and its implications.
    

He does not instantly understand the complete state of galactic affairs.

Independent reviewers can confirm that the Charon structure is artificial without necessarily identifying its origin, purpose, or operational condition.

### 2150–2153: Investigation and Strategic Development

Kenji increasingly:

- Directs resources toward extraterrestrial intelligence and strategic assessment.
    
- Uses ASID to investigate information relevant to humanity's future.
    
- Develops Akatsuki's industrial and commercial presence in Shanxi.
    
- Assesses the dangers associated with alien technology and ancient artifacts.
    
- Advances independent FTL and other capabilities that reduce humanity's dependence on mass relays.
    

ASID discovers and neutralizes the Arca Monolith around 2152–2153, before Jack Harper, Ben Hislop, and Eva Coré encounter it.

This operation provides ASID with an opportunity to develop its understanding of Prothean artifacts and related risks. It does not automatically give the organization comprehensive knowledge of Prothean technology or indoctrination.

### 2151–2155: Broader Strategic Preparation

Kenji increasingly understands:

- The strategic importance of independent FTL.
    
- The technological gap between humanity and established galactic powers.
    
- The political implications of humanity's first contact.
    
- The danger of Reaper artifacts and indoctrination.
    
- The need for industrial redundancy and long-term resilience.
    
- The importance of developing Amatsu as a permanent settlement and industrial center.
    

During this period, Kenji also begins discovering indications that human authorities are planning to activate Relay 314.

This discovery must be gradual and subtle.

In Chapter 13, he encounters information through an ordinary corporate, scientific, or government interaction. He asks unusually specific questions about authorization, scheduling, and decision-makers, then discreetly asks his staff to investigate.

His intentions should not immediately be apparent to the reader.

### 2155–2156: Relay 314 Intervention

Kenji's investigation establishes that human authorities intend to open Relay 314.

In Chapter 14, he attempts to prevent or delay activation through legitimate institutional and political channels.

He understands that:

- Human authorities possess independent decision-making powers.
    
- Akatsuki does not control the relevant institutions.
    
- His remembered knowledge is not sufficient evidence to compel postponement.
    
- Different organizations have legitimate political, scientific, commercial, and strategic reasons to proceed.
    

His first intervention fails.

Immediately afterward, he orders construction of two ODPS directly above Akatsuki's Shanxi settlement.

This is a defensive response to the realization that Akatsuki cannot control every external decision that might endanger its people and assets.

### 2157: The Threshold and First Contact

Kenji makes a second attempt to prevent or delay Relay 314 activation in Chapter 15.

It also fails.

The chapter ends with activation.

Kenji's subsequent knowledge must be based on actual events and intelligence rather than assumptions that the original Mass Effect storyline will unfold unchanged.

### 2157 Onward: Divergent History

After the First Contact crisis begins, the timeline diverges substantially from the original Mass Effect events.

Kenji must respond to actual developments rather than relying on an obsolete expectation of canon events.

His memories remain useful, but their reliability must be reassessed as the timeline changes.

## 6. Knowledge of Specific Threats

### The Reapers

Kenji remembers the Reapers as an existential threat and knows their return is connected to the cycles of galactic civilization.

He understands the broad danger of:

- Their technological superiority.
    
- Their adaptive nature.
    
- Their numbers and experience.
    
- Indoctrination.
    
- The vulnerability of centralized civilizations and infrastructure.
    
- The possibility that conventional military superiority will be insufficient.
    

However, he does not possess complete technical knowledge of Reaper construction, capabilities, or weaknesses.

His memories do not provide a complete solution to the Reaper threat.

He must develop practical countermeasures through research, experimentation, intelligence, and industrial preparation.

### Indoctrination

Kenji knows that indoctrination is a major threat.

He can use this knowledge to establish precautions, compartmentalization, intelligence procedures, and safeguards.

He cannot assume:

- Every unusual behavior indicates indoctrination.
    
- Every artifact is necessarily dangerous in the same way.
    
- Technological security makes anyone immune.
    
- ASID can reliably identify every indoctrinated individual.
    
- His own memories provide a complete understanding of the phenomenon.
    

Indoctrination remains a serious and evolving intelligence problem.

### Prothean Technology and Artifacts

Kenji remembers the importance of Prothean technology and artifacts.

He does not automatically know the location, condition, or complete significance of every artifact.

ASID must discover and investigate such objects independently.

The neutralization of the Arca Monolith is a specific intelligence success, not proof that ASID understands all Prothean technology.

### Cerberus

Kenji may remember Cerberus as a major human extremist organization from the original setting.

He cannot assume that its structure, membership, capabilities, or history will develop identically in this timeline.

Jack Harper's development must be driven by events that actually occur in this story.

Kenji does not automatically know Jack's future decisions or the eventual structure of his organization.

### Relay Network

Kenji recognizes the broad function and strategic significance of mass relays after the Charon discovery.

However, he does not automatically know:

- The exact operational status of every relay.
    
- Every relay's location.
    
- The complete history of relay construction and activation.
    
- The precise political and technical procedures governing relay access.
    
- The full strategic implications of every relay-related decision.
    

Relay 314 becomes a specific concern only after he learns that human authorities intend to activate it.

## 7. ASID and Independently Acquired Intelligence

ASID is an intelligence organization, not an extension of Kenji's personal memory.

After 2148, its responsibilities expand to include extraterrestrial intelligence, galactic political developments, alien technology, Prothean and Reaper artifacts, and indoctrination risks.

Its information must come from actual intelligence work.

### ASID's Knowledge Development

ASID may acquire information through:

- Scientific and industrial research.
    
- Open-source intelligence.
    
- Human institutional and commercial contacts.
    
- Technical analysis of recovered or discovered materials.
    
- Independent investigations.
    
- Human intelligence sources.
    
- Security and counterintelligence operations.
    

Its conclusions must reflect the quality and limitations of its sources.

### Arca Monolith

Around 2152–2153:

- ASID discovers the Arca Monolith in the Shanxi system.
    
- It identifies the artifact as a significant threat.
    
- The organization neutralizes or destroys it before Jack Harper's group discovers it.
    
- No one is exposed to the artifact.
    
- The incident is handled quietly.
    

This event does not give ASID complete knowledge of indoctrination, Prothean history, or Reaper technology.

### ASID Limitations

- ASID is capable but not omniscient.
    
- It may possess incomplete or contradictory reports.
    
- Intelligence sources may be unreliable or compromised.
    
- Analysis can be wrong.
    
- Operations can fail.
    
- Important information may remain undiscovered.
    
- ASID does not automatically know what Kenji remembers.
    
- ASID does not automatically have access to Kenji's private AI systems or research.
    

Kenji's foreknowledge can guide investigations, but ASID must still do the work.

## 8. Knowledge Management and Private Research

Kenji's private Knowledge Management AI maintains an organized repository of:

- Previous-life memories and references.
    
- Technical concepts and research notes.
    
- Experimental results.
    
- Verified engineering data.
    
- Intelligence assessments.
    
- Historical and scientific records.
    
- Unresolved hypotheses and questions.
    

The repository must distinguish between:

1. **Remembered information:** Claims based on Kenji's previous-life knowledge.
    
2. **External information:** Claims obtained from records, research, or other people.
    
3. **Observed information:** Findings directly obtained through observation or measurement.
    
4. **Experimental findings:** Results produced by testing.
    
5. **Verified conclusions:** Claims supported by sufficient evidence.
    
6. **Unresolved hypotheses:** Ideas that remain uncertain or unproven.
    

The AI can organize, cross-reference, and evaluate these categories.

It must not silently convert remembered information into verified fact.

### Private System Restrictions

Kenji's private Engineering Assistant and Knowledge Management AI remain isolated from Akatsuki's corporate network.

- No automatic synchronization with corporate systems.
    
- No unrestricted ASID access.
    
- No routine corporate administrative access.
    
- Research is selectively released through validated technical packages.
    
- Corporate engineering teams must independently evaluate and develop released technology.
    

This separation protects Kenji's private research and preserves the distinction between his personal knowledge and Akatsuki's institutional capabilities.

## 9. Knowledge and Character Relationships

Kenji should not use foreknowledge to manipulate every person he meets.

- Supporting characters have their own expertise, motives, and information.
    
- Experts can identify mistakes in Kenji's assumptions.
    
- People can reach conclusions independently of him.
    
- Kenji can withhold information when disclosure would be dangerous, but secrecy can complicate relationships.
    
- His knowledge does not make him automatically persuasive or trustworthy.
    
- He must earn cooperation through actual decisions and relationships.
    
- Other characters must not become passive recipients of Kenji's knowledge.
    
- Professional disagreements should remain possible even when Kenji possesses information others lack.
    

Kenji's memories may give him an advantage, but his decisions must still be tested against the judgment of people who understand their own fields.

---

## 10. Narrative Restrictions

- No alien characters or direct alien interactions before the established first-contact period.
    
- No explicit recognition of the Mass Effect setting before the Charon Relay discovery in 2148.
    
- No deliberate Reaper preparations before Kenji's recognition.
    
- No references to future events as certainties when they remain dependent on an altered timeline.
    
- No treating remembered game or setting details as complete intelligence.
    
- No omniscient ASID reports.
    
- No assumption that Kenji knows the location or significance of every Prothean or Reaper artifact.
    
- No automatic knowledge of the precise future actions of canon characters.
    
- No treating theoretical technology as a completed, tested, and deployable system.
    
- No assumption that ASID knows everything Kenji remembers.
    
- No assumption that Kenji immediately shares his private memories with employees, governments, or military organizations.
    
- No treating the neutralization of one artifact as proof that all similar threats are understood or controlled.
    
- No making Kenji's strategic decisions appear infallible merely because he has foreknowledge.

## 11. Updating This Tracker

Update this document when:

- Kenji discovers information that changes his understanding of the galaxy.
    
- A remembered fact is experimentally verified or disproved.
    
- An important divergence from the original timeline occurs.
    
- ASID discovers significant new intelligence.
    
- Kenji learns something about a character, organization, technology, or threat that affects later decisions.
    
- A major assumption is revised in light of new evidence.
    
- Kenji's understanding of the Reapers, indoctrination, Prothean technology, or galactic politics materially changes.
    

For each significant development, record:

- **When:** The year or chapter in which the information becomes available.
    
- **Source:** Memory, observation, research, intelligence, or experimentation.
    
- **What is known:** The actual information acquired.
    
- **Confidence:** Whether it is remembered, suspected, reported, tested, or verified.
    
- **Limitations:** What remains unknown or uncertain.
    
- **Consequences:** How the new knowledge affects subsequent decisions.
    

Do not record every minor discovery. Focus on information that affects the story, character decisions, strategic planning, or future continuity.

---

## Central Knowledge Rule

**Kenji may know the shape of the story he remembers, but he must discover the world he actually inhabits.**

His memories give him a starting advantage. Evidence, experience, independent intelligence, and the decisions of other people determine what he can truly know.
