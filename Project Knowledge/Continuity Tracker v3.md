## 3. Kenji and Family Background

- Full name: Himura Kenji.
    
- American, of Japanese ancestry.
    
- Awakens in 2137, physically 20 years old.
    
- His mother, Linda Himura, was an automotive mechanic.
    
- Linda left him the family home and a small but respectable repair shop.
    
- The shop provides Kenji's initial income, tools, and fabrication facilities.
    
- His consciousness merger is brief; he does not experience a prolonged identity crisis.
    
- Kenji's knowledge of fictional technologies provides principles and design direction, not instant manufacturing capability.
    
- His technological progress depends on research, engineering, materials, manufacturing capacity, personnel, and time.

## 4. Akatsuki Technologies

- Correct name: **Akatsuki Technologies**.
    
- Founded in 2137 by Kenji.
    
- Akatsuki means dawn or daybreak.
    
- It begins as a small technology business and grows into a medical, technological, industrial, and defense corporation.
    
- Its development depends on the contributions of employees, researchers, engineers, executives, and other professionals.
    
- It is not a one-man invention factory.
    
- By 2148, advanced AI and droids perform most routine corporate, administrative, manufacturing, and logistical work.
    
- Human employees remain important in strategy, leadership, research, judgment, external relations, legal matters, and accountability.
    
- AI autonomy follows advanced automation with delegated authority, not unrestricted independent rule.
    
- Akatsuki's industrial and military development must remain consistent with its actual resources and production capacity.

## 5. AI and Robotics Continuity

- Akatsuki uses specialized, non-sapient AI systems.
    
- Each AI is exceptionally capable within its assigned role but deliberately limited outside it.
    
- AI cores have immutable fundamental architecture, specialization, and core operating instructions.
    
- Authorized operational parameters, objectives, permissions, and external reference data can be adjusted without rewriting the core.
    
- Droids can change roles by receiving compatible AI cores, provided their chassis and equipment support the new function.
    
- Core transfers require authentication and validation.
    
- AI systems communicate through controlled, authenticated interfaces.
    
- Akatsuki uses compartmentalization, hardware-enforced restrictions, network isolation, anomaly monitoring, and independent verification of consequential commands.
    
- These protections are advanced but not infallible.
    
- AI systems can disagree because of differing objectives, information, assumptions, and risk assessments.
    

### Kenji's Private AI Systems

Kenji has six specialized personal assistants:

1. Executive Assistant.
    
2. Strategic Analysis AI.
    
3. Engineering Assistant AI.
    
4. Security Assistant AI.
    
5. Financial and Legal Assistant AI.
    
6. Knowledge Management AI.
    

The Engineering Assistant and Knowledge Management AI are private systems, separate from Akatsuki's corporate network. Kenji selectively releases validated research for corporate use.

ASID does not automatically have access to these private systems or Kenji's research.

### Human Personal Staff

Kenji also retains human personal staff, including a Chief of Staff with genuine authority to challenge him.

AI and human staff complement each other. Automation does not eliminate the need for human relationships, judgment, professional interaction, or personal responsibilities.

## 7. Settlements and Off-World Infrastructure

### Callisto

- Established before Kenji's 2148 realization.
    
- A corporate settlement, not sovereign territory.
    
- Supports research, manufacturing, mining, resource processing, spacecraft maintenance and construction, power, and logistics.
    
- It is distinct from Amatsu and the Shanxi settlement.
    
- It serves as an important foundation for Akatsuki's off-world industrial and technological development.
    

### Amatsu

- Kenji chooses the name **Amatsu (天園)**, meaning "Heavenly Garden."
    
- He names it during the later colonization and settlement process.
    
- The name is his choice, not a name he learns from existing records.
    
- He does not know the planet's hidden Prothean significance.
    
- Its settlement begins during the 2151–2155 period.
    
- By 2157, it is a substantial and functioning colony, with permanent civilian habitation, industrial facilities, research, administration, and orbital infrastructure.
    
- It eventually becomes Akatsuki's headquarters, principal industrial center, and political heart.
    
- It must not be portrayed as an instantly developed, fully mature world.
    
- The planet's original Mass Effect designation is author-only information and must never appear in narrative prose, dialogue, or in-universe documents.
    

### Shanxi

- Akatsuki establishes a genuine commercial and industrial presence between 2150 and 2153.
    
- Its activities include resource extraction, processing, manufacturing, spacecraft maintenance, shipping, logistics, research, and exploration support.
    
- It is not a disguised military base.
    
- Akatsuki's settlement is on the opposite side of the planet from the principal human settlement.
    
- Its prewar presence gives Akatsuki legitimate commercial and logistical reasons to operate in the system during the First Contact War.
    

**Orbital Defense Platforms**

- Kenji orders construction of two ODPS after his first failed attempt to prevent Relay 314 activation in Chapter 14.
    
- Both platforms are positioned directly above Akatsuki's Shanxi settlement.
    
- No additional ODPS are authorized elsewhere in Shanxi under the current arrangement.
    
- No third platform should be introduced without an explicit author decision.
    
- Their purpose is to protect Akatsuki's settlement and assets.
    
- Their construction and deployment must respect the limitations imposed by the settlement's political and geographic circumstances.

## 8. ASID Continuity

**Full name:** Akatsuki Security and Intelligence Directorate.

- ASID predates Akatsuki's ownership of Amatsu and its own military.
    
- It originates in corporate espionage, theft, kidnapping, and assassination threats.
    
- Its early responsibilities include executive protection, counterintelligence, corporate espionage, vetting, threat assessment, and facility security.
    
- After 2148, it expands into extraterrestrial intelligence, galactic political analysis, alien technology, Prothean and Reaper artifacts, indoctrination risks, and hostile human organizations.
    
- ASID is capable but not omniscient.
    
- It can encounter incomplete information, operational failures, compromised sources, and unexpected threats.
    
- ASID does not have unrestricted access to Kenji's private AI systems or research.
    
- Its expansion must be gradual and supported by personnel, infrastructure, intelligence sources, and experience.
    

### Arca Monolith

- Located in the Shanxi system.
    
- ASID discovers and neutralizes it around 2152–2153.
    
- The operation occurs before Jack Harper, Ben Hislop, and Eva Coré discover it.
    
- No one is exposed to the artifact.
    
- It is handled as a quiet intelligence operation, not a dedicated dramatic chapter.
    
- Desolas Arterius' original Monolith operation does not occur.
    
- Do not invent a direct connection between Kenji and Jack Harper through this incident.
    
- Jack, Ben, and Eva remain available for independent story developments.

## 9. Fleet and Military Continuity

### Spacecraft Classes

Akatsuki's principal combat spacecraft classes are:

|Class|Length|General role|
|---|---|---|

|Frigate|500 meters|General combatant, escort, and independent operations|
|Fast Battlecruiser|750 meters|Heavier combatant for rapid response and fleet operations|
|Line Cruiser|1,000 meters|Heavy fleet combatant, endurance, and command responsibilities|

The larger classes are long-term development ambitions during the 2148 frigate trials, not already completed or operational ships.

### Akatsuki Fleet in 2148

- Six operational 250-meter corvettes.
    
- One 500-meter frigate under trials.
    
- The frigate is not yet commissioned or combat-ready.
    
- The corvettes have actual anti-piracy operational experience, including convoy protection, interdictions, and responses to raids and attempted hijackings.
    
- Human military personnel participate in the frigate trials.
    
- Do not describe the fleet as a mature navy.
    
- The precise distribution of corvettes between patrol, escort, and Callisto defense remains unconfirmed.
    

### Future Fleet Development

- The 750-meter Fast Battlecruiser and 1,000-meter Line Cruiser are discussed as preliminary ambitions during the 2148 frigate trials.
    
- Expanded corvette and frigate production is also part of the longer-term plan.
    
- These programs depend on industrial capacity, finances, personnel, and the success of earlier shipbuilding efforts.
    
- Do not imply that the larger classes were already under construction in 2148.

### Naval Doctrine

- Akatsuki spacecraft are built around gravitic technology.
    
- Gravitic propulsion, shields, inertial manipulation, and artificial gravity are foundational systems.
    
- Akatsuki gravitic shields replace conventional Mass Effect-style kinetic barriers.
    
- Advanced torpedoes are the primary offensive weapons.
    
- Drones support reconnaissance, targeting, combat, and torpedo delivery.
    
- Coil-gun and laser turrets provide secondary and defensive fire.
    
- Fast Battlecruisers and Line Cruisers may carry small spinal mass accelerators.
    
- Corvettes and Frigates do not require spinal accelerators.
    
- Larger ships generally operate from the back line, deploying torpedoes and drones rather than acting as frontline gun platforms.
    
- Do not make every ship a conventional gun-focused combatant.
    
- The fleet's doctrine should be presented through credible operational decisions, not lengthy technical explanations.
    

### Defense Business

By 2148, Akatsuki is an established defense contractor with actual customers, contracts, deployed systems, test records, and production capability.

Its offerings include sensors, electronic warfare, targeting, communications security, ship protection, armor, power systems, propulsion components, drones, robotics, AI, weapons, and fire-control systems.

It is a credible military-industrial organization, not yet a mature independent navy.

### FTL

- Akatsuki develops independent gravitic FTL without relying on element zero or mass relays.
    
- Experimental frigate testing begins around 2148–2150.
    
- Additional spacecraft integration follows in 2151–2152.
    
- Wider operational deployment occurs from 2153 to 2157.
    
- Independent FTL does not automatically provide instantaneous or equivalent FTL communications.
    
- Drive performance targets are not proof that every production vessel can achieve those speeds reliably.
    
- Operational deployment requires training, maintenance, logistics, spare parts, and supporting infrastructure.
    

**Alliance Fleet Integration — Unresolved Detail**

Existing planning documents refer to one or two complete fleets equipped with Akatsuki FTL drives by 2157. The exact distinction between Akatsuki-operated fleets and Systems Alliance-operated fleets must be clarified before assigning ownership, command, or composition in the narrative.

Do not silently treat the two organizations' fleets as interchangeable.

## 10. Relay 314 and First Contact War Continuity

### Charon Relay Discovery

- The Charon Relay is discovered in 2148.
    
- Kenji recognizes the implications from his incomplete memories of another life.
    
- He understands that the relays connect distant stars and that humanity is entering a much larger galactic environment.
    
- His knowledge is incomplete and must not become unexplained omniscience.
    
- His immediate response is verification, independent assessment, and strategic preparation.
    
- He does not immediately reveal everything he remembers.
    

### Kenji's Attempts to Prevent Relay 314 Activation

**First attempt — Chapter 14**

- Kenji discovers that human authorities intend to open Relay 314.
    
- He attempts to prevent or delay activation through legitimate political, scientific, commercial, and institutional channels.
    
- He requests postponement, offers Akatsuki's research and technical resources, and proposes further investigation and safety reviews.
    
- His efforts fail because he lacks authority over independent human institutions, cannot provide sufficient verifiable evidence, and faces competing institutional and commercial interests.
    
- Some additional precautions may be accepted, but activation remains authorized.
    
- Kenji does not resort to sabotage, coercion, or actions that compromise his principles.
    
- Immediately following this failure, he orders construction of the two Shanxi ODPS.
    

**Second attempt — Chapter 15**

- Kenji makes another substantial attempt to prevent or delay activation.
    
- This is a continuation of the earlier effort, not a repetition of the entire political struggle.
    
- His remaining political, commercial, and institutional influence is used to seek a compromise or further delay.
    
- This attempt also fails.
    
- Chapter 15 ends at Relay 314 activation.
    
- Do not depict the Turian attack, the destruction of the exploration vessel, or the Council's response in Chapter 15.
    

### First Contact War

- Fixed year: 2157.
    
- Kenji awakens exactly twenty years earlier, in 2137.
    
- The AU first engagement is a human tactical victory.
    
- The Turian expeditionary force that enters human space is decisively defeated; most of it is destroyed or captured.
    
- Humanity does not conquer Palaven or destroy the Turian Hierarchy.
    
- The Hierarchy remains larger, more industrialized, experienced, and powerful overall.
    
- The Council intervenes.
    
- The conflict changes the political balance and makes Akatsuki technology and military capabilities a major intelligence concern.
    
- Akatsuki's independent FTL gives humanity important strategic options.
    
- Do not make the human victory effortless or consequence-free.
    
- The First Contact War must remain a conflict with political, military, industrial, and diplomatic consequences.

## 15. Continuity Rules

1. The Master Story Bible establishes overarching canon.
    
2. The Technology and Character References establish their respective technical and character details.
    
3. Approved chapter text establishes what has actually occurred in the narrative.
    
4. This tracker records chronology, completed events, and continuity-sensitive decisions.
    
5. Do not silently contradict approved chapters to accommodate a new idea.
    
6. Distinguish confirmed facts from proposed developments and unresolved decisions.
    
7. When a continuity conflict appears, identify it explicitly and resolve it before drafting further chapters.
    
8. Do not add technical or organizational detail merely because it is possible. Include it when it matters to the story.
    
9. Do not treat an outline, proposal, or assistant suggestion as established canon until the author confirms it.
    
10. Preserve the distinction between Akatsuki Technologies, its private military capabilities, and the Systems Alliance.
    
11. Maintain the established chronology of human characters and organizations.
    
12. No non-Mass Effect franchise-specific names, terminology, classifications, or explicit references may appear in chapter prose, dialogue, narration, or in-universe documents. Source-franchise references may remain in private planning materials only.
    
13. Every completed chapter must include an Author's Notes section following the chapter text. The notes should briefly communicate the author's intention, creative approach, and intended reader takeaway without simply retelling the plot.
