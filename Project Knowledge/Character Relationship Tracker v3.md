# ROAD TO THE STARS — CHARACTER RELATIONSHIP TRACKER

**Document Status:** Revised Working Canon  
**Purpose:** Track important relationships, interpersonal dynamics, and changes in trust, loyalty, and influence. This document helps preserve character agency and prevents supporting characters from becoming interchangeable assistants to Kenji.

---

# 1. Relationship Principles

- Supporting characters have independent goals, priorities, expertise, and personal lives.
    
- Kenji's intelligence and resources do not automatically make people loyal to him.
    
- Professional respect, friendship, trust, affection, and loyalty are distinct.
    
- Disagreement should be possible, including disagreement with Kenji.
    
- Relationships develop through shared experiences, decisions, conflicts, and demonstrated reliability.
    
- Avoid inventing personal histories or relationships before they become relevant to the story.
    
- Do not make every relationship transactional.
    
- Human relationships remain important even in an organization dominated by AI and droids.
    
- Characters should have responsibilities and interests outside their interactions with Kenji.
    
- Recurring characters should not exist solely to explain technology, deliver information, or validate Kenji's decisions.
    
- Do not assume that professional respect develops into friendship, personal loyalty, or affection.
    
- Characters may change their opinions of Kenji as his influence, secrecy, and ambitions grow.
    

# 2. Himura Kenji

Kenji is the central character, founder of Akatsuki Technologies, and the principal driver of its technological and strategic development.

Important characteristics:

- Intelligent, pragmatic, ambitious, and disciplined.
    
- Values competence and reliability.
    
- Can become overly focused on work and long-term objectives.
    
- Needs people capable of challenging his assumptions.
    
- Does not naturally need to dominate every conversation or decision.
    
- Must maintain friendships, professional acquaintances, and a personal life outside the company.
    
- Has incomplete memories and knowledge of his new universe.
    
- Must learn to distinguish personal recollections from verified information.
    

## Relationship Development

Kenji's increasing wealth, technological capabilities, and organizational influence should complicate his relationships.

People may respect his achievements while questioning his judgment, secrecy, or concentration of power.

He should earn trust rather than expect it because of his position.

His relationships must also evolve independently of Akatsuki's corporate growth. Professional success should not automatically produce personal fulfillment.

# 6. Kenji's Personal AI Assistants

Kenji maintains six specialized personal AI assistants.

They are non-sapient, role-specific systems with immutable cores. They are not substitutes for human friendships or personal relationships.

|AI system|Function|
|---|---|
|Executive Assistant|Scheduling, correspondence, appointments, and administration|
|Strategic Analysis AI|Corporate developments, proposals, strategic options, and risks|
|Engineering Assistant AI|Technical calculations, simulations, and private engineering work|
|Security Assistant AI|Personal security monitoring and threat assessment|
|Financial and Legal Assistant AI|Financial commitments, contracts, and legal analysis|
|Knowledge Management AI|Research references, technical records, and knowledge organization|

The Engineering Assistant and Knowledge Management AI are isolated from Akatsuki's corporate network and protect Kenji's private research.

Their communication should remain concise, role-appropriate, and consistent with their specialized architecture.

They should not be portrayed as human personalities, emotional companions, or unrestricted general-purpose intelligences.

# 7. Kenji's Human Personal Staff

Kenji retains human personal staff alongside his AI assistants.

These are functional positions, not established named characters.

## Chief of Staff

- Coordinates Kenji's human staff and priorities.
    
- Ensures decisions receive appropriate follow-through.
    
- Has genuine authority to challenge Kenji.
    
- Can tell him when his workload, priorities, or decisions have become unreasonable.
    
- Must not be reduced to a subordinate who agrees with everything.
    

## Personal Assistant

- Handles everyday arrangements, appointments, travel, and personal matters.
    

## Executive Secretary

- Handles sensitive correspondence, documents, and meeting administration.
    

## Personal Security Detail

- Provides physical protection and coordinates with ASID.
    
- Works alongside Kenji's personal battle droids rather than being automatically replaced by them.
    

## Personal Physician

- Oversees medical care, enhancements, and health monitoring.
    

## Personal Driver/Pilot

- Provides transportation and related support.
    

The number of people in these positions and their individual identities remain open.

Human staff should have professional judgment, individual personalities, and lives outside their assigned duties.

# 8. Akatsuki's Broader Workforce

Akatsuki's growth should introduce capable people with independent professional interests.

Important relationship categories include:

- Senior executives.
    
- Scientists and researchers.
    
- Engineers and technical specialists.
    
- Manufacturing and operations personnel.
    
- Human resources and administrative staff.
    
- Security professionals.
    
- Military officers and defense specialists.
    
- Government and commercial partners.
    

The organization is heavily automated, but it must not become socially sterile.

Human employees should be able to develop:

- Friendships.
    
- Rivalries.
    
- Mentorships.
    
- Professional disagreements.
    
- Personal relationships.
    
- Independent ambitions.
    
- Concerns about corporate policy and technological development.
    

Employees should not automatically share Kenji's priorities or understand his long-term objectives.

Do not create a large roster of named employees before their roles become relevant.

# 9. ASID Relationships

ASID personnel operate in a sensitive organization where trust, discretion, and professional competence matter.

Important relationship dynamics include:

- Kenji's relationship with ASID leadership.
    
- Cooperation between intelligence professionals and corporate management.
    
- Tension between operational secrecy and executive oversight.
    
- Conflicts between security priorities and personal or commercial interests.
    
- The need for independent assessments rather than unquestioning loyalty.
    

ASID should not be treated as an extension of Kenji's personal thoughts.

Its professionals must exercise judgment and sometimes disagree with him.

## Relationship Boundaries

- ASID protects Akatsuki and its legitimate interests.
    
- ASID has its own operational responsibilities and professional standards.
    
- Its intelligence assessments can be incomplete or incorrect.
    
- Kenji does not automatically know everything ASID discovers.
    
- ASID does not automatically have access to Kenji's isolated private research systems.
    
- Cooperation does not eliminate institutional boundaries.

# 12. Relationship Development After 2148

The discovery of the Charon Relay changes Kenji's priorities, but it should not make his relationships purely strategic.

As Akatsuki expands, Kenji must navigate:

- Government officials who want access to his technology.
    
- Military professionals who need to assess Akatsuki's capabilities.
    
- Scientists and engineers who want intellectual independence.
    
- Employees concerned about the consequences of rapid automation.
    
- Commercial partners who may become competitors.
    
- Friends and colleagues who may question his growing secrecy.
    
- People who distrust the concentration of power in one corporation.
    
- Individuals who disagree with his assessment of the extraterrestrial threat.
    
- People whose personal interests conflict with Akatsuki's expansion.
    

These relationships should create genuine interpersonal friction and opportunities for character development.

## 12.1 The Relay 314 Crisis

Kenji's efforts to prevent or delay Relay 314 activation create an important relationship test.

Relevant dynamics include:

- Political and scientific authorities who disagree with his assessment.
    
- Institutional leaders who have legitimate reasons to proceed.
    
- Military professionals concerned about security and readiness.
    
- Commercial interests that favor continued exploration.
    
- Allies and colleagues who may support additional precautions without agreeing to halt activation.
    

Kenji's failure must not turn every opposing character into an antagonist.

People can disagree with him for reasonable reasons, even when their decisions have serious consequences.

## 12.2 First Contact and the First Contact War

The altered First Contact War introduces new relationships and tensions.

- Human military leaders must assess Akatsuki's independent capabilities.
    
- Alliance officers may appreciate Akatsuki's contributions while questioning its autonomy.
    
- Political leaders may seek greater oversight of its technology.
    
- Military cooperation does not automatically create personal loyalty.
    
- Turian characters should retain their own institutional, military, and personal motivations.
    
- Human victory should not make every human character agree on what should happen next.
    

The consequences of the war should change relationships gradually rather than instantly resolving existing disagreements.

# 14. Character Agency and Narrative Rules

1. Supporting characters must have independent motivations and judgment.
    
2. Kenji's intelligence and resources must not automatically resolve interpersonal conflicts.
    
3. Professional respect must not be confused with friendship or loyalty.
    
4. Characters may disagree with Kenji without becoming antagonists.
    
5. Characters may support Kenji in one matter while opposing him in another.
    
6. Relationships should develop through believable interactions and shared experiences.
    
7. Avoid making every supporting character unusually competent, articulate, agreeable, or strategically insightful.
    
8. Give characters different communication styles, priorities, strengths, and weaknesses.
    
9. Avoid creating unnecessary named characters or elaborate personal histories.
    
10. Preserve established relationships when revising chapters unless a deliberate narrative change is approved.
    

# 15. Core Rule

**Kenji may build Akatsuki, but he does not own the agency of the people within it.**

The people around him should remain believable individuals with their own judgments, loyalties, disagreements, ambitions, relationships, and lives.

Their importance should come from who they are and what they contribute to the story—not simply from their proximity to the protagonist.
