Draft Chapter 1 of *Road to the Stars*, titled **Getting Started**.

Use the approved **Chapter 1 — Getting Started: Scene-by-Scene Drafting Brief** and all applicable Project Knowledge documents.

Follow the Project Instructions and Writing and Style Guide strictly.

### Writing Requirements

- Write in first-person POV from Himura Kenji's perspective.

- Use past tense throughout.

- Begin immediately with meaningful activity. Do not open with scenery, background exposition, or a lengthy introduction.

- Establish Kenji through his actions, decisions, dialogue, and practical problem-solving.

- Follow the five-scene progression in the approved drafting brief.

- Naturally establish his age, background, inherited house, mechanic shop, and limited starting resources.

- Introduce Med-Pods as his first major technological project.

- Establish why specialized non-sapient AI will be necessary for his research and development.

- End with Kenji beginning work on the AI framework.

### Characterization

Kenji must be intelligent, capable, confident, pragmatic, independent, resourceful, and proactive.

Do not portray him as timid, incompetent, socially awkward, or unnecessarily indecisive.

Do not dwell on the consciousness merger, previous-life memories, or philosophical questions about his identity.

### Prose and Pacing

- Write natural, engaging novel prose.

- Use simple mixed tone, allowing humor, technical discussion, and serious moments to emerge naturally.

- Keep descriptions concise and relevant.

- Use natural dialogue where appropriate.

- Avoid excessive internal monologues and introspection.

- Keep technical explanations understandable.

- Do not turn the chapter into an engineering report.

- Avoid padding, repetitive explanations, and artificial drama.

### Continuity Restrictions

- The story takes place in 2137.

- Kenji is twenty years old.

- His resources and facilities are limited.

- Akatsuki has not yet been formally established.

- The AI is not yet operational.

- Do not introduce ASID or premature major security threats.

- Preserve all established Project Knowledge and continuity decisions.

### Final Instructions

Write the complete chapter as polished novel prose, not an outline, summary, or screenplay.

Follow the approved scene progression while retaining freedom to write natural dialogue, transitions, and incidental details.

Do not introduce major developments belonging to later chapters.

Do not provide an introduction, explanation, or outline before the chapter. Deliver the chapter directly.
