Draft Chapter 3 of *Road to the Stars*, titled **Proof of Concept**.

Use the approved **Chapter 3 — Proof of Concept: Scene-by-Scene Drafting Brief** and all applicable Project Knowledge documents.

Follow the Project Instructions and Writing and Style Guide strictly.

### Writing Requirements

- Write in first-person POV from Himura Kenji's perspective.

- Use past tense throughout.

- Continue naturally from Chapter 2 without repeating its events or unnecessarily recapping them.

- Follow the five-scene progression in the approved drafting brief.

- Begin with active fabrication, assembly, or another meaningful task.

- Show Kenji turning his Med-Pod designs into physical components.

- Demonstrate the practical use of his specialized AI in engineering calculations, diagnostics, and testing.

- Show individual subsystem testing before integration.

- Include credible integration and calibration problems.

- Demonstrate Kenji's ability to diagnose and resolve those problems.

- Complete the first functional Med-Pod prototype.

- End with a successful controlled proof-of-concept demonstration and recognition of the remaining development requirements.

### Characterization

Kenji must remain intelligent, capable, confident, pragmatic, independent, resourceful, and proactive.

Show his technical competence through his decisions and actions.

He should be systematic without becoming excessively cautious, and confident without being reckless.

Do not give him contrived failures or make him struggle with problems he should reasonably be able to solve.

### Technology and AI Requirements

The Med-Pod must be a genuine technological achievement.

Its initial prototype should work, but it should not be instantly perfected or commercially ready.

The specialized AI remains non-sapient. It can assist with calculations, simulations, diagnostics, and data analysis, but Kenji remains responsible for engineering decisions and implementation.

Do not make the AI omniscient or capable of independently solving every problem.

Keep technical explanations understandable and relevant to the immediate scene. Do not turn the chapter into an engineering manual.

### Prose and Pacing

- Write natural, engaging novel prose.

- Use simple mixed tone.

- Keep descriptions concise and relevant.

- Integrate technical details into the action.

- Use natural dialogue where appropriate.

- Avoid excessive introspection and internal monologues.

- Avoid repetitive explanations, artificial drama, and unnecessary setbacks.

- Maintain forward narrative momentum.

### Continuity Restrictions

- The story takes place in 2137.

- Kenji is twenty years old.

- The workshop remains a modest inherited facility.

- Akatsuki has not yet been formally established.

- The AI remains specialized and non-sapient.

- The Med-Pod prototype is functional by the end of the chapter.

- The prototype has not undergone proper clinical validation or received commercial approval.

- Do not introduce human clinical trials without appropriate professional oversight and safety procedures.

- Do not introduce ASID or major security threats prematurely.

- Preserve all established Project Knowledge and continuity decisions.

### Final Instructions

Write the complete chapter as polished novel prose, not an outline, summary, or screenplay.

Follow the approved scene progression while retaining freedom to develop natural dialogue, transitions, and incidental details.

Do not introduce major developments belonging to later chapters.

Do not provide an introduction, explanation, or outline before the chapter. Deliver the chapter directly.
