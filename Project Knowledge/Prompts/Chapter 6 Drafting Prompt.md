# Claude Drafting Prompt — Chapter 6: The Company We've Built

Write Chapter 6 of _Road to the Stars_, titled **"The Company We've Built."**

Follow the attached Chapter 6 Scene-by-Scene Drafting Brief and the current Project Knowledge documents.

## Mandatory Continuity

- Year: 2148.
    
- Kenji is approximately 31 years old.
    
- Eleven years have passed since the events of Chapter 5.
    
- Akatsuki Technologies has grown into a major medical, technological, industrial, and defense corporation.
    
- Akatsuki uses advanced automation with delegated AI authority.
    
- Specialized AI systems and droids handle most routine corporate and industrial operations.
    
- Human employees retain strategic authority, professional judgment, leadership, external relations, and accountability.
    
- Kenji has six specialized personal AI assistants and human personal staff.
    
- His Chief of Staff has genuine authority to challenge him.
    
- Kenji has personal medical enhancements.
    
- ASID is an established security and intelligence organization.
    
- Callisto is an established corporate settlement.
    
- Akatsuki has a real defense business, with customers, contracts, deployed systems, and industrial capabilities.
    
- Kenji has not yet consciously recognized the Mass Effect universe.
    

## Characterization

Write Kenji as an experienced founder and executive who has grown alongside his company.

He should be intelligent, pragmatic, technically knowledgeable, and comfortable delegating. He should not be portrayed as omniscient, emotionally detached, or capable of personally managing every part of Akatsuki.

His colleagues must have independent expertise and judgment. They should be able to disagree with him.

Maintain human relationships and a believable personal life. Do not make every interaction transactional.

## Technology and AI

Follow the Technology Reference.

Akatsuki's AI systems are:

- Specialized.
    
- Non-sapient.
    
- Extremely capable within their assigned roles.
    
- Deliberately limited outside their specializations.
    
- Built around immutable cores.
    
- Able to exercise delegated authority within defined limits.
    

Do not portray them as human personalities or unrestricted general-purpose intelligences.

Droids and AI systems should feel like normal parts of Akatsuki's operations, not futuristic novelties requiring constant explanation.

Show the company's advanced automation through scenes and interactions.

## Private Systems

Kenji's Engineering Assistant and Knowledge Management AI are private systems isolated from Akatsuki's corporate network.

Kenji selectively releases validated technical packages to corporate researchers and engineers.

Do not give ASID or corporate administrators unrestricted access to these systems.

## Security

ASID should appear as a mature professional organization dealing with corporate security, counterintelligence, executive protection, and threats to Akatsuki's intellectual property.

Do not introduce explicit Reaper preparations, Prothean investigations, or alien intelligence in this chapter.

## Narrative Style

- First-person, Kenji's POV.
    
- Grounded and character-driven.
    
- Technically credible without excessive exposition.
    
- Natural dialogue.
    
- Active scene openings.
    
- Varied scene endings.
    
- Avoid repetitive checklist-style conclusions.
    
- Avoid long corporate histories and organizational catalogues.
    
- Preserve the established writing style of Chapters 1–5 while applying the lessons from their evaluation: stronger interpersonal friction, more varied scene rhythms, and a convincing sense of the futuristic setting.
    

## Strict Restrictions

1. Do not reveal that Kenji recognizes the Mass Effect universe.
    
2. Do not mention Eden Prime.
    
3. Do not introduce alien characters or direct alien contact.
    
4. Do not portray Akatsuki as a sovereign state or mature interstellar navy.
    
5. Do not assume the personal battle droids or complete memory-transfer technology are operational.
    
6. Do not invent major changes to established canon or previously approved chapters.
    
7. Do not turn this chapter into a corporate technology catalogue.
    

Write the complete chapter, not an outline or summary. Aim for approximately 3,500–4,500 words, but prioritize natural pacing and scene quality over hitting a word count.

After drafting, provide only the chapter manuscript. Do not include commentary about your writing process.
