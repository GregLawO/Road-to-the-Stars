Draft Chapter 2 of *Road to the Stars*, titled **Building the Foundation**.

Use the approved **Chapter 2 — Building the Foundation: Scene-by-Scene Drafting Brief** and all applicable Project Knowledge documents.

Follow the Project Instructions and Writing and Style Guide strictly.

### Writing Requirements

- Write in first-person POV from Himura Kenji's perspective.

- Use past tense throughout.

- Continue naturally from Chapter 1 without repeating its events or unnecessarily recapping them.

- Follow the five-scene progression in the approved drafting brief.

- Develop Kenji's specialized non-sapient AI system.

- Demonstrate its practical usefulness through engineering calculations, data organization, design assistance, and technical analysis.

- Show Kenji testing and verifying the AI's results.

- Establish the limitations of his initial hardware and computational resources.

- Show incremental improvements to his workshop and basic automation.

- Return to the Med-Pod project and demonstrate meaningful progress in its development.

- End with Kenji preparing to fabricate and integrate the Med-Pod's major components.

### Characterization

Kenji must remain intelligent, capable, confident, pragmatic, independent, resourceful, and proactive.

Show his preference for automation through practical decisions rather than repeatedly explaining it.

He should use the AI as a productivity tool, not depend on it blindly.

### AI Restrictions

The AI must remain specialized and non-sapient.

It must not:

- Develop consciousness or a personality.

- Behave like a human companion.

- Become an omniscient assistant.

- Solve every engineering problem automatically.

- Replace Kenji's own technical judgment.

Its capabilities must remain appropriate to its hardware, software, and available information.

### Prose and Pacing

- Write natural, engaging novel prose.

- Use simple mixed tone.

- Keep descriptions concise and relevant.

- Use dialogue where it contributes to the story.

- Integrate technical explanations naturally into scenes.

- Avoid excessive introspection and repetitive technical details.

- Do not turn the chapter into a technical manual.

- Avoid artificial setbacks and unnecessary drama.

- Maintain forward narrative momentum.

### Continuity Restrictions

- The story takes place in 2137.

- Kenji remains twenty years old.

- His workshop remains a modest inherited facility.

- Akatsuki has not yet been formally established.

- The AI becomes functional but remains limited.

- Workshop improvements must be proportionate to his resources.

- The Med-Pod remains under development.

- No integrated functional Med-Pod prototype is completed in this chapter.

- Do not introduce ASID or major security threats prematurely.

- Preserve all established Project Knowledge and continuity decisions.

### Final Instructions

Write the complete chapter as polished novel prose, not an outline, summary, or screenplay.

Follow the approved scene progression while retaining freedom to develop natural dialogue, transitions, and incidental details.

Do not introduce major developments belonging to later chapters.

Do not provide an introduction, explanation, or outline before the chapter. Deliver the chapter directly.
