# Claude Drafting Prompt — Chapter 7: A Place Among the Stars

Write Chapter 7 of _Road to the Stars_, titled **"A Place Among the Stars."**

Follow the attached Chapter 7 Scene-by-Scene Drafting Brief and the current Project Knowledge documents.

## Mandatory Continuity

- Year: 2148.
    
- Kenji is approximately 31 years old.
    
- Callisto is an established Akatsuki corporate settlement.
    
- It is a genuine inhabited and industrialized off-world settlement, not merely a research outpost or military base.
    
- Its operations include civilian habitats, research, manufacturing, mining and resource processing, power generation, transport, logistics, and spacecraft maintenance or construction.
    
- Callisto is corporate territory in the practical sense of being operated by Akatsuki, but it is not a sovereign state.
    
- Akatsuki's AI and droid workforce performs most routine operational work.
    
- Human personnel remain important in technical, leadership, research, interpersonal, and exceptional operational roles.
    
- Akatsuki remains part of the wider human economy and political environment.
    
- Kenji has not yet recognized the Mass Effect universe.
    

## Portrayal of Callisto

Make Callisto feel like a functioning place where people live and work.

Include practical details of life in a Jovian system:

- Radiation protection.
    
- Habitats and life-support infrastructure.
    
- Transportation.
    
- Power and industrial systems.
    
- Supply chains and maintenance.
    
- Social spaces and ordinary routines.
    

Do not turn the chapter into a technical tour or an encyclopedia entry.

The settlement should not be implausibly self-sufficient. Its operations still depend on logistics, replacement components, maintenance, and external trade.

## Characterization and Conflict

Kenji should be familiar with Callisto and its operations.

He should not behave like a tourist discovering space for the first time.

Include interactions with a small number of human personnel who have their own expertise, responsibilities, and opinions.

At least one disagreement should have a legitimate basis. Kenji should listen, assess the issue, and make a decision rather than automatically imposing his preference.

Show that building a community requires attention to human needs, not just industrial output.

## Technology and Automation

Follow the Technology Reference.

AI systems are specialized, non-sapient, and operate under delegated authority. Droids handle most routine industrial and logistical work.

Do not portray automation as eliminating every operational problem. Equipment wears down, supply chains have constraints, and unusual circumstances require human judgment.

Use technology as part of the setting rather than repeatedly explaining how it works.

## Strict Restrictions

1. Do not introduce Amatsu or Shanxi.
    
2. Do not mention Eden Prime.
    
3. Do not reveal Kenji's recognition of the Mass Effect universe.
    
4. Do not introduce alien characters or direct alien contact.
    
5. Do not portray Callisto as a sovereign state or military installation.
    
6. Do not invent a major catastrophe simply to create drama.
    
7. Do not make every human character agree with Kenji.
    
8. Do not contradict the established chronology or previously approved chapters.
    

## Narrative Style

- First-person, Kenji's POV.
    
- Grounded, character-driven prose.
    
- Natural dialogue and purposeful interactions.
    
- Active scene openings.
    
- Varied scene rhythms and endings.
    
- Avoid repetitive technical checklists.
    
- Avoid long exposition and corporate promotional language.
    
- Maintain continuity with Chapters 1–6.
    

Write the complete chapter, not an outline or summary. Aim for approximately 3,000–4,000 words, prioritizing natural pacing and scene quality.

After drafting, provide only the chapter manuscript. Do not include commentary about your writing process.
