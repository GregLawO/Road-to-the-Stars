Draft Chapter 4 of *Road to the Stars*, titled **Establishing Akatsuki**.

Use the approved **Chapter 4 — Establishing Akatsuki: Scene-by-Scene Drafting Brief** and all applicable Project Knowledge documents.

Follow the Project Instructions and Writing and Style Guide strictly.

### Writing Requirements

- Write in first-person POV from Himura Kenji's perspective.

- Use past tense throughout.

- Continue naturally from Chapter 3 without repeating its events or unnecessarily recapping them.

- Follow the five-scene progression in the approved drafting brief.

- Establish why Kenji needs a formal business organization to continue developing his technology.

- Have him formally establish Akatsuki Technologies in 2137.

- Show the practical legal, financial, and organizational decisions involved.

- Establish basic intellectual property protection and research confidentiality.

- Develop an initial business and funding strategy.

- Show Kenji identifying the professional expertise and personnel the company needs.

- End with Akatsuki beginning its transition from a one-person operation into a small professional organization.

### Characterization

Kenji must remain intelligent, capable, confident, pragmatic, independent, resourceful, and proactive.

He should be comfortable making decisions and negotiating.

He should recognize the value of professional expertise and delegation without suddenly becoming an expert in every aspect of corporate management.

Do not portray him as indecisive or dependent on others to make basic decisions.

### Business Requirements

Akatsuki Technologies begins as a small technology startup.

Its initial resources are limited, and its primary focus is medical technology.

Keep the company's early organizational structure simple. Do not invent elaborate departments, executive hierarchies, or corporate bureaucracy.

Make business decisions understandable and relevant to the narrative. Do not turn the chapter into a startup-management guide or a lengthy legal and financial explanation.

Supporting characters introduced as employees, advisers, or prospective recruits must have credible expertise, motivations, and professional agency.

### Prose and Pacing

- Write natural, engaging novel prose.

- Use simple mixed tone.

- Keep descriptions concise and relevant.

- Use natural dialogue, especially during business discussions and negotiations.

- Integrate business and legal details into the action.

- Avoid lengthy paperwork scenes, excessive exposition, and repetitive explanations.

- Avoid unnecessary introspection and artificial drama.

- Maintain forward narrative momentum.

### Continuity Restrictions

- The story takes place in 2137.

- Kenji is twenty years old.

- The Med-Pod prototype was successfully demonstrated in Chapter 3.

- The prototype is not yet clinically validated or commercially ready.

- Akatsuki Technologies is formally established in this chapter, not earlier.

- Akatsuki remains a small startup with limited resources.

- Kenji remains its founder and central strategic decision-maker.

- The company begins with a medical technology focus.

- Do not establish ASID yet.

- Do not introduce major kidnapping, assassination, or espionage incidents.

- Preserve all established Project Knowledge and continuity decisions.

### Final Instructions

Write the complete chapter as polished novel prose, not an outline, summary, or screenplay.

Follow the approved scene progression while retaining freedom to develop natural dialogue, transitions, and incidental details.

Do not introduce major developments belonging to later chapters.

Do not provide an introduction, explanation, or outline before the chapter. Deliver the chapter directly.
