### Writing Requirements

- Write in first-person POV from Himura Kenji's perspective.
    
- Use past tense throughout.
    
- Continue naturally from Chapter 4 without repeating its events or unnecessarily recapping them.
    
- Follow the five-scene progression in the approved drafting brief.
    
- Show Akatsuki beginning its actual business operations.
    
- Develop Kenji's efforts to recruit or engage qualified medical, engineering, and business professionals.
    
- Arrange an independent professional evaluation of the Med-Pod.
    
- Give the medical professionals credible questions, concerns, and independent opinions.
    
- Show Kenji responding constructively to professional feedback.
    
- Establish a practical development and validation roadmap.
    
- Introduce Akatsuki's first meaningful commercial opportunity.
    
- Include business discussions and negotiations that demonstrate Kenji's commercial judgment.
    
- End with Akatsuki having made tangible progress as a functioning medical technology company.
    

### Characterization

Kenji must remain intelligent, capable, confident, pragmatic, independent, resourceful, and proactive.

He should be comfortable negotiating and making decisions while recognizing the importance of qualified professionals.

He must not automatically get his way. Other characters should be allowed to disagree, challenge his assumptions, and make independent decisions.

Supporting characters must feel like actual professionals with their own expertise, motivations, and judgment. Do not make them unquestioning admirers of Kenji.

### Medical and Technical Requirements

The Med-Pod is functional but has not yet undergone comprehensive clinical validation.

Distinguish clearly between:

- Demonstrating that the machine functions.
    
- Establishing that its diagnostic and treatment capabilities are reliable.
    
- Validating its safety and effectiveness through appropriate testing.
    
- Obtaining the necessary regulatory approvals for clinical deployment and commercial sale.
    

Professional evaluation must not be portrayed as automatic approval.

The AI can assist with analysis, calculations, diagnostics, and documentation, but it does not replace medical expertise.

Do not portray the Med-Pod as a universal cure or make it instantly capable of treating every medical condition.

### Business Requirements

Akatsuki remains a small startup with limited resources.

Its first commercial opportunity must be credible and proportionate to its current capabilities.

Do not give it enormous contracts, immediate mass-production capacity, or sudden corporate wealth.

Keep the company's organizational structure simple and appropriate to its current size.

Make negotiations and business relationships meaningful to the narrative without turning the chapter into a business manual.

### Prose and Pacing

- Write natural, engaging novel prose.
    
- Use simple mixed tone.
    
- Begin with an active situation rather than scenery or background exposition.
    
- Keep descriptions concise and relevant.
    
- Use natural dialogue.
    
- Integrate technical and commercial explanations into scenes.
    
- Avoid excessive introspection, repetitive explanations, and unnecessary exposition.
    
- Avoid artificial drama and contrived setbacks.
    
- Maintain forward narrative momentum.
    

### Continuity Restrictions

- The story takes place in 2137.
    
- Kenji is twenty years old.
    
- Kenji remains in first-person POV and past tense.
    
- Akatsuki Technologies was formally established in Chapter 4.
    
- The company remains a small startup.
    
- The Med-Pod prototype was successfully demonstrated in Chapter 3.
    
- The prototype is not yet clinically validated or commercially approved.
    
- Do not establish ASID or introduce major security threats prematurely.
    
- Preserve all established Project Knowledge and continuity decisions.
