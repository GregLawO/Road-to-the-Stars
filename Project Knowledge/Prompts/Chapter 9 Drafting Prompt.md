# Claude Drafting Prompt — Chapter 9: Among Our Own

Write Chapter 9 of _Road to the Stars_, titled **"Among Our Own."**

Follow the attached Chapter 9 Scene-by-Scene Drafting Brief and the current Project Knowledge documents.

## Chapter Purpose

This chapter focuses on Kenji's personal and social life in 2148.

The previous chapters have established:

- Akatsuki's growth into a major corporation.
    
- Its extensive automation and human workforce.
    
- Its established settlement on Callisto.
    
- Its operational corvettes and experimental frigate.
    

Now show the person behind the organization.

Kenji must not feel like a hermit whose only relationships are professional or transactional.

## Characterization

Kenji is approximately 31 years old.

He is successful, intelligent, and accustomed to responsibility, but he remains a human being with personal interests, humor, friendships, and ordinary needs.

He should be comfortable interacting with people without constantly thinking about business.

His Chief of Staff and other human staff help him maintain a personal life, but they do not manufacture artificial friendships for him.

Do not make Kenji emotionally detached, socially incompetent, or universally admired.

## Supporting Characters

Use a manageable cast.

Characters should:

- Have independent personalities.
    
- Possess their own ambitions and concerns.
    
- Disagree with Kenji when appropriate.
    
- Interact with one another independently of him.
    
- Treat him as a person rather than merely the founder of Akatsuki.
    

Use established characters where appropriate, but do not force existing characters into scenes where they do not naturally belong.

Do not invent extensive biographies for new supporting characters.

## Scene and Dialogue Requirements

Build the chapter around a social gathering or personal occasion.

Include:

- Natural conversation.
    
- Humor and warmth.
    
- At least one meaningful disagreement.
    
- A personal conversation about the changes in Kenji's life.
    
- An ordinary activity or shared experience unrelated to corporate work.
    

Do not turn the gathering into a disguised executive meeting.

Corporate matters may come up naturally, but they should not dominate the chapter.

Avoid long philosophical monologues and forced emotional revelations.

## Continuity and Technology

Maintain the established 2148 setting.

Kenji has six specialized personal AI assistants and human personal staff. The AI systems can support his schedule and administrative needs, but they do not replace human companionship.

Do not introduce operational personal battle droids or completed memory-transfer technology unless explicitly established in approved continuity.

Do not introduce Amatsu or Shanxi.

Kenji has not yet recognized the Mass Effect universe.

## Strict Restrictions

1. No alien characters or direct alien contact.
    
2. Do not mention Eden Prime.
    
3. Do not reveal Kenji's recognition of the Mass Effect universe.
    
4. Do not turn the chapter into a corporate progress report.
    
5. Do not make every character defer to Kenji.
    
6. Do not introduce an elaborate romance or major personal subplot without established groundwork.
    
7. Do not contradict Chapters 1–8.
    

## Narrative Style

- First-person, Kenji's POV.
    
- Natural, dialogue-driven prose.
    
- Character-focused and emotionally grounded.
    
- Use humor and understated warmth.
    
- Give conversations room to breathe without becoming repetitive.
    
- Avoid exposition disguised as dialogue.
    
- Maintain continuity with the established writing style.
    

Write the complete chapter, not an outline or summary. Aim for approximately 2,500–3,500 words, prioritizing natural character interactions and pacing.

After drafting, provide only the chapter manuscript. Do not include commentary about your writing process.
