# Claude Drafting Prompt — Chapter 10: The Universe Just Got Bigger

Write Chapter 10 of _Road to the Stars_, titled **"The Universe Just Got Bigger."**

Follow the attached Chapter 10 Scene-by-Scene Drafting Brief and the current Project Knowledge documents.

## Core Event

In 2148, the discovery of the object in the Pluto system known as the Charon Relay triggers Kenji's realization that he is living in the Mass Effect universe.

This is the first time since his awakening in 2137 that he consciously recognizes the setting.

The realization must occur only after the evidence establishes that the object is artificial and extraordinary.

## Discovery and Verification

Present the discovery through a credible scientific process.

Researchers should:

- Identify an anomalous object.
    
- Gather observations.
    
- Evaluate competing explanations.
    
- Establish that it is artificial.
    
- Recognize that its construction and apparent function are beyond known human engineering.
    

Do not have researchers immediately identify the Protheans, the Citadel, or the wider relay network.

Kenji should initially approach the discovery as a scientific and corporate development.

Only when the evidence becomes sufficiently clear should he recognize the Charon Relay.

## Kenji's Realization

The realization should be significant but restrained.

He understands that:

- The Mass Effect setting is real.
    
- Humanity is approaching contact with other civilizations.
    
- His memories of the games and setting may provide useful context.
    
- The future he remembers is not guaranteed to happen exactly as he remembers it.
    
- Akatsuki's technological development has already changed the timeline in unknown ways.
    

Do not give him perfect recall of every plot event, character, faction, or technology.

He has memories, not an infallible encyclopedia.

Avoid an extended internal monologue reciting Mass Effect lore.

## Immediate Response

Kenji should behave like an experienced executive facing an extraordinary discovery.

He should:

- Secure the relevant information.
    
- Authorize additional investigation.
    
- Ensure the scientific process remains credible.
    
- Consider the implications for Akatsuki and humanity.
    
- Avoid publicly making claims he cannot substantiate.
    

He should not immediately reveal his memories or make an implausible announcement about future galactic events.

## Strategic Implications

Allow the discovery to begin changing his priorities.

He may start considering:

- Extraterrestrial civilizations.
    
- Advanced technology beyond humanity's current capabilities.
    
- The implications of eventual contact.
    
- Independent FTL development.
    
- Intelligence and security requirements.
    
- Humanity's technological and strategic vulnerabilities.
    

However, this is only the beginning of his reassessment.

Do not have him immediately launch a comprehensive Reaper War preparation programme.

The next chapters will address the consequences of this realization.

## Character Interaction

Include at least one meaningful conversation with a trusted human character.

The other person should recognize that the discovery has affected Kenji.

Kenji may withhold his private knowledge, but he should not become completely isolated or incapable of interacting normally.

Avoid making the other character merely a device for exposition.

## Strict Continuity Restrictions

1. The year is 2148.
    
2. Kenji is approximately 31.
    
3. The Charon Relay is discovered in the Pluto system.
    
4. Kenji has not consciously recognized Mass Effect before this event.
    
5. No direct alien contact occurs in this chapter.
    
6. Do not introduce Amatsu or establish the Shanxi settlement.
    
7. Do not mention Eden Prime in any narrative form.
    
8. Do not contradict the established fleet, company, technology, or Callisto continuity.
    
9. Do not assume that Kenji's memories provide perfect knowledge of the future.
    
10. Do not portray the relay discovery as an immediate understanding of the entire galactic situation.
    

## Narrative Style

- First-person, Kenji's POV.
    
- Grounded, character-driven prose.
    
- Scientific caution and credible discovery procedures.
    
- Natural dialogue.
    
- Restrained emotional reaction.
    
- Avoid lore dumps and repetitive technical explanations.
    
- Maintain continuity with Chapters 1–9.
    
- Let the revelation change the story's direction without abandoning its established tone.
    

Write the complete chapter, not an outline or summary. Aim for approximately 3,500–4,500 words, prioritizing natural pacing and the significance of the discovery.

After drafting, provide only the chapter manuscript. Do not include commentary about your writing process.
