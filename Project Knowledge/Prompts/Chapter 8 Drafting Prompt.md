# Claude Drafting Prompt — Chapter 8: The Fleet We've Built

Write Chapter 8 of _Road to the Stars_, titled **"The Fleet We've Built."**

Follow the attached Chapter 8 Scene-by-Scene Drafting Brief and the current Project Knowledge documents.

## Mandatory Fleet Continuity

The year is 2148.

Akatsuki has:

- Six operational corvettes, each approximately 250 metres long.
    
- One experimental frigate, approximately 500 metres long, undergoing trials.
    

The six corvettes are operational vessels with actual service experience. Their work has included commercial convoy escort, anti-piracy operations, protection of isolated industrial and mining facilities, attempted hijacking responses, and interdiction of pirate staging areas.

The 500-metre frigate is not commissioned or combat-ready. It is an experimental vessel and technology demonstrator.

Do not add cruisers, additional frigates, or a larger fleet.

## The Frigate and Gravitic FTL

The frigate is the first major testbed for Akatsuki's Gravitic Spacetime Drive.

Its testing programme includes:

- Propulsion and systems integration.
    
- Gravitic field calibration.
    
- Controlled drive activation.
    
- Short-range FTL tests.
    
- Navigation and emergence accuracy.
    
- Extended testing and reliability assessment.
    
- Emergency shutdown and recovery.
    

A successful first activation or short-range test does not mean the drive is mature, reliable, or ready for widespread deployment.

Do not give Akatsuki a fully operational fleet of FTL-equipped warships in 2148.

## Character and Organizational Continuity

Kenji is an experienced corporate leader and technically knowledgeable founder.

He should not personally command the fleet or replace professional military personnel in their duties.

Show the human crews, engineers, and military specialists who operate and maintain these vessels.

Akatsuki's AI and droid workforce handles most routine industrial, administrative, and logistical tasks, but human personnel retain professional judgment, command authority, and responsibility for high-consequence decisions.

AI systems are specialized, non-sapient, and operate within delegated authority.

## Tone and Presentation

This chapter should feel like a working day around an established spacecraft operation.

Show the vessels and their capabilities through scenes, conversations, decisions, and testing activity.

Avoid:

- A fleet specification catalogue.
    
- A long technical lecture about the drive.
    
- Treating the corvettes as invincible.
    
- Treating the experimental frigate as a completed superweapon.
    
- Making Kenji the fleet's tactical commander.
    
- Inventing a major battle merely to demonstrate the ships' capabilities.
    

Operational risks and past losses may be acknowledged where appropriate.

## Strict Restrictions

1. No alien characters or direct alien contact.
    
2. No mention of Eden Prime.
    
3. Do not introduce Amatsu or Shanxi.
    
4. Do not reveal Kenji's recognition of the Mass Effect universe.
    
5. Do not contradict the established six-corvette and one-experimental-frigate fleet.
    
6. Do not portray the experimental frigate as commissioned or combat-ready.
    
7. Do not assume the personal battle droids or memory-transfer technology are operational.
    

## Narrative Style

- First-person, Kenji's POV.
    
- Grounded, character-driven prose.
    
- Natural dialogue.
    
- Technically credible without excessive exposition.
    
- Meaningful interaction and professional disagreement.
    
- Varied scene endings.
    
- Maintain continuity with Chapters 1–7.
    

Write the complete chapter, not an outline or summary. Aim for approximately 3,500–4,500 words, prioritizing natural pacing and scene quality.

After drafting, provide only the chapter manuscript. Do not include commentary about your writing process.
