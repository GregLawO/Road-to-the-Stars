#### Chapter 12: Things Best Left Undisturbed

**Arc:** II — Hidden War  
**Period:** 2150–2152  
**Primary POV:** Himura Kenji, first person  
**Tense:** Past  
**Primary Setting:** Akatsuki Shanxi Staging Operations, ASID Secure Briefing Room, Survey Sciences Facility  
**Estimated Structure:** 4 scenes  
**Chapter Function:** Depict the physical establishment of Akatsuki’s commercial and industrial settlement in the Shanxi system, the quiet intelligence operation that neutralizes the Arca Monolith before human exposure, and the initial survey planning for the planet Kenji names Amatsu.

---

#### 1. Chapter Objectives

By the end of this chapter, the reader should understand:

- **Shanxi Commercial Expansion:** Akatsuki’s settlement on Shanxi takes physical shape as a genuine industrial and commercial hub (mining, manufacturing, logistics), situated on the opposite side of the planet from the main human colony and explicitly distinct from a military base.
- **Neutralization of the Arca Monolith:** ASID uncovers and quietly neutralizes the Arca Monolith in the Shanxi system around 2152. The operation is presented indirectly via an executive intelligence briefing—nobody is exposed or indoctrinated, and Jack Harper’s group never encounters the artifact.
- **Amatsu Survey & Naming:** Early orbital survey returns arrive for the garden world that Kenji officially names Amatsu (天園, "Heavenly Garden"). Colonization logistics are scheduled for 2153 onward, with Kenji remaining unaware of the hidden Prothean beacon.
- **Institutional Security & Human Balance:** Demonstrates how ASID's expanded remit functions in practice alongside ongoing corporate expansion, while Kenji maintains his human personal anchors.

---

#### 2. Scene One — Industrial Groundwork on Shanxi (2150–2151)

**Purpose:** Establish the reality of Akatsuki's Shanxi settlement as a commercial and industrial engine.  
**POV:** Kenji, first person  
**Setting:** Akatsuki Shanxi Settlement Staging & Operations

##### Opening Situation

Kenji reviews operational telemetry and logistics reports as Akatsuki’s industrial facilities on Shanxi become fully active.

##### Events and Actions

- Kenji reviews site progress with local operations and logistics managers.
- Heavy automated machinery, resource extraction grids, and processing plants are established.
- The narrative explicitly reinforces that the settlement is a legitimate commercial enterprise on the opposite side of the planet from the main Alliance settlement, providing genuine economic justification for Akatsuki’s presence.
- Practical logistical friction (equipment transit times, environmental seal maintenance, supply line delays from the Sol System) grounds the expansion in physical reality.

##### Important Details & Characterization

- Kenji delegates operational management while ensuring corporate standards are maintained.
- Local administrators exercise independent judgment regarding construction priorities.

**Transition:** From industrial expansion on the surface, the focus shifts to security and intelligence operations in the system.

---

#### 3. Scene Two — The Silent Perimeter (2152)

**Purpose:** Convey the discovery and quiet destruction of the Arca Monolith without artificial action sequences or artifact exposure.  
**POV:** Kenji, first person  
**Setting:** ASID Secure Briefing Room

##### Opening Situation

Director Kateryna Hrytsenko delivers an after-action intelligence briefing in ASID’s secure suite regarding an anomalous alien artifact detected during deep system survey sweeps.

##### Events and Actions

- Hrytsenko reports that ASID survey teams identified an active, anomalous alien structure (the Arca Monolith) in the Shanxi system.
- ASID executed a cold, compartmentalized containment operation, destroying and neutralizing the object completely.
- Hrytsenko confirms zero personnel were exposed to prolonged field emissions or psychological impairment.
- Jack Harper, Ben Hislop, and Eva Coré are noted in background intelligence files as unrelated regional figures, completely uninvolved with the site.
- Kenji approves the closure of the file, reflecting on the silent, unseen hazards present in unexplored space.

##### Important Continuity Rules

- The event appears strictly through an operational briefing, not a dedicated action chapter.
- No characters suffer indoctrination or exposure.
- ASID remains an intelligence organization executing routine containment, not an all-knowing entity.

**Transition:** With deep-system hazards cleared, Kenji turns to long-term colonization planning.

---

#### 4. Scene Three — Naming Heavenly Garden (2152)

**Purpose:** Establish the initial survey and naming of Amatsu for future colonization.  
**POV:** Kenji, first person  
**Setting:** Survey Sciences Facility

##### Opening Situation

Dr. Elena Voss and Marcus Hale present refined environmental and orbital survey returns for a prime garden world discovered along survey routes.

##### Events and Actions

- Voss presents data showing ideal atmospheric, soil, and biological baselines for long-term human habitation.
- Kenji formally registers the world under the name **Amatsu (天園)**, meaning "Heavenly Garden."
- Kenji selects the name for personal and practical reasons; he does NOT know the planet contains a hidden Prothean beacon.
- Preliminary logistics and transport schedules for 2153–2155 colonization are framed.

##### Important Continuity Rules

- **Eden Prime Rule:** The original Mass Effect planet name _Eden Prime_ must NEVER appear anywhere in narrative prose, dialogue, or internal thoughts.
- Amatsu must be presented as an emerging survey prospect, not an instantly fully-built planetary capital.

**Transition:** Planning complete, Kenji anchors himself back in his everyday personal life.

---

#### 5. Scene Four — The Weight of Unseen Work (Late 2152)

**Purpose:** Close the chapter with executive balance and human perspective.  
**POV:** Kenji, first person  
**Setting:** Himura Auto / Residence Kitchen

##### Opening Situation

Kenji spends an evening away from executive suites, balancing long-term strategic burdens against human relationships.

##### Events and Actions

- Brief interaction with Helena Vasquez or Dolores, enforcing personal discipline and diet/rest routines.
- Kenji reviews the 2152 operational summary: Shanxi industrial output is scaling, the Arca Monolith threat is permanently erased, and Amatsu colonization prep is underway.
- Reflective closing on how civilizational survival depends as much on the threats quietly eliminated as the structures visibly built.

#### 7. Chapter-Wide Characterization Requirements

##### Kenji

Portray him as analytical, pragmatic, and capable. He delegates operations, respects intelligence boundaries, and accepts the quiet necessity of security operations without becoming paranoid or autocratic.

##### Supporting Characters

- **Kateryna Hrytsenko:** Direct, clinical, and precise; treats artifact destruction as a cold matter of corporate security.
- **Dr. Elena Voss:** Methodical and scientifically rigorous when evaluating Amatsu's survey data.
- **Marcus Hale:** Focused on engineering feasibility and resource constraints.
- **Helena Vasquez:** Authoritative Chief of Staff who maintains Kenji's operational discipline.

---

#### 8. Style and Pacing Requirements

1. Write in first-person past tense throughout.
2. Begin immediately with operational activity.
3. Show expansion through concrete logistics, survey reports, and intelligence briefings.
4. Maintain natural dialogue with distinct character voices.
5. Avoid non-Mass Effect franchise terms (_Halo, Elysium, Terminator, Protoss, T-800_).
6. Apply the **Eden Prime Rule**: The original Mass Effect planet name _Eden Prime_ must NEVER appear in narrative prose, dialogue, or internal thoughts.
7. Ensure all AI systems operate as specialized, non-sapient tools with immutable cores.
