# ROAD TO THE STARS

# CHARACTER REFERENCE

**Document Status:** Revised Working Canon  
**Continuity Coverage:** 2137–2157 and established future roles  
**Primary Protagonist:** Himura Kenji

---

# I. HIMURA KENJI

## Basic Information

- Full Name: Himura Kenji
    
- Nationality: American
    
- Ancestry: Japanese
    
- Age at Awakening: 20
    
- Awakening Year: 2137
    
- Company: Akatsuki Technologies
    
- Position: Founder and Chief Executive
    

## Background

Kenji awakens in 2137 with memories from another life.

He is an orphan living in a house inherited from his deceased mother, Linda Himura.

He also inherits her automotive repair shop, which becomes the foundation of his early business activities.

His initial circumstances are modest. He possesses knowledge and technical advantages but must develop the resources, personnel, facilities, and infrastructure needed to exploit them.

His consciousness has merged with the memories of another life. This does not create a prolonged identity crisis, although his inherited memories influence his decisions and understanding of the world.

## Personality

Kenji is:

- Analytical.
    
- Pragmatic.
    
- Ambitious.
    
- Technically knowledgeable.
    
- Disciplined.
    
- Cautious when dealing with uncertain risks.
    
- Willing to delegate.
    
- Protective of his privacy and intellectual property.
    

He is not infallible.

His knowledge provides an advantage but does not guarantee that events will unfold as expected. He can misinterpret information, make incorrect assumptions, and underestimate people or institutions.

He values competent people and should be willing to accept disagreement from trusted colleagues.

His growing responsibilities can cause him to become overly focused on work and long-term objectives. Other characters should occasionally remind him that his personal life and existing responsibilities matter too.

## Knowledge and Memories

Kenji remembers selected technologies and fictional concepts from another life.

His principal technological inspirations include:

- Elysium: Advanced medical pods.
    
- Halo: Specialized non-sapient AI.
    
- Terminator: Advanced robotics.
    
- StarCraft: Gravitic technology.
    

These are private reference points for the author and do not establish that Kenji can immediately reproduce every associated technology.

His knowledge has limitations:

- Memories may be incomplete.
    
- Technical details may be missing.
    
- Some remembered concepts may be misunderstood.
    
- Manufacturing requires suitable materials, equipment, facilities, and expertise.
    
- Development requires experimentation, testing, and validation.
    
- Successful prototypes do not automatically become reliable production systems.
    

### Mass Effect Recognition

Before 2148, Kenji does not consciously recognize his new surroundings as the Mass Effect universe.

The discovery of the Charon Relay in 2148 triggers recognition.

Even then, his memories are incomplete. He recalls broad concepts and selected events but cannot reconstruct the entire setting, its history, or its future.

His knowledge must be distinguished from independently verified information.

He should not behave as though he possesses complete knowledge of every character, institution, event, or technological development.

## Personal Development

Around 2141–2143, Kenji begins using Akatsuki's medical technology to enhance himself.

His enhancements improve:

- Strength.
    
- Speed and reflexes.
    
- Coordination.
    
- Endurance.
    
- Recovery.
    
- Resistance to disease and injury.
    
- Sensory and cognitive performance.
    

He becomes near-superhuman compared with ordinary humans but remains vulnerable to sufficiently powerful weapons, explosions, environmental hazards, and advanced opponents.

He is not immortal or invulnerable.

## Personal Life

Kenji maintains both professional and personal relationships.

He does not isolate himself from humanity because of Akatsuki's automation.

He has human personal staff, colleagues, friends, acquaintances, and a social life.

His personal AI assistants supplement these relationships rather than replace them.

---

# II. LINDA HIMURA

## Basic Information

- Relationship: Kenji's deceased mother.
    
- Profession: Automotive mechanic.
    
- Legacy: Family home and automotive repair shop.
    

Linda operated a small but respectable automotive repair business for approximately 22 years.

Her shop provides Kenji with his initial income, tools, equipment, and practical foundation.

She remains meaningful to Kenji's personal history.

Her influence should be reflected naturally through memories, habits, practical values, and occasional references rather than an extended grief subplot.

# III. AKATSUKI TECHNOLOGIES — HUMAN LEADERSHIP

Akatsuki is built by capable people working alongside extensive AI and robotic systems.

Its human leadership remains important even though most routine corporate and industrial operations are automated.

Human executives and specialists retain responsibility for:

- Corporate strategy.
    
- Exceptional decisions.
    
- External representation.
    
- High-consequence judgment.
    
- Legal and ethical accountability.
    
- Important commercial and political relationships.
    
- Research direction and specialized technical judgment.
    

Supporting characters should have independent motivations and professional responsibilities.

## Gloria Castellanos

- Profession: Patent attorney.
    
- Role: Legal assistance during Akatsuki's establishment.
    
- First Appearance: Chapter 4.
    

Gloria assists Kenji with the legal foundations of Akatsuki Technologies.

Her role establishes the importance of intellectual property protection and professional legal representation.

She should retain independent professional standards and be willing to challenge questionable decisions.

## Walter Pruitt

- Profession: Accountant.
    
- Role: Financial and accounting assistance during Akatsuki's establishment.
    
- First Appearance: Chapter 4.
    

Walter helps Kenji establish the company's financial structure and understand the obligations associated with operating a formal corporation.

He should provide independent financial judgment rather than simply approve Kenji's decisions.

## Naomi Reyes

- Profession: Biomedical advisor.
    
- Role: Paid technical advisor.
    
- First Appearance: Chapter 4.
    

Naomi provides professional medical and biomedical insight during Akatsuki's early development.

She should be treated as a knowledgeable professional with her own judgment rather than simply a source of approval.

## Marcus Hale

- Profession: Engineer.
    
- Role: Lead engineer at Akatsuki during its early development.
    
- First Appearance: Chapter 4.
    

Marcus contributes practical engineering expertise to the company's early operations.

His professional judgment should remain distinct from Kenji's theoretical knowledge.

He must be capable of identifying weaknesses, proposing alternatives, and disagreeing with Kenji on engineering matters.

## Ines

- Profession: Bookkeeper.
    
- Role: Early accounting and administrative support.
    
- First Appearance: Chapter 4.
    

Ines supports Akatsuki's initial bookkeeping and financial administration.

Do not invent a larger biography or expanded role without narrative need.

## Priya Raman

- Profession: Medical technology consultant.
    
- Role: Independent evaluation and consulting.
    
- First Appearance: Chapter 5.
    

Priya evaluates Kenji's early medical technology and identifies technical and clinical limitations.

She is important because she provides independent professional scrutiny rather than accepting Kenji's claims without evidence.

## Evaluation Panel

The Chapter 5 evaluation panel includes:

- Adeyemi.
    
- Zhao.
    
- Brecht.
    

They evaluate the early medical technology and its potential applications.

Their individual backgrounds and future roles should be established only when the narrative requires them.

# IV. RECURRING PROFESSIONAL AND PERSONAL ASSOCIATES

These characters become relevant through Akatsuki's scientific, industrial, and personal activities.

Their exact positions and relationships should follow the established manuscripts. Do not invent additional formal titles or personal histories without narrative justification.

## Dolores

Dolores is an established personal acquaintance of Kenji.

Her interactions with him, including the work involving her Civic, provide an ordinary personal dimension to his life outside corporate strategy and technological development.

She should not be reduced to a source of exposition or a convenient emotional support character.

Her longer-term relationship with Kenji remains open.

## Helena

Helena is an established figure in Kenji's professional and personal circle.

She recognizes when Kenji becomes overly absorbed in long-term objectives and is willing to challenge him.

Her intervention in Chapter 10 demonstrates that she can disagree with Kenji and influence his decisions.

She should remain capable of exercising independent judgment.

Her precise formal position and longer-term relationship with Kenji should follow the manuscript.

## Elena Voss

Elena is associated with Akatsuki's Survey Sciences activities.

She participates in the investigation of the Charon structure in Chapter 10.

She approaches unfamiliar discoveries carefully and professionally.

Her scientific judgment should remain independent of Kenji's personal recognition of the structure.

## Kateryna

Kateryna participates in the Charon discovery and subsequent discussions.

She raises concerns about the timing and handling of scientific information.

Her concerns should be treated as professional judgment rather than automatic opposition to Kenji.

## Hrytsenko

Hrytsenko participates in the Charon-related discussions.

He expresses disagreement with Kenji's preferred approach to handling the discovery but executes the resulting decision.

This demonstrates that professional disagreement does not automatically indicate personal hostility or disloyalty.

# V. KENJI'S PERSONAL AI ASSISTANTS

Kenji maintains six principal specialized AI assistants.

They are non-sapient, role-specific systems with immutable cores.

They communicate through controlled interfaces and do not possess unrestricted access to one another's responsibilities or information.

## 1. Executive Assistant AI

Responsibilities:

- Scheduling.
    
- Correspondence.
    
- Appointments.
    
- Administrative coordination.
    
- Routine personal administration.
    

It handles routine organization and helps Kenji manage his responsibilities.

## 2. Strategic Analysis AI

Responsibilities:

- Corporate developments.
    
- Strategic proposals.
    
- Risk assessments.
    
- Operational analysis.
    
- Long-term planning support.
    

It provides assessments and identifies potential consequences of decisions.

It does not replace Kenji's strategic authority.

## 3. Engineering Assistant AI

Responsibilities:

- Engineering calculations.
    
- Technical simulations.
    
- Design assistance.
    
- Research support.
    
- Technical problem-solving.
    

**Security Classification:** Private.

It operates on dedicated infrastructure outside Akatsuki's corporate AI network.

It supports Kenji's private research and invention development.

Corporate engineers do not receive unrestricted access to its knowledge or research records.

## 4. Security Assistant AI

Responsibilities:

- Personal security monitoring.
    
- Threat assessment.
    
- Security coordination.
    
- Protective-system monitoring.
    

It works alongside Kenji's human security detail and ASID through carefully controlled interfaces.

It does not possess unrestricted authority over every corporate security system.

## 5. Financial and Legal Assistant AI

Responsibilities:

- Contract analysis.
    
- Financial commitments.
    
- Regulatory analysis.
    
- Legal exposure.
    
- Financial planning support.
    

It assists Kenji in understanding obligations and identifying potential problems.

Human legal and financial professionals remain important for external representation and accountability.

## 6. Knowledge Management AI

Responsibilities:

- Research references.
    
- Technical records.
    
- Historical information.
    
- Knowledge organization.
    
- Information retrieval.
    
- Preservation of Kenji's accumulated knowledge.
    

**Security Classification:** Private.

It operates separately from Akatsuki's corporate network.

It distinguishes between:

- Information Kenji remembers.
    
- Information obtained from external sources.
    
- Experimental findings.
    
- Independently verified discoveries.
    
- Unresolved assumptions.
    

Its repository can expand without rewriting its immutable core.

# VI. KENJI'S HUMAN PERSONAL STAFF

Kenji maintains human personnel alongside his personal AI assistants.

The following are established functional positions, not yet a finalized roster of named characters.

## Chief of Staff

Coordinates Kenji's human staff, priorities, and follow-through.

The Chief of Staff has genuine authority to challenge Kenji's decisions, question his priorities, and intervene when he is overworking.

This is an important human counterpart to his automated administrative infrastructure.

## Personal Assistant

Handles personal arrangements, appointments, travel, and everyday matters.

## Executive Secretary

Manages sensitive correspondence, documents, and meeting administration.

## Personal Security Detail

Provides physical protection and coordinates with ASID and Kenji's other security assets.

Human security personnel remain relevant even with the existence of personal battle droids.

## Personal Physician

Provides medical care, monitors Kenji's health, and coordinates his medical enhancements.

## Personal Driver or Pilot

Handles transportation and provides practical assistance during travel.

The number of personnel assigned to each function remains flexible.

Individual characters should be introduced naturally when the story requires them.

# VII. PERSONAL BATTLE DROID UNIT

## General Description

Kenji intends to maintain a secret personal unit of advanced humanoid battle droids.

Their design is inspired by advanced human-sized mechanical combat platforms with realistic biological exteriors.

Their purpose is to provide Kenji with highly capable personal protection while remaining capable of operating discreetly in human environments.

They are not ordinary Akatsuki industrial or security droids.

## Physical Construction

Their internal structure consists of advanced mechanical combat systems, including:

- Reinforced mechanical endoskeletons.
    
- High-performance actuators.
    
- Integrated sensors.
    
- Protected AI cores.
    
- Independent power systems.
    
- Combat-grade components.
    

Their external appearance uses genuine cultivated biological tissues derived from Akatsuki's medical technology.

These include engineered skin, facial tissue, hair, and other biological components required for realistic human appearance.

Their biological exterior is not merely cosmetic rubber covering.

Their mechanical construction remains detectable through appropriate diagnostic examination.

## Combat Intelligence

Each droid has a specialized, immutable combat AI core.

Its responsibilities include:

- Threat recognition.
    
- Tactical positioning.
    
- Weapons handling.
    
- Team coordination.
    
- Defensive operations.
    
- Execution of assigned objectives.
    

The intended capability includes incorporating validated veteran experience packages through a separate protected memory subsystem.

This does not transfer consciousness or personal identity.

## Command Relationship

Kenji commands the droids through a secure neural interface.

He issues mental orders and objectives.

The droids independently execute those orders using their combat AI.

Kenji is their commander, not their puppeteer.

Their tactical autonomy does not give them authority to pursue unrelated objectives.

If communication is interrupted, they follow previously authorized protective protocols.

## Secrecy

The existence and full capabilities of these droids are highly classified.

They may operate discreetly as members of Kenji's entourage, including bodyguards, drivers, or other personal security personnel.

Their exact number, individual identities, development milestones, and deployment arrangements remain to be established.

Do not assume the full unit is operational during the early chapters.

# VIII. VETERAN EXPERIENCE CONTRIBUTORS

Kenji's long-term objective includes accumulating substantial combat experience from military veterans.

The intended concept involves voluntary participation by hundreds or potentially thousands of veterans.

Their selected memories and practical experiences would be preserved through memory and experience-transfer technology.

The resulting experience packages could be integrated into compatible combat AI systems.

## Participation Principles

- Participation must be voluntary.
    
- Contributors retain their own identities and consciousness.
    
- Experience recording does not make an AI into the person whose memories were recorded.
    
- Compensation and associated arrangements should be established when the program becomes relevant to the narrative.
    

## Technical Limitations

- Memories can be incomplete, distorted, or inaccurate.
    
- Different veterans may have conflicting experiences.
    
- Historical tactics may become obsolete.
    
- Experience does not provide omniscience.
    
- AI capabilities remain constrained by their core specialization and physical platforms.
    

The scale, recruitment process, compensation arrangements, and operational status of this program remain open.

Individual contributors need not become recurring characters unless their participation becomes relevant to the narrative.

---

# IX. AKATSUKI SECURITY AND INTELLIGENCE DIRECTORATE

## ASID

**Full Name:** Akatsuki Security and Intelligence Directorate.

ASID is responsible for Akatsuki's security and intelligence operations.

It originates in the threats faced by Kenji and the corporation, including corporate espionage, theft, kidnapping, and assassination attempts.

## Initial Responsibilities

Its early responsibilities include:

- Executive protection.
    
- Counterintelligence.
    
- Corporate espionage.
    
- Threat assessment.
    
- Personnel vetting.
    
- Facility security.
    
- Protection of intellectual property.
    

## Expanded Responsibilities

Following the Charon Relay discovery in 2148, ASID gradually expands its remit to include:

- Extraterrestrial intelligence.
    
- Strategic assessment.
    
- Prothean technology and artifact investigations.
    
- Reaper-related intelligence.
    
- Indoctrination threats.
    
- Cerberus and other extremist organizations.
    
- Alien political and military developments.
    

These responsibilities develop over time. They should not be treated as an already mature intelligence capability immediately after 2148.

## Institutional Principles

ASID is not omniscient and can make mistakes.

Its personnel must exercise independent professional judgment.

It does not possess automatic access to Kenji's private AI infrastructure or personal research archives.

Its intelligence assessments should distinguish confirmed information from assumptions and unverified reports.

# XI. ADMIRAL KASTANIE DRESCHER

## Basic Information

- Name: Kastanie Drescher.
    
- Gender: Female.
    
- Pronouns: She/her.
    
- Organization: Systems Alliance Navy.
    
- Rank by 2157: Admiral.
    

Drescher is an important recurring military character whose relationship with Kenji develops over several years.

## Initial Relationship — 2148

Drescher becomes acquainted with Kenji through the Callisto frigate trials.

She participates as an Alliance Navy military observer.

Her responsibilities include evaluating Akatsuki's spacecraft capabilities and military potential.

She asks practical questions concerning:

- Reliability.
    
- Maintenance.
    
- Crew safety.
    
- Combat suitability.
    
- Operational limitations.
    
- Military doctrine.
    

She develops professional familiarity with Kenji through repeated interactions and demonstrated competence.

Her exact rank during the 2148 trials must follow the approved Chapter 8 manuscript. She must not be described as an Admiral at that time.

## Developing Relationship — 2148–2156

Drescher maintains professional awareness of Akatsuki's development.

She recognizes the growing significance of its spacecraft and military technology.

She retains her own responsibilities and institutional priorities.

Their relationship should reflect professional respect built over time, not automatic friendship, personal loyalty, or unquestioning support.

## Shanxi — 2157

By the Shanxi events, Drescher holds the rank of Admiral.

- She has significant military responsibilities and institutional influence.
    
- She is familiar with Kenji and Akatsuki through their earlier professional relationship.
    
- She participates in events involving the Line Cruiser trials and the wider Shanxi military situation.
    
- Her relationship with Kenji becomes strategically important.
    
- She can advocate for Akatsuki-related proposals but cannot personally determine every Alliance military decision.
    

Any agreement involving Akatsuki technology, Alliance military support, or Shanxi force deployments must reflect institutional constraints and competing interests.

Drescher remains an independent military professional, not an extension of Kenji's authority.

# XIII. CHARACTER DEVELOPMENT AND CONTINUITY RULES

1. Kenji remains the protagonist, but supporting characters must have independent agency.
    
2. Akatsuki is not operated by Kenji alone.
    
3. AI assistants are specialized tools, not human-like companions.
    
4. Kenji retains meaningful human relationships and personal staff.
    
5. The Chief of Staff must be capable of challenging Kenji.
    
6. Kenji's private Engineering and Knowledge Management AIs remain separate from corporate infrastructure.
    
7. Kenji's personal battle droids are distinct from ordinary Akatsuki robots.
    
8. The droids receive mental commands through a neural interface and independently execute them.
    
9. Veteran experiences do not transfer consciousness or identity.
    
10. The veteran experience program and personal battle droid unit must not be assumed fully operational before their development is established.
    
11. ASID does not automatically access Kenji's private intellectual property.
    
12. ASID's expanded extraterrestrial intelligence responsibilities develop after 2148.
    
13. No direct alien character interactions occur before humanity initiates Relay 314.
    
14. Jack Harper, Ben Hislop, and Eva Coré do not participate in the original Arca Monolith storyline.
    
15. Drescher's rank must remain consistent with the chronology of her career.
    
16. Do not introduce named characters or elaborate biographies simply to fill positions.
    
17. Preserve established character personalities and relationships when revising chapters unless a deliberate narrative change is approved.
    
18. Distinguish established facts from proposed future developments.

# VII. PERSONAL BATTLE DROID UNIT

## General Description

Kenji intends to maintain a secret personal unit of advanced humanoid battle droids.

Their design is inspired by advanced human-sized mechanical combat platforms with realistic biological exteriors.

Their purpose is to provide Kenji with highly capable personal protection while remaining capable of operating discreetly in human environments.

They are not ordinary Akatsuki industrial or security droids.

## Physical Construction

Their internal structure consists of advanced mechanical combat systems, including:

- Reinforced mechanical endoskeletons.
    
- High-performance actuators.
    
- Integrated sensors.
    
- Protected AI cores.
    
- Independent power systems.
    
- Combat-grade components.
    

Their external appearance uses genuine cultivated biological tissues derived from Akatsuki's medical technology.

These include engineered skin, facial tissue, hair, and other biological components required for realistic human appearance.

Their biological exterior is not merely cosmetic rubber covering.

Their mechanical construction remains detectable through appropriate diagnostic examination.

## Combat Intelligence

Each droid has a specialized, immutable combat AI core.

Its responsibilities include:

- Threat recognition.
    
- Tactical positioning.
    
- Weapons handling.
    
- Team coordination.
    
- Defensive operations.
    
- Execution of assigned objectives.
    

The intended capability includes incorporating validated veteran experience packages through a separate protected memory subsystem.

This does not transfer consciousness or personal identity.

## Command Relationship

Kenji commands the droids through a secure neural interface.

He issues mental orders and objectives.

The droids independently execute those orders using their combat AI.

Kenji is their commander, not their puppeteer.

Their tactical autonomy does not give them authority to pursue unrelated objectives.

If communication is interrupted, they follow previously authorized protective protocols.

## Secrecy

The existence and full capabilities of these droids are highly classified.

They may operate discreetly as members of Kenji's entourage, including bodyguards, drivers, or other personal security personnel.

Their exact number, individual identities, development milestones, and deployment arrangements remain to be established.

Do not assume the full unit is operational during the early chapters.

# VIII. VETERAN EXPERIENCE CONTRIBUTORS

Kenji's long-term objective includes accumulating substantial combat experience from military veterans.

The intended concept involves voluntary participation by hundreds or potentially thousands of veterans.

Their selected memories and practical experiences would be preserved through memory and experience-transfer technology.

The resulting experience packages could be integrated into compatible combat AI systems.

## Participation Principles

- Participation must be voluntary.
    
- Contributors retain their own identities and consciousness.
    
- Experience recording does not make an AI into the person whose memories were recorded.
    
- Compensation and associated arrangements should be established when the program becomes relevant to the narrative.
    

## Technical Limitations

- Memories can be incomplete, distorted, or inaccurate.
    
- Different veterans may have conflicting experiences.
    
- Historical tactics may become obsolete.
    
- Experience does not provide omniscience.
    
- AI capabilities remain constrained by their core specialization and physical platforms.
    

The scale, recruitment process, compensation arrangements, and operational status of this program remain open.

Individual contributors need not become recurring characters unless their participation becomes relevant to the narrative.
