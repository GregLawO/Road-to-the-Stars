# Unresolved Plot Threads Register — Road to the Stars

**Document purpose:** Track unresolved questions, planned developments, deferred decisions, and narrative commitments. Prevent forgotten plot points, premature revelations, and accidental contradictions.

**Current narrative reference point:** 2148, following the planned events of Chapter 10.

**Status definitions:**

- **Open:** Requires future narrative development.
    
- **Planned:** Direction has been established, but events have not yet occurred.
    
- **Deferred:** Intentionally postponed until the story reaches the appropriate point.
    
- **Resolved:** Addressed in an approved chapter or superseded by an explicit decision.
    

This register tracks story-level matters. It is not a substitute for the Continuity Tracker, Knowledge Tracker, or Technology and Resource Tracker.

---

## 1. Immediate Story Threads

### 1.1 Akatsuki's Growth After the Time Skip

- Status: Planned.
    
- Chapter 6 establishes the company in 2148 after eleven years of development.
    
- Show its scale through current operations, decisions, employees, and business activity rather than a chronological history lesson.
    
- Establish that medical technology, robotics, specialized AI, manufacturing, and defense are real businesses.
    
- Show that the company has customers, contracts, production capability, and operational responsibilities.
    
- Avoid making Kenji personally responsible for every achievement.
    

### 1.2 Callisto's Corporate Settlement

- Status: Planned.
    
- Chapter 7 establishes Callisto as a functioning off-world corporate settlement.
    
- Show civilian life alongside research, mining, manufacturing, logistics, and spacecraft operations.
    
- Preserve its status as a corporate settlement, not an independent state or military installation.
    
- Give residents and employees ordinary concerns and independent priorities.
    

### 1.3 Akatsuki's Fleet

- Status: Planned.
    
- Chapter 8 establishes the fleet's capabilities and operational history.
    
- Six 250-meter corvettes are operational.
    
- One 500-meter frigate is newly constructed and undergoing trials.
    
- The frigate is not yet commissioned or combat-ready.
    
- Show the practical burdens of maintenance, personnel, logistics, and operational experience.
    
- The corvettes have previous anti-piracy experience, including real risks and losses.
    
- The fleet must not be portrayed as an established sovereign navy.
    

### 1.4 Human Relationships

- Status: Planned.
    
- Chapter 9 develops meaningful interactions with selected human military, scientific, political, and commercial figures.
    
- Use a limited cast and give each interaction a narrative purpose.
    
- Preserve the agency and independent objectives of supporting characters.
    
- Verify canon chronology before assigning specific positions or responsibilities.
    
- Do not introduce alien characters as direct interaction partners.
    

### 1.5 Kenji's Recognition of the Mass Effect Setting

- Status: Planned.
    
- Chapter 10 follows the discovery of the Charon Relay.
    
- Kenji recognizes the setting based on his casual gaming knowledge.
    
- His memory is incomplete; he must verify information and distinguish recollection from evidence.
    
- This is a strategic turning point, not an identity crisis.
    
- He does not suddenly become an expert on every character, faction, event, or piece of technology.

## 2. Strategic Developments After 2148

### 2.1 Akatsuki's Strategic Reorientation

- Status: Planned.
    
- Following Chapter 10, Kenji begins redirecting Akatsuki's long-term priorities.
    
- Changes should appear through ordinary corporate decisions: investment, hiring, research, procurement, logistics, and expansion.
    
- Avoid sudden declarations of a grand plan or chapters devoted entirely to exposition.
    
- Kenji must balance preparation against the need to maintain Akatsuki's existing business.
    

### 2.2 Shanxi Settlement

- Status: Planned.
    
- Establish a genuine commercial and industrial presence in the Shanxi system between 2150 and 2153.
    
- Activities include resource extraction and processing, manufacturing, spacecraft maintenance, shipping, logistics, research, and exploration support.
    
- The settlement must not be a disguised military base.
    
- Its strategic importance should emerge from its legitimate commercial presence before the First Contact War.
    
- Akatsuki vessels and personnel in the system during the war must have plausible ordinary operational reasons for being there.
    

### 2.3 Amatsu Settlement and Naming

- Status: Planned.
    
- After recognizing the Mass Effect setting, Kenji directs Akatsuki toward settlement of the planet.
    
- Kenji names it **Amatsu (天園)**, meaning **Heavenly Garden**.
    
- The name is chosen for practical and personal reasons, without knowledge of the planet's hidden significance.
    
- Amatsu is the planet known in the original Mass Effect setting as Eden Prime. That original name is strictly an author-side continuity reference and must never appear in the narrative.
    
- Kenji does not know that the planet contains the Prothean beacon.
    
- Amatsu eventually becomes Akatsuki headquarters, its principal industrial center, and its political heart by 2183.
    
- Do not rush its settlement or treat it as an established headquarters before the appropriate development.
    

### 2.4 ASID's Expanded Mission

- Status: Planned.
    
- ASID expands from corporate security and intelligence into extraterrestrial intelligence and strategic threat assessment after 2148.
    
- Its work includes relay intelligence, Prothean artifacts, alien technology, Citadel political structures, indoctrination, and human extremist organizations.
    
- ASID must remain fallible. It can misinterpret evidence, miss threats, suffer compromise, and make mistakes.
    
- Its expansion should be gradual and supported by personnel, expertise, and resources.

## 3. Arca Monolith and Cerberus-Related Threads

### 3.1 Arca Monolith Neutralization

- Status: Planned.
    
- The Arca Monolith is located on Shanxi.
    
- ASID discovers and neutralizes it quietly around 2152–2153.
    
- This occurs before Jack Harper, Ben Hislop, and Eva Coré can discover it.
    
- The operation is one of many routine intelligence activities, not a standalone adventure or dedicated chapter.
    
- Its destruction may be conveyed through a briefing, report, or indirect reference.
    
- No member of Harper's group is exposed to the artifact.
    

### 3.2 Jack Harper's Independent Development

- Status: Open.
    
- Harper may develop into a pro-human ideological leader and the eventual founder of Cerberus.
    
- His development must not depend on the Arca Monolith.
    
- Preserve his ambition, worldview, and independent decisions.
    
- Do not assume he becomes benevolent or aligned with Kenji.
    
- A meeting with Kenji must be independently justified and must not be forced through the Monolith storyline.
    

### 3.3 Ben Hislop and Eva Coré

- Status: Open.
    
- Their future roles and relationships remain to be developed.
    
- They may remain associated with Harper or pursue independent paths.
    
- Neither is exposed to the Arca Monolith.
    
- Do not assume their loyalty, ideology, or relationship with Kenji before it is established.

## 4. Technology and Military Developments

### 4.1 Gravitic Spacetime Drive

- Status: Planned.
    
- First experimental FTL frigate enters testing around 2148–2150.
    
- Additional ship integration follows around 2151–2152.
    
- Operational deployment and broader production occur around 2153–2157.
    
- The technology must progress through testing, reliability improvements, training, maintenance, technical support, and operational doctrine.
    
- Independent FTL does not automatically provide faster-than-light communications.
    
- Do not treat target speeds as guaranteed performance on every vessel.
    

### 4.2 Systems Alliance Integration

- Status: Planned.
    
- Akatsuki's FTL technology is integrated into Alliance vessels through procurement, testing, and technical cooperation.
    
- By 2157, at least one or two Alliance fleets have substantial operational deployment of Akatsuki FTL drives.
    
- This requires completed trials, trained crews, maintenance support, spare components, standardized integration, and operational doctrine.
    
- The Alliance must retain its own command structure and military agency.
    
- Akatsuki does not simply become the Alliance Navy.
    

### 4.3 Akatsuki's Military-Industrial Development

- Status: Planned.
    
- Akatsuki is already an established defense contractor by 2148.
    
- It has military customers, contracts, deployed equipment, production capacity, and a record of reliability.
    
- Its offerings include sensors, electronic warfare, targeting, communications security, ship protection, propulsion, robotics, specialized AI, and weapons.
    
- Its military capabilities grow through industrial development rather than sudden technological leaps.
    
- It becomes a credible military-industrial organization, not yet a mature sovereign navy.
    

### 4.4 Kenji's Personal Enhancements

- Status: Planned.
    
- Kenji begins using Akatsuki medical technology for personal enhancement around 2141–2143.
    
- The motivation is the increasing threat of kidnapping and assassination.
    
- Enhancements improve physical and cognitive capabilities but do not make him invulnerable or immortal.
    
- He remains vulnerable to firearms, explosives, heavy weapons, environmental extremes, and sufficiently advanced opponents.
    
- He remains a civilian executive and delegates military command to professionals.

## 5. First Contact War

### 5.1 Relay 314 Activation

- Status: Planned.
    
- The human exploration fleet discovers the Charon Relay in 2148.
    
- Humanity initiates Relay 314 in 2157.
    
- Chapter 15 ends with the initiation of the relay opening.
    
- The initial Turian engagement and subsequent war belong to the next narrative phase.
    

### 5.2 First Human-Turian Engagement

- Status: Planned.
    
- The first engagement is a human tactical victory.
    
- The human exploration fleet is not annihilated.
    
- The victory must be credible and grounded in preparation, technology, command decisions, and circumstances.
    
- Avoid making it an effortless demonstration of human superiority.
    

### 5.3 Turian Expeditionary Force Defeat

- Status: Planned.
    
- A Turian expeditionary force enters human space and is decisively defeated.
    
- Most of the force is destroyed or captured.
    
- Humanity does not conquer Palaven or destroy the Turian Hierarchy.
    
- The Hierarchy remains more populous, industrialized, experienced, and powerful overall.
    
- The outcome changes the political balance and forces the Council to intervene.
    

### 5.4 Akatsuki's Role in the War

- Status: Planned.
    
- Akatsuki contributes through its technology, fleet, industrial capacity, logistics, and Shanxi presence.
    
- Its ships and personnel must have plausible operational assignments.
    
- The company should not single-handedly win the war.
    
- Professional military officers retain command responsibilities.
    
- The war should have costs, casualties, uncertainty, and political consequences.

## 6. Postwar and Long-Term Threads

### 6.1 Akatsuki's Political Status

- Status: Deferred.
    
- Following the First Contact War, Akatsuki evolves toward a quasi-sovereign organization.
    
- Amatsu becomes its principal center.
    
- The company's political power should emerge from industrial, technological, economic, and military influence.
    
- Its relationship with the Systems Alliance must remain a meaningful source of tension and negotiation.
    

### 6.2 Galactic Diplomacy and Alliances

- Status: Deferred.
    
- Direct interspecies relationships begin only after first contact in 2157.
    
- Develop alliances and rivalries through mutual interests, distrust, competing priorities, and political realities.
    
- Do not assume the Council or other governments automatically accept humanity's position.
    

### 6.3 Reaper Intelligence

- Status: Deferred.
    
- Research develops through Prothean evidence, recovered technology, intelligence, and later direct encounters.
    
- Knowledge remains incomplete and can be misinterpreted.
    
- Reaper intelligence and indoctrination must be treated as serious threats.
    

### 6.4 Civilizational Resilience

- Status: Deferred.
    
- Develop distributed industry, logistics, settlements, food production, medical capacity, independent FTL routes, and redundant infrastructure.
    
- These systems must be built over time.
    
- No single technology or organization should make civilization invulnerable.
    

### 6.5 Sovereign's Arrival at Amatsu

- Status: Deferred.
    
- In 2183, Sovereign arrives at Amatsu and encounters a transformed, technologically advanced world.
    
- The confrontation is a major event, not an effortless defeat.
    
- The Reapers remain intelligent, adaptive, numerous, and dangerous.
    
- The story does not use the Crucible.
    

### 6.6 Reaper War Resolution

- Status: Deferred.
    
- The Reaper threat is defeated or neutralized through preparation, logistics, military capabilities, coalition warfare, intelligence, and sacrifice.
    
- The resolution should account for the cost of the war and the political consequences of survival.
    
- The galaxy must have a credible future beyond the conflict.

## 7. Narrative and Continuity Commitments

1. Keep the pre-contact period restricted to human direct interactions.
    
2. Do not reveal Amatsu's hidden significance to Kenji before he has evidence.
    
3. Never use the name Eden Prime in narrative prose or in-universe documents.
    
4. Do not create a dedicated Arca Monolith adventure.
    
5. Do not expose Harper, Hislop, or Coré to the Monolith.
    
6. Do not turn Akatsuki into a sovereign state or mature navy prematurely.
    
7. Do not make Kenji omniscient or infallible.
    
8. Do not let technological development ignore industrial, personnel, logistical, and reliability constraints.
    
9. Do not expand the opening sequence with unnecessary chapters for individual milestones.
    
10. Use time skips and compressed developments where appropriate.
    
11. Show strategic changes through ordinary events and operations rather than constant exposition.
    
12. Keep supporting characters independent and consequential.
    
13. Update this register when a major thread is resolved, changed, or introduced.
    
14. Do not treat planned events as established manuscript facts until they appear in an approved chapter.
    

**Authority:** The approved manuscript establishes completed events. The Master Story Bible establishes core canon. This register records future commitments and unresolved matters without overriding either.
