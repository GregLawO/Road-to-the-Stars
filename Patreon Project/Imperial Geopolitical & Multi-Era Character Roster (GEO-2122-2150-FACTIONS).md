### IMPERIAL GEOPOLITICAL & MULTI-ERA CHARACTER ROSTER

DOCUMENT REF: GEO-2122-2150-FACTIONS



PURPOSE: Master faction architecture, sovereign allies, global territorial states, and canon character lifecycle registry across Pre-War (2122–2132), World War III (2132–2136), and Post-War (2136–2150+).

---

PART I: GLOBAL GEOPOLITICAL FACTIONS (2122–2150)

1. THE EMPIRE OF JAPAN & THE IMPERIAL SOVEREIGN DOMAIN (IJN-)
- Pre-War Status (2122–2132): Reconstituted Shinto-Imperial Constitutional Monarchy governed from the Sovereign Imperial Capital of Kyoto under the restored Chrysanthemum Throne of Emperor Himura Kenji (born 2092; 23 in 2115; 30 in 2122), backed by Clan Himura's quiet gravitic and Quantum-Optical VI monopoly. Secretly fortifies the Japanese Home Islands, orbital platforms, and deep-space shipyards using Solaris-4 cores.
- Post-War Status (2136–2150+): Following the decisive 2136 victory in World War III, the Empire of Japan maintains strict territorial boundaries limited exclusively to sovereign Japanese territory—the Japanese Home Islands (with Kyoto as the Imperial Capital and Tokyo serving as a major commercial and maritime metropolis), Sakhalin, the Kurils, key Western Pacific islands, Crown colony worlds, and Japanese Vassal Clan garden colonies in space. Under its strict Doctrine of Sovereign Non-Interference, Japan governs no foreign mainland territories, never interferes in the domestic affairs of neighbors or allies unless explicitly requested by their sovereign governments, and maintains formal military alliances strictly with Taiwan and South Korea.
- Technological & Military Doctrine:
  - Strict Anti-Smart-AI Edict: Enforced across all Imperial territory since 2119; relies solely on deterministic Quantum-Optical VI systems.
  - Sovereign Gravitic Monopoly: Retains exclusive domestic control over Solaris-9 Unified Gravitic Cores (75% maximum mass-shedding hard limit) and Chōju cellular longevity protocols.
  - Starfleet & Ground Armies: Commands 1,500 active IJN- warships (ceiling announced in 2136 and built out by about 2142; no increase afterward) and 25,000,000 standing ground troops (5,000,000 human commanders/specialists + 20,000,000 Samurai-Form Automata + 250,000 Oni-Mecha across 25 Armies/Legions), alongside 100,000 Imperial Hatamoto Guard, 500,000 Imperial Shock Corps, 12,000 Compliance Audit-Marshals, and 10,000 Gravitic Shinobi.
2. SOLE SOVEREIGN ALLIES OF IMPERIAL JAPAN (TAIWAN & SOUTH KOREA)  
   Hereditary Daimyō and Vassal Clan titles belong exclusively to Japanese noble houses sworn to the Chrysanthemum Throne. Across the entire globe, Taiwan and South Korea are the only formal sovereign allies of the Empire of Japan, bound to Kyoto by equal mutual-defense treaties rather than Imperial rule:
- Taiwan / Formosa (Sole Maritime Sovereign Ally)
  - Political Status: Fully independent sovereign island republic; maintains no feudal Daimyō clans and no Imperial Japanese administration.
  - Historical Alignment: Partnered quietly with Japanese maritime-semiconductor and quantum-optical crystal consortia prior to 2132. During World War III (2132–2136), Taiwan fought alongside Imperial Japan as a formal treaty ally to defend the Taiwan Strait and Western Pacific against the Eurasian-Continental Pact and hostile Western orbital task forces.
  - Role & Alliance: One of Japan's two sole treaty allies; hosts licensed, Kinki-Kyoku-blackboxed Helios-series commercial assembly facilities under bilateral treaty and coordinates joint sea-lane security patrols requested under the East Asian Mutual Defense Accord.
- The Republic of Korea / South Korea (Sole Continental Sovereign Ally)
  - Political Status: Fully independent sovereign nation governing the Korean Peninsula; maintains no feudal Daimyō clans and no Imperial Japanese administration.
  - Historical Alignment: Rejected the Eurasian-Continental Pact in 2132 and requested Imperial Japanese military assistance under their mutual-defense pact when Pact forces invaded the peninsula. Fought side-by-side with Imperial Expeditionary forces during the 2134 Manchurian-Korean campaign to liberate and reunify the peninsula, afterward resuming full sovereign self-governance.
  - Role & Alliance: One of Japan's two sole treaty allies; cooperates in joint maritime defense, commercial shipbuilding, and licensed Helios-series civilian orbital infrastructure while remaining strictly separate from sovereign IJN- warship command, Solaris-9 manufacture, and Chōju Tier I secrets.
3. NON-ALLIED NEIGHBORS & RIVAL WESTERN BLOCS (CHINA, UNAS & EU)  
   Outside of Taiwan and South Korea, the Empire of Japan maintains no military alliances with any nation or bloc on Earth:
- Post-Pact China / Independent Chinese Republics (Non-Allied Sovereign Neighbors)
  - Political Status: Fully independent sovereign Chinese states; there is no Imperial-administered China and no formal military alliance with Kyoto.
  - Historical Alignment: Following the collapse of the Beijing-Moscow Eurasian-Continental Pact in 2136, Emperor Himura refused any territorial annexation or foreign occupation of the Chinese mainland—adhering strictly to Imperial non-interference and withdrawing all Imperial forces upon victory.
  - Role & Relations: Maintains normal diplomatic and commercial trade relations with Kyoto, purchasing licensed commercial Helios-2 and Helios-5 reactors for domestic energy and orbital reconstruction while respecting the 2136 non-aggression and demilitarization accords.
- The United North American States (UNAS — Rival Belligerent & Post-War Commercial Customer)
  - Pre-War & WWIII Status (2122–2136): Formed from the political-economic consolidation of the United States, Canada, and Mexico in the early 22nd century. Never allied with Imperial Japan; clashed directly with Imperial naval and orbital forces across the Pacific and Earth-Luna corridor during World War III while simultaneously fighting the Eurasian-Continental Pact—a three-way global conflict in which UNAS orbital yards at Puget Sound and forward Pacific bastions were neutralized by Imperial Solaris-7 strikes.
  - Post-War Status (2136–2150+): Remains Earth's second major industrial and orbital power. Dependent on commercially licensed Imperial Helios-2 / Helios-5 / Helios-8 gravitic reactors (hard-capped at 25%–50% mass reduction and blackboxed by Kinki-Kyoku) to lift heavy orbital infrastructure, fueling intense rivalry and resentment among American military hawks toward Japan's Solaris-9 supremacy.
- The European Union / Euro-Atlantic Federation (EU — Rival Belligerent & Post-War Commercial Customer)
  - Pre-War & WWIII Status (2122–2136): Never allied with Imperial Japan; fought a brutal continental war across Central and Eastern Europe against the Eurasian-Continental Pact while its orbital blockade units and Kourou equatorial space-elevator tether were neutralized by Imperial forces during World War III (2132–2136).
  - Post-War Status (2136–2150+): Rebuilding its continental infrastructure and orbital elevators using commercially imported Imperial Helios-series reactors. Forms the core European bloc of the post-war United Nations and early Systems Alliance (2149).
4. THE EURASIAN-CONTINENTAL PACT (DEFEATED / DISSOLVED 2136)
- Pre-War & WWIII Status (2122–2136): Expansionist continental coalition led by neo-authoritarian blocs in Moscow and Beijing that launched coordinated terrestrial and orbital strikes in 2132—igniting World War III in an attempt to seize Japanese gravitic facilities and conquer South Korea, Taiwan, and Eurasian resource basins.
- Post-War Status (2136–2150+): Catastrophically defeated in 2136 by Imperial Japan's Solaris-7 warships and Samurai-Form Automata Legions alongside South Korean and Taiwanese allied forces on the Asian front (and bled dry on its western front by separate conflicts with the EU and UNAS). Dissolved into independent sovereign republics under international demilitarization treaties.
5. THE SYSTEMS ALLIANCE (SSV- — FOUNDED 2149)
- Origin & Mandate: Established in 2149 following the discovery of the Prothean Mars Archives (2148) and the activation of the Charon Mass Relay. Conceived by UNAS, EU, and UN representatives as a unified supranational human defense and exploration force for extra-solar space.
- Relationship with the Empire of Japan: Japan—alongside its two sovereign allies Taiwan and South Korea—refused to subordinate its Sovereign Throne in Kyoto, its 1,500-ship IJN- Starfleet, or its Solaris-9 / Quantum-Optical VI technologies to Systems Alliance command. Under the 2149 Kyoto-Arcturus Concordat, the Systems Alliance polices wider human colonial space (using SSV- warships built with licensed, blackboxed Imperial Helios-8 cores capped at 50% mass reduction), while the Empire of Japan exercises absolute sovereign jurisdiction over its Japanese territory, Crown colonies, Japanese Vassal Clan worlds, and border relay quarantine zones—providing military aid strictly to Taiwan and South Korea when formally requested.

PART II: CLAN HIMURA & IMPERIAL HOUSEHOLD CORE CHARACTERS (2122–2150+)

1. Emperor Himura Kenji (born 2092)
- Lifecycle Ages: 23 in 2115 (Secret Awakening of Solaris-4 & Quantum-Optical VI prototypes) | 30 in 2122 (Pre-War Era begins; clandestine Crown Foundry expansion) | 40–44 in 2132–2136 (World War III; leads the Imperial restoration from Kyoto and commands from the IJN Amaterasu-no-Yaiba) | 57 in 2149 (Charon Relay activation; biologically preserved in his late 20s via Tier I Chōju longevity protocols).
- Volitional Lock: none (author ruling). The Imperial Family carries no Lock.
- Role & Arc: Architect of the Solaris gravitic revolution, the Anti-Smart-AI Edict, and the restored Chrysanthemum Empire. Pragmatic, unsentimental, and fiercely protective of Japanese sovereignty; enforces strict non-interference toward neighbors while honoring mutual-defense treaties with Taiwan and South Korea. Teaches his heir patience over a throne that will not open for centuries.
2. Empress Himura Kaguya (born 2093)
- Lifecycle Ages: 22 in 2115 | 29 in 2122 | 39–43 in 2132–2136 | 56 in 2149 (biologically mid-20s via Tier I Chōju).
- Volitional Lock: none (author ruling).
- Role & Arc: Sovereign Consort and Grand Matriarch of the Inner Palace in Kyoto; oversees the Throneside Medical Bureau (Chōju administration), ancestral archives, and domestic cultural restoration across Japan and its Crown worlds.
3. Fleet Admiral Togo Kazuhiko (born 2085)
- Lifecycle Ages: 37 in 2122 | 47–51 in 2132–2136 | 64 in 2149 (biologically late 30s via Tier II Chōju).
- Role & Arc: Supreme Commander of the Combined Imperial Japanese Starfleet (Rengō Uchū-Kaigun). Designed the Standoff Supremacy Principle and the 1:2:4 line-of-battle doctrine during World War III; commands the 10,000m Imperial Flagship IJN Amaterasu (IJN-TNS-MC) post-war.
4. High Censor Kurosawa Renji (born 2090)
- Lifecycle Ages: 32 in 2122 | 42–46 in 2132–2136 | 59 in 2149 (biologically mid-30s via Tier II Chōju).
- Role & Arc: Director-General of the Imperial Compliance Directorate (Kansatsu-In / IJN-KNS-) and Chief Guardian of Kinki-Kyoku. Enforces the Anti-Smart-AI Edict, blackbox seals on Helios export cores, and the 2149 Charon Relay quarantine.
5. General Yamashita Takeo (born 2093)
- Lifecycle Ages: 29 in 2122 | 39–43 in 2132–2136 | 56 in 2149 (biologically mid-30s via Tier II Chōju).
- Role & Arc: Supreme Commander of the Imperial Ground Forces and the 25 Automata Legions. Led the 2134–2136 continental campaign at the formal request of South Korea to break the Eurasian-Continental Pact, withdrawing all Imperial ground armies back to sovereign Japanese territory upon victory.
6. Lord Commander Tachibana Haruka (born 2102)
- Lifecycle Ages: 20 in 2122 | 30–34 in 2132–2136 | 47 in 2149 (biologically late 20s via Tier II Chōju).
- Role & Arc: Commander of the Imperial Hatamoto Guard and the 500-ship Imperial Guard Fleet (IJN-KNE-). Master duelist in Silver Samurai (Gin-Musha) exosuit armor and personal shield of Emperor Himura.
7. Crown Prince Himura Kiyoshi (born 2114) (decision on name)
- Lifecycle Ages: 8 in 2122 | 18 in 2132 | 22 in 2136 | 25 in 2139 (Tier I Chōju granted after he stops demanding the throne) | 35 in 2149 | 40 in 2154 (Shepard's arrival).
- Role & Arc: Legion ground-pounder in a Hata-Musha exosuit, with a Hatamoto shadow imposed by Tachibana. Bridge figure between the Imperial line and the Mass Effect era. No Volitional Lock (author ruling). No foreknowledge, not even dream-level.
8. Himura Ayame (born 2126) and Himura Haruto (born 2136) (decision on names)
- Ayame: 8 in 2134, 23 in 2149. Haruto: 8 in 2144, 13 in 2149.
- Tier I Chōju (author ruling). No Lock, no foreknowledge.

PART III: CANON MASS EFFECT HUMAN CHARACTERS (MULTI-ERA TIMELINE)

To maintain strict chronological realism across the 2122–2150+ timeline, all canon Mass Effect human characters appear only in eras matching their canonical birth years and lifecycles:

1. Pre-War & World War III Veterans (Active in 2122–2136 & Post-War Senior Command)
- David Anderson (born 2137): Not yet born during Pre-War or WWIII; 12 years old in 2149 (grows up in London during post-war European reconstruction; enlists in the Systems Alliance Marines in 2155).
- Steven Hackett (born 2134): Toddler during WWIII (2 years old in 2136); 15 years old in 2149 (raises himself on the streets of Buenos Aires before joining the Systems Alliance Navy in 2152).
- Jon Grissom (born 2112): 10 in 2122 | 20–24 in 2132–2136 (serves as a decorated UNAS orbital fighter/marine pilot during World War III, clashing with Imperial Japanese orbital wings over the North Pacific before surviving the 2136 armistice) | 37 in 2149 (leads the first human expedition through the Charon Mass Relay in 2149; respected across both UNAS and Imperial diplomatic circles as a war veteran hero).
- Alec Ryder (born 2129): 3–7 years old during WWIII | 20 in 2149 (joins the Systems Alliance N7 program alongside Jon Grissom; fascinated by Imperial Quantum-Optical VI systems while quietly resenting Japan's strict ban on true AI research).
- Jack Harper / The Illusive Man (born ~2110–2115): ~10 in 2122 | ~20–24 in 2132–2136 (UNAS intelligence/mercenary operative during WWIII who witnesses Imperial Solaris-7 warships and Samurai-Form Automata firsthand in Asia) | ~37 in 2149 (obsessed with breaking Japan's Solaris-9 and Chōju monopoly to ensure unilateral human supremacy; secretly funds espionage rings targeting Kinki-Kyoku and Helios export plants).
- Henry Lawson (born ~2115): ~17–21 during WWIII | ~34 in 2149 (ruthless corporate geneticist who attempts to reverse-engineer Imperial Chōju cellular-rejuvenation protocols via black-market samples, failing to replicate the sovereign stabilizer matrix).
2. Post-War & Charon Relay Generation (Children/Youth in 2149; Active in 2150s–2180s)
- Kahlee Sanders (born 2150): Born one year after the Charon Relay activation; later becomes a leading Systems Alliance biotics and VI/AI specialist under Jon Grissom.
- Commander Shepard (born April 11, 2154): Born five years after the Charon Relay activation.
- Kaidan Alenko (born 2151): Born two years after the Charon Relay activation.
- Ashley Williams (born 2158): Born nine years after the Charon Relay activation (granddaughter of General Williams, a UNAS/Systems Alliance officer active in the 2140s–2150s).
- Miranda Lawson (born 2150): Created by Henry Lawson one year after the Charon Relay activation using stolen, incomplete corporate approximations of early Chōju cellular-longevity research.
- Jacob Taylor (born 2157): Born eight years after the Charon Relay activation.
- James Vega (born 2157): Born eight years after the Charon Relay activation.
- Kasumi Goto (born 2160): Born eleven years after the Charon Relay activation to Japanese-diaspora parents; later becomes an infamous master thief targeting both Systems Alliance and Japanese Vassal Clan vaults.
- Kai Leng (born ~2156): Born seven years after the Charon Relay activation; trained in N7 black-ops before defecting to Cerberus.
- Zaeed Massani (born ~2140): 9 years old in 2149; grows up in the chaotic post-WWIII border zones before co-founding the Blue Suns.

PART IV: SYSTEMS ALLIANCE & UNAS POLITICAL/MILITARY FIGURES (2136–2150)

- General Williams (born ~2105–2110): UNAS veteran of the North American/Pacific orbital defense front in WWIII; rises to Systems Alliance command in 2149 and advocates cautious détente with the Empire of Japan.
- Admiral Boris Mikhailovich (born ~2105): Former Eurasian-bloc naval officer who survived the 2136 defeat, integrated into the post-war UN orbital defense force, and became a hardline Systems Alliance admiral in 2149 deeply suspicious of both alien species and Imperial Japanese secrecy.
- Admiral Kastanie Drescher (born ~2108–2115): Senior Systems Alliance fleet planner in 2149 who relies heavily on licensed Imperial Helios-8 reactors to rapidly construct the Alliance's first dreadnought and cruiser hulls after the Charon Relay opens.
- Ambassador Anita Goyle (born ~2112): Chief Systems Alliance diplomatic envoy to the Imperial Court in Kyoto in 2149–2150; constantly negotiates transit rights through Imperial-quarantined relay corridors and commercial Helios-8 reactor quotas.
