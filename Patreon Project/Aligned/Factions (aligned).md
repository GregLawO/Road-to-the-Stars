# Factions File: Road to the Stars

---

## 1. CLAN HIMURA

### 1.1 The Imperial Family

- **Members:** Emperor Himura Kenji, Empress Himura Kaguya, Crown Prince Himura Kiyoshi, Himura Ayame (born 2126), Himura Haruto.
- **Seat:** Kyoto-Heian Imperial Citadel from the 2122 Restoration. Kenji is Sovereign and Clan Head (Tōshu). The Crown Foundries, the Solaris patents, the Quantum-Optical VI architecture and the Throneside Medical Bureau are his alone.
- **Longevity:** Kenji and Kaguya hold Tier I from 2116. The children hold it too, and Kiyoshi receives it after he stops demanding the throne.
- **Lock and foreknowledge:** No one in the family carries the Volitional Lock. Kenji and Kaguya alone hold foreknowledge, and Kaguya holds only the dream-level warning. Kiyoshi, Ayame and Haruto hold none.
- **Armed arm:** The Imperial Guard holds the pre-war Guard fleet and the Hatamoto Guard under Tachibana. Clan Himura also commands its own Legions.
- **POV pool:** Kenji, Kaguya (rare), Kiyoshi.
- **Standing friction:**
  - Kiyoshi resents the Hatamoto shadow Tachibana imposes.
  - Kaguya expects her children to resent parents who do not age, and expects Kiyoshi to resent a throne that will not open for centuries.
  - Kenji's household alone keeps the full Solaris Mote. Every vassal and ally holds a capped, blackboxed unit.

### 1.2 Clan Factions

- **Masamune:** Counts prices, opposes overreach, and has declined Chōju since 2116.
- **Ryunosuke:** Argues for aggressive patrols and for claiming the Earth-Luna corridor. After the 2132 outbreak he demands seizure of mainland enclaves.
- **Shizuka:** Governs shifts, tonnage and rations, and cuts projects that do not ship.
- **Kōji:** Presses the succession question until Kiyoshi chooses to stay a soldier.

## 2. VASSAL CLANS

- **Status:** Hereditary Daimyō and Vassal Clan titles belong only to Japanese houses on sovereign Japanese territory and Japanese space colonies. Heads keep Kyoto residences under Sankin-Kōtai.
- **History:**
  - All seven pledged vassalage in secret in 2113.
  - Their retainers were conscripted into the Legions as officers and NCOs in 2118.
  - Each clan commanded its own Legions in its own region during the Restoration War.
  - All seven became sworn Vassal Clans in 2122.
  - Kinki-Kyoku then recalled their Helios-1 hardware and reissued it as sealed Helios-3.
- **Oversight:** Kinki-Kyoku and Kansatsu-Kyoku audit export-grade hardware. Vassal staff carry no Volitional Lock.
- **Standing friction:** The vassals once held full Helios-1 cubes. They now hold sealed units that cannot be opened, issued by the house they helped crown.

## 3. FOREIGN ALLIES

Taiwan and South Korea are the Empire's only treaty allies. Japan governs no foreign territory. Imperial forces enter allied territory only on formal request, and field command stays with the ally.

### 3.1 Taiwan (Formosa)

- **Government:** Fully independent island republic. No Imperial administration and no Daimyō clans.
- **Treaty:** Signed 2123. Blackboxed Helios-3 reactors, Ashigaru-3 automata and Exo-Rigs are supplied covertly to its cadres.
- **POV:** Non-POV.
- **Standing friction:** Taiwan receives sealed hardware it cannot open. Imperial advisors critique its doctrine, and it keeps its own command.

### 3.2 South Korea (Republic of Korea)

- **Government:** Fully independent. No Imperial administration.
- **Treaty:** Signed 2123, with the same Helios-3 supply as Taiwan. From 2130 Imperial Legion advisors and Ashigaru-3 automata arrive, and Han Jae-won fortifies the DMZ.
- **POV:** Han Jae-won, later NCO Seo Min-jae.
- **Standing friction:**
  - Seoul's staff and field officers disagree over fixed-bunker doctrine and automata-first doctrine.
  - Staff resent Imperial advisors and rate treaty-supplied automata as expendable.
  - Korea is shut out of Solaris cores and Chōju.

### 3.3 Systems Alliance (SSV-)

- **Established:** Five years after the Reclamation War.
- **Makeup:** A supranational human defense and exploration force chartered by UNAS, EU and UN representatives. It absorbs the former UNAS, EU and Russian POV share.
- **Terms with the Empire:** The Kyoto-Arcturus Concordat, negotiated by Ambassador Anita Goyle. The Alliance polices wider human extra-solar space on licensed, blackboxed Helios-7 hulls. Japan keeps jurisdiction over its territory, Crown worlds, Vassal garden colonies and dormant relays bordering Imperial space.
- **Named figures:** Goyle, Grissom, Drescher, Mikhailovich (admiral), Williams. Hackett, Anderson and Ryder arrive as youth.
- **Standing friction:** The Alliance runs capped hardware it cannot open. Drescher resents the cap, and Mikhailovich distrusts the quarantines.

## 4. Nations & Organizations

### 4.1 UNAS

- **Posture:** Never allied with Japan. Sanctions tighten over the closed Pacific sea lanes from 2126. Fights both the Pact and Imperial forces in the war.
- **POV:** Williams, Grissom. Harper works as UNAS intelligence.
- **Standing friction:** Hawks resent Imperial supremacy, and Williams's call for détente draws suspicion.

### 4.2 EU

- **Posture:** Never allied with Japan. Sanctions tighten alongside UNAS. Brussels reads the 2123 end of Kurogane and steel exports as exhaustion.
- **POV:** Marchand.

### 4.3 Russian Federation

- **Posture:** Moscow and Beijing formalize the Eurasian-Continental Pact in 2126. Kinetic-rod platforms and fusion dreadnoughts take shape at Baikonur and Vladivostok from 2127.
- **POV:** Mikhailovich.

### 4.4 CPF

- **Posture:** Pact member, Beijing-aligned.
- **POV:** Zhao Linfeng.
- **Standing friction:** Moscow and Beijing share the Pact without sharing a command culture.

### 4.5 DPRK

- **Posture:** Pact member. Fortifies the northern side of the DMZ in 2130, opposite Han.
- **POV:** Ri Hyok-chol.

### 4.6 Cerberus

- **Status:** Not formally established until the 2150s. Until then the group is the Harper-and-Lawson precursor network.
- **Members:** Jack Harper and Henry Lawson. Harper's UNAS intelligence work begins to touch Asian technology traffic in 2128.
- **Aim:** Penetrate Kinki-Kyoku blackbox seals and Chōju protocols.
- **Limits:** Both are outsiders to every Imperial secret. Miranda Lawson is not born before 2150.
