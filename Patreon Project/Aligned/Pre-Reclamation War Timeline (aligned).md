# Timeline: 2095 to Just Before the Reclamation War

Entries 1 to 8 are yours, with model names changed to the new numbering. Everything after them is mine, and invented details are marked (decision). Kenji is born 2090. This version adds the fleet build program, so that Clan Himura and then the Empire hold all 236 hulls of the pre-war fleet when the war starts: docks, hull schedule, cores, crews, feedstock, ordnance and the first wartime batch already on the slips. Changed or added entries: 10, 14, 15, 16, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24 and 25, plus the Hull Build Program at the end. This copy has also been aligned with Technology.md and Factions.md: the fleet cores now follow their Solaris-3 and Solaris-5 numbering, and the 2122 reissue uses the Helios-3 name. A second pass applies the author's new rulings: the Silver Samurai are renamed Kurogane Yoroi, automata models follow their cores (Ashigaru on Helios for the vassals and export, Bushi and Ō-Yoroi on Solaris for the Imperial clan), and Solaris and Helios technology needs Kurogane from generation 3.

1. Early 2095 - MC awakens in the 5 year old body of Himura Kenji.
2. Early 2105 - At 15 years old, Kenji completes the Protoss-based Solaris Technology designs and prototypes (proto-Solaris).
3. Mid 2106 - Clan Himura starts secretly implementing the new technology into their industrial businesses.
4. Early 2107 - At 17 years old, he completes the first stable Solaris prototype core and the Solaris-based Helios Technology design and prototypes. The core is left unnumbered, since the name Solaris-1 now belongs to the 2110 core (decision).
   1. Kenji completes the development of the 'Kurogane Alloy Series', a hyper-dense atomic-lattice alloy ten to fifty times stronger than Grade 5 titanium. At its strongest it dissipates nuclear-scale kinetic impacts by dispersing the shockwave across its crystalline matrix while vaporizing a sacrificial outer layer to shed lethal plasma heat.
   2. Mid 2107: Excavation of the Kii-Yamato Core-Forge begins beneath the Kii mountains. The first hall runs on prototype cores (decision).
5. 2108 - At the age of 18, Kenji completes the first deterministic Quantum-Optical VI lattice prototype (decision).
6. 2110 - At the age of 20, Kenji completes development of Solaris-1, especially the 'Mote' for powering the Kurogane Yoroi armor series.
   1. Himura Zaibatsu becomes one of Japan's biggest and most profitable Zaibatsu as the world's only Kurogane-1 Alloy supplier.
   2. Clan Himura begins planning for its Imperial Restoration ambition by approaching other ancient clans and obtaining their support via exclusive supply of Helios Technology.
   3. Rival Zaibatsu begin serious corporate espionage attempts on Himura Zaibatsu.
   4. The first Solaris-1I Early Forge core is installed in the Core-Forge, and Kōgyō-1 foundry automata begin building the later halls (decision).
   5. Himura Zaibatsu forms a counter-espionage section under Kurosawa Renji (decision).
7. 2111 - At the age of 21, the first Bushi-1 automata come off the Kii-Yamato lines, powered by Solaris-1 Motes (decision).
   1. A 40 m test frame lifts off from the Core-Forge on a cluster of Solaris-1 Motes. It is the first gravitic lifter, and Himura Zaibatsu presents it as a heavy-cargo logistics prototype (decision).
8. 2112 - At the age of 22, Kenji completes development of Helios-1, especially the 'Cube' for powering the Armored Exoskeletal Powered Suits (AEPS).
   1. At the age of 22, Kenji marries.
   2. Ō-Yoroi-1 heavy automata enter production with the Bushi-1, and Ashigaru-1 automata on Helios-1 cubes begin for the vassal and export lines (decision).
9. 2113 - Clan Himura's 250 retainers fully adapt to the Kurogane Yoroi armor, built in Kurogane-1M from its first iteration, and its associated weapons.
   1. Clans Date, Takeda, Mori, Chōsokabe, Otomo, Hojo and Shimazu secretly pledge vassalage in support of Clan Himura's bid for the Imperial throne. They receive the schematics for the Helios-1 Technology base, especially the AEPS.
   2. Rival Zaibatsu begin resorting to corporate sabotage.
   3. Kenji's first child is born (Kenji is 23). He is named Kiyoshi (decision, from the files).
   4. Kenji completes the Solaris-1A (0.8 m sphere), the first core large enough to drive a hull (decision).
10. 2114 - Clan Himura's vassal clans fully adapt and equip their retainers with the AEPS.
    1. Himura Zaibatsu registers an orbital logistics subsidiary and a lunar ice-mining concession at a permanently shadowed polar crater (decision).
    2. Solaris-1A lifters begin carrying cargo to low orbit as ordinary commercial freight (decision).
    3. The subsidiary builds a small low-orbit yard with one dock, declared as a freight-module works. It is the seed of the later low-orbit sovereign shipyard (decision).
11. 2115 - At the age of 25, Bushi-1 and Ashigaru-1 production scales up across the Core-Forge halls (decision).
    1. Kurosawa's section breaks several sabotage rings.
    2. Tachibana Haruka's family, a line of hereditary Himura retainers, begins training her in swordsmanship and close-quarters tactics (decision).
    3. Kōgyō-1 automata begin excavating a hidden base inside the leased lunar crater. It is the future lunar-shadow slipway (decision).
12. 2116 - At the age of 26, Kenji brings the proto-Chōju longevity protocol to its first stable result (decision).
    1. He and Kaguya receive the first Tier I immersions (decision).
    2. Masamune Himura declines treatment from the start (decision).
    3. A 60 m Solaris-1A test craft makes the first crewed flight to the Moon. Togo Kazuhiko, a former naval officer in Kenji's circle, trains the first crews (decision).
13. 2117 - At the age of 27, Solaris-1 Chrysalis core production reaches full output at the Kii-Yamato Core-Forge (decision).
    1. Rival Zaibatsu pressure Himura suppliers and banks.
    2. Kenji completes the Solaris-1B (2.2 m sphere), the frigate-class core (decision).
14. 2118 - At the age of 28, Clan Himura is short of people to command its automata, so the vassal clans' AEPS retainers are conscripted into the Automata Legions as officers and NCOs (decision).
    1. They train alongside Clan Himura's own Kurogane Yoroi retainers.
    2. Himura Zaibatsu's output makes it Japan's largest industrial group.
    3. Japan's coalition government loses the confidence of its creditors (decision).
    4. The lunar-shadow slipway opens Docks 1 and 2 (one corvette bay and one frigate bay). The keel of the first 250 m corvette prototype is laid in Dock 1. A Solaris-1I Early Forge core, cast at Kii-Yamato and flown up as freight, powers the base (decision).
15. 2119 - At the age of 29, a rival Zaibatsu's self-modifying logistics system corrupts its own supply data (decision).
    1. Kenji seals the Quantum-Optical VI lattice as deterministic and read-only.
    2. He issues the Anti-Smart-AI Edict across Himura Zaibatsu and all vassal domains.
    3. An uncrewed probe makes the first Sequential Spatial-Fold test jump, covering one light-year (decision).
    4. The keel of the 500 m frigate prototype is laid in Dock 2 (decision).
    5. Kenji completes the Solaris-3 family (3M, 3A and 3B), 25% of Solaris-9's capabilities. From generation 3, Solaris and Helios technology needs Kurogane-1I or 1M to function. Solaris-3M refits the Kurogane Yoroi suits as Kurogane Yoroi-3 and powers the Imperial Legions' Bushi-3 and Ō-Yoroi-3 automata, which are plated in Kurogane-1M (decision).
16. 2120 - At the age of 30, the 25 Legions are raised, each of 5,000 men and automata, and begin staging near Kyoto.
    1. Each Legion has 1,000 human officers and NCOs and 4,000 automata, the same 1:4 ratio the later fireteams use (decision). That is 25,000 humans and 100,000 automata in all.
    2. Command of the Legions is split by clan so that every Vassal clan commands at least 2 (decision on the final split): Clan Himura 5, Clans Date and Takeda 4 each, Clans Chōsokabe and Shimazu 3 each, and Clans Mori, Otomo and Hojo 2 each. That totals 25.
    3. Each clan is assigned a region of the home islands to take over (decision):
       - Clan Himura, 5 Legions: the Kinki region (Kyoto, Osaka, the Kii Peninsula and Nara), plus the Tokyo-Yokohama ports and the Tokyo commercial districts, which are the main prizes.
       - Clan Date, 4 Legions: Tōhoku and Hokkaido.
       - Clan Hojo, 2 Legions: the Kantō hinterland around Tokyo.
       - Clan Takeda, 4 Legions: the Chūbu mountains, the Hokuriku coast and the Tōkai corridor between Tokyo and Kyoto.
       - Clan Mori, 2 Legions: the Chūgoku region of western Honshu.
       - Clan Chōsokabe, 3 Legions: Shikoku and the Inland Sea ports.
       - Clan Otomo, 2 Legions: northern Kyushu, including Hakata.
       - Clan Shimazu, 3 Legions: southern Kyushu and the Ryukyu islands.
    4. The counter-espionage section grows into Kinki-Kyoku, the arm for technical secrecy and counter-espionage inside Himura Zaibatsu (decision).
    5. Tachibana Haruka (18) joins the Himura household as a sworn retainer (decision).
    6. In late 2120 the eight clans finalize the Restoration plan, with each clan's region and start date fixed (decision).
    7. The corvette prototype completes trials in lunar shadow. The frigate prototype finishes fitting out and completes trials in early 2121. Both fly on Solaris-1A and Solaris-1B cores. They are the only two hulls Clan Himura owns, and they stay hidden at the lunar base (decision).
17. Early 2121 - At the age of 31, Japan's coalition government is paralyzed by debt, population decline and Zaibatsu infighting, and the Restoration War begins (decision: early 2121).
    1. The Kii foundries have delivered the 100,000 Legion automata (decision). Clan Himura's 5 Legions field 20,000 Imperial automata, about 15,000 Bushi-3 and 5,000 Ō-Yoroi-3 on Solaris-3M cores. The 20 vassal-commanded Legions field about 80,000 Ashigaru-1 on Helios-1 cubes. Foundry output replaces battlefield losses throughout the war.
    2. Every clan moves into its assigned region at once. Rival Zaibatsu security forces and loyal government garrisons contest the cities, the major ports and the Zaibatsu strongholds in each region (decision).
    3. Corporate sabotage peaks, and Kurosawa's section breaks the last major rival rings.
    4. Kyoto and the Kinki region are secured in late 2121 (decision). The regional campaigns continue through the winter, and the clans with the smallest Legions, Mori, Otomo and Hojo, take the longest to finish their regions (decision).
    5. The two prototype hulls take no part in the war, because Kenji will not reveal them (decision).
    6. Imperial troops carry gravitic weapons from the first day of the war.
18. Spring 2122 - At the age of 32, the Restoration War ends after a little over a year. Kiyoshi is 8 or 9.
    1. Kurogane Yoroi retainers, vassal officers and NCOs in AEPS, and the 25 Legions complete the capture of the home islands. Each clan's Legions fight under its own commanders in its own region.
    2. The Chrysanthemum Throne is restored in the Kyoto-Heian Imperial Citadel, with Tokyo as the commercial and maritime metropolis.
    3. Kenji becomes Emperor and Clan Head (Tōshu), Kaguya becomes Empress, and the seven vassal clans become sworn Vassal Clans under Sankin-Kōtai.
    4. The Anti-Smart-AI Edict becomes national law. The Kansatsu-In and Kinki-Kyoku are formally founded.
    5. Surviving hostile Zaibatsu are dissolved or absorbed (decision).
    6. Kinki-Kyoku recalls the Helios-1 AEPS cubes from the vassal clans and reissues them as sealed, blackboxed Helios-3 units, at 75% of the matching Solaris-3 generation, so only Kenji's own household keeps the full Solaris Mote (decision).
    7. The former national maritime and space-defense personnel, about 9,000 people, are absorbed into the new Imperial Navy (IJN-DNK-). They are the first trained hands for the coming fleet (decision).
    8. The Throne enrolls the hereditary Fudai retainer households, about 2,900 families, as the crew pool for the Imperial Guard (decision).
    9. The Imperial Guard is established at once. Its ground arm is the Hatamoto Guard, about 1,500 sworn retainers drawn from the 250 Kurogane Yoroi retainers and the Fudai households, and Tachibana Haruka (20) is appointed to command it. Its fleet is the Imperial Guard Fleet (IJN-KNE-). The Guard holds the Kyoto garrison and the close protection of the Emperor, the Empress and Kiyoshi (decision).
    10. With the Helios-3 reissue the vassal suits are rebuilt as AEPS-3 in Kurogane-1M plate, the vassal Ashigaru-1 are reissued as Ashigaru-3, and Helios-pattern mass accelerators arrive with the Helios-3 schematics. Kurogane Yoroi and AEPS-3 suits can now be defeated only by gravitic or relativistic-velocity weapons (decision on the 2122 date).
19. 2123 - At the age of 33, Taiwan and South Korea sign mutual-defense and maritime-technology treaties with the Throne.
    1. Blackboxed Helios-3 reactors, Ashigaru-3 automata and Exo-Rigs are covertly supplied to their cadres.
    2. Kurogane exports end and steel exports are cut, because the Crown now needs all Kurogane for hull plating (decision). UNAS and EU observers read this as exhaustion. Licensed Helios-3C and Helios-3I units, which need Kurogane-1I to function, keep shipping with it sealed inside Crown-built components (decision). The yards draw domestic steel, Kurogane and ore from the lunar concession, from under 2 million tons in 2124 to about 27 million tons in 2131 (decision).
    3. The keel of the 1,000m flagship IJN Amaterasu-no-Yaiba is laid in Capital Dock 1 of the lunar-shadow slipway (decision).
    4. The Crown starts the slipway expansion. The Lunar-Shadow Slipway grows from 2 docks to 36 by 2129: 14 corvette bays, 6 frigate bays, 10 interceptor docks and 6 capital docks. Kōgyō-3 automata dig and fit the docks, and Kinki-Kyoku guards every shaft (decision). The opening schedule is in the Hull Build Program.
    5. Kōgyō production (Kōgyō-3, then Kōgyō-5 from 2126) is raised to 12,000 units by 2128. About 9,000 work in the lunar-shadow slipway and the low-orbit yard, under about 1,400 human Crown shipwrights (decision).
    6. The Imperial Naval Academy opens in Kyoto. Its first graduates join in 2125, then about 2,500 a year (decision).
    7. Kinki-Kyoku finishes the Type-90 gravitic torpedo family (Kozuka, Hari, Kusari and Kage-Ya). Capacitor and penetrator lines open at Kii-Yamato, run by Kōgyō automata (decision).
20. 2124-2125 - The first production hulls are commissioned from the lunar-shadow slipway (decision).
    1. In 2124 the fleet commissions 4 corvettes and 2 frigates. The two prototype hulls are refitted with Solaris-3A and Solaris-3B cores and commissioned among them as the first IJN-KNE-LE and IJN-KNE-EL. In 2125 it commissions 12 corvettes and 3 frigates. That is 21 hulls, all Imperial Guard (decision).
    2. The low-orbit sovereign shipyard grows to three docks. They build hull sections and plate modules, and the sections are flown to the lunar-shadow slipway for final assembly (decision).
    3. A frigate surveys candidate planetoids 25 light-years out with the fold drive proven in 2119. Seeding of the Proscribed Vault Sanctuaries waits for the 750m cruisers in 2127 (decision).
    4. Tier II Chōju cycles begin for Togo, Kurosawa, Yamashita and Tachibana, and each receives the Volitional Lock after his first immersion, and Hatamoto officers follow in batches as they reach command rank (decision).
    5. Tokyo's civilian economy recovers on licensed commercial technology.
    6. The Solaris-1C (4.5 m octahedron) is completed in 2124 and the Solaris-1D (7.0 m octahedron) in 2125, and both fly on test hulls. The Solaris-3C and Solaris-3D follow in 2125 as the first fleet-grade cores of those forms (decision). The keels of the first 750m Interceptor Cruisers are laid in 2125 (decision).
    7. Shimazu, Chōsokabe and Otomo turn traitor in 2125. Shimazu sells copies of the Helios-3 schematics in return for support to replace the Himura as the Imperial Clan. Taiwan and South Korea take no part in the sale. The three clans remain sworn Vassal Clans in name and keep their Legion commands (decision).
21. 2126 - At the age of 36, Amaterasu-no-Yaiba is commissioned with her dual-redundant Solaris-3D core pair.
    1. Ayame is born (decision).
    2. Moscow and Beijing formalize the Eurasian-Continental Pact. UNAS and EU sanctions tighten over the closed Pacific sea lanes.
    3. The first Line Cruiser keels are laid in the capital docks (decision).
    4. The fleet commissions 18 corvettes, 4 frigates and the flagship this year, and stands at 44 hulls (decision).
    5. Kenji completes the Solaris-5 family's M, A and B cores (50% of Solaris-9's capabilities). Hulls commissioned from 2128 carry Solaris-5 (decision).
22. 2127-2129 - The 750m Interceptor Cruisers enter service under Kinki-Kyoku and Shadow Squadron (2 in 2127, 4 in 2128, 6 in 2129), and Line Cruisers follow from 2128 (1 in 2128, 2 in 2129) (decision).
    1. Seeding of the Proscribed Vault Sanctuaries begins with the first IJN-KNK-SP cruisers in 2127, using the fold drive proven in 2119 (decision).
    2. In 2128 the Imperial Guard completes its escorts: all 64 corvettes and 16 frigates are in service. The Imperial Navy's own escorts begin to commission that year, 16 corvettes and 4 frigates (decision).
    3. The Legions are rebuilt to 25 Legions of 5,000 men and automata after the war's losses, and the clan command split does not change, because the supply of human officers and NCOs is the limit (decision). The Imperial Legions' Bushi and Ō-Yoroi move to Solaris-5M as Bushi-5 and Ō-Yoroi-5, and the Kurogane Yoroi suits become Kurogane Yoroi-5. The loyal clans' Ashigaru move to Helios-5 with the 2128 issue, and the traitor clans' Ashigaru stay at Ashigaru-3.
    4. Pact orbital kinetic-rod platforms and fusion dreadnoughts take shape at Baikonur and Vladivostok.
    5. In 2128 Jack Harper's UNAS intelligence work begins to touch Asian technology traffic (decision).
    6. The fleet stands at 149 hulls at the end of 2129, with about 15,700 human crew. Each crew drills six months in lunar shadow under Togo's staff before its hull is commissioned. The Imperial Guard hulls are crewed by the Fudai retainer households. The Navy and Shadow Squadron hulls are crewed by the absorbed maritime personnel and Academy graduates (decision).
    7. Kenji completes the Solaris-5C and Solaris-5D in 2127. Hulls from 2128 carry Solaris-5, while the 2127 interceptors and all earlier hulls keep Solaris-3. In 2128 Kinki-Kyoku issues sealed Helios-5 units to the loyal Vassal Clans (Date, Takeda, Mori and Hojo). Shimazu, Chōsokabe and Otomo stay on Helios-3 (decision).
23. 2130 - At the age of 40, Kenji's design-bay refinement holds the Solaris-5B core at the 45% mass-shedding cap under lower load.
    1. Kiyoshi, 17, asks to join the cadet company.
    2. Han Jae-won and Ri Hyok-chol fortify opposite sides of the DMZ. Ashigaru-3 automata and Imperial Legion advisors arrive in Korea.
    3. The fleet commissions 43 hulls and stands at 192. The last Imperial Guard Line Cruiser and the last three Kinki-Kyoku and Shadow Squadron interceptors commission, so those two branches are complete (decision).
24. 2131 - At the age of 41, the Hakata port seizure exposes a replaced customs seal and a late registry timestamp.
    1. Marchand and Williams cross-check optical tracks of Japanese hulls over Luna, and Brussels votes on sanctions.
    2. The last pre-war Tier II immersions take place. Masamune again declines.
    3. Sabotage is stopped at the low-orbit shipyard.
    4. Wolfpack drills run under Togo in lunar shadow.
    5. The last 44 hulls commission: 28 corvettes, 6 frigates, 6 interceptor cruisers and 4 Line Cruisers, all Imperial Navy (decision).
25. Early 2132 - At the age of 41 to 42, the pre-war fleet totals 236 hulls (85 Imperial Guard, 15 Kinki-Kyoku and Shadow Squadron, 136 Imperial Navy), 73 on Solaris-3 cores (commissioned through 2127) and 163 on Solaris-5 cores (from 2128), with escort packs at 1:4.
    1. The human crews total 27,700. The Imperial Guard takes about 8,600 from the Fudai households, and the other 19,100 come from the absorbed maritime personnel and about 17,500 Academy graduates over seven years (decision).
    2. The ground forces stand at 25 Legions, 125,000 men and automata in all. Each clan's Legions remain garrisoned in the region it took in the Restoration War: Himura 5 (Kinki and the Tokyo ports), Date 4 (Tōhoku and Hokkaido), Takeda 4 (Chūbu), Chōsokabe 3 (Shikoku), Shimazu 3 (southern Kyushu and the Ryukyus), Mori 2 (Chūgoku), Otomo 2 (northern Kyushu) and Hojo 2 (Kantō). Chōsokabe, Shimazu and Otomo have been traitors since 2125 but still hold their Legion commands. The Imperial Guard, about 1,500 Hatamoto under Tachibana, holds the Kyoto garrison and guards the Imperial Family (decision).
    3. A Pact patrol meets the unfinished DMZ left flank and its waiting automata.
    4. The yards do not stop. All 36 docks and the low-orbit yard keep working at the 2131 rate, and a 2132 batch is on the slips: 12 corvettes, 3 frigates, 2 interceptor cruisers and 2 Line Cruisers. They are the first wartime replacements and reach the fleet from late 2132 (decision).
    5. Reserves held for the war (decision): spare cores (48 Solaris-5A, 12 Solaris-5B and 10 Solaris-5C), a quarter-year stock of Kurogane plate at the low-orbit yard, and 22,000 Type-90 rounds in the lunar depot beyond the full magazines aboard the escorts.
26. October 12-13, 2132 - At the age of 42, Kurosawa and Sano recover the Pact's encrypted launch codes at Yokohama, stamped with a seal from a Japanese port office. Mikhailovich confirms the launch, the Imperial picket takes station, and the Reclamation War begins on October 14. Kiyoshi is 19.

### Supplies

- Cores for the 236 hulls: 247 A-type (160 corvette primaries plus the auxiliary cores on frigates, interceptors, Line Cruisers and the flagship), 40 B-type, 35 C-type and 2 D-type, which is 324 in all. The 73 hulls through 2127 carry 92 Solaris-3 cores (74 A, 14 B, 2 C and the flagship's 2 D). The 163 hulls from 2128 carry 232 Solaris-5 cores (173 A, 26 B and 33 C). The Core-Forge peaks at about 64 ship cores in 2131 and runs a year ahead of the hulls.
- Feedstock: 113 million tons over eight years, rising from under 2 million tons in 2124 to about 27 million tons in 2131.
- Shipboard automata: about 8,500 Bushi, Ō-Yoroi and Kage automata (Imperial models only), about 1,100 a year, well under a tenth of Kii-Yamato line output. Marine lockers hold about 5,000 Kurogane Yoroi-pattern exosuits.
- Ordnance: the corvette and frigate magazines hold about 11,000 Type-90 rounds (6,720 and 4,320), and the lunar depot holds 22,000 more.
- People: 27,700 ship crew by 2131, as set out in entry 25, on top of the 25,000 humans in the Legions.
