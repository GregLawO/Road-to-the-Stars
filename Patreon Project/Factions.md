## 1. CLAN HIMURA

### 1.1 The Imperial Family

- **Members:** Emperor Himura Kenji, Empress Himura Kaguya, Crown Prince Himura Kiyoshi, Himura Ayame (born 2126), Himura Haruto.
- **Seat:** Kyoto-Heian Imperial Citadel from the 2122 Restoration. Kenji is Sovereign and Clan Head (Tōshu).
- **Armed arm:** The Imperial Guard holds the pre-war Guard fleet and the Hatamoto Guard under Tachibana. Clan Himura commands 5 Legions in the Restoration War and 25 Legions of 100,000 by 2132 (author ruling). The vassal clans' Legions are separate.
- **POV:** Kiyoshi is the Main POV. Kenji, Kaguya, Ayame and Haruto are non-POV.
- **Standing friction:**
  - Kiyoshi resents the Hatamoto shadow Tachibana imposes.
  - Kaguya expects her children to resent parents who do not age, and expects Kiyoshi to resent a throne that will not open for centuries.
  - Kenji's household alone keeps Solaris cores. Every vassal and ally holds a Helios unit.

### 1.2 Clan Factions

- **Masamune:** Counts prices and opposes overreach. Non-POV.
- **Ryunosuke:** Argues for aggressive patrols and for claiming the Earth-Luna corridor. After the 2132 outbreak he demands seizure of mainland enclaves. Main POV (Imperial Fleet).
- **Shizuka:** Governs shifts, tonnage and rations, and cuts projects that do not ship. Main POV (internal affairs, and foreshadowing Kenji's moves and decisions).
- **Kōji:** Presses the succession question until Kiyoshi chooses to stay a soldier. Non-POV.

## 2. VASSAL CLANS

- **Status:** Hereditary Daimyō and Vassal Clan titles belong only to Japanese houses on sovereign Japanese territory and Japanese space colonies. Heads keep Kyoto residences under Sankin-Kōtai.
- **History:**
  - 2113: All seven pledge vassalage in secret.
  - 2118: Their retainers are conscripted into the Legions as officers and NCOs.
  - 2121 to 2122: Each clan commands its own Legions in its own region during the Restoration War.
  - 2122: All seven become sworn Vassal Clans. The Throne gifts every clan the Kurogane-1, AEPS and Ashigaru schematics.
  - Late 2125: The treachery of Shimazu, Chōsokabe and Otomo is found. Right after, Kinki-Kyoku issues Helios-3 units to the loyal clans only, in secret, and gives them the schematics.
  - 2127: The CPF steals the Kurogane-1, AEPS and Ashigaru schematics from Otomo.
  - Reclamation War: The war exterminates the traitor clans.
- **Oversight:** Kinki-Kyoku and Kansatsu-Kyoku audit export-grade hardware.
- **Standing friction:**
  - The loyal clans hold Helios-3 units issued by the house they helped crown and manufacture their own cores from its schematics, but Solaris cores stay with Himura alone.
  - The traitor clans were never issued Helios-3, and they resent it.
  - Ashigaru is the vassal class. The Imperial automata and the Kurogane Yoroi stay with Himura alone.
  - The traitor clans keep the Kurogane-1, AEPS and Ashigaru schematics and refuse to sell them, so that their foreign backers cannot abandon them. The CPF steals them anyway.

## 3. FOREIGN ALLIES

Taiwan and South Korea are the Empire's only treaty allies. Japan governs no foreign territory. Imperial forces enter allied territory only on formal request, and field command stays with the ally.

### 3.1 Taiwan (Formosa)

- **Government:** Fully independent island republic. No Imperial administration and no Daimyō clans.
- **Treaty:** Signed 2123. Hardware and schematic terms sit in Technology section 1.
- **POV:** Non-POV.
- **Standing friction:** Taiwan receives Helios-3 hardware, but the only schematics it can buy are Helios-1 and Kurogane-1. Imperial advisors critique its doctrine, and it keeps its own command.

### 3.2 South Korea (Republic of Korea)

- **Government:** Fully independent. No Imperial administration.
- **Treaty:** Signed 2123, on the same hardware and schematic terms as Taiwan. From 2130 Imperial Legion advisors and treaty Ashigaru-1 automata arrive on the DMZ, and Han Jae-won fortifies it.
- **POV:** Han Jae-won, Secondary POV, later NCO Seo Min-jae, Secondary POV.
- **Standing friction:**
  - Seoul's staff and field officers disagree over fixed-bunker doctrine and automata-first doctrine.
  - Staff resent Imperial advisors and rate treaty-supplied automata as expendable.
  - Korea is shut out of Solaris cores and Chōju, and can buy only the Helios-1 and Kurogane-1 schematics.

### 3.3 Systems Alliance (SSV-)

- **Established:** Five years after the Reclamation War.
- **Makeup:** A supranational human defense and exploration force chartered by UNAS, EU and UN representatives. It absorbs the former UNAS, EU and Russian POV share.
- **Terms with the Empire:** The Kyoto-Arcturus Concordat, negotiated by Ambassador Anita Goyle. The Alliance polices wider human extra-solar space on licensed, blackboxed hulls (Technology section 4). Japan keeps jurisdiction over its territory, Crown worlds, Vassal garden colonies and dormant relays bordering Imperial space.
- **Standing friction:** The Alliance runs capped hardware it cannot open. Drescher resents the cap, and Mikhailovich distrusts the quarantines.

## 4. Nations & Organizations

### 4.1 UNAS

- **Posture:** Never allied with Japan. Sanctions tighten over the closed Pacific sea lanes from 2126. Fights both the Pact and Imperial forces in the war.
- **POV:** Williams and Grissom, Main POV. Harper, Main POV, works as UNAS intelligence.
- **Standing friction:** Hawks resent Imperial supremacy, and Williams's call for détente draws suspicion.

### 4.2 EU

- **Posture:** Never allied with Japan. Sanctions tighten alongside UNAS. Brussels reads the 2123 end of Kurogane and steel exports as exhaustion.
- **POV:** Marchand, Secondary POV.

### 4.3 Russian Federation

- **Posture:** Moscow and Beijing formalize the Eurasian-Continental Pact in 2126.
- **POV:** Mikhailovich, Secondary POV.

### 4.4 CPF

- **Posture:** Pact member, Beijing-aligned.
- **POV:** Non-POV. Zhao Linfeng appears through other POVs and carries none.
- **Standing friction:** Moscow and Beijing share the Pact without sharing a command culture.

### 4.5 DPRK

- **Posture:** Pact member. Fortifies the northern side of the DMZ in 2130, opposite Han.
- **POV:** Ri Hyok-chol, Secondary POV.

### 4.6 Cerberus

- **Status:** Not formally established until the 2150s. Until then the group is the Harper-and-Lawson precursor network.
- **Members:** Jack Harper (UNAS intelligence, founder of Cerberus, Main POV) and Henry Lawson (private entrepreneur who seeks immortality, Main POV).
- **Aim:** Penetrate Kinki-Kyoku's technical secrets and Chōju protocols.
- **Limits:** Both are outsiders to every Imperial secret.
