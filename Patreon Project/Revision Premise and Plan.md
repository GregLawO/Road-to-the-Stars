# Revision Premise and Plan

Names in **bold** are my choices (decision), open to overrule. Everything else is the author's ruling.

---

## 1. Premise

- **Representatives replace generic OCs.** Each faction or group has a few recurring POV characters instead of a slew of throwaway voices. High-ranking figures become recurring anchors in the group they identify with, with few POVs each, so room opens up for the line characters.
- **Kenji is the main POV**, as the self-insert. Mass Effect used the same model, with most POV on Shepard.
- **Frontline view.** Junior officers and NCOs carry the frontline action, not only high-ranking overview POVs.
- **Equal screen time.** Every group except Kenji's gets the same share per chapter in which it is active. Weigh a group's importance before giving it a slot.
- **Group rules:**
  - Taiwan, South Korea and the Imperial ministers split the Tripartite share, since Imperial Japan is a Tripartite member.
  - The UNAS and EU are one group and split a share.
  - Russia, the CPF and the DPRK are one group and split a share.
  - Harper's group gets its own share. It is an enduring thorn in Kenji's side, so Harper mostly appears for it.
  - The Vassal Clans are a group of their own.
- **Where shares go.**
  - The Pact share passes to Harper's group once the Pact is defeated.
  - The UNAS, EU and Russians move under the Systems Alliance group once it is established.
  - The Vassals bank their slots until the garden colonies exist.
- **After A12** the novel focuses on the galactic scene, and most POVs go to canon Mass Effect characters.
- **Line characters** are named by me. The author supplies their profiles later.

## 2. The Imperial Family

- **Ages.** Kenji is b. 2092 (about 30 at the 2122 Restoration War) and Kaguya b. 2093. The 2115 breakthrough is unchanged, and Kenji is 23 then. Every Himura age in the outline follows from these.
- **Children.** Three in total.
  - **Himura Kiyoshi** (b. 2114) is 8 in 2122, 18 at the 2132 outbreak, 22 in 2136, 35 in 2149 and 40 when Shepard arrives in 2154.
  - **Himura Ayame** (b. 2126) and **Himura Haruto** (b. 2136) come a decade or two after the heir.
- **Kiyoshi's role.** A Legion ground-pounder in a Hata-Musha exosuit, with a Hatamoto shadow that Tachibana imposes and he resents. He is the Imperial presence in Shepard's story, with Tachibana as his mentor. He takes slots from Kenji's pool.
- **Chōju and the Lock.** All three children receive Tier I Chōju. Kiyoshi gets it at 25 (2139), after he stops demanding the throne. The Imperial Family carries no Volitional Lock. Neither Kiyoshi nor his siblings get even the dream-level foreknowledge.
- **The succession arc.** Agelessness means the throne will not open for centuries, so Kenji teaches his heir patience, the way the Dúnedain did. Kaguya's worry is her children's resentment: how they will take parents who do not age, and the eldest resenting a throne that never opens to him.

## 5. Keeping the three eras relevant after A12

- **Clan plots resolve by A9.** Masamune dies in 2139 and Shizuka retires. Ryunosuke moves into the Vassal group as envoy. Kōji's succession push ends when Kiyoshi chooses to stay a soldier. The Clan characters fade out of the limelight.
- **Kiyoshi is the bridge** into the galactic era, with the Lock-free apex-family angle as his tension.
- **Line characters carry into the ME era.** Aoki, Ibuki and Ōtsuka get Tier III (130–160 years), so they are about 50 in 2157 and serve as veteran Imperial detachments. The grunts find their way into the ME action. Sano's tier follows her position.
- **Anchors stay as background.** Kenji, Kaguya, Togo, Tachibana and Kurosawa remain the shield behind the galactic action.
- **Others migrate.** Harper and Lawson continue as the thorn. The Russians, UNAS and EU merge into the SA group alongside Grissom, Hackett, Anderson and Ryder.
