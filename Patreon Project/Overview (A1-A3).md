# Story Bible: Mass Effect AU Novel (trimmed for A2 and A3)

Only details the author has explicitly settled. Trimmed: Reaper mechanics, Imperium structure, Parts D to G structure, open-question lists.

## The Protagonist

Kenji Himura, of Japanese descent. A1 stays as published: he is not named and has no POV there; he appears only as the hand behind the nameless card. From A2 he carries POV, in the first person (author ruling, 3 November 2026).

No character knows he is a transmigrator, and none ever learns how he knows what he knows. This holds for Hannah, Brooks, and the whole inner circle.

## The marine

**Gunnery Sergeant Dominic Vance, USMC, retired (author ruling; name and rank are placeholders (decision)).** Locked recurring OC and main cast. Profile: Dominic_Vance_Character_Profile.md.

- **Origin.** Dying in summer 2051, he is Kenji's first recruit. He trades his memories, experience, and expertise for treatment through a two-way upload that copies them out and gives him his lungs back. Kenji uses the copy to create and hone the security droids' combat abilities; the droids' routines are the only place it lives. No character is told where the droids' fighting style comes from.
- **Role.** First member and first leader of the secret network. Brings in Ashby (autumn 2051); the two are Kenji's security detail when Kenji recruits the lawyers. Expands the team twice before Byron (July 2052). Scouts and recruits Brooks and brings her to Kenji.
- **Robots (author ruling, 3 November 2026).** Oyama Industries sells Level 1 to Level 3 robots. The organization's own security droids guard only its private sites: Level 2 at first, Level 3 from summer 2053 (decision), and Level 3 droids guard every Level 2 pod around the clock (author ruling, 5 November 2026). Two Level 5 droids are with Kenji at all times from 2055 and look human.

## The foundation (author ruling, 3 November 2026)

- **Recruits.** Vance, then Ashby, then the lawyers (October 2051), then a larger team, then Byron Pyrrus, owner of Oyama Industries (July 2052). Later Danny Shepard (autumn 2052) and Senator Eleanor Whitcombe (February 2053), who leads the organization's political factions.
- **Main cast.** Byron, the lawyer Helen Marchetti, and Whitcombe are main cast. Names and details are placeholders.
- **Oyama.** Builds the Primitive Med-Pods (Kenji builds none), L1 to L3 robots, L1 powered suits, Tokamak reactors, L1 anti-gravity, and Gundarium Alpha. It is Cerberus's first target.
- **Fronts.** Three clinics through Tallis Bay: Galveston, Marseille, and Singapore, opened summer 2053. They sell cloned organs pre-approved through the political faction.

## The 2059 bombing and the 2065 withdrawals

Set (author ruling).

- **2065.** Kenji is a major investor in Manswell's expedition from the start, through a puppet finance firm. When other lenders pull out, he becomes the largest contributor so the expedition goes through. Nobody squeezes Manswell on his behalf. Brooks and the network see coordinated withdrawals and conclude someone is working against Manswell, and possibly against Kenji.
- **The conspiracy.** The author-side label is "Cerberus." It is a different organization from canon's and has no link to Harper or the Third Hand. Its method is shadow control of the world's most advanced technologies, Kenji's above all, through cutouts, firms, regulators, and licensors. It has no technology his network could trace. It hears of the technology in 2055 and wants the technologies he lets spread (anti-gravity and inertial dampening, robotics, the Level 1 Med-Pod, later the gravitic and warp drives), not the Level 2 Med-Pod, which it cannot find or take (author ruling, 5 November 2026). It tries three times to buy Oyama's patents in 2058. At the third, its lawyer threatens Oyama's side with violence from "Cerberus," and that is how the network learns the name (author ruling). It raids Galveston in November 2058, bombs it in 2059 (reason open), and forges a letter that closes the clinic in 2061. His network uncovers it as one organization, not who leads it, and the two are at secret war. The story calls it "Cerberus" or "they," never "it."

## Story Structure (A2 and A3 only)

- **Order.** Written chapter by chapter. The Prologue is a brief in-universe history, impersonal, with no sign of who wrote it. Act I is planned in Act_I_Outline.md.
- **Release plan.** Chapters release weekly in batches of five. A2 is 20 micro-chapters, A3 is 10.
- **Casts.** Micro-chapter casts are in Excerpt_Bank.md; the revised Act I casts are in Act_I_Outline.md.
- **Act I** opens in 2050 with Kenji (A2.1), then runs forward.

## Open questions touching A2 and A3

- **Tier definitions.** The Med-Pod and Anti-Gravity levels (public L1, private L2) are decisions pending the author's definitions.
- **Dates.** The exact dates of A2.1 to A2.15 are proposals. The wedding chapter (A2.11) and Vance's ledger (A2.7) overlap in date (open).
- **Two departures in 2065.** A2.17 shows a scheduled departure in 2064; B1 announces Manswell's expedition in 2065. A2.17 should be read as an ordinary passenger crossing.
- **Hannah's firm.** She is a Navy officer in 2053 (A1.1) and runs complex security in 2059 (A1.4). Decision: she leaves after the 2059 bomb and founds a private firm in 2060.
- **The marine.** His final name and rank are placeholders.
- **A2.13.** Carries two beats (the bomb night's memory and the approval); a split is available if it runs long.
