## 2. KUROGANE ALLOY SERIES

- **Nature:** A hyper-dense atomic-lattice alloy. It disperses a nuclear-scale impact through its crystal matrix and vaporizes a sacrificial outer layer to shed plasma heat. The series caps at 50 times the strength of Grade 5 titanium (Tier 5).
- **Why it exists:** Ordinary alloys fail under a Solaris field.
- **Designation:** Tier number plus a letter, as in Kurogane-1I. I is industrial grade and M is military grade. The schematics gifted to the clans and stolen by the CPF cover I grade only. M grade stays with Clan Himura.
- **Production methods:** Tiers 1 to 3 share one method, so any holder of the Kurogane-1 schematics can discover 2 and 3 from it. Kurogane-1 to 3 are no longer secret. Tiers 4 and 5 use a different method that only Kenji holds.

## 3. SOLARIS CORES

Every core is a seamless matte-black sphere or octahedron suspended in a magnetic vacuum cradle. It makes no exhaust and no flame, only a sub-audible thrum in the deck plates and a faint golden-amber glow along its phase conduits. A deterministic Quantum-Optical VI regulates each one. Solaris cores equip Clan Himura only, and every core stays in Kenji's hands. The Solaris patents are his alone.

The **mass-shedding cap** is how much of its apparent mass a core can shed. A core's minimum apparent mass is 100% minus the cap. Generation shares compare against Solaris-9. Speed and acceleration scale in step with a core's share (derived), so the last column is the multiplier on the ship table in section 7.

| ---------- | ------------------ | ----------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Solaris-1  | 10%                | 15%               | 40%                                  |

| Solaris-7  | 75%                | 60%               | 300%                                 |

### Core forms

- **Solaris-1:** The Mote (suit core), the Early Forge core (the first Core-Forge hall) and the Chrysalis (production core).
- **Solaris-3:**
  - Suit cores.
  - Smaller spheres for corvettes, frigates and auxiliary power.
  - Larger octahedrons for Interceptor and Line Cruisers.
  - A dual-redundant pair for the flagship.
  - Giant stationary octahedrons for forge, slipway and shipyard use.
- **Solaris-5, 7 and 9:** Forms (open).

### Casing and anti-capture

- **Casing:** Seamless, with no hatch. Cutting torches and tomographic scans trip optical tripwires.
- **Hearth-Quench:** Fuses the core lattice into inert slag in 0.4 seconds.
- **Hearth-Collapse:** Implodes the hull or facility into a micro-singularity.
- **Dead-man keys:** Both keys sit in a locked crimson-and-brass shrine-box beside the chief engineer's console.

## 5. FLIGHT, FTL AND STEALTH

- **Reactionless gravimetric thrust:** The core supplies thrust, power, inertial dampening and shield projection from one closed loop.
- **Inertial dampening:** 99.5%. Crews wear heavy magnetic crash harnesses and feel a gravitational tug in hard turns.
- **Sequential Spatial-Fold FTL:** Fleet hulls cruise at 12 light-years per day.
- **Tactical micro-jumps:** Fold-based. Range and limits (open).
- **Eclipse stealth:** Waste heat is shunted into solid-state thermal sinks. Endurance is 4 hours, or 6 hours on modified Kinki-Kyoku and Shadow Squadron interceptors.

## 6. SHIELDING

- **Faceted directional barriers:** Angled golden-amber deflector planes project ahead of the prow and flanks. Solaris hulls and Kurogane Yoroi carry them.
- **Bubble shields:** Conventional spherical shields, carried by every Helios unit.
- **Phase-Gated Firing Apertures:** Millisecond tunnels open through the barrier for outgoing fire.

This covers the Pact and Western defenses and every bubble shield. Only gravitic barriers bend reduced-inertia rounds off course and force an early mass-lock, and a target with one needs saturation salvos.

## 8. WEAPONS

### Type-90 Gravitic Torpedoes

- **Propulsion:** Solid-state capacitor banks drive a reactionless forward gravitic drive. There is no reactor and no propellant.
- **Flight profile:** A boost burn of about 200 G, then a silent coast ("Whisper-Glide"), then a delayed terminal mass-lock that restores full mass at impact. Rounds shed 30% of their mass in flight at Solaris-3. Later generations (open).
- **Guidance:** Passive optical and infrared tracking. The Kage-Ya also maps the target's mass distribution.
- **Tamper denial:** A lattice collapse and thermite-potted electronics destroy a captured round.
- **Hammer and needle:** Light Kozuka rounds go ahead of heavy Kage-Ya rounds to strip point defense, overload emitters and crack armor just before the impactor arrives.
- **Dead-stick sniping:** Against predictable convoys or fixed targets, rounds can launch cold with dormant capacitors and wake about 10 seconds before arrival, skipping the boost and Whisper-Glide signatures.
- **Bombardment:** Routine bombardment uses shipboard slugs rather than torpedoes. Torpedoes are for surgical strikes on shielded targets.

### Guns and beams

- **Axial spinal Macrocannons:** Only on 750 m and larger hulls. Slugs travel at 2.0% c on interceptors and 3.0% c on Line Cruisers and the flagship. Firing needs the whole hull aligned.
- **Iai-Pivot and Keystone Lock:** The hull pivots its bow to align the spinal gun. At discharge it snaps back to 100% rest mass for 15 milliseconds to absorb the recoil.
- **Turreted Macrocannons:** A 10 m class fires a 10 kg slug at 0.5% c. A 100 m class fires a 100 kg slug at 1.5% c and sits on Line Cruisers and the flagship.
- **Twin Energy Lances:** Lightspeed thermal strikes at twice Macrocannon range. They are the answer to probes and bomber wings.
- **Point defense:** Point-defense Energy Lances and Macrocannons ring each hull.
- **Singularity Cannons and Protocol Mirror-Shatter:** (open)

### Personal arms

- **Conventional coil-rifles and oiled mono-blade katanas:** Carried by vassal and allied troops.
- **Helios-pattern mass accelerators:** Anyone who builds Helios-3 can build them. They are the answer to Kurogane-3 armor. The loyal clans, the traitor clans and the foreign powers all build them.

## 9. COMPUTING

- **Quantum-Optical VI:** A deterministic, read-only lattice. It is non-sentient and cannot develop self-awareness or goal drift. No automaton can read or copy its own firmware, so a captured frame yields nothing. The architecture is Kenji's alone.
- **Anti-Smart-AI Edict:** It covers Himura Zaibatsu and all vassal domains, and is national law. Smart-AI use by foreign powers is (open).
- **Duties:** Fire control, target tracking, mass-modulation timing and telemetry. Living officers keep command authority.
- **Bridge signature:** Silent columns of golden-amber kanji, and a single bronze temple-bell tone at target lock.

## 10. AUTOMATA

- **Command Lock:** Every automaton accepts orders only from its assigned living officer or NCO. Its VI is etched on a fixed read-only crystal.
- **Posts:** Automata hold fixed posts in silence with dimmed ember optical slits. They move only on an authorized command or a security trigger.
- **Alcoves:** Magnetic wall-charging clamps hold them dormant in ship corridors. At general quarters the slits flare golden-amber and the clamps release with a synchronized clack.
- **Naming:** Each frame carries the iteration number of its core (Ashigaru-1 runs on Helios-1, Bushi-3 on Solaris-3M). A variant letter follows, as in Ashigaru-3B.
- **Control and tamper denial:** Orders arrive by line-of-sight infrared and ultraviolet pulses. A spoofed order is clipped and the unit reverts to local defense. If a fireteam's NCO and officer chain die and no authorized commander re-links within 15 minutes, the automata hold a perimeter around the fallen. Cutting into the cranial crystal or overriding the Command Lock triggers Hearth-Quench.
- **Look (Imperial classes):** Angular samurai plating in golden-bronze and obsidian-charcoal. The optical slits glow amber at general quarters.

## 13. MEDICINE AND SECRECY

### Chōju (longevity treatment)

- **Throneside Medical Bureau:** Kenji's alone.
- **Tensei-Shori:** A 14-day immersion. Recipients emerge ravenously hungry.
  - **Days 1 to 2:** Scan. Optical tomography catalogs the damage and sets a reset profile aimed at the recipient's peak age of 24 to 28.
  - **Days 3 to 6:** Purge and renewal. Nano-capsules and viral vectors flush senescent cells and replace degraded mitochondrial DNA.
  - **Days 7 to 11:** Telomere lock. Enzymes restore telomere caps and install a governor that stops both shortening and runaway cancer.
  - **Days 12 to 14:** Re-sculpting. In a gravimetric suspension bath, stem cells rebuild bone, cartilage, skin, lenses and heart muscle. Memory and personality are untouched.
- **Tier I (the Imperial Family):** The full procedure every 40 years with continuous stabilization. Indefinite prime, 300+ years minimum. Kenji, Kaguya, Ayame and Haruto hold it (dates in section 1.2). Kiyoshi receives it after he stops demanding the throne.
- **Tier II:** The full procedure with boosters every 35 to 40 years, for 180 to 300 years of 25-to-30-year-old strength. It is given to senior officials and Hatamoto officers as they reach command rank, and it carries the Volitional Lock.
- **Declined:** Masamune Himura has declined treatment since 2116.
- **Tier III:** Commercial longevity. Terms (open).
- **Tier IV (export):** Prohibited through the war, so foreign diplomats and magnates stay on conventional medicine of 100 to 120 years. Post-war status (open).
- **Look:** Unblemished skin, dense muscle, sharp posture and clear eyes. A recipient looks decades younger, distinguishable only by the weight of their gaze.

### The Volitional Lock (author-side: never state in-universe)

Chapters may allude only to its effects through the character's experience: a locked jaw, stiffened fingers, a sentence clamping off mid-word, or a nosebleed or fainting under overdrive.

- **What it is:** A neuro-linguistic conditioning session given right after a recipient's first Tier II immersion. The Imperial Family carries none. It makes conscious betrayal of the Throne, the proscribed technologies or the secret directorates physically impossible. It lasts for life and needs no implant.
- **What it leaves alone:** Judgment, opinions, loyalties, emotions and free will. A conditioned official can argue, disagree, refuse an unlawful order, defy a superior or resign.
- **How it blocks:** Jaw lock, frozen vocal cords, stiffened fingers, or a cancelled motor impulse while the subject stays fully conscious.
- **What it protects:** The nature of the Compliance Directorate (Kansatsu-In) and Kinki-Kyoku, agent identities, hidden foundry and Vault Sanctuary coordinates, Solaris and Chōju architecture, and the House of Himura. It covers speech, writing and signaling. It also blocks knowing sale of a Solaris core, Chōju formula or classified record to a foreign power, and abandoning a protected post at an enemy's demand.
- **Who triggers it:** Uncleared outsiders, foreign diplomats and Vassal Clan subjects. Conditioned insiders pass freely.
- **What it cannot stop:** Silence is allowed, though a cover story cannot inadvertently reveal a protected truth. Unwitting mistakes pass. Bribes, blackmail, torture and truth serums change nothing. Conditioned operatives can still be killed, captured or impersonated. Contractors, Helios export technicians, Vassal Clan staff and foreign personnel carry no Lock. Under intoxication, delirium or sleep-talking, slips drift to harmless topics.
- **Protection tiers** (a perceptual lean the operative can override, never a command):
  1. Emperor Himura and the Imperial Household.
  2. The operative's own family, with the High Censors, Fleet Admirals and Inner Council Lords.
  3. Empire and Crown assets: foundries, Vault Sanctuaries, Solaris cores, warships, sovereign archives.
  4. Cohort: the operative and fellow conditioned peers and subordinates.
  5. Outsiders, with no default priority.
- **Hostage Release Valve:** The one exception. Only a held family member opens it. The operative may then disclose general Crown infrastructure, standard equipment, non-classified routines, their own identity, rank and duties, and general unit structures. Tier 1 and 2 identities and whereabouts, safe houses, peers' names, faces and addresses, and anything that could lead to them (a duty roster, a transit schedule) stay blocked. Reporting the hostage situation to Directorate command always passes.
- **Overdrive:** Forcing a blocked Tier 1 or 2 secret through willpower causes a sudden nosebleed, blinding migraine or fainting before a syllable escapes.
