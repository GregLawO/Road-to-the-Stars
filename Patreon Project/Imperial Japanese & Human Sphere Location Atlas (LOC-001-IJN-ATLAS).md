### IMPERIAL JAPANESE & HUMAN SPHERE LOCATION ATLAS (2122–2150+)

DOCUMENT REF: LOC-001-IJN-ATLAS

PURPOSE: NARRATIVE & WORLD-BUILDING SETTING REFERENCE

---

PART I: PRE-WAR LOCATIONS (2122–2132 — THE CHRYSALIS YEARS & SILENT BUILD-UP)

1. The Kyoto-Heian Imperial Citadel & Amaterasu Palace (Kyoto, Japan)
- Role & Atmosphere: The newly restored sovereign capital of the Empire of Japan under Emperor Himura Kenji (born 2092) and Empress Himura Kaguya (born 2093). Traditional dark-cedar architecture, stone ramparts, and manicured zen gardens sit directly above freshly excavated subterranean bunkers housing early Solaris-4 shield generators and the Throneside Medical Bureau.
- Narrative Utility: Stage for Emperor Himura's quiet statecraft, Sankin-Kōtai meetings with sworn Japanese Vassal Clans, Tier I and Tier II Chōju longevity protocols, and sovereign bilateral treaties with Taiwan and South Korea.
2. The Kii-Yamato Subterranean Core-Forge (Kii Peninsula, Japan)
- Role & Atmosphere: The original secret underground Crown Foundry excavated beneath the rugged Kii mountains. Cavernous basalt halls lit by golden-amber photonic strips house early Solaris-4I Forge reactors, crystal presses, and thousands of motionless Ashigaru-4 and Ō-Yoroi-4 automata holding fixed posts in complete silence with dimmed ember optical slits.
- Narrative Utility: Demonstrates the hidden industrial might of Clan Himura while foreign intelligence agencies still believe Japan is an isolated island nation.
3. Taipei Maritime & Quantum-Optical Enclave (Taiwan — Sovereign Ally)
- Role & Atmosphere: A reinforced coastal command and semiconductor center in sovereign Taiwan, smelling of sea salt, ozone, and quantum-optical crystal fabrication lines.
- Narrative Utility: Setting for bilateral commercial and maritime-security coordination between sovereign Taiwanese officials and Imperial Japanese envoys—with zero Imperial administration and zero local Daimyō clans—securing Taiwan Strait sea lanes prior to 2132.
4. Seoul & The Korean Peninsula Defense Line (Republic of Korea — Sovereign Ally)
- Role & Atmosphere: Fortified subterranean command rooms in sovereign Seoul and frozen observation posts facing the northern Eurasian-Continental Pact border.
- Narrative Utility: Highlights the exclusive treaty alliance between Kyoto and Seoul—where sovereign Republic of Korea commanders coordinate joint intelligence and commercial shipbuilding with Imperial Japanese liaisons while maintaining complete national self-governance.
5. NORAD-Orbital Command & Cheyenne Mountain Citadel (Colorado, UNAS)
- Role & Atmosphere: The heavily fortified strategic nerve center of the United North American States, packed with multi-tiered fusion-telemetry consoles and satellite tracking walls.
- Narrative Utility: Where General Williams and UNAS orbital intelligence officers coordinate high-orbit surveillance over the Pacific—growing increasingly alarmed as reconnaissance satellites detect impossible 45% mass-shadow drops and reactionless accelerations from Japanese vessels.
6. The Brussels Strategic Directorate & Kourou Equatorial Lift (European Union)
- Role & Atmosphere: Glass-and-steel administrative towers in Brussels and the European Union's primary equatorial space-elevator tether in French Guiana.
- Narrative Utility: Backdrop for European diplomats drafting economic sanctions against Kyoto during the resource crises of the 2120s, while EU intelligence analysts track Japanese deep-space tonnage from Lunar Station Copernicus.
7. Baikonur-Orbital & Vladivostok Pacific Bastion (Eurasian-Continental Pact)
- Role & Atmosphere: Heavily armored nuclear-thermal orbital drydocks and frozen Pacific coastal missile fortresses controlled by the neo-authoritarian Moscow-Beijing Pact.
- Narrative Utility: Where Pact commanders oversee brute-force dreadnought construction and orbital kinetic rod platforms in preparation for their 2132 preemptive strike against Japan.
8. The Earth-Luna Dark Corridor & Lunar Station Copernicus (High Earth Orbit & Luna)
- Role & Atmosphere: The unlit void between Earth's geostationary belt and Luna, contrasted with the pressurized Western intelligence hub at Lunar Station Copernicus.
- Narrative Utility: Shadow Squadron (IJN-KGE-SP) and Kinki-Kyoku (IJN-KNK-SP) cruisers prowl in Eclipse stealth hunting foreign espionage probes, while Western analysts watch impossible mass-shadow anomalies over Japan right up until the 2132 outbreak of World War III.
9. The 25-Light-Year Proscribed Vault Sanctuaries (Deep Space Beyond Sol)
- Role & Atmosphere: Hollowed-out rogue planetoids and icy Kuiper/interstellar asteroids seeded between 2122 and 2132 by IJN-KNK-SP cruisers, sealed behind automated Solaris-4 traps and silent sentinel automata.
- Narrative Utility: Off-site failsafe vaults storing emergency Solaris core blueprints and proscribed archives, ensuring Clan Himura's survival even if Earth is struck.

PART II: WORLD WAR III LOCATIONS (2132–2136 — THE IRON TEMPEST)

1. The Shattered Earth-Luna Orbital Debris Ring & Tycho Crater Bunker (Low Earth Orbit to Luna)
- Role & Atmosphere: A blinding void graveyard choked with pulverized fusion hulls and crackling golden-amber External Shear Barriers, anchored by scarred UNAS and EU siege bunkers at Tycho Crater on Luna.
- Narrative Utility: Where Fleet Admiral Togo Kazuhiko's 1:2 Frigate-Corvette wolfpacks execute Kage-Ōkami standoff torpedo ambushes in 2132, followed by the mid-war debut of Solaris-7 warships executing 100,000 km Single-Fold Tactical Micro-Jumps to break rival orbital fleets.
2. The Korean Peninsula & Manchurian Campaign Fronts (Sovereign Korea & Continental Asia)
- Role & Atmosphere: Smoke-choked mountain valleys, frozen rail hubs, and fortified Pact missile bastions battered by orbital strikes and relentless artillery duels.
- Narrative Utility: Following formal requests for aid from sovereign South Korea, General Yamashita Takeo leads podless "Golden Rain" autonomous orbital drops—unleashing Kikai-Bushi automata and giant Oni-Kikai heavy mecha to break the Pact's continental armies before withdrawing all Imperial troops back to sovereign Japanese soil upon victory in 2136.
3. The Taiwan Strait & Western Pacific Naval Bastions (Sovereign Taiwan & Western Pacific)
- Role & Atmosphere: Storm-lashed sea lanes and fortified island defense grids lit by coherent Energy Lance beams and incoming kinetic rods.
- Narrative Utility: Where Imperial Japanese Starfleet squadrons and sovereign Taiwanese naval forces repel Pact saturation strikes against the Home Islands, Taiwan, and Western Pacific hubs.
4. The Vladivostok & Sea of Japan Naval Crucible (Western Pacific)
- Role & Atmosphere: Frozen coastal missile fortresses, submarine pens, and shattered Pact orbital defense grids along the Sea of Japan.
- Narrative Utility: Neutralized by Imperial 1:2 Frigate-Corvette wolfpacks firing Type-90C Kage-Ya torpedoes and IJN-TNU-LE(S) Close-Support Corvettes hovering in upper atmosphere.
5. The Bering Strait & North Pacific Orbital Front (Alaska & Kurils)
- Role & Atmosphere: Sub-zero storm-lashed orbital corridors and Aleutian staging bases where UNAS orbital interceptor wings clash with Imperial Japanese squadrons over the North Pacific.
- Narrative Utility: Setting where young UNAS pilot Jon Grissom (age 20–24) witnesses mid-war Solaris-7 Japanese cruisers execute reactionless Iai-Pivots and Single-Fold Tactical Micro-Jumps firsthand.
6. The Cascadia & Puget Sound Orbital-Naval Yards (Pacific Northwest, UNAS)
- Role & Atmosphere: Rain-swept coastal drydocks and burning fusion-reactor shipyards along the Seattle-Bremerton corridor, lit by golden-amber streaks of podless orbital drops.
- Narrative Utility: Precision Imperial strike corridor designed to neutralize UNAS orbital projection capabilities across the Pacific without attempting continental occupation.
7. The Baikonur & Ural Orbital-Industrial Bastions (Eurasian-Continental Pact)
- Role & Atmosphere: Heavy nuclear-thermal dreadnought yards and kinetic-rod launch rings deep inside the Eurasian interior.
- Narrative Utility: Shattered by long-range Imperial Type-90D O-Yumi siege salvos and deep-penetration orbital drops that cripple the Pact's strategic war production.
8. The Kourou Equatorial Space-Elevator & Orbital Tether Ruins (French Guiana, EU)
- Role & Atmosphere: The European Union's primary equatorial launch complex and orbital lift tether, reduced to twisted carbon-nanotube wreckage amid steaming jungle ruins.
- Narrative Utility: Surgical Imperial Shock Corps (IJN-TSG-) orbital operation that severs EU heavy-lift access to orbit, forcing Brussels out of the Pacific theater.
9. High-Output Wartime Orbital Forges & Demilitarized Manchurian Ruins
- Role & Atmosphere: Blinding Japanese orbital shipyard rings glowing around the clock under Solaris-7I Wartime Forge reactors, contrasted with shattered Pact command bunkers on the mainland.
- Narrative Utility: Where UNAS intelligence operative Jack Harper (age ~20–24) witnesses Imperial Gravitic Shinobi and Volitional-Locked officers in action—sparking his lifelong obsession with stealing Solaris and Chōju secrets.

PART III: POST-WAR & CHARON RELAY LOCATIONS (2136–2150+ — THE IRON AEGIS)

1. The 10,000m Throne-Citadel IJN Amaterasu (Mobile Sovereign Capital, Sol & Crown Space)
- Role & Atmosphere: Emperor Himura's newly commissioned 10,000-meter Imperial Flagship (IJN-TNS-MC), commanded by Fleet Admiral Togo Kazuhiko. Blends severe cathedral-free naval lethality with sacred cedar-and-gold throne halls, 12 Axial Singularity Cannons, and a 500-ship Imperial Guard screen.

- Narrative Utility: The supreme seat of post-war power where Emperor Himura enforces the 1,500-ship fleet cap, receives Systems Alliance envoys like Ambassador Anita Goyle, and demonstrates controlled standoff supremacy.
2. The Takamagahara Orbital Shipyard Ring (Geostationary Orbit above Kyoto)
- Role & Atmosphere: The perfected post-war orbital megastructure powered by twelve linked Solaris-9I Forge cores. Features a stark technological divide: sealed Crown slipways building Solaris-9 warships alongside automated blackbox modules manufacturing 50%-capped Helios-8 export drives for the Systems Alliance.

- Narrative Utility: Visualizes Imperial Japan's absolute industrial monopoly and High Censor Kurosawa Renji's strict Kinki-Kyoku anti-bleed enforcement.
3. Reunified Seoul, Taipei & Independent Chinese Republics (Sovereign East Asia, 2136–2150+)
- Role & Atmosphere: Rapidly rebuilding, prosperous sovereign nations across East Asia operating with zero Imperial Japanese occupation, garrisons, or Governors-General under the 2136 Imperial Settlement.

- Narrative Utility: Showcases Emperor Himura's strict Doctrine of Sovereign Non-Interference—where sovereign allies South Korea and Taiwan, alongside independent post-Pact Chinese states, rebuild using licensed commercial Helios-2 and Helios-5 reactors while trading peacefully with Kyoto.
4. London, Buenos Aires, Geneva & Arcturus Station (UNAS, EU & Systems Alliance, 2136–2150+)
- Role & Atmosphere: Rebuilding post-war Earth cities where young Steven Hackett (b. 2134) and David Anderson (b. 2137) grow up, paired with Arcturus Station (2149+), humanity's first extra-solar fleet hub constructed by Admiral Kastanie Drescher and Jon Grissom using licensed Japanese Helios-8 structural cores.

- Narrative Utility: Stage for the 2149 Kyoto-Arcturus Concordat negotiations between Ambassador Anita Goyle, Admiral Boris Mikhailovich, and the Imperial Court in Kyoto.
5. Promethean Station (Mars, 2148) & The Charon Relay Quarantine Perimeter (Outer Sol, 2149+)
- Role & Atmosphere:
  
  - Promethean Station, Mars (2148): Subterranean Prothean ruins excavated by Systems Alliance teams—unlocking Element Zero (Eezo) and Mass Effect physics outside Imperial jurisdiction and inspiring young N7 officer Alec Ryder (age 20 in 2149) to pursue true AI research.
  - Charon Relay Quarantine Perimeter (2149+): The freezing, dark edge of the Sol system where Admiral Jon Grissom (age 37) leads the first human expedition through the Charon Relay, while Henkyō-Kyoku (IJN-KNS-SP) Interceptor Cruisers silently quarantine dormant relays bordering Imperial Crown space.
6. Japanese Vassal Clan Colonies, Ame-no-Iwato & Fringe Black-Ops Labs (2136–2150+)
- Role & Atmosphere:
  - Japanese Vassal Clan Garden Colonies: Extrasolar agricultural and industrial worlds stewarded by hereditary Japanese noble houses sworn to Kyoto using 50%-capped Helios-8 labor frames, patrolled overhead by IJN-TNU- 1:2 Frigate-Corvette divisions.
  - Ame-no-Iwato: A heavily fortified sovereign Crown world housing Proscribed Vault Sanctuaries and serving as the staging base for the 5 Deep-Space Exploration Fleet squadrons (IJN-TNK-).
  - Outer-Belt Black-Ops Labs: Unmapped asteroid stations outside both Systems Alliance and Imperial borders where Jack Harper (age ~37) and Henry Lawson (age ~35) fund covert raids against Helios-8 shipments and conduct illegal genetic experiments—culminating in the creation of Miranda Lawson in 2150.
