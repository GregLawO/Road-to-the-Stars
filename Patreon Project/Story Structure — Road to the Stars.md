### Story Structure — Road to the Stars

---

Starting Timeline, Word Count Target, and Core Realism Locks (2130–2150+):

- Story Opens in 2130–2131: Drops readers onto the razor's edge of World War III before the October 14, 2132 Eurasian-Continental Pact (Russian, CPF, and DPRK) strikes.
- Full-Length Epic Scope (2 Batches / 10 POV Segments per Chapter — ~120,000 Words Total): Across 12 chapters (A1 to A12), each chapter contains 2 five-segment batches (Batch 1: .1 to .5; Batch 2: .6 to .10) at 500–1,500 words per POV segment (~10,000 words per chapter; ~120,000 words across 24 batches).
- Universal Inexperience in Space Warfare: The 2122 Imperial Restoration War was an Earth-bound conflict. When orbital war erupts in 2132, no nation—including the Empire of Japan—has fought a real void battle. Imperial crews make costly 3D tactical mistakes, mismanage Solaris-4 thermal-sink timers, exit micro-jumps poorly, and suffer severe G-strain under 99.5% inertial dampening.
- Allied Growing Pains (Taiwan and South Korea): Having fought no major war since the 20th century, Taiwanese and South Korean forces suffer early doctrinal panics, broken formations, and automaton-command errors that nearly break the alliance before Restoration War veterans retrain them under fire. Under pre-war bilateral mutual-defense treaties, Taiwan and South Korea—Imperial Japan's sole sovereign allies—receive limited, Kinki-Kyoku-blackboxed Helios-2 defensive systems prior to 2132 (whereas global commercial Helios exports to non-allied nations begin only after the 2136 Settlement).
- Two-Front War Alignment (No War Between UNAS/EU and Russia/CPF/DPRK): The UNAS and EU are NOT at war with Russia, the CPF, or the DPRK. Instead, the East Asian Alliance (Empire of Japan, Taiwan, and South Korea) fights a two-front global war against the Eurasian-Continental Pact in Asia/orbit and against the Western UNAS/EU coalition in the Pacific, Earth-Luna corridor, Puget Sound, and Kourou.
- Strictly Grounded Enemy Tactics (UNAS, EU, Russia, CPF, DPRK): Adversaries have zero ungrounded knowledge of Solaris cores, gravitic lattices, or Choju protocols. Their commanders operate strictly at their real-world chemical, nuclear-thermal, radar/optical, and conventional ballistic tier—using saturation decoys, area-denial flak clouds, dense static minefields, subterranean terrain, and observed optical tracking data rather than impossible sensors or contrived plot devices.
- Extended Clan Himura Integration: Grand Uncle Masamune (cautious council elder who refuses Choju longevity), Cousin Ryunosuke (ambitious, hawkish foil who starts as a 500m Frigate Captain in 2132 before earning Line Cruiser command in 2134), and Lady Shizuka Himura (indefatigable foundry and wartime logistics matron) are woven directly into the court, shipyard, and wartime arcs across Chapters A1 through A7.
- Act-Level Pacing Balance (12-Chapter Spine — 60% Action / 40% Setup, Intrigue, and Aftermath): 7 action-weighted chapters and 5 intrigue/setup/aftermath-weighted chapters, with every chapter mixing both action POVs and intrigue/aftermath POVs.

Rules followed:

- Acts are chapters (A1 to A12), releasing in strict chronological order (2130–2150+).
- Each chapter is drafted in 2 batches of 5 segments (Batch 1: A1.1 to A1.5; Batch 2: A1.6 to A1.10), 500–1,500 words per POV segment.
- Batch 1 of each chapter opens with an Excerpt from Annals of the Chrysanthemum Restoration or Post-Settlement Naval Monograph, Kyoto (no author name, incomplete scholar perspective, zero state secrets).
- Batch 2 of each chapter opens with a Recap telling only what Batch 1 showed the reader.
- Every batch ends with a Technology Reference List naming only new technologies shown on the page in that batch.
- Ending codes per segment: P (Physical/Tactical Pivot, max 2 per batch), Q (Unanswered Question), B (Betrayal/Secret Exposed), W (Warning/Threat), E (Emotional/Cost Beat). Strongest hook sits on Segment 5 of each batch (.5 and .10).
- Strict canon locks: Sovereign Imperial Capital is Kyoto (Tokyo is a commercial/maritime metropolis); Taiwan and South Korea are Imperial Japan's sole sovereign allies (UNAS and EU are non-allied rival belligerents during WWIII); strict non-interference (no Daimyo clans or Imperial rule over China, Korea, or Taiwan); Emperor Kenji Himura does NOT know he is in the Mass Effect universe until the 2148 Mars Prothean Archives discovery and 2149 Charon Relay activation; Anti-Smart-AI Edict (Quantum-Optical VIs only); Volitional Lock shown strictly through physical symptoms (never quoted or called "conditioned"); silent post-holding automata; zero doubt from Emperor Kenji Himura regarding his own tech; and zero living aliens or Citadel appearances through 2150+.
