## 1. Premise

A man wakes up in a world very different from the one he knew when he last went to sleep. He is a **transmigrator** with inexplicable knowledge **and expertise** in fictional, impossible technologies and techniques. His name in this world is **Kenji Himura**, of Japanese descent (author ruling).

- **Setting:** The Mass Effect universe in 2050. He does not know this at first.
- **Type:** Alternate Universe. Canon events can be changed or broken, but the story mostly follows the canon route.
- **Knowledge principle:** Full knowledge and expertise. He knows how to build, use, and bootstrap the technologies, including tools and manufacturing. Receiving it cost him nothing.
- **Reaction on waking:** Surprised, wonders what happened, and immediately suspects he has transmigrated. No answer is ever given.
- **Caution:** Better safe than sorry. He takes his time to secretly build up his foundation.
- **Real-world constraint:** Factories, refining, and growing and training soldiers take time.
- **Method:** He delegates to subordinates and forces.
- **Voice:** First person (author ruling, 3 November 2026).

## 2. Kenji: arc, character, realization (what A2 and A3 need)

### Arc and motivation
- He grew up poor and lonely in his past life. Being wanted still makes him flinch.
- On waking he understands he is "not in Kansas anymore" and knows patience trumps ambition. He retreats from view and spends years anchoring his foundation.
- He wants a normal life with Hannah. Every gift draws a hostile reaction, and the losses push him from a quiet life toward a defended one.

### Character
- An **anti-hero**.
- **Secrecy.** He never makes public, or tells any character, that he is a transmigrator or that he woke with knowledge and expertise. Anyone outside his inner circle learns only what has passed his scrutiny.

### Realization
- He does not know he is in the Mass Effect universe when he wakes.
- **The realization comes in three waves (author ruling).**
  1. Before 2065, a small list of companies with very familiar names. He cannot place them and has no story thought and no Reaper thought (decision).
  2. In 2065, Manswell announces the Alpha Centauri expedition and he understands why the names were familiar.
  3. In 2065, his droids uncover the Mars cavern, the final confirmation.
- The reader learns this from his POV: his suspicion that he has transmigrated at his first chapter (A2.1, 2051, remembering 2050), and the Mass Effect setting at B1.2 (2065). Before 2065 no Reaper thoughts and no thought that this was once a story he read. The first wave is a feeling of recognition only. No character is told, and none ever learns how he knows.

## 4. The story, 2050 to 2065, by topic

### 4.1 Awakening and the Med-Pod (2050 to 2051)
- **2050.** He wakes with inexplicable knowledge and expertise, suspects he has transmigrated, and chooses to build his foundation in secret.
- He wakes in March 2050, first builds an automated scrapper and leaves it in a junkyard, then a nano-disassembler that breaks scrap into base elements, then rents a bay from Delgado (author ruling, 4 November 2026) and builds the Med-Pod in about six months in the back bay of Delgado's tire shop (A2.1).
- **The safety net comes first (author ruling).** Selling his inventions at once would make many enemies. He builds a Level 2 Med-Pod that restores a dying person to full health and also rejuvenates and extends life.

### 4.2 Tiers and technology
- **The tier system and the factory (2051 to 2052).** In 2051 he builds a full Level 2 Med-Pod in private. **He builds no Primitive pod himself (author ruling, 3 November 2026).** Oyama builds Primitive pods after Byron is recruited in July 2052, and sells them under its own name from January 2058.
- He writes the tier sheet himself: Primitive for what may leave the room, a higher level for what stays behind. The Primitive grade must always be one the world could have invented unaided.
- He invents the name Janus when Marchetti asks who owns the designs, for the foundation she files (A2.1, A2.2).
- Only Level 2 and better Med-Pods rejuvenate and extend longevity (author ruling).

**Technology by 2060 (decision).** Each is shown in Act I through someone who touches it. Med-Pod and Anti-Gravity levels are decisions pending the author's tier definitions.

### 4.3 Recruitment and the network (2051 to 2053)
- **Who and why.** He recruits veterans and experts, each serving seven years (renewable by choice) in exchange for being restored to their prime. They become the secret organization that protects him. Recruits are the dying, the crippled, the abandoned, and skilled professionals with terminal illnesses. Each goes through treatment, a quiet offer, a training upload, and a placement.
- **Order (author ruling, 3 November 2026).** Vance (summer 2051), Ashby (autumn 2051), the lawyers Helen Marchetti and Samuel Ortega (October 2051), with Vance and Ashby standing guard; Vance expands the team twice (winter 2051 and spring 2052); Byron Pyrrus (July 2052). Danny Shepard joins autumn 2052; Senator Eleanor Whitcombe February 2053.
- **The network's founding (2051 to 2052).** It begins in summer 2051 with Vance, who brings in Ashby, a radio technician Vance found after the trenches. The two are Kenji's security detail. Vance expands the team twice: four fighters in winter, four more fighters plus three intelligence staff in spring, before Byron. The intelligence team finds his buyers first.
- **Fighters and droids.** The fighters wear Level 1 powered-suit frames, because Kenji lacks money for materials and the mini-reactor a Level 2 suit needs. After Byron is recruited, Oyama builds the first Level 2 security droid squad to a design Kenji hands over for internal use (not for sale), with Kenji's data crystals and Level 2 Dumb AI. The droids carry Vance's combat routines. Kenji builds two Level 5 droids himself, with Oyama money, between October 2052 and summer 2053. They look and move like men and are with him at all times. By autumn 2053 the platoon has forty fighters.
- **Vance (2051 to 2062, author ruling).** Dying in a hospice in summer 2051, he is Kenji's first recruit. He trades his memories, experience, and expertise for treatment through a two-way upload that copies them out and gives him his lungs back. Kenji uses the copy to hone the droids' combat abilities; the routines are the only place it lives. Vance is the network's first member and leader. He recruits Danny Shepard in autumn 2052 and loses him in the 2059 bomb. He stops walking behind Kenji in summer 2053, when the two Level 5 guards are finished. After the forged letter closes Galveston in October 2061 he concludes guards and locked doors cannot defend against paper, and opens a file on Maya Brooks. Kenji approves it (A2.13), and Vance recruits her in Marseille in 2062 and brings her to Kenji a week later. (Profile: Dominic_Vance_Character_Profile.md; A2.7, A3.1.)

### 4.4 Clinics and elite sales (2053 onward)
- **The clinics.** Three clinics open in summer 2053 through the Janus Foundation: Galveston, Marseille, Singapore. Public floors run Primitive Med-Pods that Oyama builds, curing disease and mending injury and nothing more. A hidden wing treats recruits with Level 2. The clinics also offer cloned organs for sale, pre-approved through the political faction.
- **The Shepards.** In 2053 Danny asks whether the treatment can save his dying adoptive father Tom, which is how Kenji meets the Shepards. Danny is Hannah's adoptive brother and dies in the 2059 bombing.
- **Elite sales.** When the organization is ready, he offers the same treatment to the world's elites from April 2055 through Whitcombe's factions, to win favor and influence.

### 4.5 Security (author ruling, revised 3 November 2026)
- Fighters wear Level 1 frames. Private sites are guarded by security droids driven by Dumb AI on a data crystal system nobody can hack. The first droid squad (autumn 2052) is Level 2.
- From summer 2053, every site holding a Level 2 pod is guarded around the clock by Level 3 droids (author ruling, 5 November 2026; the date is a decision).
- No Level 2 pod moves without Kenji knowing. Every pod carries an anti-theft precaution: if its guards are defeated and it is taken, its core components disintegrate (author ruling, 5 November 2026).
- Oyama sells Level 1 to Level 3 robots to the public (dates are decisions: Level 1 from 2057, Levels 2 and 3 from 2066). Two Level 5 droids stay with Kenji.
- No client who knew of him would risk a long life by fighting him, because follow-up treatments come only through him. The exception is Cerberus.
- **Good protection is not complete safety (author ruling).** His network members are human and his droids are not sentient, so they make mistakes, and a dedicated enemy willing to die can still reach him and the people he loves. This is how the 2059 bomb gets through.

### 4.6 Loyalty and the memory seal
**Loyalty has four layers.**
1. **Gratitude:** the cure is real.
2. **Dependence:** follow-up treatments and every longevity treatment come only through his network.
3. **Conditioning:** the Volitional Lock. Every main member is conditioned in a session immediately after their first treatment. It blocks betrayal only; members can still disagree, refuse, defy, or resign. See ruling 37 in Human_Characters and The_Volitional_Lock_Revised.md.
4. **Compartmentalization:** each servant knows only what the role requires.

The found family is the exception and is not conditioned.

**The memory seal (decision, 5 November 2026, open to overrule).**
- Every Level 2 rejuvenation and longevity treatment seals the patient's memory, and the patient comes out remembering nothing of who, how, where, when, and why except one contact person.
  - **Who:** Kenji, the network, the staff, the foundation, Whitcombe's factions, other patients.
  - **How:** the process, the pod, the technology.
  - **Where:** the sites, routes, and people.
  - **When:** dates and schedule.
  - **Why:** his purposes and the terms of the bargain.
- The patient remembers being treated and keeps every other memory, loyalty, and will. Because the knowledge is not in the patient, there is nothing to divulge under duress, drugs, or torture.
- **The contact.** Each patient remembers one contact person, a single name and face and a way to reach them. The contact is a conditioned network member (decision) and arranges every follow-up treatment, so dependence runs through that one person. Every follow-up treatment seals the memory again.
- The patient is told in the contract before treatment, and accepting is part of the price.
- Recruits keep their memories and are covered by the Volitional Lock; managers by their upload and conditioning. Kenji leaves the seal out of the found family's treatments. Public Primitive clinics do not seal anything; their patients are only asked to keep the process secret.
- (open: how it is done, what the patient believes happened during the missing time, who the contacts are.)

### 4.7 Money, puppet companies, the hidden service
- **The puppet companies.** Many puppet companies in sectors: medical, finance and insurance, industrial automation, space logistics and mining, colony development, media and lobbying, security contracting. Each sits under layered holding companies in many jurisdictions with a servant-manager at the top, and no company knows the whole network.
- **Income comes mainly from the world's elites (author ruling), who pay heavily for Level 2 rejuvenation and longevity. That money funds the public Primitive clinics.** Every such treatment carries the memory seal. A smaller part comes from licensing the technologies he allows to spread.
- After the wedding in 2058 he slowly spreads his inventions through legal and shell companies (author ruling).
- **The hidden service.** An intelligence and wet-work organization protects the network. Collection gathers intelligence. Influence funds pro-colonial politicians and places friends in regulators. Action carries out wet work as a last resort, disguised as accident, never repeating a pattern. It takes its three layers in 2058 to 2062, after the 2059 bomb; Brooks joins in 2062.

### 4.8 Enemies: the rival and Cerberus
**The rival elite (not Cerberus).**
- A rival elite, not Cerberus, orders the raids on the clinics (December 2056 to early 2058) and the 2059 bomb that hit the Galveston clinic and killed Danny Shepard.
- There is no 2056 shakedown.

**Cerberus (author ruling).**
- **Origin.** From 2055 an organization acts against Kenji. Author-side label "Cerberus." Different from canon's, no link to Harper. The story calls it "Cerberus" or "they," never "it."
- **How it first acts.** Its first buyer approaches a client's physician in November 2055, and they have proof once he treats elites (author ruling). The proof is what a treated client's body shows and the one contact a client remembers, never what a client knows of the treatment, because of the memory seal. The physician asks the network's nurse because she is the only person his patient remembers.
- **What it wants.** Its main interest is the technologies he lets spread (author ruling, 5 November 2026). It cannot take a Level 2 Med-Pod: it does not know where any stands, every one is guarded around the clock by Level 3 droids, no pod moves without Kenji knowing, and a pod taken from its guards destroys its own core.
- **Its moves.** Three offers to buy Oyama's patents in January, April (with a forged audit), and October 2058. Oyama is their first target. In October 2061 Cerberus forges a state letter that closes Galveston. Only Galveston closes, because Whitcombe's faction stops the others and forces Galveston to reopen.
- **How the network learns the name.** In 2060, when an operative names his group Cerberus and tries to turn Marchetti and Whitcombe (Act_I_Outline).
- **What the network finds.** After these his network uncovers them as one organization, with its method and cutouts, but not who leads it. Brooks is recruited in 2062 to find out, and the unsolved 2059 file is the first thing she is handed.
- **Secret war.** Once his inventions spread and they trace them back to him, the two sides are at secret war. They use no technology his network could trace and have no legal entity. They are also behind the 2065 withdrawals.

### 4.9 2065: the realization and the expedition
Manswell announces the Alpha Centauri expedition, and Kenji realizes where he is.
- Kenji is already a major investor, through a puppet finance firm. Lenders from several economies also subscribe.
- Within months the lenders withdraw in close succession, coordinated by the conspiracy. No one in Kenji's network ordered it.
- Kenji does not withdraw. He raises his stake and becomes the largest contributor. He offers safer ship designs and Level 2 longevity treatment through the firm. Manswell accepts in exchange for a controlling stake, receives the managers' upload and conditioning, and stays on as the public face.
- Brooks and the network see the coordinated pattern and its one exception, Kenji.
- The expedition sails, founding a permanent settlement at Alpha Centauri in the late 2060s.

### 4.10 Open decisions
- **Warp tests (decision).** The first unmanned hull flies a measured light year in a day in 2063, and the drive is private until a Primitive version is sold. The first crewed crossing beyond the Solar System is in 2064, so the A2 excerpt's "no human being had gone beyond the Solar System" holds.
- **Hannah's firm (decision).** She leaves complex security after the 2059 bomb and founds a private firm in 2060, which takes its first guard jobs in 2062 and escorts the first public crossings.
