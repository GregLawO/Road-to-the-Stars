# Specific Rules (trimmed for A2 and A3)

Rules specific to this story's chapters, characters, dates, tiers, and secrets. Trimmed: later-chapter casting, 2125 count, patron-seat details, Reaper-era items. Style rules live in General_Writing_Rules.md.

**Status labels.** An entry the author states or approves is fact. (inference) is a consequence of settled facts. (open) is unsettled. (author ruling) supersedes earlier text. (decision) is my choice, open to overrule.

---

## 1. Process and format

- **Order.** The story is written chapter by chapter. No fixed word count.
- **Labels.** Chapters are A1, A2, and so on. Micro-chapters are single-POV pieces coded A1.1, A1.2. "Excerpt" means only the quoted epigraph that opens a chapter.
- **Published canon.** Only A1 is published and never rewritten. A2.1 to A2.15 are drafted and canon (7 November 2026): A2a Final, A2b v6, A2c v6. A2.16 onward and A3 are not drafted. The first release is the first batch of A2 (A2.1 to A2.5).
- **Chapter sizes.** A2 has 20 micro-chapters in four batches of 5. A3 has 10 in two batches.
- **Date-order exceptions.** A3.1 (2062) leads A3 as a step back to A1.5. A2.7 is Vance's ledger (2051 to summer 2053), ordered by its start date. A2.9 (April 2055 to November 2056) is ordered by its start date and runs past A2.10 and A2.11.
- **Micro-chapter length (A2b).** About 850 to 900 words, may hold two or three dated scenes in one POV.
- **Chapter excerpt header format (author ruling).** Each excerpt comes from Excerpt_Bank.md and sits at the very top, above any title or byline:
  1. The excerpt's title only, as a heading, no code and no "Excerpt" label.
  2. The excerpt text in italics inside quotation marks.
  3. An attribution line: a dash, then *The Road to the Stars*. No author named.
- **Saving a draft.** When a micro-chapter is added to a chapter that already has a draft, write the whole chapter file (existing micro-chapters unchanged, new one appended). Put drafts in `/mnt/user-data/outputs/`, call `present_files`, and tell the author the draft is not yet in the Project.
- **Revision commentary** does not belong in the outline or other story files.

## 2. The excerpts

- Excerpts carry no first-person voice and no sign of who wrote them.

## 10. Kenji's POV rules

- **No Reaper thoughts, and no thought that his new world is Mass Effect, before 2065 (author ruling, 4 November 2026).** Kenji may think about any fiction he remembers from his past life, such as a film. Until 2065 he does not think that the world comes from a story, and does not think about the Reapers.
- **No Kenji chapter is set in 2065 or later until B1.2.**
- **At most three sentences of past life per chapter.** They come only through small habits, such as finishing every meal.
- **Nobody, in a chapter or a document, says how Kenji knows what he knows.** No character is ever told.
- **From A2 on, Kenji carries POV. A1 stays as published: unnamed, no POV.**
- **First person (author ruling, 3 November 2026).** Every Kenji chapter is in the first person ("I"). All other POVs stay third person; no other narrator may use "I."
- **Count.** No fixed count of Kenji's or Hannah's POV chapters for now (author, 7 November 2026). Drafted Kenji chapters: A2.1, A2.4, A2.6, A2.11, A2.14.
- **Before 2065.** Full knowledge of his technology from the start. He knows the Med-Pod is the one from the film Elysium, but not how or why he knows how to build it. His unease is a cautious man's. He may suspect he has transmigrated (revealed at A2.1).
- **Speech.** Short declaratives, few metaphors, workshop and tool images when he does use one. Dry humor is rare.
- **Fate habit.** Never says "it can't get worse." Notes what arrives early or late, never calls it luck.
- **Fast Gear tell.** Squares objects on a desk and washes his hands. His own chapters never name shaking hands.
- **Fate (author ruling).** He thinks and feels as if the universe is working against him. The text mostly allows chance or human causes to explain what happens. Fate costs him materials, money, time, people, and trust, never the end state.
- **Kenji and Hannah (A2b and A2c).** Every Hannah-and-Kenji micro-chapter (A2.8, A2.13, A2.14, A2.16) has at least one scene where the two talk, and something between them is different by the end. The change shows in what they say and do, not in what an object means. Kenji tells Hannah no false thing (the A2.14 promise).

## 11. The marine (Vance)

- Conditioned from the start of his service (author ruling, 5 November 2026). Only the Shepards are not conditioned.
- Only Kenji and Vance know his memories became the droids' fighting style.

## 12. Secrecy and reveal discipline

- **Reaper secrecy.** Only Kenji, Hannah, Brooks, and Alexandra (from her twenty-first birthday) know of the Reapers, and they learn it from him much later. No chapter, document, or character outside the inner circle may refer to an unnamed enemy that the drills are for.
- **The conspiracy (Cerberus).** Cerberus hears of the technology in 2055 and wants the technologies he lets spread. It cannot find or take a Level 2 pod. The incidents are the physician's offer (Nov 2055), three offers to buy Oyama's patents (Jan, Apr, Oct 2058), and the forged letter that closes Galveston (Oct 2061). The raids (Dec 2056, Aug 2057, early 2058) and the bomb (spring 2059) are a rival elite's, not Cerberus's. There is no 2056 shakedown (A2.8): hospital chains and others only send inspection requests and complaints. The network finds one organization but not who leads it, and Brooks takes the file in 2062. No chapter shows the network knowing who leads it before the outline says so.
- **How the name is learned (Act_I_Outline, A2.9 onward).** An operative who traced the elite patients' remembered contact to Whitcombe and Marchetti calls his group Cerberus in A2.18 (2060) and tries to turn them. It is not learned from money trails, documents, or a captured raider. The story calls it "Cerberus" or "they," never "it."
- **Patient memory (decision, 5 November 2026).** Every Level 2 patient except recruits, managers, and the found family remembers nothing of who, how, where, when, or why, other than one contact person. A patient remembers being treated and may talk about anything else. No treated patient shows knowledge of the five details even under pressure. The seal is named only where a POV who knows of it is present.
- **In-universe documents** never contain the conspiracy, the true cause of the 2065 withdrawals, or the truth of the 2125 killing. POVs may show the truth where they could know it.
- **Kenji's name** may appear in documents for minor matters only.
- **Hannah's list of questions she chose not to ask** stays secret. It is shown only inside her head.
- **No human meets an alien, and the Citadel appears nowhere.**

## 13. Network, robots, and technology

- **Security (author ruling, revised 3 November 2026).** The network begins in summer 2051 with Vance, who brings in Ashby. Vance's fighters wear Level 1 powered-suit frames (the Level 2 suit needs a mini-reactor; date open). From autumn 2052 private sites are also guarded by Level 2 security droids built by Oyama, run by Level 2 Dumb AI on Kenji's data crystals. From summer 2053 Level 3 droids guard every Level 2 pod around the clock, no pod moves without Kenji knowing, and a pod taken from its guards destroys its own core (author ruling, 5 November 2026; the 2053 date is a decision). Droids stand inside private sites only. Two Level 5 droids are with Kenji at all times from summer 2053. They look and move like men, and Hannah takes them for men until spring 2060. They are told to act low-key in public. Oyama sells Level 1 to Level 3 robots to the public.
- **When Kenji uses his technology (author ruling).** He keeps advanced technology and robots from the public until a time he chooses. He never refuses to use them when someone he holds dear is in danger, and uses them whenever the use can be disguised. When he does, he makes it look ordinary.
- **The network** is founded in summer 2051 with Vance (A2.1 hook, A2.7). Recruitment order: Vance, Ashby, the lawyers (October 2051), a larger team, Byron (July 2052). It exists before Kenji meets the Shepards (2053), and the three clinics (Galveston, Marseille, Singapore) open in summer 2053.
- **Operatives carry no POV**, apart from Vance.
- **Tier examples for robots (author ruling):**
  - **Primitive (Level 1):** immobile, or needs a human controller.
  - **Level 2:** acts on its own, but moves clankily and loudly. Most advanced AI it supports is Level 2.
  - **Level 3:** moves more smoothly, still very loud. Most advanced AI it supports is Level 3.
- **Tiers.** Kenji starts the tier system in A2.1 (he builds no Primitive pod; Oyama does after July 2052): a Primitive grade for what may leave the room, a higher level for what stays. The Primitive grade must always be one the world could have invented unaided.
- **Warp.** Flights before the first crewed crossing are unmanned and private. The first crewed crossing beyond the Solar System is set in 2064 (no chapter in the current outline).
- **Hannah's firm (decision).** She leaves complex security after the 2059 bomb and founds a private firm in 2060.
- **Unconfirmed tier tables.** The Med-Pod and Anti-Gravity levels (public L1, private L2) are decisions pending the author's definitions.

## 15. Character rules

- **Honesty scale (1 lies easily, 5 cannot lie).** Tom 4, Ashby 4, Hannah 4, Ruth 4, Vance 4, Kenji 4, Manswell 3, Brooks 2.

### Each character's "Avoid" list
- **Tom.** Only comic relief or only the wise old man. Working out the secrets himself. Long speeches.
- **Ashby.** Only the nerd or only the damaged veteran. Long technical explanations of the drive. Jokes about the dead squad.
- **Manswell.** Only a fool or only a villain. Simple gratitude to his rescuer. Sudden bravery.
- **Hannah.** Only the wronged wife or grieving mother. Speeches. Convenient forgiveness.
- **Vance.** Only muscle or a tough-guy cliché. Explaining the setting. Being smarter than everyone about money or law.
- **Kenji.** Smug-genius scenes. Enjoying being thanked. Anyone being told where his knowledge came from. Past-life references over three sentences.
- **Ruth.** Only a worried mother or only a comfort. Being a victim. Working out secrets by herself. Long speeches.
- **Brooks.** Villain monologues. Cruelty for its own sake. Enjoying the count of the dead. Sudden warmth.

### Body tells
Tom turns a tool over. Ashby tilts his head and touches the stethoscope. Manswell sets his watch face down. Hannah presses her thumb along the scar. Vance takes a slow breath, and in bad moments rests a hand on the sidearm. Kenji squares objects and washes his hands. Ruth straightens a napkin or collar. Brooks's thumb walks across her fingertips.

## 17. Draft checklist

**Format**
- [ ] File opens with the excerpt header (title, italic quoted text, attribution)
- [ ] Byline and place line match the previous chapter's format
- [ ] One POV only
- [ ] Word count in range

**Style**
- [ ] Slow Gear paragraphs 1 to 5 sentences, plain sentences mostly 5 to 22 words, dialogue never a run of one-line replies
- [ ] Fast Gear paragraphs one or two lines, sentences 2 to 7 words, sparing adjectives and adverbs
- [ ] Gear shift: trigger, paragraph break, thought purge, one body detail that stays
- [ ] Ending type matches the outline
- [ ] "But" used where the second clause cuts against expectation
- [ ] Every sentence can be read once and understood
- [ ] Plain, everyday language

**Canon**
- [ ] Consistent with the outline, character profile, and these rules
- [ ] Kenji: no Reaper thoughts and no thought that his world is Mass Effect before 2065, at most three sentences of past life, no Kenji chapter set in 2065 or later before B1.2
- [ ] No in-universe document or character reveals how Kenji knows what he knows
