"Good thing we've got seven more years to finish building the heavy carriers. Come on, eat up before the tea gets cold."

Kaguya smiled softly, reaching across the tray to pick up a warm bun as the quiet winter snow continued falling over Kyoto, sealing twenty years of silent preparation behind thick stone walls before the fire of 2157 broke across the stars. **[E]**
