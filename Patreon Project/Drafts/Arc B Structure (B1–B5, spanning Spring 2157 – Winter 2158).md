Arc B Structure (`B1`–`B5`, spanning Spring 2157 – Winter 2158) with every single logical rule, registry specification, combat doctrine lock, and ship ratio integrated.

Master Canon & Logic Locks Applied

- **Japanese Surname-First Format:** Every Japanese character uses strict **Family Name + Given Name** format (*Himura Kenji*, *Himura Kaguya*, *Himura Shizuka*, *Himura Ryunosuke*, *Togo Kazuhiko*, *Tachibana Haruka*, *Kurosawa Renji*, *Takeda Shinji*, *Saigo Noboru*, *Mori Hiroshi*, *Takahashi Kenjiro*, *Matsuda Kenjiro*, *Sato Hana*).
- **Single "Vance" Rule & Unique Western Surnames:** **Elena Vance** (UNAS Secretary of State / Systems Alliance Chief Envoy) is the **only** character named Vance. All duplicate surnames are eliminated (**Claire Mercer** as SA Intelligence Director General, **Arthur Pendelton** as UNAS President, **Colonel Miller** as General Williams's XO on Shanxi).
- **Exact Naval Registries & Division Ratios (`REG-774` & `DOC-882`):**
  - **Imperial Shock Division (`IJN-TSG-`):** `100,000 bio-augmented Shock Samurai` (in **Gin-Musha** exosuits + **Ashigaru** automata) and `42 warships` (`1× 3,000m Grand Battleship IJN-TSG-GB`, `2× 1,500m Battleships IJN-TSG-BB`, `4× 1,000m Line Cruisers IJN-TSG-LC`, `5× 750m Strike Interceptor Cruisers IJN-TSG-SP`, and `10× 1:2 Shadow-Wolf packs` of `10× 500m Strike Frigates IJN-TSG-EL` + `20× 250m Strike Corvettes IJN-TSG-LE`) running on **Solaris-9** cores (`75%` mass-shedding cap).
  - **Systems Alliance (`SSV-`) Line-of-Battle Division (`1:2:8` Ratio):** `1× 1,800m Battleship (SSV-BB)`, `2× 900m Heavy Cruisers (SSV-CA)`, and `8× 225m Strike Frigates (SSV-FF)` (`11 warships` per division) running on Kinki-Kyoku-blackboxed **Helios-8C** cores (`50%` mass-shedding cap, zero Eezo), alongside separate small Eezo relay scouts like *SSV Normandy-Zero*.
  - **Throneside Guard (`Tachibana Haruka`):** Remains strictly defending Kyoto and Crown worlds (`IJN-TNS- / IJN-KNE-`), never deployed to Shanxi.
- **Total Imperial Secrecy From Aliens:** All Imperial warships (`IJN-`) remain **100% hidden under Eclipse stealth and unknown to every alien species** throughout Arc B. Only other human polities (`Systems Alliance`, `Taiwan`, `South Korea`) know the Empire's ships exist.
- **Battle of Shanxi Sequence (`B1.6`–`B1.9`):**
  - No Turian dreadnoughts: Desolas Arterius commands a border pacification task force of **Turian cruisers, frigates, and troop transports**.
  - When Desolas fires first on Shanxi (`B1.7`), the colony's **Imperial-built Laser Lance orbital platforms** and the Shanxi Defense Fleet's `1:2:8` division immediately shatter his frontal vanguard at lightspeed.
  - When Desolas attempts to flank the stationary platforms from multiple angles, General Williams transmits the emergency aid code (`B1.8`), and Rear Admiral Himura Ryunosuke's Shock Division drops **Eclipse** stealth at `110,000+ km`—enforcing Kyoto's strict rules (*do not fire first; intervene only if the colony of Shanxi is attacked and Alliance Command formally requests assistance; shoot to disable, not destroy*) to cleanly shear off every remaining Turian gun barrel, thruster, and drive housing in silent flashes of superheated metal while staying 100% invisible to alien sensors (`B1.9`).
  - When Vice Admiral Steven Hackett arrives with **Systems Alliance Second Fleet** (`1:2:8` divisions led by **1,800-meter Battleships**), the Turians and Citadel Council see those **1,800m non-Eezo Alliance Battleships** and react in shock—leading to the **Treaty of Shanxi grandfather clause** (`B3.3`, `B3.9`) while the Imperial Starfleet remains completely unknown.
- **No Premature Alien Names:** Human POVs refer strictly to **unidentified alien ships/contacts** until **High Censor Kurosawa Renji** extracts the shipboard translation lexicons and star charts from the disabled cruisers in **B2.3**.
- **Subtle Foreshadowing (Zero Royal Exposition):** Neither **Emperor Himura Kenji** nor **Empress Himura Kaguya** ever explains why the Shock Division arrived on Shanxi in January 2157 or why **Kinki-Kyoku** quietly extracted the **Prothean Arca Monolith** ahead of time (`B1.3`).
- **Plain, Natural Prose:** Everyday English throughout narration and dialogue, with zero em-dashes and alternating `[D]`, `[P]`, `[W]`, `[E]` ending tags.

Chapter B1: The Quiet Perimeter & Relay 314 (Spring–Autumn 2157)

Batch 1: The Quiet Setup (`B1.1`–`B1.5`)

- **B1.1 — General Williams (Shanxi Garrison & Command Bunker, Spring 2157):** With his XO **Colonel Miller**, Williams observes the joint ground and orbital training regimen with the Imperial Shock Division (`42 warships` + `100,000 Shock Samurai`) that arrived a week after New Year's—watching `Kōsō-Hō` energy lances neutralize Shanxi's **1:2:8 Systems Alliance Line-of-Battle Division** (`1× 1,800m Battleship`, `2× 900m Heavy Cruisers`, `8× 225m Strike Frigates`) at 110,000+ km during wargames while Imperial tenders anchor new Laser Lance orbital defense platforms above the colony.
- **B1.2 — Second Lieutenant David Anderson (Shanxi High Orbit Construction Grid, Early Summer 2157):** Stationed as a junior Alliance liaison officer, twenty-year-old Anderson inspects the newly installed Imperial-built orbital defense platforms—powered by blackboxed Helios-8C reactors and armed with heavy **Laser Lance batteries**—while learning three-dimensional sensor discipline from the Shock Division's officers.
- **B1.3 — Dr. Takahashi Kenjiro & Lady Himura Shizuka (Nara Research Vault, Summer 2157):** Behind sealed doors in Nara, Takahashi and Shizuka examine the secretly extracted **Arca Monolith**, confirming that alien kinetic barriers fail completely against coherent light and sending updated passive sensor patches to the Shock Division at Shanxi.
- **B1.4 — Captain Sato Hana (*SSV Normandy-Zero*, Relay 314, Late Summer 2157):** Ignoring Prothean warning beacons, an Alliance pathfinding flotilla begins forcing open dormant Relay 314, only to be caught in the middle of the procedure and fired upon by a routine unidentified alien border patrol squadron of cruisers and frigates.
- **B1.5 — Captain Mori Hiroshi (*IJN-KGE-SP Kurokage*, Relay 314 Dark Sector, Late Summer 2157):** Cloaked under Eclipse stealth, *Kurokage* logs the sudden border clash as the unidentified alien patrol squadron attacks the Alliance survey ships, transmitting their element-zero drive and kinetic shield readings straight to Nara and the Shock Division orbiting Shanxi.

Batch 2: The Turian Arrival & The Battle of Shanxi (`B1.6`–`B1.10`)

- **B1.6 — Rear Admiral Himura Ryunosuke & Shock Division Commander (Shanxi Off-Ecliptic Orbit, Autumn 2157):** Holding station invisible under **Eclipse** stealth as emergency backup, Ryunosuke enforces Kyoto's strict rules of engagement: *do not fire first; intervene only if the colony of Shanxi is attacked and Alliance Command formally requests assistance; shoot to disable, not destroy.*
- **B1.7 — General Desolas Arterius (*THS Invictus*, Shanxi Approach Vector, Autumn 2157):** *(Turian POV)* Emerging from Relay 314 with a border pacification task force of Turian cruisers, frigates, and troop transports (zero dreadnoughts) and ignoring Alliance hailing calls, Arterius opens fire on Shanxi—only for his vanguard cruisers' armor and weapon mounts to suddenly erupt in white-hot molten metal as the colony's new **Imperial-built Laser Lance platforms** and **1,800-meter Alliance Battleship** strike them at lightspeed without a visible beam or radar warning.
- **B1.8 — General Williams & Second Lieutenant David Anderson (Shanxi Command Bunker, Autumn 2157):** Watching the new Laser Lance platforms and Shanxi's `1:2:8` division hold off the frontal assault while the surviving alien cruisers split up to swing around Shanxi's orbital blind spots, Williams transmits the emergency joint-defense authorization code to the Imperial Shock Division in orbit.
- **B1.9 — General Desolas Arterius (*THS Invictus*, Flagship Cruiser Bridge, Autumn 2157):** *(Turian POV)* Attempting to flank the stationary platforms and Alliance battleship from multiple angles, with his sensors showing completely empty space around him, Arterius watches his tactical plot turn red as gun barrels, thruster bells, and element-zero drive housings across his remaining ships shear apart in silent flashes of superheated metal—leaving his entire task force disabled in seconds while the Imperial Shock Division remains **100% invisible under Eclipse stealth**, never seen or detected by a single alien sensor.
- **B1.10 — Vice Admiral Steven Hackett & Director General Claire Mercer (Arcturus Station, Autumn 2157):** As Hackett's **Systems Alliance Second Fleet** (two full `1:2:8` Line-of-Battle Divisions led by **1,800-meter Battleships**) races through the relay to reinforce Shanxi, Hackett and Mercer review General Williams's report—realizing the Imperial Shock Division surgically disarmed the alien task force upon request and vanished back into stealth without the aliens ever knowing an Imperial fleet exists.

- **B2.7 — Councilor Tevos & Councilor Valern (Presidium Tower, The Citadel):** *(Citadel POV)* Staring at Turian sensor logs of **1,800-meter non-Eezo human Battleships** and undetectable lightspeed weapons that neutralized a cruiser task force in under two minutes, Tevos and Valern dispatch an emergency diplomatic mission to broker a ceasefire before the Treaty of Farixen collapses.

- **B2.9 — Emperor Himura Kenji (Amaterasu Palace, Kyoto):** Reviewing the upcoming Citadel summit with his Privy Council, Kenji confirms that Kyoto's ships and off-relay worlds will remain completely unknown to all alien governments—letting the Systems Alliance serve as humanity's outer diplomatic shield.
- **B2.10 — General Desolas Arterius (Palaven Military Tribunal):** *(Turian POV)* Relieved of frontline command after the humiliating disarmament at Shanxi, Arterius obsesses over how a newly emerged species built **1,800-meter non-Eezo Battleships** and undetectable lightspeed weapons, searching border logs for the Prothean beacon he suspects exists near human space—unaware Kinki-Kyoku already lifted the **Arca Monolith** months earlier.
