### CORE PREMISE & SERIES OVERVIEW: THE IRON AEGIS OF AMATERASU

DOCUMENT REF: PRM-001-HIMURA-PREMISE

Pre-October 2132 history, the technology ladder, the vassal clans, the allies and the cast live in the Timeline, Technology, Factions and Characters and Ranks files. This file holds only what those four do not.

---

I. HIGH-CONCEPT LOGLINE

Unaware that his world belongs to the Mass Effect universe, Emperor Himura restores the Chrysanthemum Throne in Kyoto, outlaws self-modifying artificial intelligence out of hard engineering caution, and builds a reactionless gravitic fleet and silent automata army. He shatters a global coalition in the Reclamation War, then realizes the true nature of his universe when the Mars Prothean Archives surface in 2148 and the Charon Mass Relay opens in 2149.

---

II. THE PILLARS

1. **Delayed cosmic realization.** Every early edict is built from pragmatic survival and hard engineering discipline. Only the 2148 Mars Prothean Archives and the 2149 Charon Mass Relay activation, with Jon Grissom and the new Systems Alliance, let Himura piece together the galaxy he inhabits. His Iron Aegis then turns toward relay quarantines and silent preparation for the wider cosmic threat.
2. **Zero technology bleed.** Domestic Solaris cores and Chōju never leave sovereign custody. Foreign powers get only licensed, blackboxed Helios units.
3. **Strict sovereign non-interference.** Japan governs no foreign mainland territory and annexes nothing in China or Korea. It deploys ground forces abroad only when an invaded ally formally requests them, and withdraws completely once victory is secured.

---

III. ARC AFTER THE BASELINE (October 2132 onward)

- **The Reclamation War (2132 to 2136).** The Eurasian-Continental Pact attacks Japan, South Korea and Taiwan while rival Western task forces clash with Imperial cordons. Togo's wolfpacks and the mid-war hulls shatter enemy fleets. Yamashita's podless orbital drops and Oni-Kikai heavy mecha liberate Korea and break the Pact. The 2136 Imperial Settlement follows: complete mainland withdrawal and a self-imposed cap of 1,500 warships.
- **Beyond the Charon Gate (2136 to 2150+).** The Solaris-9 fleet and the 10,000-meter Throne-Citadel IJN Amaterasu face the 2148 Mars discovery and the 2149 Charon Relay activation. Under the 2149 Kyoto-Arcturus Concordat the Systems Alliance polices wider human space on licensed Japanese Helios hulls, while Kyoto quarantines border relays and hunts rogue conspirators such as Jack Harper and Henry Lawson.

---

IV. DRAMATIC TENSIONS

- **The accidental shield.** Built to protect the home islands from terrestrial superpowers and runaway AI, the fleet and the Anti-Smart-AI Edict become humanity's bulwark when the galaxy reveals itself.
- **The cost of the wall.** Himura and the High Council keep their bodies through Chōju, and the High Council seals its minds with the Volitional Lock. They become immortal, silent guardians isolated from the people they protect.
- **The ageless succession.** The throne will not open for centuries. Kenji teaches Kiyoshi patience, and Kaguya worries how their children will bear parents who never age.
- **Humanity divided at the threshold.** The Systems Alliance rushes outward with Element Zero and forbidden AI experiments while the Empire holds the line behind it.
