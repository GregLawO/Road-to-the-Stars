### IMPERIAL HISTORICAL CHRONICLE & GEOPOLITICAL SETTLEMENT (2115–2150+)

DOCUMENT REF: CHR-101-HIMURA-ANNALS

ISSUING AUTHORITY: IMPERIAL PRIVY COUNCIL & THRONESIDE ARCHIVES (KYOTO)

---

I. THE QUIET AWAKENING & KYOTO RESTORATION (2115–2122)

- 2115 — The Secret Breakthrough (Kyoto & Kii Peninsula): At age twenty-three, Himura (born 2092) oversees the first stable ignition of a prototype Solaris-4 Unified Gravitic Core and deterministic Quantum-Optical VI lattice inside the subterranean Kii-Yamato Core-Forge. Recognizing the existential danger of unshackled machine intelligence and external predation, Himura begins consolidating Clan Himura's industrial and scientific assets in total secrecy.
- 2119 — The Anti-Smart-AI Edict: Enacted across all Japanese-governed territory, outlawing self-modifying artificial intelligence and restricting all computational architecture to deterministic Quantum-Optical VIs.
- 2122 — The Kyoto Restoration: Following the brief Restoration War of 2122, the Chrysanthemum Throne is restored to full sovereign authority in the Sovereign Imperial Capital of Kyoto, with Tokyo designated as the Empire's primary commercial and maritime metropolis. Emperor Himura (age 30) and Empress Himura Kaguya (age 29) establish the modern Imperial Constitutional Monarchy, elevating loyal Japanese noble houses to sworn Vassal Clan status under Sankin-Kōtai obligations while founding the Compliance Directorate (Kansatsu-In) and Kinki-Kyoku.

---

II. THE CHRYSALIS YEARS & SOVEREIGN ALLIANCES (2122–2132)

- Secret Naval & Foundry Expansion: While foreign powers mistake Japan's reduced steel exports and closed Pacific shipyards for economic stagnation, Crown Foundries beneath the Kii mountains and in lunar shadow construct the 236-ship Pre-War Imperial Fleet (85 Household Vanguard + 15 Kinki-Kyoku/Shadow + 136 Imperial Navy) powered by Solaris-4 cores (capped at 45% mass-shedding and 99.5% inertial dampening) alongside 2,500,000 standing ground troops (500,000 human specialists + 2,000,000 Ashigaru-4 / Kikai-Bushi automata).
- Sole Sovereign Allies (Taiwan & South Korea): Adhering to its strict Doctrine of Sovereign Non-Interference, the Empire of Japan rejects all territorial annexation on the Asian mainland and establishes no feudal Daimyō clans outside Japanese soil. Instead, Kyoto signs equal bilateral mutual-defense and maritime-technology treaties with its two sole sovereign allies: the Republic of Korea (South Korea) and Taiwan (Formosa)—supplying licensed commercial Helios-2 civilian reactors and defensive infrastructure while respecting their complete national self-governance.
- The Imperial Heir (decision): Crown Prince Himura Kiyoshi (born 2114) is eight at the Restoration. Himura Ayame (born 2126) and Himura Haruto (born 2136) follow. The Imperial Family carries no Volitional Lock.
- Global Polarization: Meanwhile, the United North American States (UNAS) and the European Union (EU) institute economic sanctions against Kyoto over closed Pacific sea lanes, while the neo-authoritarian Eurasian-Continental Pact (led by Moscow and Beijing) constructs brute-force fusion dreadnoughts and orbital kinetic-rod platforms in preparation for a preemptive strike.

---

III. WORLD WAR III: THE IRON TEMPEST — THE RECLAMATION WAR (2132–2136)

- October 14, 2132 — Outbreak of World War III: The Eurasian-Continental Pact launches coordinated orbital kinetic-rod strikes and mechanized invasions against Japan, South Korea, and Taiwan—attempting to seize Japanese gravitic facilities and conquer East Asia. Simultaneously, UNAS and EU orbital task forces clash with both Pact fleets and Imperial quarantine cordons across the Pacific and Earth-Luna corridor, igniting a three-way global conflict.
- 2132–2133 — Orbital Supremacy & Allied Defense: Fleet Admiral Togo Kazuhiko's Frigate-Corvette wolfpacks (1:4 at the opening, restructured to 1:2 under his doctrine) shatter the Pact's opening orbital wave using Eclipse stealth and Type-90 Kage-Ya gravitic torpedoes, while Imperial and sovereign Taiwanese fleets defend the Taiwan Strait and Western Pacific sea lanes.
- 2134 — The Solaris-7 Transition & Korean-Manchurian Campaign: Rush-commissioned mid-war Solaris-7 warships (60% mass-shedding cap, seamless golden-amber External Shear Barriers, and Single-Fold Tactical Micro-Jumps) break the Western orbital blockade across the Earth-Luna corridor and neutralize UNAS/EU orbital projection hubs at Puget Sound and Kourou without attempting continental occupation. On the Asian mainland, acting strictly upon South Korea's formal treaty request for military aid, General Yamashita Takeo leads podless "Golden Rain" autonomous orbital drops—deploying Kikai-Bushi automata and giant Oni-Kikai heavy mecha alongside South Korean divisions to liberate the Korean Peninsula and smash Pact supply bases across Manchuria.
- 2135–2136 — Encirclement & Capitulation: Long-range Type-90D O-Yumi gravitic siege salvos cripple Baikonur and Ural war production while General Yamashita's 25 Automata Legions (about 10,000,000 troops at wartime strength) complete the Trans-Siberian encirclement. Bled dry in the east by Japan's alliance and in the west by separate wars with the EU and UNAS, the Eurasian-Continental Pact collapses and capitulates in 2136.

IV. THE 2136 IMPERIAL SETTLEMENT & SOVEREIGN NON-INTERFERENCE (2136–2147)

- Immediate Mainland Withdrawal: True to the Doctrine of Sovereign Non-Interference, Emperor Himura refuses any annexation or occupation of mainland Asia. Every Imperial Legion and Oni-Kikai unit withdraws completely from Korea and post-Pact China back to sovereign Japanese territory—leaving a reunified, independent Republic of Korea, an independent Taiwan, and sovereign, demilitarized Chinese successor republics with zero Imperial administration or Governors-General.
- The 1,500-Ship Fleet Cap & Solaris-9 Transition: Emperor Himura begins the 10,000-meter Throne-Citadel IJN Amaterasu (IJN-TNS-MC; finished after the Takamagahara ring in 2139), begins the Starfleet's transition to Solaris-9 Unified Gravitic Cores (first ignition 2137, first combat use 2140, standard baseline about 2142; 75% hard mass-shedding limit, 25% minimum apparent mass), expands the standing ground army to 25,000,000 troops across 25 Legions by about 2142, and announces a permanent, self-enforced ceiling of exactly 1,500 active IJN- warships, built out through about 2142.
- 2139 — The Heir's Chōju (decision): Crown Prince Kiyoshi, 25, receives the Tier I Chōju protocol after he stops demanding the throne. Kenji's lesson to his heir is patience over a throne that will not open for centuries.
- Licensed Helios Export Architecture: To stabilize post-war global reconstruction without compromising sovereign security, Kyoto licenses Kinki-Kyoku-blackboxed Helios-2 (25% cap) and Helios-5 (35% cap) commercial/colonial reactors to South Korea, Taiwan, independent Chinese republics, the EU, and the UNAS (South Korea and Taiwan already hold Helios-2 under their pre-2132 treaties), with Helios-8 (50% cap) following from 2142—enforced by Hearth-Quench and Hearth-Collapse anti-tamper seals.

---

V. THE MARS ARCHIVES & THE KYOTO-ARCTURUS CONCORDAT (2148–2150+)

- 2148 — Discovery of the Mars Prothean Archives: Systems Alliance excavators at Promethean Station on Mars uncover ancient Prothean ruins, unlocking Element Zero (Eezo) and Mass Effect physics outside Imperial Japanese jurisdiction and confirming the wider galactic landscape Emperor Himura had quietly prepared for since 2115.
- 2149 — Charon Mass Relay Activation & The Kyoto-Arcturus Concordat: Following Admiral Jon Grissom's transit through the Charon Relay and the formal chartering of the Systems Alliance (SSV-), Emperor Himura refuses to subordinate the Chrysanthemum Throne in Kyoto, the 1,500-ship IJN- Starfleet, or sovereign Solaris-9 / Quantum-Optical VI technologies to Alliance command. Under the 2149 Kyoto-Arcturus Concordat negotiated in Kyoto with Systems Alliance envoy Ambassador Anita Goyle:
  1. The Systems Alliance polices wider human extra-solar space using SSV- warships built with commercially licensed, blackboxed Imperial Helios-8 cores (50% mass-shedding cap).
  2. The Empire of Japan—alongside its sole treaty allies South Korea and Taiwan—retains absolute sovereign jurisdiction over Japanese territory, Crown worlds, Japanese Vassal Clan garden colonies, and dormant mass relays bordering Imperial space, quarantined by Henkyō-Kyoku (IJN-KNS-SP) Interceptor Cruisers.
  3. Five Deep-Space Exploration Fleet squadrons (IJN-TNK-) operate beyond human borders under strict Eclipse stealth and Non-Interference observation protocols.
