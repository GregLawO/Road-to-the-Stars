Chronology lives in the Timeline file, specifications in the Technology file, and profiles, heads, heirs and ranks in Characters and Ranks.

## 1. CLAN HIMURA

### 1.1 The Imperial Family

- **Members:** Emperor Himura Kenji, Empress Himura Kaguya, Crown Prince Himura Kiyoshi, Himura Ayame (born 2126), Himura Haruto.
- **Seat:** Kyoto-Heian Imperial Citadel from the 2122 Restoration. Kenji is Sovereign and Clan Head (Tōshu).
- **Technology monopoly:** Only Clan Himura holds Solaris cores, the Imperial automata classes and the Kurogane Yoroi. Kurogane-4 and 5, in development on a manufacturing method only Kenji holds, and every Solaris core stay in his household. Kurogane-1 to 3 are no longer secret. Every vassal and ally holds a Helios unit.

### 1.2 Clan Currents

Four currents run through the clan: Masamune's caution, Ryunosuke's push for an aggressive fleet, Shizuka's production discipline and Kōji's tradition. Each is set out in the character profiles.

## 2. VASSAL CLANS

- **Status:** Hereditary Daimyō and Vassal Clan titles belong only to Japanese houses on sovereign Japanese territory and Japanese space colonies. Heads keep Kyoto residences under Sankin-Kōtai.
- **Oversight:** Kinki-Kyoku and Kansatsu-Kyoku audit export-grade hardware. Vassal staff carry no Volitional Lock.
- **Standing friction:**
  - The loyal clans hold Helios-3 units issued by the house they helped crown and manufacture their own cores from its schematics, but Solaris cores stay with Himura alone.
  - The traitor clans were never issued Helios-3, and they resent it.

## 3. FOREIGN ALLIES

Taiwan and South Korea are the Empire's only treaty allies. Japan governs no foreign territory. Imperial forces enter allied territory only on formal request, and field command stays with the ally. Both treaties were signed in 2123. Hardware, schematic terms and the 2125 Ashigaru gifts are in the Timeline.

### 3.1 Taiwan (Formosa)

- **Government:** Fully independent island republic. No Imperial administration and no Daimyō clans.
- **Standing friction:** Imperial advisors critique its doctrine, and it keeps its own command.

### 3.2 South Korea (Republic of Korea)

- **Government:** Fully independent. No Imperial administration.
- **Standing friction:**
  - Seoul's staff and field officers disagree over fixed-bunker doctrine and automata-first doctrine.
  - Staff resent Imperial advisors and rate treaty-supplied automata as expendable.
  - Korea is shut out of Solaris cores and Chōju.

### 3.3 Systems Alliance (SSV-)

- **Established:** Five years after the Reclamation War.
- **Makeup:** A supranational human defense and exploration force chartered by UNAS, EU and UN representatives. It absorbs the former UNAS, EU and Russian POV share.
- **Terms with the Empire:** The Kyoto-Arcturus Concordat, negotiated by Ambassador Anita Goyle. The Alliance polices wider human extra-solar space on licensed, blackboxed Helios-7 hulls (decision: Helios-7 replaces the retired Helios-8 name). Japan keeps jurisdiction over its territory, Crown worlds, Vassal garden colonies and dormant relays bordering Imperial space.
- **Standing friction:** The Alliance runs capped hardware it cannot open. Drescher resents the cap, and Mikhailovich distrusts the quarantines.

## 4. Nations & Organizations

The UNAS, the EU, the Russian Federation and the CPF follow one technology path from the stolen schematics. It is set out in the Timeline (2127 to 2131) and Technology §3.2.

### 4.1 UNAS

- **Posture:** Never allied with Japan. Sanctions tighten over the closed Pacific sea lanes from 2126. Fights both the Pact and Imperial forces in the war.
- **Standing friction:** Hawks resent Imperial supremacy, and Williams's call for détente draws suspicion.

### 4.2 EU

- **Posture:** Never allied with Japan. Sanctions tighten alongside UNAS.

### 4.3 Russian Federation

- **Posture:** Eurasian-Continental Pact member.

### 4.4 CPF

- **Posture:** Pact member, Beijing-aligned.
- **Standing friction:** Moscow and Beijing share the Pact without sharing a command culture.

### 4.5 DPRK

- **Posture:** Pact member.

### 4.6 Cerberus

- **Status:** Not formally established until the 2150s. Until then the group is the Harper-and-Lawson precursor network.
- **Members:** Jack Harper (founder) and Henry Lawson.
- **Aim:** Penetrate Kinki-Kyoku's technical secrets and Chōju protocols.
