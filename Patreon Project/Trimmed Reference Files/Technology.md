This file holds specifications. Who received which technology, and when, is in the Timeline.

## 1. MATERIALS

### 1.1 Kurogane Alloy Series (Tier 1 in 2107)

- **Nature:** A hyper-dense atomic-lattice alloy. It disperses a nuclear-scale impact through its crystal matrix and vaporizes a sacrificial outer layer to shed plasma heat. The series caps at 50 times the strength of Grade 5 titanium (Tier 5). Ordinary alloys fail under a Solaris field, which is why Kurogane-1 came before the first stable Solaris core (author ruling).
- **Tier requirement (author ruling):** Kurogane-1 is needed for Solaris-1, Kurogane-2 for Solaris-3, Kurogane-3 for Solaris-5, and so on up the series. Helios follows the same pairing: Helios-1 needs Kurogane-1, Helios-3 needs Kurogane-2, Helios-5 needs Kurogane-3.
- **Production method (author ruling):** Kurogane-1, 2 and 3 share one production method. Any clan that holds the Kurogane-1 schematics can discover Kurogane-2 and 3 from it. This is what drives Kenji to create a different method for Kurogane-4 and 5.
- **Designation:** Tier number, then variant letter (Kurogane-1I, Kurogane-1M). I is industrial grade and M is military grade (author ruling).
- **Grades in clan and foreign hands:** All AEPS plate is I grade. The schematics gifted to the clans and stolen by the CPF cover I grade. M grade stays with Clan Himura (decision).
- **Kurogane-3M:** Held by Himura from 2126, for the Solaris-5 work.

## 2. SOLARIS CORES

Every core is a seamless matte-black sphere or octahedron suspended in a magnetic vacuum cradle. It produces no exhaust and no flame, only a sub-audible thrum in the deck plates and a faint golden-amber glow along its phase conduits. A deterministic Quantum-Optical VI regulates each one. Solaris cores equip Clan Himura forces only.

### 2.3 Casing and Anti-Capture

- **Casing:** Seamless, with no hatch. Cutting torches and tomographic scans trip optical tripwires.
- **Hearth-Quench:** Fuses the core lattice into inert slag in 0.4 seconds.
- **Hearth-Collapse:** Implodes the hull or facility into a micro-singularity.
- **Dead-man keys:** Both keys sit in a locked crimson-and-brass shrine-box beside the chief engineer's console.

Each Helios generation has 75% of the capabilities of the Solaris generation with the same number, in mass-shedding cap, sub-light velocity and acceleration, power output and shield strength. Each Helios generation needs the same Kurogane tier as its Solaris match.

### 3.2 The Reclamation War

- **Helios-5, Himura-issued (author ruling):** Himura completes it midway through the war and gifts it to the loyal clans only, as he did Helios-3.
- **Traitor versions (author ruling):** Never given the Helios-3 schematics, Shimazu, Chōsokabe and Otomo must design their own. They manage it, and create Kurogane-2, near the middle of the Reclamation War. Their Helios-5 follows later in the war (decision on the step). The war exterminates the traitor clans, and their designs fall to the foreign powers.
- **Foreign versions (author ruling):** The UNAS, the EU and the Russian Federation build their own Helios-3 and Helios-5 versions during the war, from the traitors' fallen designs. The CPF builds its own as well.
- **Effect (author ruling):** The traitors' and foreign powers' own Helios-3 and Helios-5 versions are the reason the Reclamation War lasts a little over a decade.
- **Helios-pattern mass accelerators:** Anyone who builds Helios-3 can build them: the loyal clans from 2126, and the traitor clans, the UNAS, the EU and Russia once each has its own Helios-3, during the war (decision). They are the answer to Kurogane-3 armor (see section 10).

### 3.3 Limits

- **Limits on all Helios units:**
  - Conventional spherical bubble shields.
  - No fold drive.
  - No Eclipse stealth.

## 4. FLIGHT, FTL AND STEALTH

- **Reactionless gravimetric thrust:** The core supplies thrust, power, inertial dampening and shield projection from one closed loop.
- **Inertial dampening:**
  - 99.5%.
  - Crews wear heavy magnetic crash harnesses and feel a gravitational tug in hard turns.
- **Sequential Spatial-Fold FTL:**
  - Fleet hulls cruise at 12 light-years per day (25% of Solaris-9's 48, decision).
- **Tactical micro-jumps:** None.
- **Eclipse stealth:**
  - It shunts waste heat into solid-state thermal sinks.
  - Endurance is 4 hours, or 6 hours on modified Kinki-Kyoku and Shadow Squadron interceptors.
- **Iai-Pivot and Keystone Lock:**
  - Hulls pivot their bows to align a spinal gun.
  - They snap back to 100% rest mass for 15 milliseconds at discharge to absorb recoil.

## 5. SHIELDING

- **Faceted directional barriers:**
  - Angled golden-amber deflector planes project ahead of the prow and flanks.
  - They are not the seamless second-skin barrier of later hulls.
- **Phase-Gated Firing Apertures:** Millisecond tunnels open through the barrier for outgoing fire.

## 7. WEAPONS

### 7.1 Type-90 Gravitic Torpedoes

- **Propulsion:** Solid-state capacitor banks drive a reactionless forward gravitic drive. There is no reactor and no propellant.
- **Mass-shedding:** Pre-war rounds shed 30%, the Solaris-3 cap.
- **Flight profile:**
  - A boost burn of about 200 G.
  - A silent coast ("Whisper-Glide").
  - A delayed terminal mass-lock that restores full mass at impact.
- **Guidance:** Passive optical and infrared tracking, with a gyroscope and star-tracker for navigation. The Kage-Ya also maps the target's mass distribution.
- **Tamper denial:** A lattice collapse and thermite-potted electronics destroy a captured round.

### 7.2 Guns and Beams

- **Axial spinal Macrocannons:**
  - Carried by 750 m and larger hulls only.
  - Slugs travel at 2.0% c on interceptors and 3.0% c on Line Cruisers and the flagship.
  - Firing needs the whole hull aligned.
- **Turreted Macrocannons:**
  - A 10-meter class fires a 10 kg slug at 0.5% c.
  - A 100-meter class fires a 100 kg slug at 1.5% c and sits on Line Cruisers and the flagship.
- **Twin Energy Lances:** Lightspeed thermal strikes at twice Macrocannon range.
- **Point defense:** Point-defense Energy Lances and point-defense Macrocannons ring each hull.

## 8. COMPUTING

- **Quantum-Optical VI:** A deterministic, read-only lattice. The sealing and the Anti-Smart-AI Edict are dated in the Timeline (2119, 2122).
- **Duties:** Fire control, target tracking, mass-modulation timing and telemetry. Living officers keep command authority.
- **Bridge signature:** Silent columns of golden-amber kanji, and a single bronze temple-bell tone at target lock.

## 9. AUTOMATA

- **Command Lock:**
  - Every automaton accepts orders only from its assigned living officer or NCO.
  - Its Quantum-Optical VI is etched on a fixed read-only crystal.
- **Posts:** Automata hold fixed posts in silence with dimmed ember optical slits. They move only on an authorized command or a security trigger.
- **Alcoves:** Magnetic wall-charging clamps hold them dormant in ship corridors. At general quarters the slits flare golden-amber and the clamps release with a synchronized clack.
- **Iterations:** Every frame carries the iteration number of its core: Ashigaru-1 runs on Helios-1, Bushi-3 on Solaris-3M. A variant letter follows the iteration, as in Ashigaru-3B (decision on the format).
- **Two classes (author ruling):**
  - **Ashigaru:** The vassal and export class. Helios cores only.
  - **Bushi, Kensei, Ō-Yoroi and Onmitsu:** Exclusive to the Imperial clan. Solaris cores only.
  - **Kōgyō:** The unarmed foundry frames were always Himura's and stay outside the rule (decision).
- **The switch to Imperial classes (2125 on):** dated in the Timeline.

## 10. PERSONAL ARMOR AND ARMS

- **Kurogane Yoroi (renamed from the Silver Samurai exosuit, Gin-Musha):**
  - Exclusive to Clan Himura. Laminar samurai plate of Kurogane over a Solaris-M core.
  - Iterations follow the core, and the plate follows the same tier:
    - **Kurogane Yoroi-1:** Solaris-1M and Kurogane-1M, 2110, 15% cap, 18 km/h sprint, 2.7 m leap.
    - **Kurogane Yoroi-3:** Solaris-3M and Kurogane-2M, 2120, 30% cap, 37 km/h sprint, 5 m leap. The Restoration War suit and the pre-war suit.
    - **Kurogane Yoroi-5:** Solaris-5M and Kurogane-3M, after Solaris-5, midway through the war.
  - It carries a faceted directional barrier.
  - Orbital deployment uses armored drop-shuttles. There is no podless entry.
- **AEPS (Armored Exoskeletal Powered Suit):**
  - The vassal and export suit. Plate and core follow the same tier.
  - Iterations follow the Helios core:
    - **AEPS-1:** Helios-1 Cube and Kurogane-1I, 2112.
    - **AEPS-3:** Helios-3 and Kurogane-2I. The traitor clans build their own version midway through the Reclamation War.
    - **AEPS-5:** Helios-5 and Kurogane-3I. The traitor clans build their own version during the Reclamation War, and the foreign powers follow from the fallen traitor designs.
- **Kurogane armor and weapons (author ruling):**
  - Suits of Kurogane-3 and above need relativistic-velocity projectiles or gravitic-shearing weapons to defeat. That means AEPS-5 and Kurogane Yoroi-5.
  - AEPS-1 and AEPS-3, and Kurogane Yoroi-1 and 3, use Kurogane-1 and 2. They are softer. Infantry arms cannot harm them, but heavy anti-armor weapons can (decision).
  - Imperial troops carry gravitic weapons from 2120: gravitic-shearing mono-blades and relativistic coil-rifles (date is a decision).
- **Anti-capture denial:** A flatlined wearer or breached spine housing triggers Hearth-Quench in 0.2 seconds on every Himura-issued Solaris-powered suit.
- **Biomechanical Under-Suit (BMUS):**
  - Iterations follow the Kurogane Yoroi: BMUS-1 and BMUS-3 in this era (decision).
  - Worn against the skin, with no microchip, battery or transceiver.
- **Personal weapons:** Conventional coil-rifles and oiled mono-blade katanas for the vassal and allied troops.

## 11. PRODUCTION BASE

- **Kii-Yamato Core-Forge:**
  - A subterranean hall complex beneath the Kii mountains.
  - It casts cores and builds automata and Type-90 capacitor and penetrator lines.
- **Lunar-shadow slipway:** A hidden dock complex in a permanently shadowed polar crater. It assembles the hulls.
- **Low-orbit sovereign shipyard:** It builds hull sections and plate modules, and flies them to the slipway.
- **Proscribed Vault Sanctuaries:** Hollowed planetoids 25 light-years out, seeded by interceptor cruisers, holding sealed blueprints and archives.

## 12. MEDICINE AND SECRECY

- **Chōju (proto-protocol stable 2116):**
  - A 14-day immersion.
  - Recipients emerge ravenously hungry.
  - Tier I covers the Imperial Family. Tier II covers the household circle and, as they reach command rank, Hatamoto officers (names and dates in Characters and Ranks and the Timeline).
- **Volitional Lock (author-side):**
  - It follows a recipient's first Tier II immersion.
  - It stops conscious disclosure of protected secrets by jaw lock, frozen vocal cords or stiffened fingers.
  - Overdrive causes nosebleed or fainting.
- **Zero Technology Bleed (Fūin-Kōryō):** Kinki-Kyoku guards every core, foundry and shaft.
