## 2. Kenji's Knowledge and Expertise

- **StarCraft:** Terran warp drive navigation and technology; Terran CMC powered armor; Protoss anti-gravity.
- **Elysium:** Med-Pod.
- **The Matrix:** Knowledge uploading and downloading.
- **WH40K:** Archmagos Dominus Belisarius Cawl's Primaris Project; IoM Lasgun and Bolter.
- **HALO:** UNSC cloning; Covenant energy dissipating shield; UNSC "Dumb" AI; UNSC ship building.
- **Gundam:** Axis Zeon Gundarium Gamma.
- **Starship Troopers:** Terran Federation Mobile Infantry powered suit.
- **MCU:** Anthony Stark's Palladium Arc Reactor.
- **Homeworld:** Resource collection and assembly, including the Phased Disassembler Array used by Resource Collectors. It vaporizes target matter, breaks it to base components, separates and sorts elements by atomic mass, eliminating manufacturing waste.
- **Star Trek:** Vulcan data crystal.
- **I, Robot:** Robotics.

He pairs robotics with Dumb AI to build and man his secret bases on Earth and around the Solar System. (Per rule 53, name the source of any technology other than the Med-Pod only when the author gives it.)

### Med-Pod tiers (author ruling)
| Level | Description |
| --- | --- |
| 1 (Primitive) | Cures disease and mends injury, and stops there. Never renews a body. The public clinic grade. |
| 2 | Full technology. Rebuilds a body, rejuvenates, extends longevity. Private, used in hidden wings. |

- **Only Level 2 Med-Pods and better can rejuvenate and extend longevity (author ruling).** The public clinics never offer it.
- (inference) Anyone rejuvenated or living past a natural span was treated at Level 2 or better: network recruits, the found family, elites who pay, Manswell.
- (inference) Primitive is the grade the world could have invented unaided, which is why it cures and does not renew (A2.1).
- **Who builds it (author ruling, 3 November 2026).** Kenji builds only the Level 2 pod. Oyama builds the Primitive pods after Byron Pyrrus is recruited (July 2052) and sells them under its own name from January 2058.

- **Oyama and the security droids (author ruling, 3 November 2026).** Oyama sells Level 1 to Level 3 robots (decision on dates: Level 1 from 2057, Levels 2 and 3 from 2066). The organization's security droids guard only its private sites. They are Level 2 at first, built by Oyama from a design Kenji hands over for internal use, with Kenji's data crystals and Level 2 Dumb AI. Two Level 5 droids, which Kenji builds himself with Oyama money between October 2052 and summer 2053, are with him at all times. They are told to act low-key in public. They look and move like men, so people take them for human.
- Oyama's Level 1 robot arms are on the Galveston quay by September 2057: one ties down containers in forty minutes, work that took a crew of eight a day (A2.13).
