- **StarCraft:** Terran warp drive navigation and technology; Terran CMC powered armor; Protoss anti-gravity.
- **Elysium:** Med-Pod.
- **The Matrix:** Knowledge uploading and downloading.
- **WH40K:** Archmagos Dominus Belisarius Cawl's Primaris Project; IoM Lasgun and Bolter.
- **HALO:** UNSC cloning; Covenant energy dissipating shield; UNSC "Dumb" AI; UNSC ship building.
- **Gundam:** Axis Zeon Gundarium Gamma.
- **Starship Troopers:** Terran Federation Mobile Infantry powered suit.
- **MCU:** Anthony Stark's Palladium Arc Reactor.
- **Homeworld:** Resource collection and assembly, including the Phased Disassembler Array used by Resource Collectors. It vaporizes target matter, breaks it to base components, separates and sorts elements by atomic mass, eliminating manufacturing waste.
- **Star Trek:** Vulcan data crystal.
- **I, Robot:** Robotics.

He pairs robotics with Dumb AI to build and man his secret bases on Earth and around the Solar System. (Per rule 53, name the source of any technology other than the Med-Pod only when the author gives it.)

- **Pellam Field Systems (decision, open to overrule).** A puppet company run by Anthony Strauss. It builds and sells the AEMS and AEPS-BV suits, the BMUS under-suit, and the CFCS charging station. Oyama builds the droids. It sits under layered holding companies like the other puppets and has no public link to Oyama. Spreading products across separate companies keeps Oyama from being the only name on the technology.
- Cerberus (the author-side conspiracy) has no technology of its own. It seeks control of the firms, licensors, and regulators between the allowed technologies and the public (author-side).

### Med-Pod tiers (author ruling)
| Level | Description |
| --- | --- |
| 1 (Primitive) | Cures disease and mends injury, and stops there. Never renews a body. The public clinic grade. |
| 2 | Full technology. Rebuilds a body, rejuvenates, extends longevity. Private, used in hidden wings. |

- **Only Level 2 Med-Pods and better can rejuvenate and extend longevity (author ruling).** The public clinics never offer it.
- (inference) Anyone rejuvenated or living past a natural span was treated at Level 2 or better: network recruits, the found family, elites who pay, Manswell.
- (inference) Primitive is the grade the world could have invented unaided, which is why it cures and does not renew (A2.1).
- **Who builds it (author ruling, 3 November 2026).** Kenji builds only the Level 2 pod. Oyama builds the Primitive pods after Byron Pyrrus is recruited (July 2052) and sells them under its own name from January 2058.

- **The Foundation runs on Dumb AI (author ruling, 9 November 2026).** From A2.6 (December 2053) almost everything the Foundation uses has a Dumb AI assistant, and human members mostly validate by signature. ### Robotics tiers
| Level | Description |
| --- | --- |

- **Oyama and the security droids (author ruling, 3 November 2026).** Oyama sells Level 1 to Level 3 robots (decision on dates: Level 1 from 2057, Levels 2 and 3 from 2066). The organization's security droids guard only its private sites. They are Level 2 at first, built by Oyama from a design Kenji hands over for internal use, with Kenji's data crystals and Level 2 Dumb AI. Two Level 5 droids, which Kenji builds himself with Oyama money between October 2052 and summer 2053, are with him at all times. They are told to act low-key in public. They look and move like men, so people take them for human.
- Oyama's Level 1 robot arms are on the Galveston quay by September 2057: one ties down containers in forty minutes, work that took a crew of eight a day (A2.13).
- **Droid classes (decision, 9 November 2026; sheet: Nonvolitional_Combat_Droid_Classifications.md).** The droid sheet uses Tiers I to V, and combat models exist at all five. Non-combat utility variants exist at Tier I (fixture class: one fixed task at a station) and Tier II (operator class: expert work inside one loaded domain, such as assembly, inspection, or cargo staging). They use the same cores and power as the combat models and carry no weapons. A combat chassis converts to its non-combat variant by crystal swap, and the frame and power array are reused. Tiers III to V have no non-combat variant.
- **Crystals (decision, 9 November 2026; replaces the earlier line that every crystal comes from Kenji).** A Tier II operator loaded with the crystal-manufacture domain can make blank crystals. Cores are loaded on loading devices Kenji supplies, and more devices load more cores at once, so loading does not cap output. A droid cannot write or load a core.
- **Production order (author, 9 November 2026).** Tier I fixture units are built first and form the line that makes Tier II frames. Tier II units start as guards and are converted to operator class by crystal swap as Tier III units take over the guard roles. An operator-class unit can build anything, as long as the correct data crystal is installed: data crystals, anti-gravity units, BMUS, AEMS, AEPS-BV, AEPS, CFCS, SS-LiS-NCM4 battery packs, droid frames, loading devices, and more. Swapping the crystal changes the job. The crystal carries the design and the build expertise, and the unit does not design.
- **(open)** The story's Robotics levels (1 to 5, table above) and the droid sheet's Tiers (I to V) are not mapped to each other anywhere in the files.
