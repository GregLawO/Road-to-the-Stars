I. SOVEREIGN COMMAND & TREATY AUTHORITY

- Supreme Naval Authority: All Imperial warships operate under the supreme command of the Chrysanthemum Throne in the Sovereign Imperial Capital of Kyoto, directed operationally by Fleet Admiral Togo Kazuhiko (born 2085) from the Combined Starfleet General Staff (Rengō Uchū-Kaigun Sanbō Honbu).
- Strict Fleet Ceiling (Post-2136 Settlement): Under the 2136 Imperial Settlement, the Empire of Japan enforces a permanent, self-imposed cap of exactly 1,500 active IJN- warships—with zero net hull expansion since 2136.
- Sole Sovereign Allies & The 2149 Kyoto-Arcturus Concordat: Across Earth and human space, Taiwan and South Korea are Imperial Japan's sole formal sovereign allies under equal mutual-defense treaties. Under the 2149 Kyoto-Arcturus Concordat negotiated in Kyoto with Systems Alliance envoy Ambassador Anita Goyle, the Systems Alliance (SSV-) polices wider human extra-solar space using warships powered by commercially licensed, Kinki-Kyoku-blackboxed Imperial Helios-8 cores (50% mass-shedding cap), while the 1,500-ship IJN- Starfleet retains absolute sovereign jurisdiction over Japanese territory, Crown worlds, Japanese Vassal Clan garden colonies, and border relay quarantine zones.
