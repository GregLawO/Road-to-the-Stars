### UNIFIED IMPERIAL NAVAL REGISTRY & FLEET ARCHITECTURE (2122–2150+)

**DOCUMENT REF:** `REG-774-HIMURA-IMPERIAL`

**ISSUING AUTHORITY:** Combined Starfleet General Staff (*Rengō Uchū-Kaigun Sanbō Honbu*) & Imperial Admiralty (Kyoto)

---

I. SOVEREIGN COMMAND, TREATIES & FLEET CEILINGS

- **Supreme Naval Authority:** All Imperial warships operate under the supreme command of the Chrysanthemum Throne in the Sovereign Imperial Capital of Kyoto, directed operationally by **Fleet Admiral Togo Kazuhiko** (born 2085) from the Combined Starfleet General Staff.
- **Strict Fleet Ceiling (Post-2136 Settlement):** Under the 2136 Imperial Settlement, the Empire of Japan enforces a permanent, self-imposed cap of **exactly 1,500 active `IJN-` combat warships**, with zero net hull expansion since 2136.
- **Sovereign Alliances & The 2149 Kyoto-Arcturus Concordat:**
  - **Sole Sovereign Allies:** Taiwan (Formosa) and the Republic of Korea (South Korea) under equal mutual-defense treaties.
  - **Systems Alliance (`SSV-`) Division of Jurisdiction:** Negotiated in Kyoto with Alliance envoy Ambassador Anita Goyle. The Systems Alliance polices wider human extra-solar relay space using warships powered by commercially licensed, *Kinki-Kyoku*-blackboxed **Helios-8C** cores (50% mass-shedding cap, zero FTL), while the 1,500-ship `IJN-` Starfleet retains absolute sovereign jurisdiction over Japanese territory, Crown worlds, Vassal Clan garden colonies, and dormant mass relay quarantine zones.
- **Zero Technology Bleed (*Fūin-Kōryō*):** Every `IJN-` warship is a blackboxed Solaris-9 and Quantum-Optical VI fortress. Captains are bound by blood-oath to execute **Hearth-Quench** core scrams (0.4-second neutronium slagging) and **Hearth-Collapse** micro-singularity self-destruction rather than allow a single core or hull fragment to fall into foreign or xeno hands.

III. MID-WAR TRANSITION ERA (2134–2142 — SOLARIS-7 ARASHI SERIES)

During World War III (The Iron Tempest / War of Reclamation and Retribution), Crown Foundries rush-commissioned **Solaris-7 Arashi** hulls (60% mass-shedding cap, 40% minimum apparent mass, 99.85% inertial dampening, 32 LY/day FTL, first-generation conformal golden-amber External Shear Barriers, 100,000 km tactical micro-jumps with 180s cooldown, and 8-hour Eclipse stealth).

- **Key Wartime Introductions:** Conformal shear barriers enabled podless "Golden Rain" orbital drops in 2134, the **Type-90D *O-Yumi*** strategic siege torpedo debuted in late 2135, and wartime **1,500m Battleships** and **prototype 3,000m Grand Battleships** were field-tested prior to the 2136–2142 fleet standardization.

IV. POST-WAR FLEET REGISTRY (2136–2150+ — SOLARIS-9 ERA: 1,500 COMBAT WARSHIPS)

Powered by **Solaris-9 Unified Gravitic Cores** (75% physical mass-shedding hard limit, 25% minimum apparent mass, 99.98% inertial dampening, 48 LY/day standard FTL cruise / 72 LY/day emergency flank, seamless golden-amber External Shear Barriers, 250,000 km Single-Fold Tactical Micro-Jumps with 90s cooldown, and 14-hour Eclipse stealth).

Tactical Formation Standards

- **1:2:4 Line-of-Battle Division (*Kantai Sentō-Gun* — 7 Hulls):** 1x 3,000m Grand Battleship + 2x 1,500m Battleships + 4x 1,000m Line Cruisers (2 Line Cruisers screening each Battleship). There are **45 Line Divisions** across the Starfleet (20 Guard + 20 Main + 5 Shock).
- **1:2 Shadow-Wolf Patrol Pack (*Kage-Ōkami Buntai* — 3 Hulls):** 1x 500m Frigate + 2x 250m Combat Corvettes (24 bow Type-90 torpedo tubes per pack). There are **258 active 1:2 packs** (100 Guard + 103 Main + 50 Shock + 5 Exploration).

4. Imperial Compliance Directorate Fleet (`IJN-KNS-` — 50 Organic Warships + Requisitioned Wolfpacks)

Commanded by **High Censor Kurosawa Renji** (born 2090) from the Supreme Tribunal of *Kansatsu-In*. Structured across **5 specialized Directorates / Bureaus**, each organically assigned **10 Interceptor Cruisers (`IJN-KNS-SP`)**. Under Imperial Mandate, every Directorate Interceptor Cruiser carries standing sovereign authority to **requisition 1 active 1:2 Wolfpack (1 Frigate + 2 Corvettes = 3 hulls)** per cruiser from local `IJN-TNU-` or `IJN-KNE-` sector patrols for audit cordons, quarantine enforcement, or boarding operations (up to **10 Wolfpacks / 30 hulls per Directorate**, or **50 Wolfpacks / 150 hulls** across all 5 Directorates at full mobilization):

1. **Bureau of Inspection & Audit (*Kansatsu-Kyoku* — 10x `IJN-KNS-SP` + up to 10 Requisitioned Wolfpacks):** Conducts unannounced Sovereign compliance audits of Vassal Clan domains, colonial shipyards, and licensed commercial facilities.
2. **Bureau of Legal & Treaty Enforcement (*Hōsei-Kyoku* — 10x `IJN-KNS-SP` + up to 10 Requisitioned Wolfpacks):** Enforces Imperial statutory law, *Sankin-Kōtai* obligations, and bilateral treaty compliance across sovereign borders.
3. **Bureau of Internal Security & Anti-AI Suppression (*Chian-Kyoku* — 10x `IJN-KNS-SP` + up to 10 Requisitioned Wolfpacks):** Enforces the 2119 Anti-Smart-AI Edict, hunts rogue synthetic/AI networks, and suppresses domestic espionage or sedition.
4. **Bureau of Technical Secrecy & Blackbox Custody (*Kinki-Kyoku* — 10x `IJN-KNS-SP` + up to 10 Requisitioned Wolfpacks):** Guards Crown Foundries and Proscribed Vault Sanctuaries, audits exported **Helios-series** cores (Helios-2, -5, and -8C), and enforces *Fūin-Kōryō* (Zero Technology Bleed) and Hearth-Quench anti-tamper seals.
5. **Bureau of Frontier & Border Control (*Henkyō-Kyoku* — 10x `IJN-KNS-SP` + up to 10 Requisitioned Wolfpacks):** Enforces the 2149 Charon-era dormant mass relay quarantine along Imperial borders, polices deep-space exclusion zones, and receives encrypted quarantine alerts from `IJN-TNK-` Exploration squadrons.

VII. EXPORT & ALLIED FLEET TIER (HELIOS-SERIES COMMERCIAL / ALLIANCE ARCHITECTURE)

- **Systems Alliance (`SSV-`) & Vassal/Allied Hulls:** Powered by hard-capped **Helios-8C** cores (50% apparent mass reduction, 50% sub-light velocity/power/shield output compared to Solaris-9, 99.0% inertial dampening, conventional spherical bubble shields, and **0 LY/day FTL**—forcing complete reliance on the Mass Relay network).
- **Compliance Oversight:** Audited regularly by `IJN-KNS-` Compliance Directorate cruisers; protected against reverse-engineering by identical **Hearth-Quench** and **Hearth-Collapse** anti-tamper seals.
