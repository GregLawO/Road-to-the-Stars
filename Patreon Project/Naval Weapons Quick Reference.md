### NAVAL WEAPONS QUICK REFERENCE: CHAPTERS A1–A5 (2130–2135)

ISSUING AUTHORITY: IMPERIAL ORDNANCE BUREAU AND STARFLEET GENERAL STAFF (KYOTO)

---

I. ERA RESTRICTIONS FOR CHAPTERS A1–A5 (2130–2135)

- Active Weapon Systems: Type-90 Gravitic Torpedoes (A, B, C, E variants), Axial Spinal Macrocannons (750m+ hulls only), Turreted Macrocannons (10m to 100m secondary mounts), Turret-Mounted Twin Energy Lances (Koso-Ho), and Close-In Point-Defense Railguns.
- Excluded Post-War Systems (Do NOT use in A1–A5):
  - Type-90D O-Yumi Strategic Siege Torpedo (debuts late 2135 in Chapter A6.1).
  - Axial Singularity Cannons / Tokuiten-Ho (exclusive to post-2136 3,000m Grand Battleships and 10,000m IJN Amaterasu).
  - Protocol Mirror-Shatter (post-2136 Solaris-9 flagship defense).

---

II. AXIAL SPINAL MACROCANNONS (RAIL-GRAVITIC ACCELERATORS — 750M+ HULLS ONLY)  
Accelerates hyper-dense metallic slugs (under a quarter of a ton / 250 kg through the A1–A5 era) down the ship's centerline. Requires the entire hull to align via an Iai-Pivot before firing through bow Phase-Gated Firing Apertures. Corvettes (250m) and Frigates (500m) do NOT carry spinal guns:

1. 750m Interceptor Cruiser Spinal Macrocannon
- Barrel Length: 550 meters.
- Projectile and Velocity: 120 kg slug (below 0.25 metric tons) accelerated to 2.0% of c (6,000 km/s — crosses 10,000 km in 1.7 seconds; 50,000 km in 8.3 seconds).
2. 1,000m Line Cruiser and Pre-War Flagship Spinal Macrocannon
- Barrel Length: 750 meters (Line Cruisers) / 800 meters (IJN Amaterasu-no-Yaiba).
- Projectile and Velocity: 180 kg to 220 kg slug (below 0.25 metric tons) accelerated to 3.0% of c (9,000 km/s — crosses 10,000 km in 1.1 seconds; 50,000 km in 5.6 seconds).
3. Later / Mid-to-Post-War Capital Spinal Macrocannons (1,500m+ Battleships and Flagships)
- Barrel Length: 1,150 meters to 2,500 meters.
- Projectile and Velocity: 240 kg slug (strictly below 0.25 metric tons) accelerated to a maximum achieved velocity of 3.5% of c (10,500 km/s — crosses 50,000 km in 4.8 seconds).
4. Post-2136 Exceptions (outside A1–A5, per REG-774)
- The 3,000m Grand Battleship's 2,100m spinal fires 450–500 kg slugs, and the 10,000m IJN Amaterasu's 2,500m spinal fires 500 kg slugs.

---

III. TURRETED MACROCANNONS (SECONDARY KINETIC BATTERY — 10M TO 100M)  
Mounted in armored turrets across all IJN- warship classes, in two sizes only, 10-meter and 100-meter:

1. Light Turreted Macrocannon (10-Meter Class)
- Platform: Primary turret battery on 250m Corvettes, 500m Frigates, and 750m Interceptor Cruisers (decision); light secondary battery on larger hulls.
- Projectile and Velocity: 10 kg slug accelerated to 0.5% of c (1,500 km/s — crosses 10,000 km in 6.7 seconds).
2. Heavy Turreted Macrocannon (100-Meter Class)
- Platform: Heavy broadside and dorsal turrets on 1,000m Line Cruisers, Pre-War Flagship IJN Amaterasu-no-Yaiba, and 1,500m+ Capital Ships.
- Projectile and Velocity: 100 kg slug accelerated to 1.5% of c (4,500 km/s — crosses 10,000 km in 2.2 seconds).
These are the only two turret sizes and slug masses.

---

IV. TYPE-90 SERIES GRAVITIC TORPEDOES (CONTINUOUS-BURN GUIDED PENETRATORS)  
Fired from bow launch tubes (24 tubes on Pre-War Flagship, 20 on Line Cruisers, 16 on Interceptor Cruisers, 12 on Frigates, 6 on Corvettes). Type-90 torpedoes carry no reactor and no propellant: solid-state capacitor banks (ORD-882) power a reactionless forward gravitic drive. They inherit the host warship's sub-light velocity at launch and burn at the benchmark rates below for as long as the capacitor lasts, with no human crew G-limit. They then coast in Whisper-Glide and restore 100% physical mass in the final approach (30 m Kozuka, 50 m Hari, 300 m Kage-Ya, 1,000 m O-Yumi). Benchmarks assume the 75% cap only in Era III; Pre-War warheads shed 45% and Solaris-7 warheads 60%, and the O-Yumi debuts late 2135:

Era Sub-Light Acceleration and Transit Benchmarks (From Stationary Launch):

- Era I (2130–2133 — Solaris-4 Envelope: 45% Mass-Shedding / ~200 G Boost Burn):
  - 1,000 km (Low/High Earth Orbit): Reaches 62 km/s in 32 seconds.
  - 5,000 km (Medium Orbital Engagement): Reaches 140 km/s in 71 seconds.
  - 15,000 km (Outer Orbital / Wolfpack Ambush): Reaches 242 km/s (0.08% c) in 124 seconds.
  - 50,000 km (Deep Standoff / Interplanetary Cruise): Reaches 443 km/s (0.15% c) in 226 seconds (plus inherited warship sub-light cruise velocity up to 0.1%–0.5% c).
- Era II (2134–2136 — Solaris-7 Envelope: 60% Mass-Shedding / ~300 G Boost Burn):
  - 1,000 km: Reaches 77 km/s in 26 seconds.
  - 5,000 km: Reaches 171 km/s in 58 seconds.
  - 15,000 km: Reaches 297 km/s (0.10% c) in 101 seconds.
  - 50,000 km: Reaches 542 km/s (0.18% c) in 184 seconds (plus inherited warship sub-light cruise velocity).
- Era III (2137+ — Solaris-9 Envelope: 75% Mass-Shedding / ~400+ G Boost Burn):
  - 1,000 km: Reaches 89 km/s in 23 seconds.
  - 5,000 km: Reaches 198 km/s in 50 seconds.
  - 15,000 km: Reaches 343 km/s (0.11% c) in 87 seconds.
  - 50,000 km: Reaches 626 km/s (0.21% c) in 159 seconds (plus inherited warship sub-light cruise velocity).

Sub-Munition Variants:

1. Type-90A Hari ("Needle") — Point-Defense and Kinetic-Rod Interceptor
- Role: High-agility anti-missile and anti-orbital-bombardment interceptor.
- Specs: 1.8m length; uses full 200 G to 300+ G snap-maneuvers to intercept incoming kinetic rods and nuclear warheads in low orbit; deploys a tungsten-carbide fragmentation cone (used by Haruka Tachibana's Guard Fleet in A2.7 and A3.5).
2. Type-90B Kusari ("Chain") — Non-Lethal Kinetic Disabler
- Role: Precision hull-breach and thruster-crippling round for boarding or quarantine enforcement.
- Specs: 2.8m length; blunt tungsten-copper impact cap; locks back to 100% mass in the final approach to shear off enemy engine bells or sensor masts without detonating the target's reactor.
3. Type-90C Kage-Ya ("Shadow Arrow") — Primary Anti-Ship Standoff Impactor
- Role: Main anti-warship kinetic penetrator used in Frigate-Corvette Kage-Okami wolfpack ambushes (1:4 pack in A2.4; 1:2 packs in A3.2).
- Specs: 4.0m length; depleted-uranium penetrator needle; runs dark under Eclipse stealth, then snaps to 100% physical mass at 300 meters from the target—converting accumulated sub-light momentum into pure kinetic shock that punches straight through armor and reactor citadels without an explosive warhead.
4. Type-90E Kozuka ("Utility Blade") — Atmospheric and Close-Support Micro-Torpedo
- Role: Precision tactical micro-torpedo carried by a 250m wartime prototype IJN-TNU-LE(S) Close-Support Corvette (A5.2).
- Specs: 1.2m length; throttled for atmospheric flight after orbital superiority is secured to strike enemy artillery kill-boxes and armored columns with surgical accuracy near friendly South Korean and Japanese troops.

V. DIRECTED-ENERGY AND CLOSE-IN DEFENSIVE SYSTEMS

1. Turret-Mounted Twin Energy Lances (Koso-Ho)
- Platform: High-precision directed-energy turrets across IJN- warship classes.
- Firing Doctrine: Fires at lightspeed (100% c — instantaneous across orbital ranges) through Phase-Gated Firing Apertures that open for milliseconds inside the ship's External Shear Barrier (the faceted directional barrier on Solaris-4 hulls, as in A1.2; the conformal golden-amber barrier from Solaris-7). Used in A1.2 and A4.4 to slice orbital reconnaissance probes and bomber wings cleanly apart without shrapnel spread.
2. Close-In Point-Defense Railguns (CIWS)
- Platform: Secondary defensive mounts across all IJN- hulls.
- Firing Doctrine: Fires high-cadence 20mm tungsten darts to shred close-range fighter swarms, debris, and leaker warheads during high-G orbital engagements (A2.10, A3.5).

VI. ADVERSARY WEAPON SYSTEMS (GROUNDED 2130–2135 CONVENTIONAL TIER)

- Eurasian-Continental Pact (Russia, CPF, DPRK): Orbit-to-surface tungsten kinetic bombardment rods, nuclear-tipped proximity flak shells (used to create physical shrapnel clouds in A2.4), liquid/nuclear-thermal chemical missiles (1–3 G burn, ~5–15 km/s), and ground-based electromagnetic coastal railguns (Vladivostok and Taiwan Strait, A2.5, A3.2).
- Western Coalition (UNAS and EU): Chemical-rocket orbital interceptor fighters (flown by Jon Grissom in A3.1), orbital kinetic-minefields (lunar corridor, A3.4), railgun-armed orbital defense platforms, and conventional missile/laser point-defense grids.
