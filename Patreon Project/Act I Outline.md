# Act I Outline: A1 (new version)

A1 is rewritten to plant the A2 and A3 canon (author, 9 November 2026). Kenji has no POV in A1. A2 and A3 entries follow in later segments.

**Key.** Ending: P physical event, Q quiet reversal, B held breath, W withheld answer, E emotional beat. Ending types are (decision).

---

## 1. Shared wording (decision)

Use these exact terms in A1.1, A1.4, A1.5, A2.6 and A2.8.
- **"The lower building."** The separate underground building that holds the Level 2 pods at each clinic.
- **"The frosted doors."** Where the passage up from the lower building meets the clinic. A stair or lift runs up to them, and they open into the exit lobby.
- **"The exit lobby."** A private lobby where people treated with Level 2 come out. It is posh and luxurious, and it has no license on its wall.
- The public never sees the lower building from the front step. It is one plain unmarked service building or nothing at all.
- Level 2 droids stand inside the lower building and its passage, silent and still, never in view of the exit lobby or the public waiting room. A droid makes noise only when it moves.

## 2. Spine

- Danny Shepard is Hannah's blood-related younger brother and her only living family. Their parents are dead and never appear.
- Danny is a crippled, sickly UNAS Army veteran, held in hospital after a building collapse in early 2052 and an illness that followed.
- Hannah is an active-service UNAS Navy officer: ensign, one gold stripe, in 2053. She is Danny's legal guardian. She never resigns.
- Danny is the first to buy into the offer. He wants to be whole and useful again.
- Hannah is torn between agreeing and distrusting the secrecy. She agrees for his sake.
- Hannah is the only one of the family who is not conditioned. She refuses the upload.

## 3. Chapters

### A1.1 Hannah (22), Galveston, June 2053 [I] · Q
About 1,000 words. Cast: Hannah. Kenji appears in the recall and in the exit lobby.
- **Opening detail.** The exit lobby: stone floor, leather chairs, fresh flowers, a coffee service with real cups. One wall has nothing on it where a license should hang.
- **The wait.** She waits three days in the exit lobby with her dress blues folded on the chair beside her. Staff speak in the same flat voice, bring coffee, and wear no name tags.
- **The recall (short beats, not a flashback).**
  - **The call.** She is called to the hospital at Danny's request. Kenji is not there. He has already spoken with Danny, and Danny relays the offer: the years given back are spent working with the organization, treatment continues as long as the member serves, whoever serves serves for life, and the treatment covers family. Kenji will also pay the hospital bills. She asks for a day to think it over. Danny pleads with her to agree. She holds.
  - **The next day.** She meets Kenji in person at the hospital. He introduces himself by name and confirms the terms. They match what Danny told her.
  - **The second plea.** Danny pleads again. She has grave doubts about the whole affair, and she agrees hesitantly, for her younger brother's sake. Danny signs and she countersigns as his guardian.
  - **Her request.** She asks to be there in person when Danny is treated. Kenji refuses mildly and counteroffers: she can watch through a camera feed and nothing more. She hesitates and agrees. The feed is a screen on the lobby wall. It shows only the sealed pod lid in the lower building (decision).
  - The single intake sheet she signs at the clinic is the guardian's consent. It carries no letterhead.
- **Back in the present: the debt.** Kenji pays the bills as part of the contract, and the contract asks nothing of Hannah for them. She still counts it as a debt that she and Danny owe him. In her mind the whole debt is what he did: he gave her brother back his life and a purpose. She resolves to find a way to pay it back.
- **The room (decision).** The Navy taught her to note what is missing. Here she notes what is extra, in a pocket notebook: the stone floor, the leather, the flowers, the real cups, a separate private entrance from the street. A clinic that quotes no price and posts no license has built a room for people who pay. She asks none of the questions it raises: who the room is for, and why people who come out of the lower building get a door of their own.
- **The frosted doors.** They open at ten past six. Danny walks out on his own feet, smelling of cold concrete. He asks for breakfast. She takes his wrist and checks his pulse. Kenji comes up the passage behind him.
- **Kenji's offer.** He offers Hannah the same treatment and the training session, on the same terms. She declines and gives two reasons (decision):
  - **Her oath.** "I swore an oath to the Navy in January. I can't swear another one to anyone and mean both."
  - **Someone outside.** "If both of us are inside, nobody is left outside to ask questions. Danny needs one of us who can still say no."
  - Danny says "Your call" and does not press. Kenji says the offer stays open.
- **The card.** Kenji gives her his card and tells her to use it if she needs anything. It carries his name and a number, with no company and no address.
- **Hook (Q).** She turns the card over. The back is blank. The wall behind her is still bare. She keeps the card.

### A1.2 Lucía Ferreira, a border clinic, 2058 [I] · A
About 900 words. Cast: Lucía.
- **Scene.** She carries her six-year-old son Mateo across a river to a clinic with a green cross over the door. His kidneys failed in the spring. A queue waits in the yard, silent and watchful, to see whether a neighbor's boy walks out whole.
- **Hook.** The boy, Iker, runs out barefoot and laughing. The clerk calls "Forty-one". Lucía opens her fist: her slip says fifty-eight.
- **Optional echo (decision, author decides).** One Level 1 pod carries an Oyama plate with a blank Designer line.

### A1.3 Anselm Roque, a district clinic, 2059 [I] · Q
About 900 words. Cast: Anselm.
- **Scene.** A licensing inspector of eleven years waits on a bench with a sealed closure order. His father died in a ward whose bill outlived him by six years. An old woman tells him to put the letter down. Her brother walks out carrying the door he was carried in on.
- **Hook (Q).** In the findings box he writes "Nothing found" and leaves the seal unbroken.

### A1.4 Hannah (28), Galveston, March 2059 [A] · W
About 1,000 words. Cast: Hannah. Kenji appears as her husband.
- **Premise.** Hannah is on leave. She walks to the staff door at the end of the night shift to meet Danny for breakfast. She does not know he is fighting.
- **Opening detail.** The staff plan working. The night count at shift change is done, and a named person stands at each exit.
- **Before the blast (Slow Gear).**
  - Every droid is at its post and still. The grounds are quiet and nothing patrols.
  - The third lamp in the car park has been dead eleven days, an ordinary maintenance failure.
  - The Thursday delivery van has not come. The attackers took it and use it as their breach vehicle.
  - The gate stool is empty. The guard's radio sits on the seat, live. His coffee cup is gone. These are small absences, each with a reasonable excuse.
- **Trigger and blast (Fast Gear).**
  - The window goes white. Sound follows.
  - The blast is on the lower building's side.
  - Her left forearm is burned.
  - Staff go the other way, using the passage timing planted in A1.1. The fight is not on her route.
- **After the blast.**
  - Every droid repositions from its post toward the breach. She hears a burst of heavy steps from the lower building's side, then silence as they take new positions. She takes them for machinery and gear.
  - She counts the night staff out by the name posted at each exit.
  - The staff are on the far side of the main building. One line says why the lawn is safe.
  - Her one order goes to the staff: "East lot. Walk."
- **The all-clear.** The noise stops dead. A radio voice says the lower building is clear. Her husband comes down the corridor coatless and takes the gurney rail in both hands. His knuckles go pale.
- **Hook (W).** She refuses care until the count is confirmed. Then she asks where her brother is. The chapter cuts before the answer. The cost of the fight is the author's slot.

### A1.5 Brooks (24), Marseille, July 2062 [I] · B
About 1,000 words. Cast: Brooks, and Vance unnamed. A3.1 must match every gesture.
- **Opening detail.** The hospital bracelet with a number, a barcode, and a blank strip where a name should be.
- **The ward.** She has been dying of leukemia for fourteen months. She counts the exits: two doors, one painted-shut window, a vent too narrow for a child. Eleven steps from bed to corridor door.
- **The visitor.** A man in a gray coat in July turns the visitor's chair a quarter turn to watch the door. He carries no flowers and no folder. The coat hides a sidearm. He names her Ankara tariff paper.
- **The terms.** Vance states the cost plainly: the years spent working with the organization, service for life, family covered, and the secrecy session. He will not say whose work it is. "Whose work?" is answered with "Afterward." A four-second pause, which she counts.
- **The machine.** It is under the clinic. She says yes.
- **The walk out.** Three days later the frosted doors open. She takes the stair, counting the steps, and her legs hold. Her right hand taps against her thigh. In the exit lobby Vance stands with his back to the glass so he can see her come.
- **Hook (B).** "Sixty more years, for a start. Ms. Brooks, what would you do with them?" Her fingers stop tapping and she opens her mouth.
