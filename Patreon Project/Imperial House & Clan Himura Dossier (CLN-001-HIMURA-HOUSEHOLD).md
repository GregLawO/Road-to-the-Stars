ISSUING AUTHORITY: THRONESIDE ARCHIVES & IMPERIAL PRIVY COUNCIL (KYOTO)

I. THE IMPERIAL HOUSE OF HIMURA & THE THRONE OF KYOTO

- Sovereign Seat of Power: Following the 2122 Kyoto Restoration, the Imperial House of Himura occupies the Chrysanthemum Throne at the Kyoto-Heian Imperial Citadel and Amaterasu Palace in the Sovereign Imperial Capital of Kyoto (with Tokyo serving as the Empire's primary commercial and maritime metropolis).
- Dual Authority (Crown & Clan): Emperor Himura exercises dual supremacy as the constitutional Sovereign of the Empire of Japan and as the hereditary Clan Head (Tōshu) of Clan Himura—retaining personal, unshared ownership over the Crown Foundries, the Solaris Unified Gravitic Core patents, deterministic Quantum-Optical VI architecture, and the Throneside Medical Bureau (Project Chōju).
- Territorial & Non-Interference Mandate: Under Emperor Himura's Doctrine of Sovereign Non-Interference, Imperial jurisdiction and hereditary Vassal Clan fiefs are confined strictly to sovereign Japanese territory (the Japanese Home Islands, Sakhalin, the Kurils, Western Pacific islands, Crown colony worlds, and Japanese Vassal Clan garden colonies in space). Japan maintains no Governors-General or Imperial rule over China, Korea, or Taiwan, recognizing Taiwan and South Korea as equal, fully independent sovereign treaty allies.

II. THE SOVEREIGN APEX & INNER IMPERIAL HOUSEHOLD

1. Emperor Himura Kenji (born 2092)
- Lifecycle Ages: 23 in 2115 (Secret Awakening of the prototype Solaris-4 core and Quantum-Optical VI lattice in the Kii-Yamato Core-Forge) | 30 in 2122 (Kyoto Restoration; launch of the clandestine pre-war fleet buildup) | 40–44 in 2132–2136 (World War III; directs the Imperial war effort from Kyoto and commands aboard the flagship IJN Amaterasu-no-Yaiba) | 57 in 2149 (Charon Relay activation and Kyoto-Arcturus Concordat; biologically preserved in his late 20s via Tier I Chōju longevity protocols).
- Volitional Lock: none (author ruling). The Imperial Family carries no Lock.
- Character & Governance: Pragmatic, unsentimental, and possessed of total working mastery over every Solaris and Quantum-Optical system deployed by the Crown. Driven by quiet, unstated foreknowledge of the wider galactic threat, he enforces the 2119 Anti-Smart-AI Edict, caps the post-war Starfleet at exactly 1,500 warships, and refuses any foreign annexation while building an unbreakable iron shield around Imperial territory.
2. Empress Himura Kaguya (born 2093)
- Lifecycle Ages: 22 in 2115 | 29 in 2122 | 39–43 in 2132–2136 | 56 in 2149 (biologically mid-20s via Tier I Chōju).
- Role & Authority: Sovereign Consort and Grand Matriarch of the Inner Palace in Kyoto. Directly oversees the Throneside Medical Bureau (administering Tier I and Tier II Chōju cellular-rejuvenation cycles and Volitional Lock synchronization for the High Council; she carries no Lock herself), manages the Sankin-Kōtai obligations of sworn Japanese Vassal Clans, and leads domestic cultural and Shinto-Imperial restoration across the Home Islands and Crown worlds.
3. Crown Prince Himura Kiyoshi (born 2114) (decision on name)
- Lifecycle Ages: 8 in 2122 | 18 in 2132 | 22 in 2136 | 25 in 2139 (Tier I Chōju) | 35 in 2149 | 40 in 2154.
- Role: Heir to the Chrysanthemum Throne. Legion ground-pounder in a Hata-Musha exosuit, with a Hatamoto shadow imposed by Lord Commander Tachibana that he resents. Receives Tier I Chōju at 25 once he stops demanding the throne. Carries no Volitional Lock (author ruling) and holds no foreknowledge, not even dream-level.
4. Himura Ayame (born 2126) and Himura Haruto (born 2136) (decision on names)
- Younger children of the Emperor and Empress. Both receive the Tier I Chōju protocol (author ruling). Neither carries a Volitional Lock or holds any foreknowledge.

II-B. CLAN HIMURA ELDERS & KIN (decision; from the Revision Plan)

- Grand Uncle Masamune Himura: Senior family elder and council skeptic. Declines Chōju. Dies in 2139.
- Ryunosuke Himura (born ~2104): Hawkish cousin who matures under command; becomes Vassal Fleet envoy by about 2141.
- Lady Shizuka Himura: Older kinswoman (not the Emperor's sister). Master industrialist and logistics matron; steps back from daily shifts in 2139.
- Himura Kōji (born ~2098): Court traditionalist who presses the succession question until Kiyoshi chooses to stay a soldier.

Architect of the Standoff Supremacy Principle, 1:2 Shadow-Wolf torpedo ambush doctrine, and 1:2:4 line-of-battle formations during World War III; commands the 10,000-meter Throne-Citadel IJN Amaterasu (IJN-TNS-MC) post-war.

IV. SWORN JAPANESE VASSAL CLANS & SANKIN-KŌTAI SYSTEM

- Exclusively Japanese Nobility: Hereditary Daimyō and Vassal Clan titles belong strictly to loyal Japanese aristocratic houses (such as Clan Date, Clan Shimazu, and Clan Uesugi) sworn to the Chrysanthemum Throne in Kyoto. No Daimyō clans exist in Taiwan, Korea, or China.
- Sankin-Kōtai (Alternate Attendance): Japanese Vassal Clan heads maintain secondary residences in Kyoto and rotate regular attendance at the Imperial Court, ensuring political cohesion and loyalty across the Home Islands and extra-solar garden colonies (such as Mutsu-Shinano).
- Strict Technological Separation: Japanese Vassal Clans administer local agricultural and industrial infrastructure on their assigned Japanese colony worlds using commercially licensed, 50%-capped Helios-8 labor frames and planetary militias, while all void defense above their worlds is conducted exclusively by Imperial Japanese Starfleet (IJN-TNU-) 1:2 Frigate-Corvette divisions.

V. HOUSEHOLD SECURITY, RELICS & SUCCESSION PROTOCOLS

- Succession (decision): Kiyoshi is the heir. The Imperial Family holds Tier I Chōju with no Volitional Lock, so the throne will not open for centuries. Kenji teaches his heir patience over that wait.
- The Crimson-and-Brass Shrine-Box: Every sovereign warship reactor sanctum and Crown Foundry houses a locked shrine-box containing physical Hearth-Quench (instant lattice scram) and Hearth-Collapse (total neutronium-core implosion) dead-man keys, guarded by silent Ashigaru-4 and Kikai-Bushi automata with dimmed ember optical slits.
- The 25-Light-Year Proscribed Vault Sanctuaries: Hollowed-out interstellar planetoids seeded during 2122–2132 under Kinki-Kyoku seal, storing complete Solaris-9 core blueprints, Quantum-Optical crystal presses, and Chōju medical matrices as an unassailable dynastic failsafe.
