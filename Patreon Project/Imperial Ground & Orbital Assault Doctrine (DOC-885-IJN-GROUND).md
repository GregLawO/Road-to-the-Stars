1. STRATEGIC PHILOSOPHY: THE IRON AEGIS ON LAND
- Preserving Sovereign Japanese Blood: Having endured severe demographic decline prior to the 2122 Restoration, Imperial Japan preserves its living Japanese citizenry for command, specialized tactical leadership, and elite shock units while deploying millions of deterministic Quantum-Optical VI automata to absorb frontline attrition.

- Human Command Over Machine Mass: Every Imperial ground formation is led by living Japanese Non-Commissioned Officers and Commissioned Officers clad in dual-layer Silver Samurai armor—combining the 100% passive analog Shintai-Hojō-fuku Biomechanical Under-Suit (TEC-915-IJN-BMUS) with a Solaris-9M powered neutronium-carbide exosuit (TEC-920-IJN-EXOSUIT)—directing four subordinate samurai-form automata via air-gapped tight-beam infrared/UV photonic HUD links.

- Zero Smart-AI & Air-Gapped Command Integrity: In strict compliance with the Anti-Smart-AI Edict and Seishin-Fūin ("Mind-Seal Protocol"), no combat automaton, Oni-Mecha, or exosuit carries wireless RF/Wi-Fi receivers. Tactical orders travel exclusively over line-of-sight infrared/UV laser pulses between the human leader's helmet optics and the automata's cranial sensors, rendering Imperial ground formations impervious to cyber-hijacking or signal spoofing.

- Total Vertical Integration: Imperial ground forces never fight isolated from orbit. Every planetary campaign is executed as a seamless vertical continuum linking Starfleet orbital supremacy, Assault Carrier drops, hovering Close-Support / Hospital Corvettes, and hammer-and-anvil ground sweeps.

- Allied & Vassal Doctrinal Adaptation: Imperial Legion instructors export modified, lower-tier versions of this doctrine—built around Helios-2/5/8 Exo-Rigs, commercial BMUS-H2/H5/H8 under-suits, and Bōei-series Combat Automata—to solve demographic bottlenecks for regional allies (South Korea and Taiwan) and equip Vassal Clan Planetary Defense Forces (Hanchō Bōei-Gun) without leaking sovereign Solaris-9 secrets.

Engaging a manual waist-catch feeds mechanical torque into the outer exosuit's lower-limb joint linkages—allowing a warrior to march and fight at 8–11 km/h even if their Solaris core is damaged or manually scrammed via Hearth-Quench.
- Hydro-Fluidic Thermal Regulation: Breath- and stride-driven micro-bellows circulate thermal transfer fluid into quick-swap phase-change heat cartridges slotted beneath the outer armor plates (swappable in under 30 seconds), keeping the operator safe up to 95°C internal cockpit temperatures during atmospheric re-entry or sustained Energy Lance fire.
- Shock Buffering & Metabolic Sustainment: Non-Newtonian shear-thickening impact bladders over the spine, collarbones, ribs, and joints solidify instantaneously upon ballistic or landing shock, while dynamic skeletal orthotic bracing, forward-osmosis hydration bladders, and transdermal lactic-acid/amino buffer strips sustain peak combat endurance across 24–36 hour deployments.

- Vassal Clan & Allied Export Exo-Rigs (Helios-2 / Helios-5 / Helios-8 Tier): Clean, angular composite-alloy armor stripped of sovereign neutronium-carbide alloys, golden-amber shear barriers, and podless re-entry capability, worn over commercial polymer BMUS-H2/H5/H8 under-suits and powered by hard-capped Helios-2 (25%), Helios-5 (35%), or Helios-8C (50%) micro-cores with standard spherical kinetic bubble barriers. - Extraction-Only Recovery Shuttles: Heavy gravitic recovery shuttles embarked aboard Assault Carriers are never risked during contested orbital drops; they descend solely after ground sectors are secured to ferry surviving personnel, automata, and Oni-Mecha back to orbit under 750m Interceptor Cruiser escort.

9. HISTORICAL DOCTRINAL EVOLUTION (2122–2142+)
- Pre-War Era (2122–2132 — Solaris-4 & BMUS-4): Legions numbered 2,500,000 (500,000 humans + 2,000,000 Ashigaru-4 and Ō-Yoroi-4 automata; no Oni-Mecha yet). Because Gin-Musha-4 exosuits capped at 45% mass-shedding with early faceted directional barriers, troops deployed via armored drop-shuttles rather than podless orbital jumps, relying heavily on the inner BMUS-4 under-suit to absorb inertial shock.
- Mid-War Era (2134–2136 — Solaris-7 & BMUS-7): Conformal golden-amber shear barriers unlocked podless "Golden Rain" low-orbit insertions, Kensei-7 melee frames shattered Pact power-armor lines, and 4.5m Shien-Oni-7 / 6.0m Totsugeki-Oni-7 walkers debuted in 2134–2135 to crush continental armor columns, and the Legions grew from 2,500,000 pre-war to about 10,000,000 by 2135.
- Post-War Era (2142+ — Solaris-9 & BMUS-9): Perfected 75% mass-shedding standard across 25,000,000 Legion troops, 250,000 Oni-Mecha (adding the anti-air Tenkū-Oni), 500,000 bio-augmented Shock Samurai, and 100,000 Crimson Banner Hatamoto Guard.
