### THREE-ERA QUICK REFERENCE GUIDE: THE IRON AEGIS OF AMATERASU

DOCUMENT REF: QRF-001-IJN-ERAS

ISSUING AUTHORITY: SERIES MASTER BIBLE & CONTINUITY CONTROL

---

I. ERA I: THE CHRYSALIS YEARS (2122–2132 — PRE-WAR BUILDUP)

- Core Architecture: Solaris-4 Unified Gravitic Cores (45% maximum mass-shedding cap, 55% minimum apparent mass, 99.5% inertial dampening requiring heavy magnetic crash harnesses, 18 LY/day Sequential Spatial-Fold FTL, zero tactical micro-jumps, 4-hour thermal heat-sink stealth limit).
- Sovereign Seat & Geopolitics: Restored Chrysanthemum Throne in Kyoto (with Tokyo as commercial/maritime metropolis). Emperor Himura (born 2092; age 30 in 2122) is unaware he inhabits the Mass Effect universe; all policies—including the 2119 Anti-Smart-AI Edict (Quantum-Optical VIs only) and secret Crown Foundries—are driven by terrestrial survival and sovereign defense.
- Alliances & Non-Interference: Taiwan and South Korea are Imperial Japan's sole sovereign treaty allies. Hereditary Daimyō and Vassal Clan titles belong exclusively to Japanese noble houses on sovereign Japanese territory and Japanese space colonies; there is zero Imperial administration over China, Korea, or Taiwan.
- Military Strength:
  - Fleet: 236 hidden warships (85 Household Vanguard + 15 Kinki-Kyoku/Shadow + 136 Imperial Navy; largest hull is the 1,000m Flagship IJN Amaterasu-no-Yaiba).
  - Ground Forces: 2,500,000 standing troops (500,000 human commanders/specialists + 2,000,000 Ashigaru-4 / Kikai-Bushi automata across 25 Legions).
- Export Tech: Early commercial Helios-2 reactors (25% mass-shedding cap, Kinki-Kyoku blackboxed).

---

II. ERA II: THE IRON TEMPEST (2132–2136 — WORLD WAR III)

- Core Architecture: Mid-war transition (2134) from Solaris-4 to Solaris-7 Unified Gravitic Cores (60% mass-shedding cap, 40% minimum apparent mass, seamless golden-amber External Shear Barriers, Single-Fold Tactical Micro-Jumps up to 100,000 km).
- Conflict Structure: Three-way global war triggered on October 14, 2132, when the neo-authoritarian Eurasian-Continental Pact (Moscow-Beijing) launches preemptive orbital and terrestrial strikes against Japan, South Korea, and Taiwan while rival Western blocs (UNAS and EU) clash with both Pact fleets and Imperial orbital quarantine cordons.
- Combat Operations & Sovereign Restraint:
  - Void War: Fleet Admiral Togo Kazuhiko's Frigate-Corvette wolfpacks (1:4 at the opening, restructured to 1:2) execute Kage-Ōkami standoff torpedo ambushes using Type-90 Kage-Ya torpedoes, while Solaris-7 cruisers neutralize UNAS/EU orbital projection yards at Puget Sound and Kourou without continental occupation.
  - Ground War: Acting strictly upon South Korea's formal treaty request for aid, General Yamashita Takeo leads podless "Golden Rain" autonomous orbital drops—deploying Kikai-Bushi automata and giant Oni-Kikai heavy mecha alongside South Korean forces to liberate the Korean Peninsula and smash Pact supply bases in Manchuria. The 25 Legions field about 10,000,000 troops at wartime strength.
- The 2136 Imperial Settlement: Following the Pact's 2136 collapse, Emperor Himura orders an immediate, complete withdrawal of all Imperial ground forces from Korea and post-Pact China back to sovereign Japanese soil, announces a ceiling of exactly 1,500 warships (built out by about 2142), and licenses Helios-2 (25%) and Helios-5 (35%) reactors to rebuild Earth.

---

III. ERA III: BEYOND THE CHARON GATE (2136–2150+ — POST-WAR & FIRST CONTACT THRESHOLD)

- Core Architecture: Solaris-9 Unified Gravitic Cores (first ignition 2137, first combat use 2140, standard baseline from 2142; 75% physical mass-shedding hard limit, 25% minimum apparent mass, full inertial dampening, 10,000m Throne-Citadel IJN Amaterasu mounting 12 Axial Singularity Cannons).
- Cosmic Realization (2148–2149): Upon the 2148 discovery of the Mars Prothean Archives (Element Zero / Mass Effect physics) and the 2149 activation of the Charon Mass Relay by Admiral Jon Grissom (age 37), Emperor Himura (age 57, biologically late 20s via Tier I Chōju) finally realizes he inhabits the Mass Effect universe.
- The 2149 Kyoto-Arcturus Concordat: Negotiated in Kyoto with Systems Alliance envoy Ambassador Anita Goyle:
  - The Systems Alliance (SSV-) polices wider human extra-solar space using warships powered by commercially licensed, Kinki-Kyoku-blackboxed Imperial Helios-8 cores (50% mass-shedding cap).
  - The Empire of Japan—alongside sole allies Taiwan and South Korea—retains absolute sovereign jurisdiction over Japanese territory, Crown worlds, Japanese Vassal Clan garden colonies, and dormant mass relays bordering Imperial space (quarantined by Henkyō-Kyoku IJN-KNS-SP Interceptor Cruisers).
- Military Strength (Post-War Cap):
  - Fleet: Exactly 1,500 active IJN- warships once built out (about 2142) (500 Imperial Guard + 750 Main Starfleet + 185 Shock Corps + 50 Compliance Directorate + 15 Exploration Fleet; structured in 1:2:4 battle lines and 1:2 wolfpacks).
  - Ground Forces: 25,000,000 standing troops (5,000,000 humans + 20,000,000 Samurai-Form Automata + 250,000 Oni-Mecha across 25 Armies/Legions, plus 100,000 Hatamoto, 500,000 Shock Corps, 12,000 Audit-Marshals, and 10,000 Gravitic Shinobi).
- Galactic Scope Lock: No living aliens appear on the page and the Citadel appears nowhere through 2150+ (the First Contact War occurs in 2157).
