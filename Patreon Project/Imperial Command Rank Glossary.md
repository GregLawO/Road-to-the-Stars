# UNIFIED IMPERIAL COMMAND RANK & TITLE GLOSSARY (2157)

## I. Architectural Overview & Cultural Boundary

The **Solaris Empire** maintains a strict **Public vs. Private Cultural Boundary** established by the **Emperor of Clan Himura** (an American past-life *StarCraft* Protoss player and weeaboo reborn into the deposed *Taira / Heike* imperial bloodline):

1. **Outward-Facing Public Fleet & Line Forces (`Anglophone / High-Gothic`):** Because the **Imperial Starfleet**, **Iron Order**, **Imperial Compliance Directorate (`ICD-`)**, and **Automata Legions** govern, patrol, and police the entire Sol System publicly, they utilize *Battlefleet Gothic*-inspired **Anglophone / High-Gothic** titles and Roman-Protoss legionary ranks.
2. **Inward-Facing Crown, Palace & Shadow Forces (`Japanese / Taira Tradition`):** Because the **Imperial *Hatamoto* Guard**, and **Gravitic Shinobi** are private, sacred institutions serving **Clan Himura's** restored *Taira / Heike* throne away from public administration, they retain ancestral Japanese titles.
3. **House Shepard Exclusion Rule (`Purely Corporate`):** **House Shepard** holds **zero military ranks, noble peerages of command, or political offices** within the Empire. They operate strictly as private executives of **Shepard Enterprises** (`SEV` hull prefix), the Crown-backed prime megacorporation manufacturing hulls and hardware under exclusive patent licenses owned by **Clan Himura**.

## IV. Specialized Branch Glossaries

### A. The Imperial Guard (`INS` & *Hatamoto* — Japanese Tradition)

* ***Sō-Taishō* (Imperial Guard General):** Highest-ranking military officer in the Empire, answering solely to the **Emperor of Clan Himura** and commanding both the 1,200-ship Throneside Fleet and the 100,000-strong **Imperial *Hatamoto* Guard**.
* ***Kantai-Bugyō* (Throneside Fleet Commissioner):** Senior *Taira*-lineage flag officer commanding the Custodian battle-squadrons guarding the Imperial Throneworld, Luna, and the Sovereign-Class Bastions.
* ***Hatamoto-Bugyō* (Commissioner of the Inner Sanctum):** Commander of the **Imperial *Hatamoto* Guard** in crimson-and-black *ō-yoroi* armor, personally responsible for the physical security of the Imperial Family and the Crown Patent Vaults.
* ***Kenshi* (Sworn Guard Blade):** Elite biological warrior of the *Hatamoto* Guard wielding mono-molecular *ōdachi* and gravitic-assisted power armor under the restored **Taira Crimson Banner (*Akahata*)**.

### B. Imperial Starfleet (`INS` — Anglophone / High-Gothic Tradition)

* **Lord High Admiral:** Supreme operational commander of the outward-facing **Imperial Starfleet** (`1,520` line warships), executing Imperial strategic directives across the Sol System.
* **Solar Admiral:** Commander of a numbered **Imperial Armada** (such as the Jovian, Saturnian, or Inner-System Armadas).
* **Shipmaster / Captain:** Commanding officer of a capital warship (`Battleship`, `Grand Cruiser`, `Fleet Carrier`, `Citadel Dreadnought`, or `Sovereign-Class Bastion`), seated within the armored internal Bridge Citadel.
* **Void-Chief:** Senior enlisted specialist overseeing **Macrocannon** turret batteries, **Energy Lance** capacitors, or **Solaris Unified Gravitic Core** containment.

### C. Iron Order (`IOV`) & Imperial Compliance Directorate (`ICD-`)

* **Grand Master of the Order:** Supreme leader of the **Iron Order** (`605` warships and `75,000` Siege-Plated **Shock Knights**), specializing in high-intensity planetary assaults and orbital fortress breaches.
* **Lord High Censor:** Head of the **Imperial Compliance Directorate (`ICD-`)**, reporting directly to the **Emperor of Clan Himura** to audit **Shepard Enterprises** shipyards, noble houses, and frontier colonies for **Solaris** patent leaks or Anti-Smart-AI violations.
* **Chief Audit-Marshal / Audit-Marshal:** Overt legal enforcement officers (`12,000` total) empowered to declare orbital customs quarantines, seize unlicensed hardware, and convene summary tribunals.
* ***Kage-Kampaku* (Shadow Regent):** Operational head of the **10,000 Gravitic Shinobi**, the covert *metsuke*-inspired intelligence arm of the Compliance Directorate operating in phase-cloaked stealth suits.
