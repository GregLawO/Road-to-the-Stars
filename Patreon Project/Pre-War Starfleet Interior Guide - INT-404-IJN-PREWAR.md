### PRE-WAR IMPERIAL WARSHIP INTERIOR & ATMOSPHERE GUIDE

DOCUMENT REF: INT-404-IJN-PREWAR

Hull classes, cores, the sunken bridge, automata alcoves, the shrine-box and the 1:4 packs are covered in the Technology file. This file keeps the sensory setting only.

---

1. OVERALL LOOK
Armored naval steel meets aristocratic Japan. Corridors follow the hull's angular lines, narrowing toward the prow, braced by dark-gunmetal ribs, heavy blast doors and matte-black anti-spall bulkheads. Fiber-optic conduits run in bundled overhead trays and recessed amber-gold strips cast low warm light over brushed steel deck plates. Intersections carry dark-cedar and brushed-brass plaques with the Himura crest. Officers' spaces use frosted-glass shōji-style partitions and tatami-textured matting.

2. COMMAND BRIDGE (Kanbō)
A dim, tiered pit with a raised dark-lacquered captain's chair flanked by katana racks, facing a floating golden-amber tactical globe. Officers in dark-indigo, crimson-trimmed uniforms man sunken stations. In hard turns the crew feels a heavy gravitational tug through magnetic harnesses. VI terminals show cascading golden-amber kanji and chime a single bronze bell tone at target lock.

3. WAR ROOM (Sakusen-Shitsu)
A sound-baffled planning chamber aft of the bridge with a long dark-lacquered table projecting a star map. Frosted partitions show enemy dispositions, and armed Ashigaru-4 stand motionless beside the blast doors.

4. CAPTAIN'S READY ROOM (Kanchō-Shitsu)
Woven tatami, cedar paneling, a tokonoma alcove with a scroll and an armor stand, and an electric cast-iron kettle for green tea between watches.

5. REACTOR SANCTUM (Chūsū-Shitsu)
A matte-black core hangs in a magnetic vacuum pit behind triple-interlocked blast doors, with copper-gold phase conduits pulsing slow amber light. On the catwalk the crew feels a sub-audible bass in teeth and sternum, and the air smells of ozone, cold machine oil and coolant. The locked crimson-and-brass shrine-box holds the Hearth-Quench and Hearth-Collapse keys, available only to the captain and chief engineer.

6. GUNNERY AND TORPEDO DECKS
Cavernous bays of copper-wound accelerator rails, hydraulic breeches and gantry cranes. Reloads slam slugs or torpedoes into place with deafening clangs, in hot lubricant vapor and sharp ozone.

7. LAUNCH AND BOARDING BAYS (Hasshin-Kanpan)
Scuffed deck plates with magnetic landing strips and amber beacons, smelling of cold vacuum, hydraulic fluid and scorched paint, with exosuit racks beside the airlock chutes.

8. CORRIDORS
Two meters wide on corvettes and frigates, so crew turn sideways past dormant automata in wall alcoves. Their slits glow a low ember. At general quarters hundreds flare golden-amber together with a synchronized clack of unlocking clamps.

9. INFIRMARY (Imu-Shitsu)
Armored near the hull's center, bright white-and-steel with surgical arms, stasis cradles and regeneration tanks. Cold antiseptic air and blue-white panels contrast with the amber of the rest of the ship.

10. QUARTERS, MESS AND KAMIDANA
Sailors sleep in folding triple-tiered bunks, and junior officers share two-berth cabins. Long steel mess tables serve rice, miso soup, grilled fish and green tea in lacquerware. A cedar-paneled kamidana alcove near the officer deck holds a Shinto shrine, the battle honors scroll and the crimson-and-gold Himura standard.

11. THE BRIG AND SEALED VAULTS (Rōgoku / Fūin-Shitsu)
On larger cruisers and especially Kinki-Kyoku and Shadow Squadron ships: dim crimson corridors, seamless cell doors with narrow viewing slits and magnetic locks. Every vault is wired into the ship's self-destruct circuit.

12. BRANCH FLAVOR
- **Household Vanguard (IJN-KSH-):** hereditary Himura retainers, dark red lacquer and polished brass, ancestral crests, and on the flagship tatami dais seating and a private war room.
- **Kinki-Kyoku and Shadow Squadron (IJN-KNK- / IJN-KGE-):** dim red lighting, sealed black-box vault racks, quiet intelligence officers and matte-black Kage-4 infiltration automata. (The Technology file names the infiltration frame Onmitsu.)
- **Imperial Navy (IJN-DNK-):** crisp, disciplined and practical, focused on drill and gunnery.
