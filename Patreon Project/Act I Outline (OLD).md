## Movement 1: The Three Doors

A1 (the body) is live and unchanged.

**The A2 plan in plain terms (author ruling).**
1. **The problem (2051).** Kenji sees that selling his inventions will make him powerful enemies, even cost him his life. Before he sells anything, he builds a safety net.
2. **The Level 2 Med-Pod (2051).** Heals a dying person fully, rejuvenates, slows aging for a lifetime. Only Level 2 does any of this. A cruder Primitive grade cures disease and injury.
3. **The protectors (summer 2051 to 2055).** Veterans and experts serve him for life, a life given for a life, in exchange for being restored. Before they agree he tells them about a loyalty clause that keeps them from telling outsiders. Order: Vance (summer 2051), then Ashby, then the lawyers (October 2051), then a larger team, then Byron (July 2052). Whitcombe joins February 2053.
4. **The fronts (summer 2053).** Three clinics through a charity foundation called Janus: **Galveston, Marseille, and Singapore** (decision). Public floors run the Primitive pods Oyama makes; patients are asked to keep the true process secret. Hidden wings run Level 2, for recruits and later paying elites, and every Level 2 treatment seals the patient's memory of who, how, where, when, and why, leaving only a contact person (decision, 5 November 2026). **The fronts also sell cloned organs, pre-approved through the network's political faction.**
5. **The elites (from spring 2055).** Through Whitcombe's factions he sells health and youth to people who will pay a great deal, using the sales to buy favor and influence. Politicians get health and long life but not youth. Rich private clients who can leave public life get full rejuvenation (decision). Every buyer understands without a word that he holds the bigger stick. The treatment also seals their memory.
6. **The Shepards (2053).** Danny Shepard asks him to save Danny's dying adoptive father, Tom. This is how Kenji meets the Shepards.
7. **Time passes (2053 to 2058).** The organization grows. He gets to know Hannah, they marry in June 2058, and he spreads his inventions through Oyama and shell companies.
8. **Good protection is not complete safety.** His people are human and make mistakes. A dedicated enemy willing to take losses can still reach him and the people he loves.

**How the realization begins.** **Wave 1 (October 2058 to 2064).** A short list of names that sound familiar. It starts with the name Cerberus, which is also an old myth, so a vague feeling is natural. Later the list holds company names: Hahne-Kedar, Ariake, and Sirta in 2063, and nine names by 2064. He cannot say where he heard them.

### A2a: the safety net (2051 to early 2053). Every chapter builds the foundation and introduces a new main-cast member.

1. **A2.1 Kenji (31), Texas City, 2051 [I] · B. The Med-Pod, the problem, and the plan.** About 1,000 words, first person. He builds no Primitive pod.
   - **Opening detail.** He is seating the last gauge on the finished Level 2 Med-Pod in the back bay of Delgado's tire shop when Delgado comes in the back door with his injured dog in his arms. The build diagram is pinned on the wall with MED-POD across the top.
   - **The build (Slow Gear, short).** A long steel cradle under a clear lid, with a tank made from a scrapped pressure vessel. Almost all parts came from a scrapper he built; his yard wages paid for the rest. It is exactly the Med-Pod from the film Elysium. He knows how it works down to every weld and setting, but not how or why he knows. He woke in March 2050 in a flat three streets away, in a thirty-year-old body not his, and told no one. He built an automated scrapper and left it in a junkyard to collect metal. Next a Phased Disassembler Array, a very crude version, slow at breaking scrap into base elements, then the tools that make the tools. Last, he rented the bay from Delgado and built the pod. No Reaper thought and no thought that this world was once a story.
   - **The test.** Delgado's dog Pancho was hit by a truck that morning, hip broken in two places. The vet wants twenty-two hundred dollars or will put him down. Kenji says keep the hip level, the cycle takes ninety minutes. The dog walks out and runs the bay. Delgado asks nothing about the machine and thanks him at the door.
   - **The problem.** Kenji washes his hands twice and looks at the pod. It rebuilds a body from the inside, and with the dials reversed makes a person younger. Every government, hospital chain, drug company, and criminal will want it. Whoever finds out will take the pod and the man who built it.
   - **The plan.** He writes on the back of the diagram. Step one: protectors. Step two: a factory. He will pay for protectors with the pod: anyone dying or broken comes out whole and swears themselves to him, with a failsafe for loyalty added during treatment. Two columns: Primitive (what he intends to share, a cruder machine curing disease and injury that the world could have built without him; he will not build it himself) and Level 2 (what he keeps: heals completely, makes young again, slows aging for half a lifetime). He pins the sheet back with the plan facing out.
   - **The terms.** Service is for life, a life given for a life (author ruling, 4 November 2026).
   - **Hook (B).** The first name on his list is Dominic Vance, a retired Marine gunnery sergeant dying of ruined lungs in a Houston hospice. On the first Monday of July 2051 he stands in the corridor outside Vance's room, with a rented van and gurney at the curb. In a minute he will ask a dying man to trust a stranger with what is left of his life.

He found them because both went on medical leave from the same firm in the same month, and the firm builds charitable foundations. He needs several, in different countries, to own the things he will design. She asks who owns them and who signs. A trustee signs. He gives the name "Janus." He tells them about a session that stops them from telling anyone outside the organization; they will know it but not be able to say it. She calls it a clause she cannot read, amend, or waive. Ortega, whose daughter turns nine in March, tells her to sign. She writes down that she was told first, and dates it. When she insists on keeping it, Kenji says he will find someone else, and she tears the page up and signs.

Kenji arrives at nine with the same two men and reads the service contract standing. He stops at the clause that keeps treatments going for as long as someone serves.
   - **Hook (Q).** She asks what happens to everyone's contract if he dies. He says Janus holds them and asks her to be trustee. She says a trustee can refuse him. He says he would find another trustee. He chose her because she asked about his death before the money, and will read a page to the end. She writes *trustee* in the margin beside her own name.
