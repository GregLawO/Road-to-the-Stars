### IMPERIAL GROUND & ORBITAL ASSAULT DOCTRINE (RIKUSEN & KŌKA SENTŌ KYŌRI)

DOCUMENT REF: DOC-885-IJN-GROUND

Legion structure, the 1:4 fireteam and ranks are in Characters and Ranks (section 8.3). Pre-war automata and suits are in the Technology file. This file covers the war and post-war doctrine.

---

1. PHILOSOPHY
- **Preserve Japanese lives.** After the 2122 demographic decline, living Japanese serve as command, tactical leadership and elite shock units, while deterministic automata absorb frontline attrition.
- **Human command over machine mass.** Every formation is led by living officers in armor directing four automata through air-gapped tight-beam infrared and ultraviolet links.
- **No wireless receivers.** No automaton, Oni-Mecha or exosuit carries RF or Wi-Fi, so formations cannot be hijacked or spoofed.
- **Vertical integration.** Ground forces never fight cut off from orbit. Starfleet orbital supremacy, carrier drops, hovering Close-Support Corvettes and hammer-and-anvil sweeps work as one.
- **Allied and vassal doctrine.** Imperial instructors export a weaker doctrine built on Helios-tier suits and automata to South Korea, Taiwan and the vassal militias, without leaking Solaris secrets.

2. POST-WAR FORCE (about 2142)
- 25 Legions, with the post-war scale in the Registry. 250,000 Oni-Mecha (10,000 per Legion), 100,000 Hatamoto Guard, 500,000 Shock Corps Samurai, 12,000 Audit-Marshals and 10,000 Gravitic Shinobi.
- **Hatamoto Guard:** sworn hereditary retainers in lacquered crimson-and-black armor guarding the Imperial Family and Throneside citadels.
- **Shock Corps:** bio-augmented samurai in matte-black and burnt-iron armor, operating in fast assault squads from Interceptor Cruisers.
- **Audit-Marshals:** Directorate inquisitors in Deep Imperial Indigo and Bright Gold, enforcing the Edict and patent quarantines.
- **Gravitic Shinobi:** covert reconnaissance, sabotage and assassination specialists in midnight-indigo weaves with personal Eclipse cloaking.

3. FIRETEAMS
- **Regular fireteam:** one living NCO with four automata. One heavy-weapons Bushi, one marksman Bushi, and two Kensei melee frames that sprint and leap to close with gravitic-shearing blades under overwatch.
- **Officer command fireteam:** a living officer with a signals and electronic-warfare Bushi, a close-protection Kensei and two heavy Ō-Yoroi shield-wall frames.

4. ONI-FORM MECHA (Oni-Kikai)
Sculpted as demons in golden-bronze and obsidian-charcoal plating with horned sensor heads and amber fangs, dropped podless from orbit.
- **Shien-Oni (4.5m):** support and electronic-warfare walker with twin autocannons, a Kozuka micro-torpedo pod and a 25-meter shield umbrella over advancing fireteams.
- **Totsugeki-Oni (6.0m):** heavy walker-killer with a 45mm mass-accelerator cannon, a Heavy Energy Lance and a Kanabō or nodachi for melee.
- **Tenkū-Oni (7.5m):** post-war anti-air and theater-shield walker with flak cannons, a Sky-Lance battery and a 150-meter shear canopy.

5. PODLESS "GOLDEN RAIN" INSERTION
- **Phase 1.** The fleet secures orbit, then Assault Carriers descend to low orbit under Interceptor Cruiser escort.
- **Phase 2.** Warriors, automata and Oni-Mecha step out of ventral bays with no pods, using conformal shear barriers against re-entry plasma while the under-suit sheds heat, and shed apparent mass to land silently like golden meteors.
- **Phase 3.** Fireteams land already oriented on target and begin hammer-and-anvil advances within seconds.
- **Recovery.** Extraction shuttles are never risked in contested drops and descend only after the ground is secured.

6. ATMOSPHERIC CLOSE SUPPORT AND MEDEVAC
- Imperial Legions field no ground artillery. Two Close-Support / Hospital Corvettes per carrier hover over advancing Divisions with Macrocannon, Energy Lance and Kozuka fire.
- When an officer or NCO is critically wounded, an armored skiff drops from the Corvette and delivers them to its 40-bed Quantum-Optical surgical hospital within 90 seconds.
- Wartime prototypes (2135) hover only at 5 to 10 km (decision).

7. TACTICS
- **Open field, hammer and anvil.** Tenkū-Oni lock down airspace, Shien-Oni and Ō-Yoroi advance as the anvil while Bushi pin the enemy, then Kensei and Totsugeki-Oni slam into the flanks.
- **Urban.** Heavy bombardment is restricted, so Onmitsu automata and Gravitic Shinobi infiltrate tunnels ahead of the advance while Shock Corps samurai clear high-rises vertically.
- **Subterranean fortress.** Corvettes punch ventilation shafts with Twin Energy Lance beams and Shock Corps drop into the command levels.

8. PLANETARY DEFENSE
- Home Islands and Crown worlds: buried planetary shear-shield generators, automated orbital defense platforms and regular IJN-TNU- garrison divisions.
- Vassal garden colonies: militias with export-tier suits and Bōei automata, geofenced to planetary defense while IJN-TNU- wolfpacks hold the sky above.
