### LOCATION ATLAS (2132 to 2150+)

DOCUMENT REF: LOC-001-IJN-ATLAS

Kyoto-Heian Citadel, the Kii-Yamato Core-Forge, the lunar-shadow slipway, Seoul, Taipei and the Proscribed Vault Sanctuaries are covered by the Technology, Timeline and Factions files and are not repeated here.

---

PART I: PRE-WAR WESTERN AND PACT LOCATIONS (to 2132)

1. **NORAD-Orbital Command & Cheyenne Mountain (Colorado, UNAS).** Williams and UNAS orbital intelligence track Japanese hulls over the Pacific, increasingly alarmed by reactionless accelerations.
2. **Brussels Strategic Directorate & Kourou Equatorial Lift (EU).** Brussels drafts sanctions while analysts track Japanese tonnage from Lunar Station Copernicus. Kourou is the EU's equatorial space-elevator tether in French Guiana.
3. **Baikonur-Orbital & Vladivostok Pacific Bastion (Pact).** Nuclear-thermal drydocks and coastal missile fortresses where Pact commanders build dreadnoughts and kinetic-rod platforms.
4. **The Earth-Luna Dark Corridor & Lunar Station Copernicus.** The unlit void between geostationary orbit and Luna. Shadow Squadron and Kinki-Kyoku cruisers hunt foreign probes there while Western analysts watch impossible tracks over Japan.

---

PART II: RECLAMATION WAR LOCATIONS (2132 to 2136)

1. **The Earth-Luna debris ring and Tycho Crater bunker.** A graveyard of pulverized hulls where Togo's wolfpacks ambush and the later micro-jump lines break the blockade.
2. **The Korean Peninsula and Manchurian fronts.** Smoke-choked valleys and frozen rail hubs where Yamashita's Golden Rain drops break the Pact's armies before the Imperial withdrawal.
3. **The Taiwan Strait and Western Pacific bastions.** Storm-lashed sea lanes lit by Energy Lance beams and incoming rods.
4. **The Vladivostok and Sea of Japan crucible.** Missile fortresses and submarine pens neutralized by wolfpacks and hovering Close-Support Corvettes.
5. **The Bering Strait and North Pacific front.** Where Jon Grissom (20 to 24) watches Imperial cruisers pivot and micro-jump.
6. **Puget Sound.** Rain-swept drydocks struck to end UNAS orbital projection without a landing.
7. **Baikonur and the Urals.** Dreadnought yards and kinetic-rod rings shattered by O-Yumi salvos.
8. **Kourou ruins.** The severed EU tether, steaming jungle wreckage.
9. **Wartime orbital forges and Manchurian ruins.** Where Jack Harper (about 20 to 24) sees Gravitic Shinobi and Lock-carrying officers in action, starting his obsession with Solaris and Chōju secrets.

---

PART III: POST-WAR AND CHARON RELAY LOCATIONS (2136 to 2150+)

1. **The 10,000m Throne-Citadel IJN Amaterasu.** Cedar-and-gold throne halls inside a naval fortress, commanded by Togo. Seat of the 1,500-ship ceiling and of envoys such as Ambassador Goyle.
2. **The Takamagahara Orbital Shipyard Ring (geostationary above Kyoto).** Crown slipways build Solaris-9 hulls beside automated modules making export Helios drives.
3. **Reunified Seoul, Taipei and the independent Chinese republics.** Rebuilding with no Imperial garrisons or Governors-General.
4. **London, Buenos Aires, Geneva and Arcturus Station.** Where young Steven Hackett and David Anderson grow up, and humanity's first extra-solar fleet hub built by Drescher and Grissom on licensed Helios cores.
5. **Promethean Station (Mars, 2148) and the Charon Relay quarantine perimeter (2149+).** The Prothean dig that inspires Alec Ryder, and the dark edge of Sol where Grissom crosses the relay while Henkyō-Kyoku cruisers watch dormant relays.
6. **Vassal Clan garden colonies, Ame-no-Iwato and outer-belt black-ops labs.** Garden colonies run by Japanese noble houses under IJN-TNU- patrols. Ame-no-Iwato is a fortified Crown world holding Vault Sanctuaries and the base for the Exploration squadrons. In the belt, Harper (about 37) and Lawson (about 35) fund raids on Helios shipments and run illegal genetic work that produces Miranda Lawson in 2150.
