# TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION SHEET: THE POWER SUIT (REACTOR-POWERED)

Classification: Private design. Not sold.
System Status: Not yet in service

## I. OVERVIEW

The reactor-powered Power Suit is a two-part suit. An inner muscle suit is worn against the body, and an outer powered armor is worn over it. A mini-reactor in the armor powers both parts. The inner suit adds muscle power to the wearer's own movements, and the outer armor carries the weight and protects the body.

## II. OPERATION

The wearer puts on the inner suit first, then steps into the outer armor, which closes over it. The mini-reactor runs the joints, the inner suit, and every other system. The wearer controls the suit by moving. The armor carries the weight down its legs to the ground.

## III. FUNCTIONALITY

1. **Load carrying.** The armor takes the weight of the wearer and of the load through its legs to the ground.
2. **Muscle assist.** The inner suit gives the wearer extra muscle power on every movement.
3. **Armor.** The outer armor protects the wearer's body. Rated NIJ RF3 (Level IV). The helmet resists rifle fire, and a bullet that strikes it glances off.
4. **Jump.** An anti-gravity unit in the armor takes weight off the wearer. The reactor supplies it continuously, so jumps have no cooldown and no limit.
5. **Hover and flight.** The anti-gravity unit holds the wearer in the air and moves them up to 10 meters above the ground.
6. **Orbital jump.** The wearer jumps from the ground to orbit, and the anti-gravity unit slows the descent so the wearer lands safely.
7. **Energy-dissipating shield (introduced later).** A shield dissipates incoming energy before it reaches the wearer, up to a fixed limit. Once the limit is reached, the shield breaks.
8. **Heavy energy weapons.** The suit powers a lasgun, which is introduced later in the story, or a lascannon on a shoulder mount.
9. **Hardpoints.** The armor carries mounting points for a back rack for weapons, a combat knife, extra armor, and ammunition bags. A large hardpoint on each elbow connects the suit to vehicles and machinery.
10. **Magnetic soles.** Each boot carries a magnetic sole that grips metal surfaces, including in low gravity.
11. **Sneak mode.** The wearer can switch the suit to silence its footsteps.
12. **Helmet.** Four cameras on the visor feed a display on the inside of the visor, with a heads-up readout. A light is mounted on each side. The helmet can be linked to the weapon systems of a vehicle or ship.
13. **Fit.** The inner suit and outer armor come in male and female builds. The female build is more streamlined.
14. **Standard parts.** Every part is made to one standard, so a part from one suit fits any other suit.
15. **Personalization.** Each wearer may paint their armor in their own color, pattern, or lettering, and add a personal emblem. Maker's marks are never allowed.

## IV. TECHNOLOGIES EMPLOYED

1. **Inner muscle suit.** A close-fitting layer that adds muscle power to the wearer's own movements.
2. **Outer powered armor.** A shell worn over the inner suit. It holds the reactor, the joints, the hardpoints, and the helmet.
3. **Mini-reactor.** A small reactor in the outer armor that powers every part of the suit.
4. **Anti-gravity unit.** The private grade of anti-gravity, supplied by the reactor.
5. **Energy-dissipating shield.** A shield powered by the reactor.
6. **Magnetic soles.** Boot soles that grip metal.
7. **Helmet sensors.** Visor cameras, display, and heads-up readout.

## V. LIMITS

- Hover and flight: up to 10 meters.
- Shield: a fixed limit on the energy it can dissipate, then it breaks.

## VI. SAFEGUARDS

- **Sale.** Never sold. The design is never released.
- **Marking.** No plate, no maker's name, and no serial a stranger could read.
- **Supply.** The armor is cast from stock the Phased Disassembler Arrays produce.
