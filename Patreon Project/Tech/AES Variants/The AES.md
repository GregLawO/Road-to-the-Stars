# TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION SHEET: THE ARMORED EXOSKELETAL SUIT (AES)

Classification: Public build, sold by Oyama Industries.
System Status: Active

## I. OVERVIEW

The Armored Exoskeletal Suit (AES) is an unpowered steel exoskeleton with armor. It straps over the wearer's clothes and carries the weight of the wearer and of everything the wearer lifts, passing that weight down its legs to the ground. It lifts five times what a person can lift alone.

## II. OPERATION

The wearer steps into the frame and straps in at the legs, hips, and torso. The wearer moves and the frame moves with them. A load lifted by the wearer goes down the frame's legs and into the floor instead of into the wearer's spine. The joints tick as they take weight.

## III. FUNCTIONALITY

1. **Load carrying.** The frame takes the weight of the wearer and of the load through its legs to the ground.
2. **Lift multiplication.** 5 times a normal person's capacity, up to a maximum of 250 kg (551 lb).
3. **Armor.** Steel plate over the frame protects the wearer's body. Rated NIJ RF2.
4. **Fit.** The frame is hinged at the hip and knee and straps over ordinary work clothes.

## IV. TECHNOLOGIES EMPLOYED

1. **Steel exoskeleton frame.** Hinged at the hip and knee. Cast and assembled by Oyama.
2. **Armor plate.** Steel plate fitted over the frame.
3. **Mechanical load path.** Linkages carry the load down the legs to the ground.

## V. LIMITS

- Maximum lift: 250 kg (551 lb).
- Protection: NIJ RF2.

## VI. SAFEGUARDS

- **Sale.** Sold openly, per unit, delivered.
- **Marking.** Oyama's plate carries Oyama's name and a blank Designer line.
- **Supply.** Oyama's plants cast the frames from stock the Phased Disassembler Arrays produce.

## VII. GUNDARIUM ALPHA BUILD

Once the material is available, Oyama also sells the AES with its frame and armor made of Gundarium Alpha, a strong, light structural alloy. It is lighter and stronger than the steel build.
