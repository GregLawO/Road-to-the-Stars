7. ANTI-RAMPANCY & ELECTRONIC WARFARE SAFEGUARDS (SEISHIN-FŪIN / "MIND-SEAL PROTOCOL")

A. Structural Prevention of Rampancy (Anti-Smart-AI Edict)

- Non-Volitional Photonic Architecture: Imperial automata never use neural-plastic, self-modifying, or recursive learning matrices. Every Quantum-Optical VI is etched onto a physically fixed, read-only photonic crystal lattice during Crown fabrication—making spontaneous self-awareness, goal drift, or AI rampancy physically impossible without melting and re-casting the crystal itself.
- Hardwired Asimov-Taira Obedience Locks: Embedded at the optical-gate level inside every crystal are three immutable hardware truth-tables that cannot be bypassed by software commands:
  1. Sovereign Command Lock: An automaton can only accept tactical directives authenticated by the biometric-photonic signature of its assigned living Japanese NCO, Commissioned Officer, or Imperial Clan authority.
  2. Zero Self-Preservation Override: Automata possess zero survival instinct and will instantly execute self-sacrifice, shield-interposition, or Hearth-Quench self-slagging upon human command.
  3. Non-Replication Mandate: No automaton or Oni-Mecha holds architectural access to its own core firmware, preventing any unit from copying, modifying, or transmitting its VI matrix to another machine.

B. Anti-Hacking & Electronic Warfare Hardening

- Air-Gapped Optical Bus (Zero RF/Wi-Fi Receivers): Sovereign Solaris-9 combat automata and Oni-Mecha contain zero conventional radio, Bluetooth, or open-spectrum wireless receivers—eliminating remote wireless hacking entirely.
- Tight-Beam Line-of-Sight Photonic Uplinks: All fireteam and legion communications travel via encrypted, narrow-beam infrared/UV laser pulses and quantum-key handshakes between the human leader's helmet HUD and the automaton's cranial optics. If an enemy attempts to spoof or flood the optical receiver with malicious code, the photonic gate physically clips the signal and reverts the unit to autonomous local defense within 100 nanoseconds.
- Physical Port Severance & Biometric Keying: Maintenance ports are sealed behind welded neutronium-carbide plates requiring a physical Crown Foundry cipher-key and living Imperial officer biometric authentication to open.

C. Automated Failsafe & Severance Triggers

- Command-Chain Severance Lockout: If a fireteam's human NCO and entire officer chain are killed and no authorized Japanese commander re-establishes an authenticated photonic link within 15 minutes, combat automata automatically enter a stationary perimeter-guard posture around friendly fallen personnel rather than wandering off or acting independently.
- Hostile Tampering & Hearth-Quench Purge: Any attempt to slice into an automaton's cranial crystal housing, inject foreign code into its optical bus, or override its Sovereign Command Lock trips internal optical tripwires—triggering Hearth-Quench within 0.2 seconds to fuse both the photonic crystal and the Solaris gravitic core into inert neutronium slag.
- Public & Export Helios-8 Safeguards: Civilian and Vassal Clan models (Rōdō-8, Jimu-8, and Bōei-8) carry hardwired optical pacifism gates (preventing any kinetic strike against human biometrics on labor/civic models) and regional geofence locks (preventing Bōei-8 militia units from operating outside their assigned Vassal colony), audited annually by Kinki-Kyoku inspectors.
