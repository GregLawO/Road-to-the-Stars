SECTION 1: MATERIAL CHARACTERISTICS & CELL SPECS

**A. Electro-Chemical Specifications**

- **Gravimetric Energy Density:** 850 Wh/kg (utilizing lightened sulfur-carbon composite cathodes, eliminating heavy cobalt/nickel dependencies).
- **Volumetric Energy Density:** 1,200 Wh/L. Packaged tightly within an ultra-thin structural matrix to minimize internal suit footprint.
- **Nominal Cell Voltage:** 2.2V per cell, configured in high-voltage series-parallel arrays to output a steady 650V bus to the suit's primary power grid.

**B. Thermal & Environmental Resilience**

- **Optimal Operating Range:** Maintained between 15°C and 35°C by the suit's internal thermoelectric cooling grid.
- **Thermal Runaway Threshold:** Critically stable up to 85°C. Structural phase transitions and self-venting safety valves prevent thermal runaway under this limit.
- **Extreme Sub-Zero Tolerance:** Maintains 85% efficiency down to -40°C due to the solid-state electrolyte bypassing traditional liquid freezing constraints.

SECTION 2: DISCHARGE & LIFECYCLE CAPABILITIES

**A. Power Output Dynamics**

- **Sustained Discharge Rate:** Delivers a continuous 135 kW output to efficiently manage standard scouting, lifecycle infrastructure, and basic locomotion.
- **Peak Surge Rating (Burst):** Capable of safely discharging up to 15 Megawatts (MW) for up to 3.0 seconds to satisfy the extreme voltage demands of gravitic core shifts or overcharged modular laser flashes.
- **Specialist Burst Ceiling:** The heavy variant array accommodates brief 50 MW surges specifically to feed the shoulder-mounted Heavy Beam-Cannon, though it incurs immediate thermal penalties.

**B. Degradation & Lifecycle Metrics**

- **Tactical Mission Lifespan:** Rated to deliver **48 hours of normal scouting operations or 24 hours of high-intensity combat operations** per charge cycle.
- **Cycle Life Tolerance:** Retains >90% initial capacity after 1,500 full depth-of-discharge (DoD) cycles before requiring standard depot-level cell refurbishment.
- **Self-Discharge Rate:** Less than 0.5% total capacity loss per month when stored in an unpowered, isolated state inside standard deployment crates.

SECTION 3: INTEGRATED BATTERY VULNERABILITIES

- **High-Current Degradation:** Engaging the 15-minute "Tactical Flash-Charge" on the field station induces localized copper-dendrite stress, reducing the cell's long-term cycle lifespan by 1.5% per event.
- **Sulfur Cathode Structural Swelling:** Microscopic volume expansion occurs during maximum rapid discharge phases. If the outer composite structural shell is physically cracked by kinetic penetration, moisture ingress will cause immediate internal shorting.
- **Total Depletion Interruption:** Allowing the matrix to drop below 0.5% total voltage completely de-authorizes the smart-grid micro-zoning firmware, requiring a hard factory restart at a dedicated logistical base.
