# Road to the Stars: Scene-by-Scene Outline, A1 to A12 (2130 to 2150+)

Covers every chapter before the First Contact War (2157). Each POV segment holds two or three dated scenes in one POV. Scene splits are (decision), open to overrule. A1 to A3 carry full scene beats. A4 to A12 stay close to the sketched casts.

---

## ERA I: THE EVE OF THE TEMPEST (2130 to 2132)

### A1. THE QUIET EDGE (2130 to 2131) \[Intrigue-weighted, 6 Intrigue + 4 Action\]

**Batch 1.** Opens with the excerpt "On the Quiet Decade," *Annals of the Chrysanthemum Restoration*.

**A1.1. Emperor Himura (33, 2130, Kyoto). Intrigue. Ending E.**

1. Himura reads Pacific threat telemetry at a worktable in the Kyoto-Heian Imperial Citadel, a smear of ink still on one knuckle.
2. Envoys from Seoul and Taipei finalize the mutual-defense protocols. He states the cost and the risk before anyone signs.
3. In council, Grand Uncle Masamune warns against provoking continental giants before crews are tested in space. Cousin Ryunosuke demands aggressive preemptive patrols. Himura keeps the patrol schedule, adds retraining drills, and refuses preemption.
4. After the meeting Masamune checks whether Himura has eaten and slips dried plums into his pocket.

**A1.2. Cruiser Captain, Shadow Squadron (generic OC, 750m IJN-KGE-SP, 2130). Action. Ending P.**

1. In lunar shadow under Eclipse stealth, the captain tracks unmarked Russian and CPF reconnaissance probes by passive sensors only.
2. An inexperienced helm officer forgets a 3D lunar-gravity drift vector. The hull slides toward a probe's optical cone while the thermal sinks run down.
3. The captain corrects the drift and orders a Twin Energy Lance shot through a Phase-Gated Firing Aperture in the faceted barrier. One probe is sliced apart. A second probe's sensor mast begins to turn toward the cruiser.

**A1.3. Empress Kaguya Himura (31, 2131, Throneside Medical Bureau, Kyoto). Intrigue. Ending E.**

1. Kaguya oversees the final pre-war Tier II Chōju immersions. Lady Shizuka reads out medical and foundry shift rosters beside her.
2. Grand Uncle Masamune declines treatment. He says an elder should step aside in his own time.
3. Kaguya watches senior commanders come out of the tanks younger, hungry, and quiet, and feeds them first.
4. She pours Masamune's tea before her own.

**A1.4. UNAS Orbital Intelligence Analyst (generic OC, 2131, Cheyenne Mountain). Intrigue. Ending Q.**

1. The analyst briefs General Williams on optical acceleration tracks and radar dropouts from Japanese hulls in lunar orbit.
2. Williams asks what the tracks show and gets only observed data. No exhaust, no drive signature.
3. Kyoto's diplomats bargain over steel quotas in the same week. Williams orders wider optical coverage. The analyst is left with a track no known drive can produce.

**A1.5. Lord Commander Haruka Tachibana (29, 2131, low-orbit sovereign shipyard). Action. Ending B.**

1. Tachibana walks Lady Shizuka's inspection route in a Gin-Musha exosuit, scanning hands and shadow angles.
2. A UNAS-funded sabotage team reaches a Solaris-4A conduit. In zero-G she engages her Solaris-4M Ember core, closing angles and distance.
3. She stops the team before the breach. One captured saboteur wears a yard worker's badge. Someone inside the yard let them in.

**Batch 2.** Opens with a recap of Batch 1.

**A1.6. Sovereign Taiwanese Naval Liaison (generic OC, 2131, Taipei). Intrigue. Ending Q.**

1. The liaison inspects treaty-supplied, Kinki-Kyoku-blackboxed Helios-2 shield emitters and finds them sealed beyond any technician's reach.
2. Taiwan Strait patrol grids are reworked with Imperial officers. The liaison admits Taiwan has no modern combat experience.
3. An Imperial officer corrects a grid, politely. The liaison wonders how much of the Imperial confidence is earned.

**A1.7. High Censor Renji Kurosawa (41, 2131, off Kyushu). Action. Ending W.**

1. In a midnight storm Kurosawa leads silent Ashigaru-4 automata onto an illegal smart-AI relay rig.
2. They neutralize armed mercenaries. Kurosawa notes a replaced seal and a delayed timestamp on the rig's logs.
3. A captured mercenary asks what the Directorate keeps in its vaults. Kurosawa's jaw clamps shut and he presses two fingers to his breastbone. He cannot answer.
4. A manifest shows a port listing. He is about to name it to his officer and does not.

**A1.8. Sovereign South Korean Defense Planner (generic OC, 2131, Seoul). Intrigue. Ending E.**

1. In a Seoul bunker the planner argues for static DMZ bunker defense against treaty-supplied Helios-2 automaton fireteams.
2. An Imperial Legion advisor shows how automata take the first losses. The planner's staff bristle.
3. The planner keeps one bunker plan and trials one fireteam. The doctrinal friction is plain on the table.

**A1.9. Fleet Admiral Kazuhiko Togo (46, 2131, IJN Amaterasu-no-Yaiba). Intrigue. Ending P.**

1. Togo runs live-fire 3D void exercises behind lunar shadow, with Ryunosuke observing on the sunken bridge.
2. Crews feel the 99.5% inertial dampening as a heavy gravitational tug in the harnesses. Several make rookie mistakes. Togo rubs the bridge of his nose and critiques doctrine first.
3. A formation pivot sends two Frigates onto crossing vectors. Togo orders a hard correction.

**A1.10. Kinki-Kyoku Scout Captain (generic OC, IJN-KNK-SP, early 2132). Action. Ending W.**

1. On an 18 LY/day Sequential Spatial-Fold run, the captain evades Pact hunter-killer satellites near Sol.
2. The captain seeds a Proscribed Vault Sanctuary in a planetoid 25 light-years out. The sentinel automata take their posts in silence.
3. On the return leg the cruiser intercepts Pact fleet mobilization chatter. The captain has the transcript and cannot decide who may hear it first.

## ERA II: THE IRON TEMPEST (2132 to 2136)

**Batch 1.** Opens with the excerpt "The Autumn Opening," *Post-Settlement Naval Monograph, Kyoto*.

2. They intercept a Pact covert extraction team and dismantle the smuggling ring.
3. Kurosawa recovers encrypted launch codes forty-eight hours before the strike. The codes carry a seal from inside a Japanese port office.

1. Mikhailovich walks a drydock catwalk and inspects welds himself.
2. He briefs his staff on masking real kinetic-rod carriers among hundreds of cheap radar-decoy freighters.
3. Moscow, the CPF, and the DPRK confirm launch. Mikhailovich is about to tell his staff what he expects the Japanese picket to do, and does not.

**A2.3. Togo (47, Oct 14, sunken bridge of IJN Amaterasu-no-Yaiba). Action. Ending P.**

1. The first Pact wave is shattered by torpedo and spinal fire.
2. Mikhailovich's staggered second-wave decoy strike slips past the inexperienced Imperial orbital picket.
3. Two Imperial cruisers are scarred. Togo grips his hands behind his back and orders the line to re-form.

**A2.4. Corvette Captain, IJN-DNK-LE Wolfpack (generic OC, 250m, Oct 15). Action. Ending P.**

1. The wolfpack, still organized 1:4 (one Frigate and four Corvettes, 36 bow tubes), launches a Type-90C salvo at a CPF dreadnought group.
2. The CPF commander detonates conventional nuclear flak along the approach vector, leaving a shrapnel cloud across the torpedo path.
3. The wolfpack redlines its 4-hour thermal sinks to escape.

**A2.5. Sovereign Taiwanese Destroyer Captain (generic OC, Oct 16). Action. Ending W.**

1. The captain breaks formation to chase retreating CPF landing craft.
2. A coastal railgun kill-zone opens. A 750m IJN-DNK-SP takes heavy armor damage shielding the crippled hull.
3. The Imperial cruiser's captain hails the Taiwanese bridge. The destroyer captain is about to answer for the break in formation.

**Batch 2.** Opens with a recap of Batch 1.

**A2.6. Himura (35, Oct 18, Kyoto). Intrigue. Ending E.**

1. The Privy Council reviews the opening orbital and coastal scars.
2. Ryunosuke demands the seizure of coastal mainland enclaves. Himura refuses. Masamune warns against overreach.
3. Himura orders fleet-wide retraining in 3D void geometry and sits down to a meal with the council.

**A2.7. Tachibana (30, Oct 22, low Earth orbit). Action. Ending P.**

1. Imperial Guard cruisers hunt Pact kinetic-rod platforms.
2. Tachibana's gunners stagger Type-90A Hari interceptors against incoming rods.
3. A platform releases a rod at Kyoto. The Haris must hit it in seconds.

**A2.8. South Korean Frontline Battalion Commander (generic OC, Nov). Action. Ending E.**

1. The commander holds a frozen DMZ ridge against DPRK artillery and armor.
2. Static bunker doctrine and mismanaged treaty-supplied Helios-2 automaton squads cost the ridge's left flank.
3. The commander pulls back survivors and counts the missing.

**A2.9. General Williams (\~26, Nov, UNAS). Intrigue. Ending W.**

1. Williams orders UNAS orbital wings into the Pacific to enforce a blockade zone against Japan.
2. He presses his knuckles on the plotting table and tells his staff the blockade will cost ships.
3. A staff officer asks what Washington expects from Moscow. Williams does not answer.

**A2.10. Cousin Ryunosuke Himura (\~28, Dec, 500m Frigate IJN-DNK-EL, Western Pacific). Action. Ending Q.**

1. Ryunosuke pushes his frigate into a UNAS orbital picket near geostationary orbit.
2. Western fighter-interceptor swarms exploit blind spots in his pre-war turret arcs.
3. He extracts and starts a notebook of every error he has made. He wonders what his cousin would have done differently.

### A11. THE LABS IN THE DARK (2150) \[Intrigue-weighted, 6 Intrigue/Aftermath + 4 Action\]

**Batch 1.** Opens with the excerpt "The Shadow Behind the Wall," *Post-Settlement Naval Monograph, Kyoto*.

**A11.1. Henry Lawson (\~35, 2150). Intrigue. Ending B.** In a hidden outer-belt lab he decants his infant daughter Miranda using incomplete approximations of stolen Chōju protocols. An alert warns that Audit-Marshals have traced his courier.

**A11.2. Kurosawa (60, 2150). Action. Ending W.** He leads a raid on Lawson's lab and captures degraded research logs just after Lawson flees with the infant.

**A11.3. Alec Ryder (21, 2150). Intrigue. Ending Q.** He remembers the Imperial Audit-Marshal whose jaw locked at the mention of true AI and resolves to pursue advanced AI research outside Kyoto's jurisdiction.

**A11.4. Empress Kaguya Himura (50, 2150). Aftermath. Ending E.** She reviews deep-space quarantine dispatches with Himura as humanity steps onto the galactic stage in two irreconcilable pillars.

**A11.5. Tachibana (48, 2150). Action. Ending P.** In Gin-Musha armor she repels a covert mercenary strike on the Takamagahara Orbital Shipyard Ring and protects the sealed Solaris-9 slipways.

**Batch 2.** Opens with a recap of Batch 1.

**A11.6. Himura (2150). Intrigue. Ending B.** He reads the captured Lawson logs with Kurosawa, recognizes the genetic signature of Miranda Lawson, and sees how hard outside factions are trying to steal Imperial longevity.

**A11.7. Kansatsu-In Audit-Marshal (generic OC, 2150). Action. Ending P.** The Audit-Marshal chases a syndicate courier through an asteroid field after the Takamagahara raid, disables its thrusters, and recovers logs exposing a mercenary cruiser staged near a quarantined relay.

**A11.8. Admiral Boris Mikhailovich (Systems Alliance, \~45, 2150). Intrigue. Ending W.** On Arcturus Station he argues to the Alliance Admiralty that Japan's relay quarantines are an intolerable threat to expansion.

**A11.9. Yamashita (57, 2150). Aftermath. Ending E.** He inspects the 25 Automata Legions in quiet readiness on Japanese soil and reflects on the long peace since 2136.

**A11.10. Shadow Squadron Cruiser Captain (generic OC, 2150). Action. Ending W.** The captain detects the unflagged mercenary cruiser carrying stolen gravitic torpedo components toward the quarantined relay.

### A12. PROTOCOL MIRROR-SHATTER (2150+) \[Action-weighted, 7 Action + 3 Intrigue/Aftermath\]

**Batch 1.** Opens with the excerpt "The Unseen Edge," *Post-Settlement Naval Monograph, Kyoto*.

**A12.1. Sovereign South Korean Frigate Captain (generic OC, 2150). Action. Ending P.** With a modernized Korean patrol squadron and an Imperial IJN-TNU-LE 1:2 pack, the captain runs a disciplined pincer ambush on syndicate raiders threatening Busan's orbital drydocks.

**A12.2. Kansatsu-In Audit-Marshal (generic OC, 2150). Action. Ending B.** The Audit-Marshal interdicts a courier ship carrying stolen gravitic warhead schematics near the outer relay perimeter.

**A12.3. Togo (65, 2150). Action. Ending P.** From IJN Amaterasu he executes Protocol Mirror-Shatter against stolen gravitic torpedoes and cripples the mercenary cruiser with twin Energy Lances. The twelve Singularity Cannons stay hidden from watching Alliance observers.

**A12.4. Mikhailovich (\~45, 2150). Aftermath. Ending Q.** He boards the crippled mercenary cruiser from his Alliance frigate, shaken by the surgical precision of Japan's hidden defenses.

**A12.5. Exploration Frigate Captain (generic OC, IJN-TNK-EL, 2150). Action. Ending W.** Drifting under Eclipse stealth in an uncharted system, the captain records ancient non-human geometric hull plating, then engages the 60 LY/day exploration fold drive as the derelict's optical sensors flicker.

**Batch 2.** Opens with a recap of Batch 1.

**A12.6. Kurosawa (60, 2150). Intrigue. Ending W.** He delivers the Exploration Squadron's encrypted telemetry crystal to Himura in Kyoto under absolute seal.

**A12.7. Tachibana (48, 2150). Action. Ending P.** An Imperial Guard rapid-response squadron secures the perimeter around the Exploration Squadron's return corridor.

**A12.8. Ambassador Goyle (\~38, 2150). Intrigue. Ending Q.** She meets Himura after the Mirror-Shatter incident and senses a power behind Japan's restraint that the Alliance cannot fathom.

**A12.9. Jon Grissom (38, 2150). Action. Ending E.** He leads an Alliance patrol along the outer relay frontier and watches an Imperial 1:2:4 Line Division hold silent, immovable station.

**A12.10. Himura (biologically late 20s, 2150+). Aftermath. Ending W, strongest series hook.** He stands alone in the Kyoto-Heian Imperial Citadel before the telemetry projection of the derelict, knowing the countdown to the wider galactic war has begun and the Iron Aegis stands ready.
