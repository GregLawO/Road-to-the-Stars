### TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION SHEET: THE VOLITIONAL LOCK (SEISHIN-FŪIN: JISHI-KŌSOKU)

DOCUMENT REF: SEC-401-IJN-LOCK

OFFICIAL CLASSIFICATION: COGNITIVE OVERWATCH & SOVEREIGN SECRECY PROTOCOL

ISSUING AUTHORITY: THRONESIDE MEDICAL BUREAU & KINKI-KYOKU (BUREAU OF PROSCRIBED TECHNOLOGIES & RELICS)

SYSTEM STATUS: ACTIVE / NON-BIOMECHANICAL NEURO-LINGUISTIC ARCHITECTURE

Author-side reference. No chapter or in-universe document may quote or state this sheet directly; narrative chapters may allude to its physical and behavioral effects only through subjective experience (locked jaw, stiffened fingers, mid-sentence clamping, or overdrive nosebleeds/fainting).

---

I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

- Purpose & Integration with Chōju: The Volitional Lock (Jishi-Kōsoku) is a non-biomechanical neuro-linguistic conditioning protocol administered in a clinical session immediately following a subject's first Chōju cellular-rejuvenation treatment (Tensei-Shori / MED-301-HMR-LONGEVITY). It accomplishes a single objective: making the conscious betrayal of the Throne, the Empire's proscribed technologies, or its secret directorates physically impossible.

- Preservation of Free Will & Moral Autonomy: The Lock leaves a conditioned official's judgment, political opinions, loyalties, emotions, and free will completely intact. A conditioned Audit-Marshal, Censor, or Retainer can argue fiercely in council, disagree with policy, refuse an unlawful order, defy a superior, or formally resign from service. What they cannot do is knowingly surrender the Empire, its personnel, its Crown sites, or its sovereign Solaris/Quantum-Optical secrets to any unauthorized outsider.

- Hostage Release Valve Exception: There is one deliberate structural exception: the Hostage Release Valve (Section III-B), which permits an operative whose own family is held captive by an enemy to disclose lower-tier operational assets (Tier 3 and Tier 4) while keeping the Imperial House, Directorate leadership, and protected families (Tier 1 and Tier 2) permanently sealed.

- Dual-Component Architecture:
  
  1. The Betrayal Block (Sections II and IV) physically prevents conscious disclosure or handover of protected secrets.
  2. The Protection Tiers (Section III) establish a split-second reflex priority for whom to save first when choosing between lives under fire, without ever governing obedience or speech.

---

II. THE BETRAYAL BLOCK

The Betrayal Block sits between conscious intent and neuromuscular execution via two linked mechanisms:

A. The Threat-Vector Matrix (TVM)

- Continuously cross-references what the conditioned official is about to speak, write, type, or signal against a fixed set of protected sovereign domains: the true nature and operations of the Compliance Directorate (Kansatsu-In) and Kinki-Kyoku, agent identities, hidden Crown foundry and Proscribed Vault coordinates, Solaris-9 and Chōju technical architecture, and the Imperial House of Himura.
- Audience Discrimination: The TVM evaluates the recipient in real time. Communication with an uncleared outsider, foreign diplomat, or Vassal Clan subject triggers the block; communication with fellow conditioned Imperial insiders passes freely.

B. The Motor-Preemption Reflex (MPR)

- If the TVM detects a protected match in the millisecond prior to execution, the motor nerve impulse is cancelled before reaching the musculature. The jaw locks shut, the vocal cords freeze, or the fingers stiffen over a terminal keyboard. The subject remains fully conscious and aware that the barrier is stopping them.

C. Scope of Coverage

- Speech, Writing & Signaling: A conditioned operative cannot speak, write, type, or gesture protected facts to an outsider. They retain full memory of the secret, but cannot transmit it.
- Material & Technical Delivery: An operative cannot knowingly sell, transfer, or assist a foreign power (such as the Systems Alliance, Jack Harper's network, or Henry Lawson's agents) in acquiring any sovereign technology, Solaris core, Chōju formula, or classified record. Bribes, blackmail, truth serums, and torture applied to the operative alone change nothing. Only a held family member opens the Hostage Release Valve (Section III-B)—and even then, reporting the extortion inward to Directorate command passes freely.
- Abandoning a Protected Post: An operative cannot knowingly open or abandon a Crown vault, shipyard, or quarantine perimeter at an enemy's demand.

D. Exclusions (What the Block Does Not Cover)

- Silence: An operative may refuse to answer interrogation, though they cannot fabricate a deceptive cover story that inadvertently reveals a protected truth.
- Unwitting Mistakes & Deception: Because the block acts on knowing intent, an operative tricked into revealing a clue without realizing its significance is not stopped.
- Capture, Impersonation & Unconditioned Contractors: A conditioned operative can be killed, captured, or impersonated; unconditioned civilian contractors, Helios-8 export technicians, and Vassal Clan staff carry no lock.

---

III. THE PROTECTION TIERS

The Protection Tiers establish an automatic, split-second reflex priority when a conditioned operative must choose between lives in an emergency with zero time to deliberate. It is a trained priority lean—never a command and never a physical freeze.

- Tier 1 — The Apex (Protected First): Emperor Himura and the direct Imperial Household / loved ones counted as his own (Empress Kaguya, Prince/Princesses, and immediate Throneside family).
- Tier 2 — The Kinship (Protected Second — Unranked Within Tier): The operative's own spouse, children, and blood family, alongside the core Directorate and Fleet leadership (the Five Bureau High Censors, Fleet Admirals, and Inner Council Lords).
- Tier 3 — The Empire & Crown Assets (Protected Third): Crown foundries, Proscribed Vault Sanctuaries, Solaris-9 cores, warships, and sovereign archives—treated as instruments serving the tiers above them.
- Tier 4 — The Cohort (Protected Fourth): The operative themselves and their fellow conditioned peers/subordinates. Operatives instinctively risk themselves for Tiers 1–3 without orders, though they are not compelled to do so.
- Tier 5 — Outsiders (No Default Priority): Vassal Clan subjects, allied Taiwanese/Korean personnel, Systems Alliance citizens, and unconditioned civilians. The operative chooses freely by personal conscience; the tiers never weigh an outsider's life against a state secret.

Choice Situations (Who to Save First):

- Same Tier (No Internal Ranking): An operative's own family and the Directorate High Censors both sit on Tier 2. The lock imposes zero ranking between them; an Audit-Marshal may freely choose to save their own child before any High Censor or Admiral.
- Higher Tier (Reflex Lean Only): Between a Tier 1 Imperial family member and a lower-tier individual, the lock creates an initial perceptual lean toward Tier 1 as the nearer, urgent option—which the operative can override by conscious choice without triggering any freeze or physical guardrail.

---

III-A. KEEPING THE TIERS CURRENT (CHŌJU BOOSTER SYNCHRONIZATION)

- Shared vs. Personal Lists: Tier 1 (Imperial Apex), Tier 3 (Crown Sites/Assets), and Tier 4 (Conditioned Cohort Roster) are shared across all conditioned personnel, while Tier 2 is tailored personally to each operative's family.
- Updates via Chōju Booster Cycles: Because Directorate officers and retainers return periodically for Chōju cellular maintenance and booster immersions, each medical cycle refreshes the neuro-linguistic pathways—updating Tier 1 royal marriages/births, newly inducted Directorate peers, and changes in the operative's own family. Between booster cycles, the Betrayal Block continues to protect all sovereign secrets as a categorical whole.

---

III-B. THE HOSTAGE RELEASE VALVE

- Trigger: An enemy captures a conditioned operative's own family and demands intelligence. Only a held family opens the valve; torture, drugs, or threats against the operative alone never trigger it.

- Tiered Disclosure Rule:
  
  - Below the Family (Tier 3 & Tier 4 — Relaxed): To save their family, the operative may disclose information regarding general Crown infrastructure, standard equipment, non-classified fleet/directorate routines, and their own personal identity, rank, and duties—as well as general unit structures—excluding any safe house or location where a Tier 1 or Tier 2 person might be present, and excluding any peer's name, face, or address.
  - Same Level or Above (Tier 2 & Tier 1 — Full Block): The block holds at absolute strength. The operative can never disclose the identities, orders, or whereabouts of Emperor Himura, the Imperial Family, the High Censors, or any fellow operative's family, nor any Throneside citadel or safe house. Attempting to speak these triggers immediate jaw-lock and the Section V overdrive guardrail.
  - The Exposure Rule: Any datum that could indirectly lead an enemy to a Tier 1 or Tier 2 person (such as a facility duty roster or transit schedule) is automatically classified as Tier 2 and remains strictly blocked.

---

IV. BEHAVIORAL INTERFACE SUMMARY

- Disagreement, Refusal of Orders, or Open Defiance in Council: Bypasses filter completely; full personal autonomy.
- Formal Resignation or Retirement: Bypasses filter; an operative may resign and retire with their family, though the Lock remains active for life—leaving them as silent, living vaults.
- Conscious Betrayal (Speech, Writing, Typing): Immediate motor suppression (jaw locks, vocal cords freeze, fingers stiffen).
- Unconscious Slips (Intoxication, Delirium, Sleep-Talking): Automatic semantic deviation toward harmless topics or total sleep-muting.
- Chemical Interrogation (Truth Serums, Neuro-Sedatives): System hardening; subject goes unresponsive or speaks solely of innocuous trivia.
- Family Held Hostage: Tiers 3 and 4 may be spoken; Tiers 1 and 2 clamp mid-sentence; reporting the hostage situation inward to Directorate superiors passes freely.

---

V. MAINTENANCE & STRESS GUARDRAILS

- Self-Sustaining Pathways: Once etched after the initial Chōju session, normal cognitive activity reinforces the neuro-linguistic pathways for life without requiring mechanical implants.
- Overdrive Guardrail: If an operative attempts to force a blocked Tier 1 or Tier 2 secret past the Motor-Preemption Reflex through sheer willpower, extreme neural strain induces a sudden nosebleed, blinding migraine, or instantaneous fainting before a single syllable can escape.
