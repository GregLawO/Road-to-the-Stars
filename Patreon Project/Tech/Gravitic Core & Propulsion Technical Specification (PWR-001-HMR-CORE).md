- Physical Appearance & Sensory Signature: Every Solaris and Helios core is a seamless, matte-black sphere or octahedron forged from compressed neutronium-alloy, suspended inside a magnetic vacuum cradle. Active cores emit zero exhaust, zero chemical flame, and zero electromagnetic roar—only a sub-audible, bone-deep bass thrumming felt in the deck plates and a faint golden-amber corona along external phase conduits.

- Quad-Function Integration: A single closed-loop core supplies:
  
  1. Continuous kilowatt-to-terawatt electrical and thermal power.
  2. Reactionless gravimetric sub-light thrust and internal inertial dampening.
  3. Apparent inertial mass modulation.
  4. Shield projection and (on Solaris models) Sequential Spatial-Fold FTL transit.

- Deterministic VI Regulation: Every core is governed by a non-sentient, hard-wired Quantum-Optical VI operating on pulsed photonic crystals—calculating metric-tensor harmonics at picosecond intervals while enforcing the Anti-Smart-AI Edict.
