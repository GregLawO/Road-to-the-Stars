Document Control: CLASSIFIED - COMBAT DROID SPECIFICATIONS

Subject: Nonvolitional Combat Droid Chassis, Neural Processing Units (NPUs), Core Matrices, & Power Systems

Deployment Standard: Standard Nonvolitional AI Cores & Frontline Defense Heuristics

Nonvolitional Combat Droids are purely operational platforms driven by specialized algorithmic sub-routines rather than volitional neural scans. Designed strictly for frontline attrition, close-quarters shock assault, deep infiltration, and concealed VIP protection, these units execute tactical directives with zero morale failure, absolute obedience, and instantaneous target acquisition.

Tiers I and II also exist as non-combat utility models, described under NON-COMBAT UTILITY VARIANTS after Tier V. Tiers III to V have no non-combat variant.

NON-COMBAT UTILITY VARIANTS (TIERS I AND II)

Added 9 November 2026. Every specification in this section is a decision, open to overrule. The variants use the same cores and power modules as the combat models of the same tier. They carry no weapons, no fire-control, and no armor rating.

TIER I UTILITY: FIXTURE-CLASS WORK DROID

Designation: Tier I Utility Droid (Fixture Class)
Core AI Integration: Tier I Nonvolitional Core (64 TB - 128 TB Chip), loaded with a single task
Primary Role: Single-task station work: lifting, loading, holding parts, welding one seam type, sorting, gauging, cleaning
Chassis Type: Fixed-mount or rail-mounted arm, or a light tracked base. No turret and no weapon mounts

Power & Energy Specifications:
Power Core: Single-Module SS-LiS-NCM4 Pack Array (6.4 MWh Capacity), carried as backup behind a mains tether
Operational Lifespan: Indefinite when tethered; 48 to 72 hours on the backup pack alone at working draw
Discharge Dynamics: Steady baseline draw. No high-current bursts, because there is no turret or firing system

Chassis Specifications: Armor: Light housing, no ballistic rating
Movement Speed: 0 km/h fixed; 5 km/h on a tracked base

Work Capabilities: Repeats one task to the tolerance its crystal sets, stops on a part it finds out of tolerance, and reports to the station's Dumb AI.
Operational Limits: Static logic. Cannot change tasks, choose between tasks, or order its own work. A new task needs a new crystal.
Protocol Safeguards: Range-bound geofencing to its station, read-only memory partitions, hard-wired kill switches, and manual master kill-switch receivers.

TIER II UTILITY: OPERATOR-CLASS WORK DROID

Designation: Tier II Utility Droid (Operator Class)
Core AI Integration: Tier II Nonvolitional Core (256 TB Dual-Layer Prism), whose task domain is set by the data crystal installed (assembly, inspection, cargo staging, site maintenance, or building any product the crystal holds)
Primary Role: Line operation, multi-step assembly, quality inspection, cargo staging, maintenance, and building any product whose crystal is installed (see TIER II PRODUCTION DOMAINS

A Tier II operator can build anything, as long as the correct data crystal is installed. The crystal holds the design and the build expertise for that product. Examples: data crystals (blank media), anti-gravity units, BMUS, AEMS, AEPS-BV, AEPS, CFCS, SS-LiS-NCM4 battery packs, droid frames, and loading devices.
- Swapping the crystal changes the job. One crystal is active at a time.
- The unit builds what the crystal holds. It does not design.
- It works from stock the disassembler arrays supply.
- A unit making a data crystal produces blank media. It cannot load a core into one.
- Tier I fixture units fill the single-step stations on the same line.

CONVERSION FROM COMBAT

A Tier I or Tier II combat chassis can be converted to the non-combat variant of the same tier. Weapons, turret, fire-control hardware, and firing-line restrictions are removed. The combat crystal is replaced with a non-combat crystal of the same tier, and the frame and power array are reused. Conversion is a crystal swap, not a reprogramming, because a core never takes a new task on its own. A converted unit returns to combat only with a new combat crystal.

POWER MATRIX INTEGRATED VULNERABILITIES & SYSTEM RISKS

- **Sulfur Cathode Swelling & Structural Failure:** Microscopic volume expansion occurs during maximum rapid discharge; if kinetic penetration cracks the outer composite casing, moisture ingress will cause immediate internal shorting.

- **Fast-Charging Degradation:** Initiating 15-minute field station "Tactical Flash-Charges" causes localized copper-dendrite formation, reducing overall cell cycle lifespan by 1.5% per fast-charge event.

- **Depletion Firmware Lockout:** Allowing any core battery matrix to drop below 0.5% total charge completely de-authorizes the smart-grid micro-zoning firmware, rendering the unit completely inoperable until a factory hard reset is performed at a dedicated logistical base.
