TECHNICAL DATA SHEET: GUNDARIUM ALPHA MATERIAL SPECIFICATION

**Project Designation:** Localized Microgravity/Vacuum Metallurgy (LMVM)  **Classification:** Advanced Non-Terrestrial Titanium Alloy Matrix

---

1. Environmental Synthesis Requirements
- **Atmospheric Pressure:** Equal to or less than 10⁻⁸ Torr. This ultra-high vacuum state must be maintained to prevent any atmospheric contamination or gaseous inclusions during the bonding process.

- **Gravitational Variance:** Equal to or less than 0.001 G. This microgravity environment is simulated via a localized, high-density Anti-Gravity Field Vector to completely eliminate gravity-induced settling or element segregation.
2. Elemental Composition (By Weight)
- **Titanium-50 (Isotopically Refined):** 84.5% (Base structural matrix providing high specific strength)

- **Neodymium-Yttrium Compound:** 9.0% (Kinetic energy absorption and shock dissipation phase)

- **Boron-Carbide Whisker Reinforcement:** 4.5% (High-modulus tensile strength and rigidity booster)

- **Exotic Polymer Binding Agents:** 2.0% (Inter-crystalline matrix flexibility filler)
3. Core Manufacturing Steps
- **Phase I: Levitated Electromagnetic Melting:** Raw elemental stocks are suspended via electromagnetic suspension fields inside the vacuum chamber. High-energy electron beams liquefy the material into a perfectly homogeneous molten mass. The lack of gravity prevents denser elements from sinking, ensuring uniform element distribution.

- **Phase II: Microgravity Crystallization:** The molten alloy undergoes a controlled, uniform isothermal cooling cycle. Without gravitational forces acting on the boundaries, the metallic crystal grains grow evenly in all three dimensions, eliminating micro-fissures, internal stress lines, and structural defects.

- **Phase III: Solid Phase Extraction:** Once the material drops below its crystallization threshold (1,650°C), the anti-gravity and vacuum fields are deactivated. The resulting defect-free solid ingot is permanently stabilized and ready for standard metallurgical machining, hydraulic stamping, or precision forging.
4. Material Characteristics & Metric Specs
- Physical Specifications
  
  - **Volumetric Density:** **4.20 g/cm³** (Approximately 5% lighter than standard aerospace-grade Titanium alloys due to zero-G atomic lattice packing and micro-polymer inclusion).
  - **Melting / Phase-Change Point:** **1,650°C (3,002°F)** structural threshold; vaporization matrix transition occurs at **3,200°C**.

- Mechanical & Tensile Performance
  
  - **Tensile Yield Strength:** **4,800 MPa (Megapascals)**. Demonstrates roughly 4 to 5 times the yield limit of military-grade rolled homogeneous armor (RHA), resisting permanent deformation under catastrophic loads.
  
  - **Ultimate Tensile Strength (UTS):** **5,400 MPa**. The material will flex, absorb, and stretch under extreme load, but will not undergo physical tearing or structural fracture below this threshold.
  
  - **Modulus of Elasticity (Young's Modulus):** **310 GPa (Gigapascals)**. Extreme structural rigidity and minimal elasticity achieved through localized boron-carbide whisker reinforcement arrays.
  
  - **Fracture Toughness:** **180 MPa·m^0.5**. High internal resistance to rapid crack propagation or surface shattering under high-stress shockwaves.

- Ballistic & Kinetic Resilience
  
  - **V50 Ballistic Limit:** Rated at **2,450 m/s** using a standard 20mm fragment-simulating projectile (FSP).
  
  - **Thermal Shock Deflection:** Dissipates localized heat spikes up to **22,000 W/cm²** without crystalline degradation or internal structural weakening, shedding plasma or energy blasts across its isotropic matrix.

- Isotropic Uniformity Metrics
  
  - **Anisotropy Delta Coefficient:** **Δ < 0.001%**. Because the grain layout crystallizes in a perfect 0G simulated space, the mechanical strength, heat transfer, and stress tolerances are identical along the X, Y, and Z axes.
  
  - **Material Fatigue Limit:** Rated to survive **>10^9 heavy load-deflection cycles** at 80% maximum yield strength before microscopic grain boundary slippage occurs.
