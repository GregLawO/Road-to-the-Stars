TECHNICAL DATA SHEET: ARMORED EXOSKELETON POWERED SUIT (AEPS)

**Classification:** Electro-Magnetically Hardened Heavy Assault Armored Exoskeletal Powered Suit

**Chassis Architecture:** Continuous 1.0-Inch (25.4 mm) Solid-Matrix Gundarium Alpha Shell with Integrated Multi-Layered Faraday Shielding  

**Primary Energy Grid:** Ultra-Lightweight Optically Isolated 3.2 Gigawatt (GW) Palladium Cold-Fusion Arc Reactor Array

**Mobility Foundation:** Hybrid Solid-State Gravitic Distortion Core & Micro-Thruster Array  

**Defensive Auxiliary:** Energy Dissipating Shield Generator & Transient Voltage Surge Suppression (TVSS) Matrix  

**Offensive Systems:** Modular Laser Carbine Array (Standard) / Heavy Beam-Cannon Tactical Hardpoint (Specialist Only)  

**Emergency Backup:** Internal Kinetic Energy Harvesting Reserve (Analog-Fused)

SECTION 1: MATERIAL CHARACTERISTICS & SPECS

A. Physical Specifications

- **Volumetric Density:** 3.58 g/cm³ (Gundarium Alpha lattice alignment, significantly reducing total dead weight).
- **Melting / Phase-Change Point:** 1,650°C structural threshold; vaporization matrix transition occurs at 3,200°C.
- **Electromagnetic Shielding Effectiveness:** >120 dB attenuation across the 10 kHz to 40 GHz spectrum, utilizing a continuous Mu-metal and copper-mesh sub-layer.

B. Mechanical & Tensile Performance

- **Tensile Yield Strength:** 4,800 MPa. Resists permanent deformation under catastrophic structural loading.
- **Ultimate Tensile Strength (UTS):** 5,400 MPa. Absorbs extreme strain before structural tearing occurs.
- **Modulus of Elasticity (Young's Modulus):** 310 GPa. High internal rigidity ensures minimal flex under high-stress shockwaves.
- **Fracture Toughness:** 180 MPa·m^0.5. Exceptional internal resistance to rapid crack propagation or fracturing from impact stress.

C. Ballistic & Resilience Metrics

- **V50 Ballistic Limit:** Rated at 2,450 m/s using a standard 20mm fragment-simulating projectile (FSP).
- **Thermal Shock Deflection:** Dissipates localized heat fluxes up to 22,000 W/cm² across the isotropic matrix without crystalline degradation.
- **Isotropic Uniformity Metric:** Anisotropy Delta Coefficient is Δ < 0.001%, producing identical load tolerances along all three structural axes.
- **Material Fatigue Limit:** Rated to survive >10^9 heavy load-deflection cycles at 80% maximum yield strength.

SECTION 2: AES POWERED CAPABILITIES

A. Mobility, Strength, and Flight Dynamics

- **Total Armor Plating Weight:** Calculated at a continuous 1.0-inch thickness over a 2-meter surface area to yield a lightened dry armor mass of 181.9 kilograms.
- **Total Suit Mass (Standard Infantry):** Balances at a lean **245 kilograms (approx. 540 lbs)** due to ultra-lightweight reactor, anti-grav, and micro-thruster architecture.
- **Total Suit Mass (Heavy Specialist):** Balances at **295 kilograms** when equipped with the shoulder-mounted Heavy Beam-Cannon and associated high-voltage hardware.
- **Gravitic Mass Displacement:** The hybrid core siphons less than 5% of the active reactor's output (down from 15%) to manipulate the local space-time relationship, dropping the suit's effective physical weight to just **10 kilograms** for the user.
- **Structural Lifting Capacity (Burst):** Calibrated musculoskeletal actuators allow for a maximum short-duration burst lift ceiling of **907 kilograms (1.0 US ton)**. This delivers optimized combat strength, enabling the user to quickly flip tactical vehicles, punch through barricades, or hoist field obstacles.
- **Structural Lifting Capacity (Sustained):** For extended, continuous transport or load-bearing operations, the system limits sustained lifting to **453.6 kilograms (0.5 US ton)** to preserve actuator integrity and manage thermal generation across the lightened framework.
- **Low-Altitude Flight and Hover:** The ultra-light chassis configuration allows the micro-thrusters and gravitic core to effortlessly achieve stable hover and active flight up to a ceiling of 10 meters above any terrain.
- **Orbital Deployment Jump Capacity:** The suit executes unassisted orbital insertions. Because of the massively reduced mass, the core requires minimal energy to completely negate terminal velocity and absorb landing impact forces.
- **Locomotive Enhancement:** Motorized actuators allow the pilot to hit sustained sprint speeds of 45 km/h on foot when hover systems are disengaged.

B. Deflection and Structural Protection

- **Energy Dissipating Shielding:** A protective plasma-barrier network wraps the outer shell. Powered directly by the 3.2 GW reactor, this shield operates with near-flawless stability on the standard infantry variant, completely free from heavy weapon-draw power spikes.
- **Armor Deflection Layer:** If shields are broken, incoming fire hits the 1.0-inch Gundarium Alpha frame. The 4,800 MPa yield strength renders the chassis immune to standard small arms, heavy machine guns, and light anti-materiel rifles.
- **Anti-Tank Blast Resilience:** The chassis maintains a fracture toughness of 180 MPa-m^0.5, enabling it to successfully halt the hyper-velocity copper plasma jets of heavy missiles.
- **Catastrophic Shock Absorption (Hardened):** Features an active magnetorheological fluid system within the motorized joints. Upon detecting an incoming heavy threat via hermetically sealed, non-electronic piezoelectric shock sensors, an internal localized flux generator instantly solidifies the fluid. This system is isolated within individual Faraday capsules, shifting crushing kinetic impact energy directly into the outer alloy shell completely unaffected by external electromagnetic disruptions.

C. Environmental, Thermal, and Electromagnetic Deflection

- **Extreme Heat Dissipation:** The Gundarium Alpha matrix withstands localized thermal energy fluxes up to 22,000 W/cm² before hitting its phase-change threshold, rendering it immune to orbital re-entry temperatures and industrial plasma fields.
- **Active Sub-Cooling Grid:** A highly streamlined refrigeration loop pumps liquid nitrogen directly behind the armor walls, safely expelling transferred thermal energy through automated steam exhaust vents.
- **Total Climate Isolation:** Backed by ultra-dense synthetic aerogel blankets and a pressurized cockpit, the suit maintains an interior temperature of 22°C and provides a 4-hour self-contained breathing envelope.
- **Nuclear EMP & HPM Immunity:** All internal data transfer is driven by non-conductive, photonic fiber-optic networks. Power distribution lines are fitted with fast-acting, solid-state gas discharge tubes and silicon avalanche diodes to safely shunt induced currents from High-Altitude Electromagnetic Pulses (HEMP) or High-Power Microwave (HPM) weapons directly into the dissipating chassis framework without disrupting system operation.

D. Integrated Directed-Energy Weaponry

- **Modular Laser Carbine Arrays (Standard Issue):** The suit interfaces directly with heavy-duty, rifle-pattern laser carbines drawing continuous charging power from the suit's 3.2 GW power supply to maintain an effectively infinite operation window. Firing modes can be toggled between low-power training pulses, standard combat flashes, and high-intensity overcharged bursts.
- **Heavy Beam-Cannon Hardpoint (Specialist Only):** A heavy, shoulder-mounted beam cannon that draws high-voltage surges directly from the reactor core to instantly melt tank armor or disable low-flying aircraft. Firing the weapon triggers brief power fluctuations that can momentarily lower the flight ceiling or slow down joint response times.

SECTION 3: AES UNPOWERED FALLBACK STATE

A. Emergency Reserve Engagement

- **System Isolation:** If the primary Arc Reactor is compromised, deactivated, or removed, the chassis automatically disengages high-voltage channels and initiates the internal Kinetic Energy Harvesting Reserve.
- **Kinetic Power Feed:** Emergency backup capacitor cells utilize energy generated by the pilot's raw physical joint movements during normal powered operations.

B. Unpowered Operational Capabilities

- **Highly Mobile Musculoskeletal Assist:** Because the total unpowered mass of the standard variant is only 245 kg, the low-voltage currents routed into the joint motors are incredibly effective. The pilot is under no risk of being crushed and can maintain an agile, high-exertion jog or tactical walk of 8 to 12 km/h.
- **Zero Payload Capacity:** The 0.5-ton continuous and 1-ton burst structural lifting thresholds are entirely unavailable. Actuators possess only enough backup power to transport the suit and the pilot.
- **Analog Systems Translation:** The primary digital HUD experiences a total blackout. The visor automatically shifts to a backup analog optical loop or retracts an armored physical viewing slit in the helmet dome.
- **Low-Priority Life Support:** The positive-pressure oxygen arrays lose power, relying instead on a passive, mechanical filtration mask built into the helmet assembly.
- **Depleted Directed-Energy Weaponry:** The heavy beam cannon is completely non-functional. The modular laser carbines lose their infinite recharging umbilical connection, operating strictly as standalone firearms powered by their individual weapon packs.

SECTION 4: AES VULNERABILITIES (POWERED AND UNPOWERED)

A. Mechanical and Structural Vulnerabilities

- **Actuator Overload and Fatigue:** Exceeding the 0.5-ton continuous lifting limit for more than 120 seconds initiates localized actuator thermal spikes. Forcing the suit beyond the 1.0-ton absolute burst limit engages a software override to lock down the joints, preventing internal hydraulic lines from rupturing.
- **High-Velocity Kinetic Compromise:** Because the armor is only 1.0 inch, direct hits from hyper-velocity kinetic energy penetrators (traveling over 2,400 m/s) striking a flat surface at a perfect 90-degree angle while the energy shield is down will breach or stress-fracture the plate layer.
- **Lobster-Shell Joint Jamming:** A heavy explosion or missile blast directly on a flexing joint (elbow, knee, hip) can warp the sliding plates, permanently freezing the pilot's limb in place.
- **Abrasive Debris Accumulation:** Extended deployment in high-grit environments can cause particulate matter to bypass the exterior shielding, increasing actuator wear and inducing motor stalls.

B. Thermal and Energy Vulnerabilities

- **Energy Shield Bleed-Through:** Rapid, sustained heavy bombardment can trigger a harmonic overload, briefly overwhelming the suit’s internal cooling systems.
- **Thermal Exhaust Vent Back-Pressure:** A well-placed incendiary or liquid-payload strike that blocks or flows into the directional steam exhaust vents will trap heat inside, overwhelming the insulation and cooking the pilot within seconds.
- **E-Bomb Grounding Saturation:** While internal systems are entirely immune to EMP field penetration, a direct hit from a dedicated tactical E-bomb or lightning strike will saturate the outer chassis chassis layer with residual surface charge. If the suit's automated grounding spikes fail to deploy into the terrain, touching unhardened friendly equipment or vehicles immediately after the strike can cause an external arc discharge.

C. Operational Vulnerabilities

- **Sensor Optronics Blindness:** Focused laser fire or high-velocity shrapnel can shatter the primary external optronics array, forcing the pilot to rely on secondary fallback sensors or blinding them entirely.
- **Weapon-Draw Power Spikes (Specialist Variant Only):** Firing the heavy beam cannon while executing an orbital jump pushes the active reactor to its limits, causing brief power fluctuations that can momentarily lower the flight ceiling or slow down joint response times. (Standard Infantry variants are immune to this spike).
- **Fuel Ring Replacement Windows:** The reactor operates on a strict 48-hour tactical lifecycle. If the palladium isotope rings are not replaced within this window, the suit drops directly into its Unpowered Fallback State.
