SECTION 1: POWER SPECIFICATIONS & UTILITY

**A. Input Channels & Generation**

- **Micro-Turbine Output:** Generates up to 150 kW of continuous power using standard military liquid fuels (JP-8, Diesel, Gasoline).
- **Solar Harvesting:** Dual 50 kW flexible QPV blankets deployable in under 120 seconds, optimized for ambient and low-light environments.
- **Grid Tethering:** Universal transformer interface accepts variable AC/DC inputs from local civilian grids or military vehicles (110V–480V).

**B. Charging Metrics (AEPS-Battery Variant)**

- **Standard Replenishment Rate:** Fully charges a depleted standard infantry variant (235 kg model) in **45 minutes** via direct umbilical connection.
- **Tactical Flash-Charge:** Boosts battery cells from 0% to 50% capacity in **15 minutes** using an overcharged, high-current surge profile (increases cell thermal stress).
- **Simultaneous Servicing:** Capable of handling up to **four suits simultaneously** in standard charging mode, extending full replenishment time to 90 minutes.

SECTION 2: OPERATIONAL MECHANICS & ARCHITECTURE

**A. Hot-Swapping Infrastructure**

- **Robotic Cradle Assist:** The charging case lid unfolds into a semi-automated alignment guide, ensuring precision placement of the suit’s lower-back battery housing.
- **Zero-Uptime Interruption:** The station feeds temporary holding power to the suit’s internal analog capacitors, keeping life support and essential electronics online while the depleted main cells are extracted.
- **Cell Extraction Cycle:** Manual mechanical release levers eject the dead battery blocks, allowing fresh, pre-charged modules to lock into the chassis matrix within **12 seconds**.

**B. Environmental & Defensive Hardening**

- **Faraday Cage Shielding:** The case features a continuous copper-mesh inner lining, protecting the sensitive internal charging transformers from Nuclear EMP or high-power microwave disruption.
- **Silent Run Sub-System:** For stealth or nighttime operations, the micro-turbine can be completely suppressed, relying strictly on internal reserve batteries and solar sheets to eliminate noise and thermal signatures.
- **Dynamic Grounding Matrix:** Built-in heavy-duty spike lines automatically discharge residual surface currents from the charging array safely into the terrain, preventing external arc accidents during rain or damp weather.

---

SECTION 3: SYSTEM LIMITATIONS & VULNERABILITIES

- **Thermal Signature Spike:** Running the micro-turbine at maximum recharge output produces a concentrated thermal plume, making the station highly visible to enemy infrared and thermal optics.
- **Grid Volatility Overload:** Plugging the station into unstable or sabotaged local power grids without enabling the safety buffers can blow the internal avalanche diodes, rendering the station non-functional.
- **Particulate Intolerance:** High-grit or dusty environments require frequent replacement of the micro-turbine's intake air filters to avoid internal turbine blade erosion and motor stalls.
