# TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION SHEET: THE ARMORED EXOSKELETON POWERED SUIT, BATTERY VARIANT (AEPS-BV)

Classification: Electromagnetically hardened heavy assault powered exoskeleton. Public build, sold by Pellam Field Systems.
System Status: Not yet in service

## I. OVERVIEW

The AEPS-BV is a powered exoskeleton with a continuous 1.0-inch (25.4 mm) solid-matrix Gundarium Alpha shell and multi-layered Faraday shielding. A solid-state lithium-sulfur nano-capacitor array with smart-grid power zoning runs the suit. A hybrid gravitic distortion core and micro-thruster array give it hover, flight, and orbital insertion.

## II. MATERIAL

- **Density:** 3.58 g/cm³.
- **Melting point:** 1,650°C. Vaporization at 3,200°C.
- **Tensile yield strength:** 4,800 MPa. **Ultimate tensile strength:** 5,400 MPa.
- **Young's modulus:** 310 GPa. **Fracture toughness:** 180 MPa·m^0.5.
- **V50 ballistic limit:** 2,450 m/s against a 20 mm fragment-simulating projectile.
- **Thermal shock:** up to 22,000 W/cm² without crystalline degradation.
- **Isotropy:** anisotropy delta below 0.001%.
- **Fatigue limit:** more than 10^9 load cycles at 80% of yield strength.
- **Electromagnetic shielding:** more than 120 dB attenuation from 10 kHz to 40 GHz, from a continuous Mu-metal and copper-mesh sub-layer.

## III. POWERED CAPABILITIES

1. **Armor plating mass.** 181.9 kg over a 2 m² surface.
2. **Suit mass, standard infantry.** 235 kg (about 518 lb).
3. **Suit mass, heavy specialist.** 285 kg, with the shoulder-mounted heavy beam cannon and its high-voltage hardware.
4. **Effective weight to the operator.** 10 kg. The gravitic core uses under 5% of the battery output.
5. **Burst lift.** 907 kg (1.0 US ton), short duration.
6. **Sustained lift.** 453.6 kg (0.5 US ton).
7. **Hover and flight.** Up to 10 meters above any terrain.
8. **Orbital jump.** Unassisted orbital insertion. A rapid capacitor discharge negates terminal velocity and absorbs the landing.
9. **Ground speed.** Sustained sprint of 45 km/h with hover disengaged.

## IV. PROTECTION

- **Energy-dissipating shield.** A plasma-barrier network wraps the outer shell and draws from the main battery grid. Power-throttling algorithms prevent premature depletion.
- **Armor.** With the shield broken, the 1.0-inch shell is immune to standard small arms, heavy machine guns, and light anti-materiel rifles.
- **Anti-tank blast resilience.** Its fracture toughness lets it halt the hyper-velocity copper plasma jets of heavy missiles.
- **Shock absorption.** A magnetorheological fluid system in the motorized joints solidifies instantly. Its flux generators sit in Faraday capsules.
- **Surge protection.** Transient voltage surge suppression.

## V. ENVIRONMENTAL AND ELECTROMAGNETIC

- **Heat.** The shell withstands up to 22,000 W/cm² before reaching its phase-change threshold.
- **Cooling.** A low-draw thermoelectric Peltier loop chills the armor walls.
- **Climate isolation.** Aerogel blankets and a pressurized cockpit hold the interior at 22°C and give a 4-hour breathing envelope.
- **EMP and microwave immunity.** All internal data travels on non-conductive photonic fiber-optic networks. Power lines carry gas discharge tubes and silicon avalanche diodes that shunt induced currents from nuclear EMP or high-power microwave weapons into the chassis.

## VI. DIRECTED-ENERGY WEAPONS

1. **Modular laser carbine arrays (standard issue).** The suit interfaces with rifle-pattern laser carbines that draw from the central battery. Training pulses and standard combat flashes drain little. High-intensity overcharged bursts rapidly reduce remaining battery life.
2. **Heavy beam-cannon hardpoint (specialist only).** A shoulder-mounted cannon that draws large voltage surges from the battery array. Firing it cuts remaining battery life and reduces flight and movement response by 15% for a short time.

## VII. UNPOWERED FALLBACK

1. **Trigger.** If the battery drops below 1% charge, is severed, or is ejected, the chassis disengages its high-voltage channels and starts the internal kinetic energy harvesting reserve.
2. **Power feed.** Backup capacitor cells store energy from the pilot's own joint movements during powered operation.
3. **Mobility.** Low-voltage current in the joint motors gives a jog or tactical walk of 8 to 12 km/h.
4. **Payload.** None. The actuators have power only to carry the suit and the pilot.
5. **Vision.** The HUD blacks out and the visor shifts to a backup analog optical loop or a physical viewing slit.
6. **Breathing.** The positive-pressure oxygen arrays lose power, and a passive mechanical filtration mask built into the helmet takes over.
7. **Weapons.** The heavy beam cannon is non-functional. The laser carbines lose their charging umbilical and run on standalone weapon packs.

## VIII. LIMITS

- **Actuator overload.** Lifting above 0.5 ton for more than 120 seconds causes actuator thermal spikes. Above the 1.0-ton burst limit, a software override locks the joints.
- **Hyper-velocity penetrators.** Kinetic energy penetrators above 2,400 m/s, striking flat at 90 degrees with the shield down, breach or stress-fracture the plate.
- **Joint jamming.** A heavy blast on a flexing joint can warp the sliding plates and freeze the limb.
- **Debris.** High-grit environments increase actuator wear and cause motor stalls.
- **Heat.** A strike that blocks the steam exhaust vents traps heat in the cockpit. If the temperature around the lithium-sulfur core passes 85°C, the suit forces an emergency energy dump.
- **E-bomb saturation.** A tactical E-bomb or lightning strike leaves residual surface charge on the chassis. Grounding spikes must be deployed at once to prevent arc discharge.
- **Optics.** Focused laser fire or shrapnel can shatter the primary optronics array, leaving secondary fallback sensors.
- **Weapon power spikes.** Heavy laser bursts and the beam cannon momentarily lower the flight ceiling and slow joint response.
- **Battery lifecycle.** 48 hours in scouting operations, 24 hours in continuous high-intensity combat. Past that, without a hot-swap or a field charger, the suit enters its unpowered fallback.

## IX. SAFEGUARDS

- **Marking.** Pellam's plate carries Pellam's name and a blank Designer line.
- **Power.** The public suit runs on batteries only.
- **Supply.** Pellam's plants make the shell from stock the Phased Disassembler Arrays produce.
