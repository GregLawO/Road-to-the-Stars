SECTION 1: MATERIAL CHARACTERISTICS & SPECS

**A. Physical Specifications**

- **Volumetric Density:** 3.58 g/cm³ (Gundarium Alpha lattice alignment, significantly reducing total dead weight).
- **Melting / Phase-Change Point:** 1,650°C structural threshold; vaporization matrix transition occurs at 3,200°C.
- **Electromagnetic Shielding Effectiveness:** >120 dB attenuation across the 10 kHz to 40 GHz spectrum, utilizing a continuous Mu-metal and copper-mesh sub-layer.

**B. Mechanical & Tensile Performance**

- **Tensile Yield Strength:** 4,800 MPa. Resists permanent deformation under catastrophic structural loading.
- **Ultimate Tensile Strength (UTS):** 5,400 MPa. Absorbs extreme strain before structural tearing occurs.
- **Modulus of Elasticity (Young's Modulus):** 310 GPa. High internal rigidity ensures minimal flex under high-stress shockwaves.
- **Fracture Toughness:** 180 MPa·m^0.5. Exceptional internal resistance to rapid crack propagation or fracturing from impact stress.

**C. Ballistic & Resilience Metrics**

- **V50 Ballistic Limit:** Rated at 2,450 m/s using a standard 20mm fragment-simulating projectile (FSP).
- **Thermal Shock Deflection:** Dissipates localized heat fluxes up to 22,000 W/cm² across the isotropic matrix without crystalline degradation.
- **Isotropic Uniformity Metric:** Anisotropy Delta Coefficient is Δ < 0.001%, producing identical load tolerances along all three structural axes.
- **Material Fatigue Limit:** Rated to survive >10^9 heavy load-deflection cycles at 80% maximum yield strength.

SECTION 2: AES POWERED CAPABILITIES

**A. Mobility, Strength, and Flight Dynamics**

- **Total Armor Plating Weight:** Calculated at a continuous 1.0-inch thickness over a 2-meter surface area to yield a lightened dry armor mass of 181.9 kilograms.
- **Total Suit Mass (Standard Infantry):** Balances at **235 kilograms (approx. 518 lbs)** due to the ultra-lightweight solid-state battery cells replacing the liquid cooling requirements of an active fusion reactor.
- **Total Suit Mass (Heavy Specialist):** Balances at **285 kilograms** when equipped with the shoulder-mounted Heavy Beam-Cannon and associated high-voltage hardware.
- **Gravitic Mass Displacement:** The hybrid core siphons less than 5% of the active battery grid's output to manipulate the local space-time relationship, dropping the suit's effective physical weight to just **10 kilograms** for the user.
- **Structural Lifting Capacity (Burst):** Calibrated musculoskeletal actuators allow for a maximum short-duration burst lift ceiling of **907 kilograms (1.0 US ton)**.
- **Structural Lifting Capacity (Sustained):** Limits sustained lifting to **453.6 kilograms (0.5 US ton)** to preserve actuator integrity and manage thermal generation across the lightened framework.
- **Low-Altitude Flight and Hover:** The ultra-light chassis configuration allows the micro-thrusters and gravitic core to effortlessly achieve stable hover and active flight up to a ceiling of 10 meters above any terrain.
- **Orbital Deployment Jump Capacity:** The suit executes unassisted orbital insertions, utilizing a rapid capacitor discharge to negate terminal velocity and absorb landing impact forces.
- **Locomotive Enhancement:** Motorized actuators allow the pilot to hit sustained sprint speeds of 45 km/h on foot when hover systems are disengaged.

**B. Deflection and Structural Protection**

- **Energy Dissipating Shielding:** A protective plasma-barrier network wraps the outer shell. Powered directly by the main battery grid, this shield operates with standard stability but is managed by aggressive power-throttling algorithms to prevent premature energy depletion.
- **Armor Deflection Layer:** If shields are broken, incoming fire hits the 1.0-inch Gundarium Alpha frame, rendering the chassis immune to standard small arms, heavy machine guns, and light anti-materiel rifles.
- **Anti-Tank Blast Resilience:** The chassis maintains a fracture toughness of 180 MPa-m^0.5, enabling it to successfully halt the hyper-velocity copper plasma jets of heavy missiles.
- **Catastrophic Shock Absorption (Hardened):** Features an active magnetorheological fluid system within the motorized joints that solidifies instantly via isolated flux generators inside Faraday capsules, entirely unaffected by external electromagnetic disruptions.

**C. Environmental, Thermal, and Electromagnetic Deflection**

- **Extreme Heat Dissipation:** The Gundarium Alpha matrix withstands localized thermal energy fluxes up to 22,000 W/cm² before hitting its phase-change threshold.
- **Active Sub-Cooling Grid:** A low-draw thermoelectric Peltier-cooling loop chills the armor walls, drawing significantly less current than standard industrial refrigeration units.
- **Total Climate Isolation:** Backed by ultra-dense synthetic aerogel blankets and a pressurized cockpit, the suit maintains an interior temperature of 22°C and provides a 4-hour self-contained breathing envelope.
- **Nuclear EMP & HPM Immunity:** All internal data transfer is driven by non-conductive, photonic fiber-optic networks. Power lines are fitted with fast-acting gas discharge tubes and silicon avalanche diodes to safely shunt induced currents from HEMP or HPM weapons directly into the chassis.

**D. Integrated Directed-Energy Weaponry**

- **Modular Laser Carbine Arrays (Standard Issue):** The suit interfaces directly with rifle-pattern laser carbines. Firing draws from the central battery cell; low-power training pulses and standard combat flashes have minimal drain, but high-intensity overcharged bursts rapidly degrade the remaining operational life.
- **Heavy Beam-Cannon Hardpoint (Specialist Only):** A heavy, shoulder-mounted beam cannon that draws massive voltage surges directly from the battery array. Firing the weapon triggers severe system power spikes, immediately reducing total remaining battery life and triggering a temporary 15% reduction in flight and movement response times.

SECTION 3: AES UNPOWERED FALLBACK STATE

**A. Emergency Reserve Engagement**

- **System Isolation:** If the primary battery array drops below 1% charge, is physically severed, or is ejected, the chassis automatically disengages high-voltage channels and initiates the internal Kinetic Energy Harvesting Reserve.
- **Kinetic Power Feed:** Emergency backup capacitor cells utilize energy generated by the pilot's raw physical joint movements during normal powered operations.

**B. Unpowered Operational Capabilities**

- **Highly Mobile Musculoskeletal Assist:** Due to the lighter 235 kg mass of the standard variant, low-voltage currents routed into the joint motors maintain an agile, high-exertion **jog or tactical walk of 8 to 12 km/h**.
- **Zero Payload Capacity:** Actuators possess only enough backup power to transport the suit and the pilot; lifting thresholds are completely unavailable.
- **Analog Systems Translation:** The primary digital HUD experiences a total blackout, forcing the visor to automatically shift to a backup analog optical loop or a physical viewing slit.
- **Low-Priority Life Support:** The positive-pressure oxygen arrays lose power, relying instead on a passive, mechanical filtration mask built into the helmet assembly.
- **Depleted Directed-Energy Weaponry:** The heavy beam cannon is completely non-functional. The modular laser carbines lose their central charging umbilical connection, operating strictly on standalone weapon packs.

SECTION 4: AES VULNERABILITIES (POWERED AND UNPOWERED)

**A. Mechanical and Structural Vulnerabilities**

- **Actuator Overload and Fatigue:** Exceeding the 0.5-ton continuous lifting limit for more than 120 seconds initiates localized actuator thermal spikes. Forcing the suit beyond the 1.0-ton absolute burst limit engages a software override to lock down the joints.
- **High-Velocity Kinetic Compromise:** Direct hits from hyper-velocity kinetic energy penetrators (traveling over 2,400 m/s) striking a flat surface at a perfect 90-degree angle while shields are down will breach or stress-fracture the plate layer.
- **Lobster-Shell Joint Jamming:** A heavy explosion or missile blast directly on a flexing joint (elbow, knee, hip) can warp the sliding plates, permanently freezing the pilot's limb in place.
- **Abrasive Debris Accumulation:** Extended deployment in high-grit environments can cause particulate matter to bypass the exterior shielding, increasing actuator wear and inducing motor stalls.

**B. Thermal and Energy Vulnerabilities**

- **Thermal Exhaust Vent Back-Pressure:** A well-placed incendiary or liquid-payload strike that blocks the directional steam exhaust vents will trap heat inside, posing an immediate risk of cooking the pilot.
- **Thermal Runaway Risk:** Sustained heavy shield bombardment or localized incendiary damage can cause severe thermal build-up. If internal temperatures around the lithium-sulfur core exceed 85°C, the suit initiates a forced emergency energy dump to prevent a catastrophic internal explosion.
- **E-Bomb Grounding Saturation:** A direct hit from a dedicated tactical E-bomb or lightning strike will saturate the outer chassis layer with residual surface charge. Grounding spikes must be deployed immediately to prevent arc discharge into friendly assets.

**C. Operational Vulnerabilities**

- **Sensor Optronics Blindness:** Focused laser fire or high-velocity shrapnel can shatter the primary external optronics array, forcing reliance on secondary fallback sensors.
- **Weapon-Draw Power Spikes (All Variants):** Unlike the reactor model, both standard and specialist variants suffer from power-draw spikes on this model. Heavy laser bursts or firing the beam cannon draws massive current, momentarily lowering the flight ceiling or slowing joint response times.
- **Battery Replacement / Recharge Windows:** **The power matrix operates on a strict 48-hour tactical lifecycle under normal scouting operations, which drops to a 24-hour lifecycle under continuous high-intensity combat conditions.** If the cells are not hot-swapped or plugged into a field charging station before this window expires, the suit drops directly into its Unpowered Fallback State.
