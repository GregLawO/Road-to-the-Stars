# TECHNICAL SPECIFICATION SHEET: THE MED-POD

Classification: Level 1 (Primitive), licensed build. Level 2, private design.
System Status: Active

## I. OVERVIEW

The Med-Pod is a bed-shaped treatment device that a person lies down in to remedy illness and injury. A steel cradle holds the patient under a clear lid. Once the lid is closed, the pod scans the body for disease, injury, and other conditions, then rebuilds it to remove the problem. It exists in two grades.

- **Level 1 (Primitive).** Cures disease and mends injury, and stops there. It never renews a body. Oyama Industries builds it, and it runs on the public floors of the clinics.
- **Level 2.** Rebuilds a body completely, rejuvenates it, and extends life. It is private and used only in the hidden wings.

Only Level 2 and better rejuvenate or extend longevity.

## II. OPERATION

An operator sets the gauges and dials and the patient lies down. The pod's controller then scans the body and carries out the treatment on its own, within the limits the dials set. The patient lies still inside the pod while it works, and nothing enters the body.

The pod mends broken bones, clears cancer, and rebuilds a normally fatal injury, as long as the brain and central nervous system remain intact. Level 2 also removes aged tissue, restores hair and skin to their prime, and rejuvenates the body. Reversing the dials rejuvenates.

If the brain or central nervous system is destroyed, the patient is beyond the pod's reach. Everything else it can rebuild. The pod runs again and again, for many patients in turn, without fault.

## III. FUNCTIONALITY

1. **Diagnosis.** The scan array maps the body at the cellular level and identifies disease, injury, and every other condition. It covers everything from a broken bone to terminal illness.
2. **Healing.** After diagnosis the pod heals or cures the patient. It regrows tissue, repairs bone, and removes cancerous cells. The length of a cycle depends on the work.
3. **Cellular regeneration (Level 2 only).** The pod repairs cellular damage and regrows tissue, removes old or damaged tissue, and returns the body to its prime. Longevity treatment resets the body's decline. It must be repeated, and every repeat is a follow-up visit.
4. **Automated and non-invasive.** The pod runs its own treatment, and the patient lies in it while it works.

## IV. TECHNOLOGIES EMPLOYED

1. **Advanced scanning and imaging.** A combination of MRI, CT, and other sensors in the cradle and lid maps the body at the cellular level in real time and builds a single body model from the result.
2. **Dumb AI.** A Dumb AI runs the pod. It is a non-sentient controller that reads the scan, plans the treatment, and carries it out within the limits the operator's dials set. It makes no choice the dials do not allow, and it does not think or decide beyond the task. Level 1 pods use the task-controller grade. Level 2 pods use the adaptive grade.
3. **Quantum computing.** The Dumb AI runs on quantum processing in its data crystal. This is what lets the pod process vast amounts of scan data at once and make precise calculations for every procedure.
4. **Data crystal.** Holds the body model, the controller, and the treatment logic. A second crystal in the frame reports the pod's position continuously.
5. **Reconstruction field.** Does the work of repair. It rebuilds tissue from the inside, whatever shape the body is in.
6. **Feedstock tank.** A reservoir behind the cradle of sorted base elements, produced by Phased Disassembler Arrays. The field draws on it for every cell it builds.
7. **Cloning and 3D bioprinting.** The pod grows replacement tissue and organs from the patient's own cells, and 3D bioprinting builds them layer by layer from the stored elements. Level 1 works at the grade of tissue and organs. Level 2 can rebuild a whole body.
8. **Stem-cell technology.** Level 1 uses stem-cell methods of the kind current medicine could reach, enough to mend damage. Level 2 uses far more advanced stem-cell technology, which drives its regeneration and rejuvenation.

## V. ATOM-LEVEL RECONSTRUCTION

1. **Atom-level reconstruction.** The pod rearranges atoms drawn from its feedstock to restore damaged tissue or make new tissue. It can repair any injury and cure disease. At Level 2 it can reverse aging by rebuilding the body at a fundamental level.
2. **Cancer and disease.** The pod rebuilds diseased cells as healthy ones, or clears them and rebuilds the tissue around them.
3. **DNA repair.** The pod corrects damaged DNA sequences in the cells it rebuilds. At Level 2 this removes inherited defects from the treated body.
4. **Seamless regrowth.** The pod restores any part of the body without scars or other signs of trauma, down to the smallest detail.

## VI. LIMITS

- The patient must be alive when the lid closes. The pod cannot reverse death.
- The pod treats the body only. Memory, skill, and will are untouched.
- A destroyed brain or central nervous system cannot be rebuilt.

## VII. SAFEGUARDS

- **Guard.** Every Level 2 pod is guarded around the clock by droids.
- **Movement.** No Level 2 pod moves without notice. The beacon reports its position at all times.
- **Anti-theft.** If a pod is taken from its guards, its core components disintegrate. The frame and tank are left behind, and they carry neither the body model nor the treatment logic.
- **Marking.** Level 2 units carry no plate, no maker's name, and no serial a stranger could read. Oyama's Level 1 plate carries Oyama's name and a blank Designer line.
