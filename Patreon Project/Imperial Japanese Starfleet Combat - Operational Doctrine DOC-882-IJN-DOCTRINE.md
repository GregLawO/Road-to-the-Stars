IMPERIAL JAPANESE STARFLEET COMBAT & OPERATIONAL DOCTRINE (TEIKOKU UCHŪ-KAIGUN SENTŌ KYŌRI)

DOCUMENT REF: DOC-882-IJN-DOCTRINE

Pre-war weapons, torpedo variants, Macrocannons, Energy Lances, shields, the 1:4 pack and the bridge are in the Technology file. This file covers doctrine from the war onward.

---

1. STRATEGIC MANDATE
- **Territorial defense.** With the Systems Alliance policing wider human space after 2149, the 1,500-vessel fleet defends the home islands, Crown colonies and Vassal garden colonies, and sends Exploration squadrons beyond human borders.
- **Zero technology bleed (Fūin-Kōryō).** Captains are bound to execute Hearth-Quench or Hearth-Collapse rather than let a core or hull fragment fall into foreign or xeno hands.
- **Human command supremacy.** Quantum-Optical VIs handle fire control, point defense and mass-modulation timing. Living officers keep all authority over maneuver, target authorization and strategy.

2. POST-WAR FLIGHT AND WEAPONS ADDITIONS
- **Iai-Pivot (Iai-Senkai)** keeps standoff range. **Keystone Lock (Kaname-Jō)** snaps hulls to full rest mass at discharge.
- **Single-Fold Tactical Micro-Jumps and Eclipse stealth (Gesshoku)** are standard on post-war hulls.
- **Singularity Cannons (Tokuiten-Hō)** are short-range micro-singularity projectors on the 3,000m Grand Battleships (4 prow) and the 10,000m flagship (12 axial). They stay hidden from observers until supreme crisis.
- **Point-defense grid and Protocol Kagami-Wari ("Mirror-Shatter")** counter copied or stolen gravitic munitions with Casimir interferometry and gravity-well decoy drones.

3. FORMATIONS
- **1:2:4 Line-of-Battle Division.** A 3,000m Grand Battleship anchors two 1,500m Battleships, each screened by two 1,000m Line Cruisers. Twenty Line Cruisers screen the flagship.
- **Assault Carrier Task Group.** The 2,500m Assault Carriers belong only to the main Starfleet and never join a line. Each embarks a 100,000-troop Division and 1,000 Oni-Mecha, with two 750m Interceptor Cruisers and two Close-Support / Hospital Corvettes.
- **1:2 Wolfpacks.** A Frigate leads two Corvettes (24 bow tubes per pack) on standoff ambush, patrol and planetary defense.
- **Interceptor Cruisers** are the fastest hulls above 500m, used for strike-team insertion and envoy missions.
- **Exploration squadrons (IJN-TNK-)** each pair a Frigate with two Corvettes beyond human borders.

4. STANDOFF SUPREMACY (Enkyori Yūsei-Shugi)
Imperial ships hold maximum safe distance and fight in three phases:
- **Phase I, extreme range (100,000+ km).** Energy Lances strip barriers and blind sensors, carriers launch VI drone swarms and Kage-Ya salvos, and wolfpacks fire unspotted ripple volleys under Eclipse stealth.
- **Phase II, range-keeping (25,000 to 100,000 km).** Battle lines use mass-shedding and Iai-Pivots to keep the enemy pinned, then open Phase-Gated Firing Apertures for Macrocannon bombardment.
- **Phase III, close range (under 20,000 km).** Commanders close only if one of three conditions holds:
  1. All torpedoes are expended, and capitals finish broken hulls.
  2. A last-resort blockade break after every long-range option has failed and ground forces must drop immediately.
  3. A dire emergency in which an ally's strategic asset, homeworld or Throne-citadel faces annihilation.

5. SPECIAL MISSIONS
- **Shadow-Wolf ambush (Kage-Ōkami Sakusen).** Packs never expose themselves to launch. They hold 50,000+ km standoff under Eclipse, fire full 24-torpedo volleys, then micro-jump or sprint to a new vector. Secondary guns come into play only under the close-range conditions above.
- **Autonomous orbital drop (Kōka-Sakusen).** Soldiers, automata and Oni-Mecha step from carrier bays in low orbit with no pods. Close-Support / Hospital Corvettes then hover overhead with artillery and medevac, and extraction-only recovery shuttles descend after the ground is secured. Interceptor Cruisers guard the carrier throughout.
- **Exploration and first contact (IJN-TNK-).** Squadrons enter uncharted systems under Eclipse stealth. On finding unknown phenomena, derelict technology or extraterrestrial life, they keep strict observational silence, record telemetry and send encrypted burst alerts to Henkyō-Kyoku and the Imperial Academy of Sciences before any contact is authorized.

6. COALITION RULES
- Inside Alliance space, Imperial ships operate only as sovereign envoys (Interceptor Cruisers) or invited reinforcements.
- Henkyō-Kyoku Interceptor Cruisers enforce the 2149 Charon Relay quarantine. No unauthorized vessel may activate a dormant primary relay bordering Imperial space.
- Every arm yields corridors and command to Throneside (IJN-TNS-) and Imperial Guard (IJN-KNE-) transponders.
