**A1.2. Audit-Marshal Sano Mitsuko (~29, 2131, Hakata port, Kyushu) (decision). Action. Ending W. (~1,600)**

1. *Warehouse before dawn (~550).* Sano stands in the dark of the Hakata customs warehouse beside a door post where an Ashigaru-4 stands with its slits dimmed. Three days ago she flagged this shipment, when the freight log and the declared cargo stopped matching. She let the cargo in, and forty minutes ago five crewmen came through the side loading door to collect one crate. She shut that door behind them and sealed it with a Directorate key, and the sentinels took their posts at every door. The crew has not yet worked out that the building is closed. Kurosawa arrives after the doors are held and stops off-angle from the entrance, out of the light. Sano gives him the crate's location, the crew count and the weapons count in three sentences, and he nods once. He steps into the open and offers the crew one chance to set the crate on the floor and walk away from it. He asks them nothing else. The crew leader asks by what authority. Kurosawa names the Kansatsu-In and the 2119 Edict, and the relay cores in the crate. He repeats the offer once. A crewman's hand goes into his coat.

*How to write it:*

- Open on the sealed side door and the sentinel beside it, slits dimmed, with Sano's key still in her hand.
- Slow gear through the whole scene.
- Sano set the trap and knows it.
- Sano's report is three plain sentences: the crate's location, five crewmen, one weapon (decision). Kurosawa nods once and says nothing to her.
- Kurosawa stays off-angle from the light until he steps out to make the offer. His voice stays even, and he never raises it.
- The offer is a few plain sentences, not a speech. He asks the crew nothing, and answers the crew leader's question about authority with the bureau's name, the Edict and the cargo.
- The sentinels hold their posts in complete silence.
- End on the crewman's hand going into his coat.

2. *The fight (~450, fast gear).* The crewman whose hand went into his coat draws and fires before the pistol clears the lining. The shot is wild and hits a pillar beside the crate (decision). Sano crosses the floor to him on the sound. She holds her sidearm because a stray round could hit the crate, and she wants him alive (decision). She takes his wrist, turns it until the pistol drops, and cuffs him, with the cuffs in her grip as the body detail. Kurosawa gives the signal, and the sentinels move for the first time. The ones nearest the crew pin the other four, who are unarmed, and the rest stay at the doors. The Directorate fires no shot and no one on its side is hurt. The sentinels then stand the four up on their knees in a row facing the crate, within earshot of Kurosawa (decision), and step back to their posts.

*How to write it:*

- Fast gear starts at the shot. Short sentences, one action each: the shot, her crossing, the wrist, the cuffs.
- Sano's reason for holding fire is one plain thought about the crate.
- The cuffs are in her grip from the moment she moves. The body detail is the cold steel against her palm.
- Kurosawa does not move or speak during the fight.
- The sentinels stay silent until Kurosawa signals, then move with the clack of clamps and amber slits. They pin and do not strike.
- The Directorate fires no shot, and no one on its side is hurt.
- There are five crewmen and one pistol. Sano cuffs the shooter, and the sentinels take the other four.
- No taunts and no speeches.
- End on the four kneeling in a row and the quiet hall, with Kurosawa walking to the crate.

3. *The manifest (~600).* Kurosawa reads a replaced seal and a late timestamp. A captured crewman asks what the Directorate keeps in its vaults. Kurosawa's jaw clamps shut and he presses two fingers to his breastbone. Sano sees it. He starts to name the port the manifest lists, looks at the crew still in earshot, and says only "Not here."

*How to write it:*

- Slow gear.
- Kurosawa states the replaced seal and the late timestamp in plain sentences.
- The crewman asks about the vaults from the kneeling row.
- Sano observes the jaw and the fingers and nothing more.
- Never name the Lock, never say he cannot speak, and never use "conditioned."
- End plainly on "Not here."
