# Human_Characters.md (trimmed for A2 and A3)

**Purpose.** Story-wide rulings, relationships, secrets, and entries for the cast of A2 and A3. A character's profile governs voice, habits, appearance, and personal facts; nothing a profile already says is repeated here. Trimmed: 2125 count and framing, age table, role-fit table, Brooks and Harper, the secret of 2125, POV roster for later chapters, Section 8A. Decisions the author left to me are marked (decision).

## 1. Rulings

**1. The Protagonist.** Kenji Himura, of Japanese descent. Body is thirty in 2050 (born 2020). Proposes to Hannah on the Moon trip in January 2058 (A2.14) and marries her in June 2058 (A2.16).

**3. The 2059 bomb.** In 2059, five months pregnant, Hannah survives a bomb at the Galveston clinic complex, but the child she carries is lost. The attack was ordered by the rival elite of A2.9 and A2.15 (unnamed), hired through underworld societies, and is not Cerberus's. The rival knows only that something important is hidden in the east wing, and the bomb is meant to flush it out; he does not know about the Level 2 pods. He bought a gate guard, a lamp contractor and a delivery driver, and accepted that the night staff would be on the dock, so the child's death was a cost he accepted. No one identifies or claims the attackers. Danny Shepard, Hannah's adoptive brother, dies outside the building in the same blast. A delivery van that should have come never arrived, a different van took its place, and the gate guard had been bought. Danny was on overwatch in the car park, outside Hannah's guard roster, saw the van riding low, and ran at it. He got the driver's door open before it went off, short of the east wing. In the corridor afterward her husband says: "Never again. No child. Not until no one can reach you." Hannah answers: "Not until then."

**5. The conspiracy.** Canon's Cerberus does not exist. The author-side label "Cerberus" belongs to the conspiracy of ruling 28.

**11. Who knows what.** Nobody in the story is ever told that Kenji is a transmigrator, and nobody learns where his foresight comes from. The reader learns it from his POV. Vance knows he traded his memories for treatment and that the droids' combat routines came from them. He is never told how Kenji knows what he knows. Nobody else is told where the droids' fighting style comes from.

**17. The Protagonist's POV.** Kenji carries POV from A2 on, **in the first person** (author ruling, 3 November 2026). His drafted chapters are A2.1, A2.4, A2.6, A2.11 and A2.14; Act_I_Outline sets the rest. The earlier fixed counts and lists of Kenji's and Hannah's POV chapters are removed for now (author, 7 November 2026). No Kenji POV is dated after 2064 until B1.2. A1 stays as published, with him unnamed and without POV.

**19. The early pool.** Before 2072 the characters old enough to carry POV are Kenji, Hannah, Brooks, Manswell, Tom and Ruth Shepard, Vance (ruling 30), Teodor Ashby (b. 2030, locked, Author-built), and the three main-cast recruits of ruling 33 (Marchetti, Byron Pyrrus, Whitcombe). Found-family servants and network operatives carry no POV. Movement 1 (A1 to A3) is exempt from the 20 percent generic-OC guide, because its OC voices carry the technology showcase.

**20. Reveal discipline.** No in-universe document may contain the truth of the 2065 withdrawals or the conspiracy, and the conspiracy never names itself on the page before Kenji's network discovers it.

**28. The conspiracy.** Cerberus acts against Kenji from 2055, when its first buyer approaches a client's physician. Its method is shadow control of the world's most advanced technologies, his above all, through cutouts, firms, regulators, and licensors. Its main interest is the technologies he lets spread, because it cannot find or take a Level 2 Med-Pod (author ruling, 5 November 2026). It is called "Cerberus" or "they," never "it." Its moves: the physician's offer (Nov 2055, A2.11), three offers for Oyama's patents (Jan, Apr, Oct 2058), and the forged letter that closes Galveston (Oct 2061). The raids (December 2056, August 2057, early 2058) and the 2059 bomb are the work of a rival elite (ruling 3), not Cerberus. The network learns the name in 2060 (ruling 34). After these the network knows it as one organization but not who leads it, and Brooks is recruited in 2062 to find out. There is no 2056 shakedown: hospital chains, a pharmacists' guild, an insurance board and a health ministry only send inspection requests and complaints (A2.8).

**30. Dominic Vance.** Gunnery Sergeant Dominic Vance, USMC, retired (name and rank are placeholders), locked recurring OC and main cast.
- In Act I he carries POV in A2.7 (2051 to summer 2053) and A3.1 (2062) only; A3.1 must match every gesture of the published A1.5.
- Kenji uses the copy of his memories to create and hone the droids' combat abilities; the routines are the only place it lives.
- First recruit and first member of the network (summer 2051). Brings in Ashby (autumn 2051), expands the team twice before Byron (July 2052), walks with Kenji until the L5 guards are finished (summer 2053, A2.7). Scouts Brooks, recruits her, brings her to Kenji.

**31. Security and operatives (author ruling, revised 3 November 2026).** The network begins in summer 2051 with Vance, then Ashby. Vance's fighters wear Level 1 powered-suit frames; date of Level 2 suits open. From autumn 2052 private sites are guarded by Level 2 security droids built by Oyama on Kenji's data crystals. From summer 2053 Level 3 droids guard every Level 2 pod around the clock (author ruling, 5 November 2026; date a decision). Two Level 5 droids are with Kenji at all times from summer 2053 (A2.7). They look and move like men; Hannah takes them for men until spring 2060. Operatives carry no POV, apart from Vance.

**32. Med-Pod tiers and longevity (author ruling).** Only Level 2 Med-Pods and better rejuvenate and extend longevity. The public Primitive grade cures and mends only. Anyone who lives past a natural span was treated at Level 2 or better. Tom and Ruth Shepard live long because Kenji gives them Level 2 treatment as found family (decision). Tom is treated in the hidden wing in 2053 because Danny asked. Ruth takes the same treatment after she meets Kenji and refuses the upload (decision). Every Level 2 treatment carries the memory seal (ruling 40) except those given to recruits, managers, and the found family.

**33. The recruits who become main cast (author ruling, 3 November 2026).** Byron Pyrrus (owner of Oyama Industries), Helen Marchetti (Houston lawyer, the Janus Foundation's trustee), and Eleanor Whitcombe (senator who leads the organization's political factions) are main cast. Kenji recruits Byron in July 2052, which is why Whitcombe is not the first elite buyer. Names, ages, and details are placeholders unless stated. Each has a profile and a POV chapter in A2 (A2.3, A2.2, A2.5).

**34. How Cerberus is named (Act_I_Outline, A2.9 onward).** The name first appears on the page in A2.18 (2060). An operative who traced the elite patients' one remembered contact to Whitcombe and Marchetti calls his group Cerberus and tries to turn them to its side. Both are conditioned, so it fails. Marchetti tells Kenji and Vance the same night, and Vance writes the word in his green book. The three 2058 offers for Oyama's patents are then understood to have been Cerberus's (decision). The network does not learn the name from money trails or documents.

**35. Oyama Industries and the robots (author ruling, 3 November 2026).** Oyama builds the Primitive Med-Pods after Byron is recruited (Kenji builds none). It also sells Level 1 to Level 3 robots, Level 1 powered suits, Tokamak reactors, Level 1 anti-gravity, and Gundarium Alpha. Byron is also the first target of Cerberus. Oyama puts its name on every plate, with a blank line where the designer should be.

**36. The terms of service (as drafted in A2.2 and A2.3).** Every recruit serves seven years. Treatment continues for as long as they serve. Renewal needs the person's own signature each time, and nobody can renew on their behalf, Kenji included. The Janus Foundation carries every term and every treatment, and Marchetti is trustee, so she alone can stop Kenji. If Janus ever stops paying, every design it owns becomes Oyama's to build and sell; Marchetti drafted that clause to protect Byron's workers.

**37. Disclosure and the two layers of conditioning (decision).** Recruits are told about the secrecy directive before they sign, and it outlasts the seven years. The first layer, a secrecy directive carried by the upload, goes to the first recruits from 2051. The second layer, which makes selling Kenji out something a person cannot do, goes to every member of the cadre two weeks after Danny's funeral in spring 2059. Vance asks to be left out of it, and Kenji agrees. Hannah, Tom, and Ruth are the found family and are not conditioned. Tom takes the training upload but refuses the directive; Ruth refuses the upload. The memory seal is a separate matter (ruling 40).

**38. What Hannah knows about Danny (decision).** Hannah knows her adoptive brother Danny is alive and believes he works for a security contractor in Houston doing gates and fences. Danny has told Tom and Ruth he works for Kenji's group; in 2053 Kenji tells them it is a company he runs, and they keep it from Hannah at his request. Hannah learns the truth about Danny and about the two guards in spring 2060, after the bomb.

**39. Kenji's promise to Hannah (author ruling).** In January 2058, on the flight around the Moon (A2.14), he tells her: "I will never say a false thing to you." From then on he answers with true sentences that say nothing. He says "I can't answer that" when he will not answer. Later chapters stay true but empty.

**40. The memory seal on Level 2 treatment (decision, 5 November 2026).** Every Level 2 rejuvenation and longevity treatment seals the patient's memory of who, how, where, when, and why, and leaves one contact person. The patient remembers being treated and the contact, and nothing else of the treatment. It covers the elites from April 2055 and anyone treated outside the recruit, manager, and found-family tracks, and is applied again at every follow-up. There is nothing to divulge under duress. It is separate from the recruits' upload layers (ruling 37). Patients are told the terms before treatment. Recruits and managers keep their memories and are covered by their own conditioning. The found family is treated without it. Public Primitive patients are only asked for discretion. Cerberus's proof from 2055 is what bodies show, plus the one contact a patient remembers.

## 5. Relationships and events between characters

### The recruitment order and the network's first years
- **The order (author ruling, 3 November 2026).** Vance (summer 2051), Ashby (autumn 2051), the lawyers in October 2051 with the two of them standing guard, a larger team, then Byron in July 2052. Vance expands the team twice: four fighters in winter, four fighters and three intelligence staff in spring, a few months before Byron.
- **Whitcombe** joins February 2053, brought by Marchetti, her colleague of twenty years. Her factions bring the first elite buyers from April 2055. The first buyers need no threat shown, because the guards stand in the room, and the treatment leaves them remembering only their contact (ruling 40).
- **The two Level 5 guards** are finished in summer 2053. Vance stops walking behind Kenji and for a month feels the empty space.
- **Elite sales and the rival (A2.9, A2.15).** A rival elite whose peers return from private checkups ten years younger sets a watcher on the clients from April 2055 and, in November 2056, gets a best guess: the back dock of the Galveston clinic. Three raids follow, all by underworld hires: Galveston in December 2056 (three men through the dock gate at three in the morning), Singapore in August 2057 (six men, side entrance), Galveston in early 2058 (twelve men, the dock). The droids stop every crew, no raider returns, and nobody calls the police. He still has never seen a door, so he orders a bomb at the handover: a bought gate guard, a bought lamp contractor and a bought driver on the Thursday delivery.
- **Cerberus (ruling 28).** The network's record of incidents is Vance's green book. The first time anyone outside asks about the technology is November 2055, when a physician offers a network nurse money. His treated patient remembers nothing of the treatment but the nurse (ruling 40). She reports it the same day and the physician leaves the country within the week (A2.11). No one learns who he worked for.

- Tom is dying of pancreatic cancer in 2053. The Shepards are the first family Kenji grows close to, the found family, not recruits on a term. Tom takes the training upload but not the directive, becomes clinic facilities chief, and is among the night staff Hannah gets out in 2059.
