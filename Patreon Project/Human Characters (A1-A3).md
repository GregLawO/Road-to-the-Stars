## 1. Rulings

**1. The Protagonist.** Kenji Himura, of Japanese descent. Body is thirty in 2050 (born 2020). Proposes to Hannah on the Moon trip in January 2058 (A2.14) and marries her in June 2058 (A2.16).

**3. The 2059 assault.** In spring 2059 the rival elite of A2.9 and A2.15 (unnamed) orders an armored assault on the Galveston clinic complex. It is not Cerberus's. The underground group's own men, not individual hires, carry it out in BMUS with AEMS or AEPS-BV suits, a few bought and more stolen from other factions and groups. The attackers' advance team removes the human gate guard before 5:31, the dead third lamp is an ordinary maintenance failure, and the attackers take the Thursday delivery van and use it as the breach vehicle. The blast is their breach, on the lower building's side. The fight runs below ground and behind the main complex, between the attackers and the droids and Security Department. The rival knows only that something important is hidden in the underground building; he does not know about the Level 2 pods. No one identifies or claims the attackers. Danny Shepard, Hannah's younger brother, leads the defenders in a BMUS and AEPS-BV and survives the blast and the fight. Hannah, on leave, is at the staff door to meet him and counts the night staff out. She is not pregnant, and no child is lost. **[Slot: the cost of the fight is the author's decision (Revision List 9E). Until then, no death and no loss.]**

**5. The conspiracy.** Canon's Cerberus does not exist. The author-side label "Cerberus" belongs to the conspiracy of ruling 28.

**11. Who knows what.** Nobody in the story is ever told that Kenji is a transmigrator, and nobody learns where his foresight comes from. The reader learns it from his POV. Vance knows he traded his memories for treatment and that the droids' combat routines came from them. He is never told how Kenji knows what he knows. Nobody else is told where the droids' fighting style comes from.

**28. The conspiracy.** Cerberus acts against Kenji from 2055, when its first buyer approaches a client's physician. Its method is shadow control of the world's most advanced technologies, his above all, through cutouts, firms, regulators, and licensors. Its main interest is the technologies he lets spread, because it cannot find or take a Level 2 Med-Pod (author ruling, 5 November 2026). It is called "Cerberus" or "they," never "it." Its moves: the physician's offer (Nov 2055, A2.11), three offers for Oyama's patents (Jan, Apr, Oct 2058), and the forged letter that closes Galveston (Oct 2061). The raids (December 2056, August 2057, early 2058) and the 2059 assault are the work of a rival elite (ruling 3), not Cerberus. The network learns the name in 2060 (ruling 34). After these the network knows it as one organization but not who leads it, and Brooks is recruited in 2062 to find out. There is no 2056 shakedown: hospital chains, a pharmacists' guild, an insurance board and a health ministry only send inspection requests and complaints (A2.8).

**35. Oyama Industries and the robots (author ruling, 3 November 2026).** Oyama builds the Primitive Med-Pods after Byron is recruited (Kenji builds none). It also sells Level 1 to Level 3 robots, Tokamak reactors, Level 1 anti-gravity, and Gundarium Alpha. Byron is also the first target of Cerberus. Oyama puts its name on every plate, with a blank line where the designer should be. Anthony Strauss also runs Pellam Field Systems, which builds the AEMS and AEPS-BV suits, the BMUS, and the CFCS (decision).

**36. The terms of service (as drafted in A2.2 and A2.3).** Every recruit serves for life, a life given for a life. Every operator, Vance included, is employed on paper by the Janus Foundation, and Marchetti is its trustee, but her name is not public as trustee. An employer inquiry about any of them goes to the foundation's line, which rings at her firm's front desk; the attendant relays it to her and the caller never speaks to her (A2.19, author ruling, 8 November 2026). Treatment continues for as long as they serve, and it covers their family. The Janus Foundation carries every contract and every treatment, and Marchetti is trustee. She can refuse Kenji and put her objection in writing, but he holds every card and can replace her with another trustee, so her refusal is a check he honors by choice, not a veto (author ruling, 9 November 2026). If Janus ever stops paying, every design it owns becomes Oyama's to build and sell; Marchetti drafted that clause to protect Byron's workers.

**37. The Volitional Lock (author ruling).** Every main member is conditioned in a session immediately after their first treatment. Recruits are told about the secrecy directive before they sign, and it lasts for life. The lock blocks one thing: knowingly betraying the organization. A member cannot tell outsiders what it is, what it does, or who runs it, and cannot knowingly sell, hand over, or help an enemy obtain its people, sites, or technology. A member who is bought or threatened alone cannot be turned. It does not compel obedience. Members can disagree, refuse, defy the Leader, or resign, and a member who leaves keeps the lock for life. A member can always report a threat to the organization. Five protection tiers (the Leader and his loved ones; every member's family and the core inner circle; the organization's sites and assets; the member and their peers; outsiders) set only a lean toward whom to protect in a forced choice. The lean is never an order, and peers on the same tier are not ranked, so a member may save their own family before the leadership. Hostage release valve: if an enemy holds a member's family, the member can disclose general information about assets and units, but never anything that would expose a Tier 2 or Tier 1 person: names, safe houses, places they are or may be, the Leader, the leadership. Updates ride on follow-up treatments. Not conditioned: hired outsiders (gate staff, contractors, carrier drivers) and the found family (Hannah, who refuses the upload). Danny is conditioned like every main member, and from June 2058 he is Tier 1 as the Leader's brother-in-law (author ruling, 9 November 2026). Clinic staff who work inside the lower building and handle Level 2 pods are members. The full sheet is The_Volitional_Lock_Revised.md. No chapter or in-universe document may state it overtly. A chapter may allude to it only subtly: that someone is or is not a member, that members stay loyal, a member who stays silent, and the recruit-facing directive as drafted in A2.2. It never names the lock or the tiers, never uses the word "conditioned," and never says a member physically cannot betray (author ruling, 8 November 2026). The memory seal is a separate system (ruling 40).

**38. What Hannah knows about Danny (author ruling and decision, 9 November 2026).** Hannah knows her younger brother Danny works for the Foundation; she countersigned his release as his guardian (A1.1). He keeps from her only Foundation information and answers "I can't say." Her written terms, drafted by Marchetti, give her scheduled contact, a visit right, and notice within a day of any serious injury to Danny or change of city. She never receives site details. Kenji agrees at once, because he can answer it truthfully. She learns what the two guards are in spring 2060.

**39. Kenji's promise to Hannah (author ruling).** In January 2058, on the flight around the Moon (A2.14), he tells her: "I will never say a false thing to you." From then on he answers with true sentences that say nothing. He says "I can't answer that" when he will not answer. Later chapters stay true but empty.

**40. The memory seal on Level 2 treatment (decision, 5 November 2026).** Every Level 2 rejuvenation and longevity treatment seals the patient's memory of who, how, where, when, and why, and leaves one contact person. The patient remembers being treated and the contact, and nothing else of the treatment. It covers the elites from April 2055 and anyone treated outside the recruit, manager, and found-family tracks, and is applied again at every follow-up. There is nothing to divulge under duress. It is separate from the recruits' upload layers (ruling 37). Patients are told the terms before treatment. Recruits and managers keep their memories and are covered by their own conditioning. The found family is treated without it. Public Primitive patients are only asked for discretion. Cerberus's proof from 2055 is what bodies show, plus the one contact a patient remembers.

## 5. Relationships and events between characters
