# Ruth Shepard: Character Profile

Items marked **(decision)** are invented gap-fills.

## 1. Basic Identity & Demographics
- **Name & nicknames.** Ruth Shepard. Tom says "Ruthie" to get out of an argument. Hannah says "Mom." Kenji says "Ma'am" before the wedding, "Mom" after. Dislikes "Mama Shepard." **(decision)**
- **Age.** Born 2003; forty-seven in 2050. **(decision)**
- **Appearance.** Small, straight-backed, short gray hair, reading glasses on her head. A cardigan in every season, a ring of school keys on her belt, each labelled in her handwriting, the last "spare, ask me." **(decision)** Sits where she can see the door and faces at once; watches hands as people speak.
- **Voice & mannerisms.** Soft Gulf Coast accent; working Spanish from students' parents. Warm, patient, complete; gentle questions, then waits. Straightens a napkin or collar that does not need it; when it is bad she folds her hands in her lap, one holding the other wrist, motionless. Warm, teasing humor about children and schools; never jokes about a child in trouble.

## 2. Psychology & Internal World
- **Core goal.** Immediate: every child in the building looked after before the day ends. Overall: her daughter happy and safe with someone who stays.
- **Needs vs. wants.** She wants to see Hannah safe. She needs to be told what is really going on in her family.
- **Internal conflict.** Her daughter's marriage against her own question. She asked Kenji "who are you?" once, got a true answer that told her nothing, and kept it to herself rather than put her doubt between Hannah and her husband.
- **Flaws.** Assumes people who look after others are good, and reads a man's devotion as proof of his honesty. Sees Kenji paying a price but assumes private grief, not a plan. Slow to see care used as control. Honesty 4 of 5: says kind things that are also true; lies only to comfort a frightened child.
- **Fears & secrets.** Fear: being helpless while someone she loves is in trouble. Secret: she has noticed strangers watching her family (a man by the back door, a machine in the car park) and told no one, not even Tom.
- **Worldview.** Grateful and watchful; refuses the knowledge upload; keeps wondering who is paying for all of it.

## 3. Backstory & History
- **Upbringing & family.** Galveston, a school district she has never left. Her mother looked after everyone and no one looked after her. **(decision)** Memory: her mother washing dishes late at night, no one noticing.
- **Defining moment.** She watched her mother die tired and unlooked-after, then watched her husband nearly die and could do nothing but make tea; she cried only in the car.
- **Education & occupation.** School administrator; second-best, hospital patient advocate. **(decision)** Owns her house and her good shoes.
- **Prior relationships.** Owes Kenji for Tom's life, and the grandchild lost in 2059.

## 4. Story Role & Stakes
- **Function.** Found family; no POV in A2 or A3. Took Level 2 treatment but refused the upload: no extra knowledge, no conditioning. Does not know who is behind the clinics or why.
- **External conflict.** The unexplained organization around her family.
- **Stakes.** A child hurt because she did not ask in time, or Hannah hurt because she kept her doubt to herself.
- **Character arc.** She says "Not yet, then" to Kenji, giving him time and promising nothing. She sets a place for Danny at Sunday dinner every week. She sits hurting people down with food or drink before asking a question, and says no politely and firmly in front of people who want a yes when a decision would harm a child.
- **Rules for the text.** No worried-mother-only or comfort-only scenes, no victim scenes, no working out secrets by herself, no long speeches.

## 5. Dialogue Voice Guide **(decision; built from this profile; sample lines are mine, not canon)**

**1. Vocabulary & Word Choice.**
- School-office warmth with authority under it. She says "we" for instructions: "Let's sit down." She offers food or drink before a hard question.
- "Mm-hm" keeps people talking. "I'll need you to..." means she is serious. She says "Tommy" to Tom when teasing or worried, and "Daniel" when worried about Danny. Working Spanish with parents.
- Samples: "Let's sit down, all of us. Tom, move your boots. Kenji, will you have tea?" / "Mm-hm. And then what happened?" / "I'll need you to wait here, Daniel."

**2. Sentence Rhythm & Punctuation.**
- A warm opening, one real sentence, then she waits. She phrases questions as invitations: "I wonder if you would tell me about it."
- She softens a refusal with one small practical offer. She does not interrupt.
- Full forms except "let's" and "I'll."
- Reply length: two or three sentences, with the question last. A one-line reply, usually "No.", is about one in five.

**3. Nervous Habits & Tells.**
- Doubting someone, she asks one kind question and does not follow it up.
- Worried, she gets more formal and uses full names. Refusing, she repeats the same sentence in the same words, no louder.
- She straightens a collar or pours tea instead of voicing a suspicion. She lies only to comfort a frightened child.

**4. Dialogue Traps (What to Avoid).**
- Never "I'm worried sick," "my baby," or "You can tell me anything."
- No deducing a secret aloud, and no comfort speeches.
- She names what she saw, not what it means.
