# Hannah Shepard: Character Profile

Items marked **(decision)** are invented gap-fills, open to overrule.

## 1. Basic Identity & Demographics
- **Name & nicknames.** Hannah Shepard. Her parents are dead and never appear. **(decision)**
- **Age.** Born 2031; twenty-two on the A1.1 morning, when her brother walks out of the clinic and she refuses the training session. **(decision)**
- **Appearance.** A pale burn scar the length of her left forearm from the 2059 blast, never covered. Short, practical hair; uniform squared and clean. Stands with weight on both feet, never leaning, hands always busy while she listens.
- **Voice & mannerisms.** Gulf Coast Texas accent that returns when tired or angry; working Spanish from the Gulf Coast yards, used to calm frightened people. **(decision)** Plain, measured, complete; says what she means in a few sentences, then stays silent until the other person speaks. Presses her thumb along the scar; when it is bad she goes very still and quiet. Dry, shipyard-grim humor at her own expense or the weather.

## 2. Psychology & Internal World
- **Core goal.** Immediate: a straight answer from someone, a day without discovering another missing record, person, or explanation. Overall: to keep her brother whole and within reach, and to learn whose work this is. **(decision)**
- **Needs vs. wants.** She wants the whole truth from the man she married and would trade a great deal of it to keep her brother safe. She needs to ask the questions she avoids.
- **Internal conflict.** Split duty and loyalty: what she tells the Navy against what she keeps for her husband and brother, and her husband against her sense of what is right. She stays, serves, and says openly when she disagrees. The cost is that she cannot fully trust him. **(decision)**
- **Flaws.** Misreads silence: takes her husband's silences as protection when they are often fear or guilt, and assumes anyone keeping a secret has a good reason until the gap is too large to excuse. Avoids questions whose answers she suspects she cannot bear. Lies only about whether she is all right.
- **Fears & secrets.** Fear: losing her only family, and being used without knowing it. Secret: a private list of every question she chose not to ask her husband, each dated, which also holds what she did not report to the Navy. Shown only inside her head. **(decision)**
- **Worldview.** Trusts a gift that heals, because she watched her brother walk out on his own feet and ask for breakfast; distrusts a gift with no giver. Distrusts people who manage the truth for others' own good.

## 3. Backstory & History
- **Upbringing & family.** Galveston shipyard district; everyone knew everyone's wages and illnesses. Her parents are dead and never appear. Her younger brother Danny is her only living family, and she is his legal guardian. Her father taught her to check the weld. **(decision)**
- **Defining moment.** The 2059 assault on the Galveston clinic complex. On leave, she walked to the staff door at the end of the night shift to meet Danny for breakfast, and counted the night staff out by the name posted at each exit. She refused care until the count was confirmed, then asked where her brother was. Underneath: someone knew more than they told her. The cost of the fight is the author's open slot (Revision List 9E).
- **Education & occupation.** Active-service UNAS Navy officer who never resigns. Standard promotion: ensign from February 2053 (one gold stripe in A1.1), lieutenant (junior grade) from February 2055, lieutenant from February 2057. Duty station on the Gulf Coast, near Galveston. From spring 2054 the Navy's liaison to the Janus-linked suppliers (Oyama and Pellam), which makes her the link through which the UNAS armed forces acquire technology from Kenji's side. She does not know Kenji is the source, only that he has influence over the people who produce it. **(decision)** Debt from Danny's hospital bills, near-zero savings; owns very little, mostly her father's toolbox and his tape measure.
- **Prior relationships.** Refused the clinic's training session in 2053 and has been polite and cold to its operators since. Her father's tape measure is the object she measures with when unsure. She and Kenji mirror each other, both nearly alone, and the "who looks after you?" motif passes between them.

## 4. Story Role & Stakes
- **Function.** Major POV; the character who notices the gaps and keeps asking.
- **External conflict.** The secrets of her husband's organization; Cerberus.
- **Stakes.** Her brother's life and her people's lives; learning what she was kept from.
- **Character arc.** She goes quiet when given a partial answer, works out afterward what not asking cost her, moves toward danger when someone in her care is threatened, and says it once, plainly, when she disagrees with an order. She never leaves behind someone she could reach.
- **Rules for the text.** No speeches, no wronged-wife-only scenes, no convenient forgiveness. No unexplained knowledge of what she has not been told.

## 5. Dialogue Voice Guide **(decision; built from this profile; sample lines are mine, not canon)**

**1. Vocabulary & Word Choice.**
- Plain, counted nouns. She opens with "Okay, so." and asks for names, times, and places. "Good" and "fine" mean yes.
- Short Spanish with frightened people: "Tranquilo. Aquí estoy." Gulf Coast ("y'all," "fixing to") comes out only when she is tired or angry.
- Grim humor, aimed at herself or the weather.
- Samples: "Okay, so. Who's on the door tonight? Not who's scheduled. Who's actually there." / "I'm good. Where's the sign-in sheet?" / "That's not what I asked."

**2. Sentence Rhythm & Punctuation.**
- Short counted items, not paragraphs. She says what she means in a few complete sentences, then stays silent until the other person speaks.
- She asks the same question twice in different words. She cuts in only after she has waited through an answer, and only on the part that dodged.
- Reply length: usually two or three sentences. Her one-line cut-ins are about one in five, never more than two in a row.

**3. Nervous Habits & Tells.**
- A partial answer makes her exact about the part she did get (names, times) and she returns to the gap later.
- Angry, she gets slower and quieter and the accent shows. She says "I'm good" quickly about being all right, then asks for a task. Afraid, she talks logistics.

**4. Dialogue Traps (What to Avoid).**
- Never "We need to talk," "How could you?", "I deserve the truth," "I feel like," "boundaries," "I trust you," or "I forgive you."
- No therapy words and no explaining her own feelings.


## 6. Story rules (moved from Specific Writing Rules, 9 November 2026)
- **Honesty.** 4 of 5.
- **Her list.** The list of questions she chose not to ask stays secret. It is shown only inside her head. It also holds what she did not report to the Navy; the Moon flight and the ring (A2.14) are the first thing she chooses not to report (decision).
- **Kenji and Hannah (A2b and A2c).** Every Hannah-and-Kenji micro-chapter (A2.8, A2.13, A2.14, A2.16) has at least one scene where the two talk, and something between them is different by the end. The change shows in what they say and do, not in what an object means. Kenji tells her no false thing (the A2.14 promise).
- **How she meets Kenji (decision).** Ten days after Danny's release she comes alone to demand the man in charge. She measures the room with her father's tape, asks who he is, and gets a true answer that tells her nothing. She says she will keep asking.
- **Her terms for Danny (decision).** In writing (Marchetti drafts it), she receives scheduled contact, a visit right, and notice within a day of any serious injury to Danny or change of city. She never receives site details. Kenji agrees at once, because he can answer it truthfully.
- **The Navy.** She never resigns, and no private firm follows. She tells Kenji she was asked to be the Navy's liaison; he answers that what she tells the Navy is hers to decide and that he will tell her no false thing.
- **Conditioning.** She refuses the upload and is not conditioned. She is the only found-family member.
- **The two Level 5 droids.** She takes them for men until spring 2060.
- **Rank (decision).** Ensign from February 2053, lieutenant (junior grade) from February 2055, lieutenant from February 2057.
- **POV in A2.** A2.8 and A2.13. No fixed count of her POV chapters for now (author, 7 November 2026).
