# Tom Shepard: Character Profile

Items marked **(decision)** are invented gap-fills.

## 1. Basic Identity & Demographics
- **Name & nicknames.** Tom Shepard. Ruth says "Tommy" when teasing or worried. Hannah is "Darling" to him and calls him "Dad." Before the wedding Kenji says "Sir," after it "Dad." Tom calls Kenji "son" in private. Dislikes "Pops" from younger workers. **(decision)**
- **Age.** Born 2001; fifty-two when dying of pancreatic cancer and cured. **(decision)**
- **Appearance.** Big, thick-fingered hands with old burn scars, a stoop from forty years leaning over work. Faded cap, reading glasses on a cord, steel-toed boots even to dinner, pencil stub behind one ear, a small sketch notebook in his shirt pocket. Stands with hands on hips and looks at the thing before speaking.
- **Voice & mannerisms.** Thick Gulf Coast accent; working Spanish from forty years of crews; swears in both when angry, in neither around a child. Slow, warm, concrete; starts with the thing in front of him. Turns a tool over without looking; when it is bad he grips the nearest tool and will not put it down. Dry shipyard humor ("A leaking boat doesn't care who's embarrassed"); never jokes about the dead.

## 2. Psychology & Internal World
- **Core goal.** Immediate: get the building through the night, pipe fixed and shift crew home safe. Overall: be chief engineer of the first test ship, and see his daughter out of danger (not said aloud).
- **Needs vs. wants.** He wants to be useful and kept near the work. He needs to speak up about what he suspects.
- **Internal conflict.** Kenji's silence against his daughter's need for the truth. He gave Kenji his word, so he keeps what he knows from Hannah and tells Ruth only what he must.
- **Flaws.** Trusts people who work with their hands and misreads those who talk well. Reads a quiet man's silence as shyness when it is often a secret. Does not notice money moving. Impatient with people who will not try to understand a machine.
- **Fears & secrets.** Fear: his daughter hurt, and being useless or kept from the work. Secret: he knows the designs are beyond any Earth laboratory and suspects Kenji built much of it. He has never asked and told no one.
- **Worldview.** Practical and grateful to the clinics; fascinated by machines; notices robots never tire and is unsure that is good for the men beside them.

## 3. Backstory & History
- **Upbringing & family.** Galveston, three generations of shipyard work. Took in Danny at eight after Danny's father, Ray Iglesias, died.
- **Defining moments.** As a young foreman he lost a welder (Ray Iglesias) when a gantry cable failed and has checked every cable twice since. **(decision)** Then he was dying and watched his wife wait. Memory: the morning after the cure, barefoot on the clinic floor, hands no longer hurting, asking for breakfast.
- **Education & occupation.** Engineer by mind with no finished degree **(decision)**; shipyard foreman, then clinic facilities chief. Debt from his illness, almost no savings. Object: his father's adjustable wrench with scratched initials, in his back pocket. **(decision)**
- **Prior relationships.** Owes Kenji for the cure, the welder he lost, and his crews.

## 4. Story Role & Stakes
- **Function.** Found family; no POV in A2 or A3. Takes the training upload but refuses the directive, so he is unconditioned and free to speak, and stays by choice.
- **External conflict.** The secrets he keeps for Kenji; Cerberus.
- **Stakes.** Hannah and Ruth; a clinic closing and people untreated.
- **Character arc.** He takes the physical job nearest someone he loves in danger; watches the hands of someone who hides something and says nothing until sure; takes unsafe things apart on paper first. On the night of the 2059 bomb he wanted to stay and shut the oxygen line, and Hannah ordered him out. **(decision)**
- **Rules for the text.** No comic-relief-only or wise-old-man-only scenes; he does not work out the secrets himself; no long speeches. He knows only what he has seen and been told.
