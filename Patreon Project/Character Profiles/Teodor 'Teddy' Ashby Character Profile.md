# Teodor "Teddy" Ashby: Character Profile

Items marked **(decision)** are invented gap-fills.

## 1. Basic Identity & Demographics
- **Name & nicknames.** Teodor Ashby, "Ashby." Vance says "kid" when worried. His old squad called him "Sparks" and he no longer answers to it. **(decision)** Kenji says "Teodor."
- **Age.** Born 2030; nineteen when the squad is destroyed, twenty-one in 2051, thirty-three in 2063 on the test range.
- **Appearance.** Lean, wiry, not tall, close-cropped dark hair, pale eyes that look slightly to one side when listening. An old mechanic's stethoscope around his neck, earpieces taped where they cracked. **(decision)** A faint hitch in the left leg in cold weather and a scar on the same knee.
- **Voice & mannerisms.** Flat Minnesota accent; some Polish from his grandmother, mostly swearing and endearments. **(decision)** Quiet, precise; repeats a number back to be sure. Observation first, explanation second. Tilts his head, goes very still, touches the stethoscope; when it is bad he takes it off and holds it in both hands. Deadpan humor about machines ("If it's loud, I'm employed. If it's quiet, I'm worried"); never jokes about the squad.

## 2. Psychology & Internal World
- **Core goal.** Immediate: the range log signed before the crews rotate. Overall: no machine fails silently again.
- **Needs vs. wants.** He wants the machines to be honest. He needs the squad's names said aloud by someone who knew them (not said aloud).
- **Internal conflict.** Justice for the squad against loyalty to Vance. The army wanted him to testify against the officer who sent them in; he refused and joined Vance, the officer was never held to account, and Ashby lost his pension. **(decision)**
- **Flaws.** Distrusts a machine that never complains and assumes it is hiding something. Expects people to show a fault before failing and is thrown when they fail without warning. Reads silence as danger and cannot tell calm from concealment. Honesty 4 of 5: silent when unsure, and he paid for that once.
- **Fears & secrets.** Fear: silence, a machine too quiet. He will not sign off on anything he has not listened to; only a deadline with lives behind it could make him, and then he signs with a written note of what he does not know. Secret: a few hours before the ambush he heard a drift in the relay radio's timing and decided it was nothing, and has told no one, including Vance. **(decision)**
- **Worldview.** Loves machines and is uneasy about them; the new drive worries him because it makes no noise and gives him nothing to catch.

## 3. Backstory & History
- **Upbringing & family.** Duluth, Minnesota, a cold port of ore ships and radio towers; a house full of repaired radios and a Polish-speaking grandmother. **(decision)**
- **Defining moment.** The ambush on bad intelligence, the relay radio's drift, and the two seconds he froze before dragging the wounded to cover; he has never forgiven himself.
- **Education & occupation.** Field technician and systems diagnostician; second-best, radio and electronics repair. Medical discharge and disability pension at first; later sends a share monthly to the dead squad's families.
- **Prior relationships.** Owes Vance, who found him after the trenches, Kenji for the treatment that fixed his knee and lungs, and the squad.

## 4. Story Role & Stakes
- **Function.** Author-built locked character; first POV A2.14 (2063). Recruited autumn 2051, a few months after Vance, as Kenji's radio and communications man; one more network engineer joins him autumn 2052 (decision). Serves for life. Conditioned from the start of his service. He and Vance guard Kenji, including when Kenji recruits the lawyers (October 2051), until the two Level 5 guards are finished in 2055. Does not know who is behind the network or why.
- **External conflict.** A drive that gives him nothing to hear.
- **Stakes.** A machine he approved failing and killing people.
- **Arc.** He reports every fault in writing at once, even when no one wants to know, and says "all right" and keeps listening when told not to worry. He teaches others to listen and reads the squad's names aloud on the day they died.
- **Rules for the text.** No nerd-only or damaged-veteran-only scenes, no long technical explanation of the drive, no jokes about the dead squad.
