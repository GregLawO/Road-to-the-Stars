Drafting Checklist — The Iron Aegis of Amaterasu

Use before and after each batch. Every item is yes or no.

---

Batch structure

1. Does this batch hold 5 POV segments?
2. Does each POV segment run 500 to 1,500 words?
3. Does batch 1 of a chapter open with its excerpt, taken word for word from the Excerpt Bank (title, italic quoted text, dash plus Annals of the Chrysanthemum Restoration or Post-Settlement Naval Monograph, Kyoto)?
4. Does batch 2 or later open with a recap of the previous batch that tells only what those POV segments showed?
5. If this batch reveals any new technology to the reader, does the batch end with a list of only those technologies?
6. Does the batch follow the chapter's ending plan, with at most 2 P endings and the strongest ending on segment 5?
7. Does each POV segment have a concrete opening that raises a question in the reader's mind?

---

POV and casting

8. Is every POV character at least age 8, alive, and present for what the segment shows?
9. Do all characters match their exact canonical ages and lifecycles for the scene's era (e.g., Emperor Himura b. 2097, Togo b. 2085, Kurosawa b. 2090, Jon Grissom b. 2112, Alec Ryder b. 2129, Hackett b. 2134, Anderson b. 2137; no post-2150 characters like Shepard or Miranda Lawson in 2122–2149 scenes)?
10. Are Quantum-Optical VIs, Ashigaru-4 / Kikai-Bushi / Oni-Kikai automata, and unexcepted Hatamoto or household servants kept out of POV?
11. Does every character know only what their timeline and reveal path allow?

---

Secrecy and sources

12. Are all excerpts and in-world documents written without first-person voice, without an author name, and without secrets the story has not yet revealed?
13. Does the prose avoid quoting SEC-401-IJN-LOCK, avoid using the word "conditioned," and show the Volitional Lock solely through physical symptoms (jaw clamp, frozen vocal cords, stiff fingers, or overdrive nosebleed/fainting)?
14. Are Emperor Himura's foreknowledge of the wider galactic threat and the Reapers kept strictly inside his own POV and reflected only through his policies prior to the 2148 Mars Prothean Archives discovery?
15. Are living aliens and the Citadel kept completely out of all 2122–2150+ scenes (First Contact War is 2157)?
16. Does the draft enforce strict Imperial non-interference—keeping hereditary Daimyo and Vassal Clan titles exclusively to Japanese noble houses on sovereign Japanese territory and Japanese space colonies, with zero Imperial administration over China, Korea, or Taiwan?

---

Technology, register, and world rules

17. Does every technology act strictly within its era tier (Pre-War 2122–2132 Solaris-4 at 45% mass-shedding with no micro-jumps; WWIII 2132–2136 Solaris-7 at 60%; Post-War 2136–2150+ Solaris-9 at 75% hard limit; foreign powers restricted to licensed blackboxed Helios-2/5/8 at 25%/35%/50%)?
18. Do Ashigaru-4, Kikai-Bushi, and Oni-Kikai automata hold fixed posts in complete silence with dimmed ember optical slits, moving and making noise only on an authorized command or security trigger?
19. Are Emperor Himura, High Censor Kurosawa, and Kinki-Kyoku engineers written with full mastery and zero doubt about how their own Solaris and Quantum-Optical systems work?
20. Does the prose keep group register strict (Quantum-Optical VI instead of AI, Audit-Marshals, Ashigaru / Kikai-Bushi instead of droids or robots, divisions or packs for escorts, Legions or Divisions for ground forces) and preserve set terms and recurring location names verbatim?

---

Two gears and clarity

21. Is roughly 80% of each POV segment written in Slow gear (1 to 5 sentences per paragraph, 5 to 22 words per sentence)?
22. Does Fast gear trigger at a clear physical cue and use 1 to 2 line paragraphs with 2 to 7 word sentences focused on external body action and one physical detail?
23. Can every sentence be read once without pausing, using plain everyday verbs, no unexplained symbolic gestures, and no tacked-on explanation tails?

---

Dialogue

24. Does dialogue carry subtext rather than lecturing the reader on worldbuilding?
25. Are speech tags kept simple (said, asked) with physical action beats instead of adverbs or theatrical tags?
26. Do characters speak in natural multi-sentence turns with distinct rhythms rather than ping-pong one-liners?
