# Characters and Ranks: Road to the Stars (2095 to October 2132)

**POV tiers (author ruling).** Main POV characters carry the most chapters. Secondary POV characters carry fewer. Non-POV characters appear only through other POVs. The full roster is in the Factions file.

**Labels.** An entry with no label is stated in the Factions, Technology or Timeline file. (profile) marks a line that came from the author's character profile, now held in this file. (decision) is my choice, open to overrule. (open) is unsettled. (author ruling) is a ruling already recorded in the core files.

**Profile format (decision).** Each main character has one profile of six numbered lines: (1) name, title and faction; (2) POV status and tech tier; (3) core goal; (4) core flaw or pressure; (5) view of the Emperor; (6) key trait. Line 5 for Kenji is his view of himself, since he is the Emperor.

**Tech tier key (decision).**

- **True Proprietary:** Solaris cores, Kurogane Yoroi, the Imperial automata, the Quantum-Optical VI and the Kurogane-4 and 5 method. Clan Himura only.
- **Vassal Gen 2:** Helios-3, AEPS-3 and Kurogane-2, whether issued units or the loyal clans' own manufacture. Also the Exo-Rigs of Taiwan and South Korea.
- **Foreign Gen 1:** The Helios-1 lineage: Helios-1, AEPS-1, Ashigaru-1 and Kurogane-1, held by the traitor clans before the war's midpoint, and the UNAS, EU and Russian versions built from the stolen schematics.
- **Stolen/Conventional:** Stolen schematics and conventional hardware, for anyone with no stated generation.

## 2. CLAN HIMURA AND THE IMPERIAL FAMILY

### 2.1 Emperor Himura Kenji

1. Emperor Himura Kenji, Tennō and Clan Head (Tōshu), Empire of Japan. Addressed as *Heika*. Sovereign since the 2122 Restoration.
2. Non-POV (author ruling). True Proprietary: he alone holds the Crown Foundries, the Solaris patents, the Quantum-Optical VI architecture, the Throneside Medical Bureau, every Solaris core, and the Kurogane-4 and 5 method begun in 2128. Tier I from 2116. No Volitional Lock. He holds foreknowledge, and only he and Kaguya do; Kaguya has the dream-level warning only.
3. Wants the Empire through the October 2132 outbreak on hulls and Legions that work as designed. He solves the problem on the table so he can get back to the work or the dinner. He keeps the Kurogane-4 and 5 method and every Solaris core in his own hands, since the 2127 theft showed the shared method leaks.
4. The pressure comes from people, never machines: the 2125 treachery, the stolen schematics, Ryunosuke's push for mainland enclaves, and Kaguya's fear for their children. He trusts his own engineering completely, never doubts a machine he built, and shows no dread.
5. Self-view: he carries the throne as work, not burden. He states the cost before anyone signs or swears, delegates the signing, and eats well. Past-life hints stay under three sentences per chapter.
6. Plain-spoken, decisive.


### 2.2 Empress Himura Kaguya

1. Empress Himura Kaguya, Sovereign Consort, Empire of Japan (Clan Himura).
2. Non-POV (author ruling). True Proprietary: Tier I from 2116, no Lock. Keeper of records, medicine and household order (profile). She knows only the dream-level warning.
3. Wants the people in front of her fed, treated and whole before the war's first burden lands. She runs the household and is a step ahead of everyone in it (decision).
4. She never asks where the warning comes from (profile). She fears her children will resent parents who do not age, and that her eldest will resent a throne that never opens to him (Factions).
5. Kenji is her partner. She says *Heika* in public and "Kenji" in private (decision). She does not pry, nag or plead after a refusal.
6. Composed, attentive.

### 2.3 Crown Prince Himura Kiyoshi

1. Crown Prince Himura Kiyoshi (Kōtaishi), Legion ground officer, Empire of Japan. 19 in October 2132.
2. Main POV (author ruling). True Proprietary: Kurogane Yoroi-3 and BMUS-3, leading a five-man fireteam of four Imperial automata (decision on the suit and the rank; he is not yet a junior officer before the war). No Lock, no foreknowledge, not even dream-level. Tier I only after he stops demanding the throne.
3. Wants a fireteam of his own and a place among soldiers earned by what he does (inference).
4. He resents the Hatamoto shadow Tachibana imposes, which tells him every day that others value his life above his men's. He is proud and impatient, and until he stops demanding the throne he pushes for something his father cannot give him. He is the son of two people who will not age, and Kōji presses the succession question until he chooses to stay a soldier.
5. He says *Chichi-sama* (decision). He loves Kenji and does not fully understand him, and he knows his father's policies only as policy and guesses at nothing behind them. Write Kenji asking him the plain practical question first, as he does about any job.
6. Proud, impatient.

- **Tachibana:** He trusts her, since she trains him, even while he resents the guard she imposes.

### 2.4 Himura Ayame and Himura Haruto

- **Ayame:** Born 2126, so 5 or 6 in October 2132. Holds Tier I. No Lock, no foreknowledge.
- **Haruto:** Born after this era. Holds Tier I. No Lock, no foreknowledge.
- **POV:** None. Ayame and Haruto are non-POV (author ruling).

### 2.5 Grand Uncle Masamune Himura

1. Grand Uncle Masamune Himura, senior elder and council skeptic, Clan Himura.
2. Non-POV (author ruling). True Proprietary by household. He has declined Chōju since 2116 and again in 2131, so he carries no Lock (inference: the Lock follows a first Tier II immersion).
3. Wants the young kept from overreach until their foundations are dry. He counts prices and opposes the seizure of mainland enclaves Ryunosuke demands after the outbreak.
4. His pessimism comes from real national decline. He is slow to trust new methods and hides affection behind gruffness. He is visibly aging because he refuses treatment.
5. Fierce, unspoken pride in the kinsman who rebuilt the house. He calls him "nephew" (profile) and challenges every plan with its casualty cost. He yields with one nod when proven wrong.
6. Gruff, cautious.

### 2.6 Ryunosuke Himura

1. Ryunosuke Himura, cousin of Kenji (profile), Imperial Fleet officer, Clan Himura.
2. Main POV, Imperial Fleet (author ruling). True Proprietary: Solaris-3 hulls once he holds a ship.
3. Wants a real command and respect earned on his own record. He argues for aggressive patrols to claim the Earth-Luna corridor, and after the 2132 outbreak demands seizure of mainland enclaves.
4. He is impulsive, prestige-minded and hurt when shut out of the inner circle. He keeps a notebook of errors only after his first real setback (profile).
5. Competitive and prickly toward Kenji, with envy under real loyalty. He arrives five minutes early to council to take the seat nearest the throne. He is never a traitor.
6. Ambitious, theatrical.

### 2.7 Lady Shizuka Himura

1. Lady Shizuka Himura, older kinswoman, master of shifts, tonnage and rations, Clan Himura.
2. Main POV, internal affairs (author ruling). True Proprietary by position. She has no foreknowledge and no Lock, and she sees only what her ledger and the household show her.
3. Wants every dock and the low-orbit yard running at the 2131 rate. The 2132 batch on the slips (12 corvettes, 3 frigates, 2 interceptor cruisers, 2 Line Cruisers) must reach the fleet from late 2132. She cuts projects that do not ship.
4. She is a workaholic who ignores her own fatigue. She reads Kenji's moves only from the orders that reach her desk, and one bottleneck could leave the front short.
5. She says *Heika* in court, work and official settings, and "Kenji" only when they speak as members of the Clan (author ruling). Her care is food and rest orders: double rations and trauma rotations, "sit down and finish the soup."
6. Brisk, blunt.

### 2.8 Himura Kōji

- **Role:** Court traditionalist. Presses the succession question until Kiyoshi chooses to stay a soldier.
- **POV:** None. Kōji is non-POV (author ruling).

## 3. THE IMPERIAL HOUSEHOLD CIRCLE

Togo, Kurosawa, Yamashita and Tachibana begin Tier II Chōju in 2124, and each receives the Volitional Lock after his or her first immersion. Hatamoto officers follow in batches as they reach command rank. Ranks and titles below are (decision) unless a file states them.

### 3.1 Fleet Admiral Togo Kazuhiko

1. Fleet Admiral Togo Kazuhiko, commander of the Imperial Fleet (decision), Empire of Japan. Former naval officer in Kenji's circle.
2. Non-POV (author ruling). True Proprietary. Tier II from 2124, with the Lock.
3. Wants his crews home. He has trained the first crews since 2116, and each hull's crew drills six months in lunar shadow under his staff before commissioning. He wants officers to learn in drills, not in coffins.
4. He blames himself silently for every casualty. His jaw locks rigid and his throat clicks if pressed on forbidden detail.
5. Fiercely loyal to the sovereign (profile). Write him reporting in bearings and seconds and never questioning an order.
6. Disciplined, exact.

### 3.2 High Censor Kurosawa Renji

1. High Censor Kurosawa Renji, head of the Kansatsu-In (decision on the title), Empire of Japan.
2. Non-POV (author ruling). True Proprietary. Tier II from 2124, with the Lock.
3. Wants every leak sealed. He guards every core, foundry and shaft. Right now he wants to learn which Japanese port office stamped the Pact's launch codes (Yokohama, October 12 to 13).
4. He assumes conspiracy until disproved and isolates himself from peers. He presses two fingers to his breastbone and his jaw clamps when the Lock strains (profile). He regrets ruining honest people who broke a seal out of curiosity.
5. Total loyalty, and fully honest with the sovereign alone (profile). Write him reporting briefly and never asking for reassurance.
6. Exact, patient.

- **Mind (profile):** Takes no pleasure in pain. Notices small discrepancies, such as a replaced seal or a late timestamp. Arrives after the trap is shut and offers one clean chance.

### 3.3 General Yamashita Takeo

1. General Yamashita Takeo, commander of the Imperial ground forces (decision), Empire of Japan.
2. Non-POV (author ruling). True Proprietary: the 25 Legions (100,000 each in 2132, growing to 400,000 each by the Settlement). Tier II from 2124, with the Lock.
3. Wants to take ground and bring soldiers back, spending steel instead of blood. He pulls troops back once the objective is secured.
4. He is impatient with proud staff and leads from too far forward. His Lock shows as jaw clamp, throat click, stiff fingers, or nosebleed under pressure.
5. Fiercely protective of the sovereign (profile). He speaks to him bluntly and plainly, with no court polish.
6. Blunt, forward.

### 3.4 Lord Commander Tachibana Haruka

1. Lord Commander Tachibana Haruka, commander of the Hatamoto Guard, the Kyoto garrison and the Imperial Guard Fleet (IJN-KNE-), Empire of Japan.
2. Secondary POV (author ruling). True Proprietary: Kurogane Yoroi-3 and gravitic-shearing mono-blades. Tier II from 2124, with the Lock.
3. Wants no threat to Kenji, Kaguya or Kiyoshi to cross a threshold. She commands about 1,500 sworn Hatamoto and the Guard Fleet (40 hulls by 2132, decision), and holds the Kyoto garrison as the war opens.
4. She treats any scratch on a protected person as her failure. Her jaw tightens when Kenji refuses to leave a forward zone, and Kiyoshi resents the Hatamoto shadow she imposes on him.
5. Unconditional loyalty shown through action, such as stepping between Kenji and a threat (profile). She reports in under four sentences.
6. Economical, lethal.


### 3.5 Audit-Marshal Sano Mitsuko

1. Audit-Marshal Sano Mitsuko (Kansatsu-Kan), Kansatsu-In, Empire of Japan.
2. Secondary POV (author ruling). True Proprietary by service, exact suit open. Her Chōju tier and Volitional Lock follow her rank and position (author ruling).
3. Wants the Yokohama trail followed to its end. With Kurosawa she recovers the Pact's encrypted launch codes at Yokohama on October 12 and 13, 2132, stamped with a seal from a Japanese port office.
4. She is junior and keeps half a pace behind Kurosawa. She has seen his answers stop mid-sentence and does not ask why, and she is learning to live with the Directorate answering some questions with silence. She never names the Lock in anyone.
5. She reaches Kenji only through Kurosawa, so write him offstage or unaddressed.
6. Careful, patient.

3. Wants foreign backing to replace Himura as the Imperial Clan, as found in 2125. He keeps the Kurogane-1, AEPS and Ashigaru schematics from his backers so they cannot abandon him.

5. The Emperor is the rival he means to replace. He resents the denied Helios-3 and does not hate Kenji. Write him as a man with a plan, not a villain.
6. Calculating, stubborn (decision).

### 6.1 Individual profiles


#### Williams

1. General Williams, UNAS.
2. Main POV. Foreign Gen 1: UNAS Helios-1 (2128), Kurogane-2 (2129), Kurogane-3 (2131), plus chemical-rocket interceptors and railgun platforms.
3. Wants his crews out of blind traps. He grounds every adaptation in sensor data.
4. UNAS hawks resent Imperial supremacy, and his call for détente draws suspicion. He carries every loss personally.
5. He has never met the Emperor (decision). He sees Japan only through tracks he can plot and cannot explain, and he never guesses at hull specs.
6. Methodical, plain.

#### Mikhailovich

1. Admiral Boris Mikhailovich, Russian Federation, Eurasian-Continental Pact. Later a Systems Alliance admiral.
2. Secondary POV. Foreign Gen 1: a Russian Helios-1 version from the stolen schematics, plus fusion and nuclear-thermal dreadnoughts and kinetic-rod platforms.
3. Wants his ships fighting and his crews alive. He confirms the Pact launch on October 13 and is competent with decoys and debris screens.
4. Moscow and Beijing share the Pact without sharing a command culture. He assumes treachery behind any closed door and bullies subordinates when stressed. Burning hulls from his early career haunt his inspections.
5. He distrusts anything behind black seals and has never met the Emperor (decision). The Emperor is the unseen hand behind the hulls he cannot explain.
6. Blunt, heavy.

#### Grissom

1. Jon Grissom, UNAS pilot (decision). Later a Systems Alliance officer.
2. Main POV. Foreign Gen 1 with conventional hardware: UNAS Helios-1 era, chemical-rocket interceptor.
3. Wants his crew through the next unknown threshold alive, with honest readings brought back.
4. He ignores his own injuries until someone sits him down. He fears missing an ambush he failed to read, and his first brushes with superior opponents shook his assumptions.
5. He judges opponents by discipline and seamanship, not flags. He respects the Imperial hulls and has no personal picture of the Emperor (decision).
6. Calm, plainspoken.

#### Harper

1. Jack Harper, UNAS intelligence, founder of Cerberus.
2. Main POV. Stolen/Conventional. He is an outsider to every Imperial secret, and his work touches Asian technology traffic from 2128.
3. Wants a way into Kinki-Kyoku's technical secrets and the Chōju protocols, which is the precursor network's aim.
4. He trusts no one he holds no leverage over. He never states his long-term goals aloud, and tells operatives only the slice they need. Honesty 1 of 5.
5. Treats the Emperor as the holder of a monopoly on survival, with a key to be stolen rather than a ruler to understand. Do not write Harper knowing the foreknowledge.
6. Patient, cold.

#### Lawson

1. Henry Lawson, private entrepreneur who seeks immortality, Harper's precursor network.
2. Main POV. Stolen/Conventional. He is an outsider to every Imperial secret.
3. Wants a way to defeat his own decay. The network's aim is the Chōju protocols.
4. He fears rotting unremembered and cannot treat people as anything but projects. Miranda Lawson is not born before 2150.
5. He sees the Imperial house as the holder of what he wants and resents being kept out. Write the resentment as clinical, never sentimental.
6. Clinical, patronizing.

#### Marchand

1. Lt. Col. Élodie Marchand (rank by 2132 is a decision), EU, Lunar Station Copernicus.
2. Secondary POV. Foreign Gen 1: EU Helios-1 version from the stolen schematics, plus conventional hardware.
3. Wants her figures to hold. Japanese freight does not match declared steel exports, and her telescope logs match Williams's tracks.
4. Brussels votes on sanctions anyway. She does not claim to know the cause, and is honest about what she can and cannot see.
5. She sees the Emperor only as a pattern in the figures. Keep him unnamed in her scenes.
6. Analytic, careful.

#### Ri Hyok-chol

1. Major Ri Hyok-chol, DPRK, Eurasian-Continental Pact.
2. Secondary POV. Stolen/Conventional: Pact conventional hardware.
3. Wants an honest estimate of the southern line to reach his chief. He walks a concrete pour and measures it himself, opposite Han, and he knows his staff's estimate is wrong.
4. He serves a system that does not reward honesty, and he is competent inside it. His chief has already written a number, so Ri decides what the chief will not hear.
5. He meets Imperial machines, not the man, so keep Kenji off the page.
6. Competent, guarded.

## 10. ADDRESS AND SPEECH (Imperial protocol, author ruling)

Japanese characters follow traditional speaking conventions and social hierarchy whatever their personality.
- Subjects address the Sovereign as *Heika* or *Tennō Heika*, never by given name or Himura-sama.
- Parents: *Chichi-sama* and *Haha-sama*. Kiyoshi says *Chichi-sama*.
- Subordinate to superior: -sama (Kurosawa-sama, Tachibana-sama, Togo-sama, Kaguya-sama, Masamune-sama). Superior or elder to junior: -san or -kun (Sano-san, Aoki-kun, Kiyoshi-kun, Chen-san). Peers and military liaisons: -dono or rank titles.
- Honorific suffixes stay inside spoken dialogue and direct address.
- Kaguya says *Heika* in public and "Kenji" in private. Shizuka says *Heika* at court, at work and in official settings, and "Kenji" only when they speak as members of the Clan. Masamune calls the Emperor "nephew."
