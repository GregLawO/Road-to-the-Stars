# Timeline: 2095 to Just Before the Reclamation War

Invented details are marked (decision). Kenji is born 2090. The Empire flies Solaris-1 and Solaris-3 until the Reclamation War. Solaris-5 comes midway through that war, so it is outside this timeline. Kurogane-1, 2 and 3 share one production method.

1. Early 2095 - MC awakens in the 5 year old body of Himura Kenji.
2. Early 2105 - At 15 years old, Kenji completes the Protoss-based Solaris Technology designs and prototypes (proto-Solaris).
3. Mid 2106 - Clan Himura starts secretly implementing the new technology into their industrial businesses.
4. Early 2107 - At 17 years old, Kenji finds that ordinary alloys fail under a Solaris field. He completes Kurogane-1 first, then the first stable Solaris prototype core in a Kurogane housing, and the Solaris-based Helios Technology design and prototypes.
   1. Kenji completes the development of the 'Kurogane Alloy Series', a hyper-dense atomic-lattice alloy ten to fifty times stronger than Grade 5 titanium. Tier 1 is ten times Grade 5, and the series caps at fifty times at Tier 5. At its strongest it dissipates nuclear-scale kinetic impacts by dispersing the shockwave across its crystalline matrix while vaporizing a sacrificial outer layer to shed lethal plasma heat.
   2. Mid 2107: Excavation of the Kii-Yamato Core-Forge begins beneath the Kii mountains. The first hall runs on prototype cores (decision).
   3. From this point each Solaris and Helios generation needs the Kurogane tier of its own number: Kurogane-1 for Solaris-1, Kurogane-2 for Solaris-3, Kurogane-3 for Solaris-5, and so on (author ruling). Kurogane-1, 2 and 3 share one production method (author ruling).
5. 2108 - At the age of 18, Kenji completes the first deterministic Quantum-Optical VI lattice prototype (decision).
6. 2110 - At the age of 20, Kenji completes development of Solaris-1, especially the 'Mote' for powering the Kurogane Yoroi armor series (the Silver Samurai armor, renamed). The first suits are Kurogane Yoroi-1.
   1. Himura Zaibatsu becomes one of Japan's biggest and most profitable Zaibatsu as the world's only Kurogane-1 Alloy supplier.
   2. Clan Himura begins planning for its Imperial Restoration ambition by approaching other ancient clans and obtaining their support via exclusive supply of Helios Technology.
   3. Rival Zaibatsu begin serious corporate espionage attempts on Himura Zaibatsu.
   4. The first Solaris-1I Early Forge core is installed in the Core-Forge, and Kōgyō-1 foundry automata begin building the later halls (decision).
   5. Himura Zaibatsu forms a counter-espionage section under Kurosawa Renji (decision).
7. 2111 - At the age of 21, a 40 m test frame lifts off from the Core-Forge on a cluster of Solaris-1 Motes. It is the first gravitic lifter, and Himura Zaibatsu presents it as a heavy-cargo logistics prototype (decision).
8. 2112 - At the age of 22, Kenji completes development of Helios-1, especially the 'Cube' for powering the Armored Exoskeletal Powered Suits (AEPS). The first suits are AEPS-1, plated in Kurogane-1I.
   1. At the age of 22, Kenji marries.
   2. The first Ashigaru-1A and Ashigaru-1B automata come off the Kii-Yamato lines on Helios-1 cubes (decision). Ashigaru is the vassal and export class, and it is all Clan Himura fields until late 2125.
9. 2113 - Clan Himura's 250 retainers fully adapt to the Kurogane Yoroi-1 armor and its associated weapons.
   1. Clans Date, Takeda, Mori, Chōsokabe, Otomo, Hojo and Shimazu secretly pledge vassalage in support of Clan Himura's bid for the Imperial throne. They receive the schematics for the Helios-1 Technology base, especially the AEPS. Every clan starts at Helios-1.
   2. Kenji's first child is born (Kenji is 23). He is named Kiyoshi (decision, from the files).
10. 2114 - Clan Himura's vassal clans fully adapt and equip their retainers with the AEPS-1.
    1. Himura Zaibatsu registers an orbital logistics subsidiary and a lunar ice-mining concession at a permanently shadowed polar crater (decision).
    2. The subsidiary builds a small low-orbit yard with one dock, declared as a freight-module works. It is the seed of the later low-orbit sovereign shipyard (decision).
11. 2115 - At the age of 25, Ashigaru-1 production scales up across the Core-Forge halls (decision).
    1. Kurosawa's section breaks several sabotage rings.
    2. Kōgyō-1 automata begin excavating a hidden base inside the leased lunar crater. It is the future lunar-shadow slipway (decision).

# PART B. ARC AFTER THE BASELINE (October 2132 onward)

### B.0 Calendar (author ruling)
- The story begins just before the Reclamation War.
- The Reclamation War begins on 14 October 2132 and lasts a little over a decade.
- The Systems Alliance is established five years after the war ends (the 2149 charter).
- (decision) The war ends with the Pact's capitulation and the Imperial Settlement in early 2144, a little over eleven years after it began. The Systems Alliance charter follows in 2149. Solaris-5 arrives mid-2138, the Korean Golden Rain campaign is 2139, and the Type-90D O-Yumi and hover prototypes arrive late 2141. The 1,500-hull ceiling is fixed at the Settlement and built out by about 2147.

### B.1 The Reclamation War (from October 2132)
- The Eurasian-Continental Pact attacks Japan, South Korea and Taiwan while rival Western task forces clash with Imperial cordons.
- **Legion expansion (author ruling).** Manpower is the limit, so Himura's automata Legions grow slowly through the war, from 2,500,000 troops to 10,000,000. The Legion count stays at 25, so each Legion grows from 100,000 to 400,000 (80,000 humans and 320,000 automata, still 1 human to 4 automata). That is about 26,000 more troops per Legion a year (derived). Each loyal clan (Date, Takeda, Mori, Hojo) grows from 3 to a maximum of 5 Legions, 20 in all (author ruling). Together they reach 5,000,000 troops, so each Legion grows from 5,000 to 250,000 (50,000 humans and 200,000 automata) (derived). New loyal Legions are raised over the war at about 2 per clan. The traitor clans' Legions grow too (next bullet) and are destroyed by 2143.
- **Foreign automata and the spread (decision).** The Pact and the Western powers field their own Ashigaru-pattern automata (Technology section 14). They are outclassed at every tier and can be jammed, but they are numerous. With 25 Legions and living officers in short supply, the Empire cannot hold every front with its best troops. It must garrison the home islands against the traitors, hold Korea and Taiwan at their allies' request, keep its temporary holds, and still drop strike forces on the Pact. Its fleet moves fast, so the strain falls on officers and Legions, not hulls.
- **The traitor war (decision).** The traitors get a real fight. Foreign backers pay for it, and each traitor clan raises up to 5 Legions, matching the loyal cap: 15 Legions in all (Shimazu, Chōsokabe and Otomo, from 8). They reach 3,000,000 troops, 200,000 per Legion at 1 human to 4 automata (derived). They fight on Helios-1 and AEPS-1 at first, then on their own Helios-3 gear midway, and on their own Helios-5, AEPS-5 and Ashigaru-5 in the war's second half (Technology 1.3). They hold Kyushu, the Ryukyus, Shikoku and the Inland Sea ports from the opening weeks. At the midpoint offensive in mid-2138 they and the coalition push past the loyal lines in Chūgoku toward the Kinki approaches. The Emperor then deploys Solaris-5 in battle, and the traitors are driven back over the next five years. Their last strongholds fall in 2143, before the 2144 Settlement.
- Togo's wolfpacks and the mid-war hulls shatter enemy fleets.
- Yamashita's podless orbital drops and the Oni-Kikai heavy walkers liberate Korea and break the Pact.
- The Imperial Settlement follows: complete mainland withdrawal and a fixed post-war fleet of 1,500 warships. It is dated early 2144 (decision).

### B.2 Beyond the Charon Gate (to 2150+)
- The Solaris-9 fleet and the 10,000 m Throne-Citadel IJN Amaterasu face the 2148 Mars discovery and the 2149 Charon Relay activation.
- Under the 2149 Kyoto-Arcturus Concordat the Systems Alliance polices wider human space on licensed Japanese Helios hulls, while Kyoto quarantines border relays and hunts rogue conspirators such as Jack Harper and Henry Lawson.

### B.3 Story milestones
- **The beginning (mobilization).** The story begins just before the Reclamation War (author ruling). The rebuilding has hardly begun and another inevitable war is on the horizon. The Empire retrains loyalists on Gen 2 variants and ships Gen 1 gear to nervous foreign allies.
- **The midpoint (the shift).** The global coalition and the traitorous vassals launch a massive offensive, believing stolen Gen 1 variants show the limits of Himura technology. Just as they push past the Gen 2 vassal lines in the Japanese home islands, the Emperor personally deploys Solaris technology in battle, shatters their calculations and turns the tide.
- **The ending (the climax).** Leading his unified alliance into the heart of the coalition's war machine, the Emperor defeats the traitor clans and breaks the superpowers' will to fight, cementing a permanent new world order.

# PART C. LOCATION ATLAS (2132 to 2150+)

Kyoto-Heian Citadel, the Kii-Yamato Core-Forge, the lunar-shadow slipway, Seoul, Taipei and the Proscribed Vault Sanctuaries are covered in the Timeline above and in the Technology and Factions files, and are not repeated here.

### C.1 Pre-war Western and Pact locations (to 2132)

1. **NORAD-Orbital Command & Cheyenne Mountain (Colorado, UNAS).** Williams and UNAS orbital intelligence track Japanese hulls over the Pacific, increasingly alarmed by reactionless accelerations.
2. **Brussels Strategic Directorate & Kourou Equatorial Lift (EU).** Brussels drafts sanctions while analysts track Japanese tonnage from Lunar Station Copernicus. Kourou is the EU's equatorial space-elevator tether in French Guiana.
3. **Baikonur-Orbital & Vladivostok Pacific Bastion (Pact).** Nuclear-thermal drydocks and coastal missile fortresses where Pact commanders build dreadnoughts and kinetic-rod platforms.
4. **The Earth-Luna Dark Corridor & Lunar Station Copernicus.** The unlit void between geostationary orbit and Luna. Shadow Squadron and Kinki-Kyoku cruisers hunt foreign probes there while Western analysts watch impossible tracks over Japan.

### C.2 Reclamation War locations

1. **The Earth-Luna debris ring and Tycho Crater bunker.** A graveyard of pulverized hulls where Togo's wolfpacks ambush and the later micro-jump lines break the blockade.
2. **The Korean Peninsula and Manchurian fronts.** Smoke-choked valleys and frozen rail hubs where Yamashita's Golden Rain drops break the Pact's armies before the Imperial withdrawal.
3. **The Taiwan Strait and Western Pacific bastions.** Storm-lashed sea lanes lit by Energy Lance beams and incoming rods.
4. **The Vladivostok and Sea of Japan crucible.** Missile fortresses and submarine pens neutralized by wolfpacks and hovering Close-Support Corvettes.
5. **The Bering Strait and North Pacific front.** Where Jon Grissom (20 to 32) watches Imperial cruisers pivot and micro-jump.
6. **Puget Sound.** Rain-swept drydocks struck to end UNAS orbital projection without a landing.
7. **Baikonur and the Urals.** Dreadnought yards and kinetic-rod rings shattered by O-Yumi salvos.
8. **Kourou ruins.** The severed EU tether, steaming jungle wreckage.
9. **Wartime orbital forges and Manchurian ruins.** Where Jack Harper (about 19 to 31) sees Gravitic Shinobi and Lock-carrying officers in action, starting his obsession with Solaris and Chōju secrets.

### C.3 Post-war and Charon Relay locations (to 2150+)

1. **The 10,000m Throne-Citadel IJN Amaterasu.** Cedar-and-gold throne halls inside a naval fortress, commanded by Togo. Seat of the 1,500-ship ceiling and of envoys such as Ambassador Goyle.
2. **The Takamagahara Orbital Shipyard Ring (geostationary above Kyoto).** Crown slipways build Solaris-9 hulls beside automated modules making export Helios drives.
3. **Reunified Seoul, Taipei and the independent Chinese republics.** Rebuilding with no Imperial garrisons or Governors-General.
4. **London, Buenos Aires, Geneva and Arcturus Station.** Where young Steven Hackett and David Anderson grow up, and humanity's first extra-solar fleet hub built by Drescher and Grissom on licensed Helios cores.
5. **Promethean Station (Mars, 2148) and the Charon Relay quarantine perimeter (2149+).** The Prothean dig that inspires Alec Ryder, and the dark edge of Sol where Grissom crosses the relay while Henkyō-Kyoku cruisers watch dormant relays.
6. **Vassal Clan garden colonies, Ame-no-Iwato and outer-belt black-ops labs.** Garden colonies run by Japanese noble houses under IJN-TNU- patrols. Ame-no-Iwato is a fortified Crown world holding Vault Sanctuaries and the base for the Exploration squadrons. In the belt, Harper (about 37) and Lawson (about 35) fund raids on Helios shipments and run illegal genetic work that produces Miranda Lawson in 2150.
