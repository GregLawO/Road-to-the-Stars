# Timeline: 2095 to Just Before the Reclamation War

Invented details are marked (decision). Kenji is born 2090. The Empire flies Solaris-1 and Solaris-3 until the Reclamation War. Solaris-5 comes midway through that war, so it is outside this timeline. Kurogane-1, 2 and 3 share one production method.

1. Early 2095 - MC awakens in the 5 year old body of Himura Kenji.
2. Early 2105 - At 15 years old, Kenji completes the Protoss-based Solaris Technology designs and prototypes (proto-Solaris).
3. Mid 2106 - Clan Himura starts secretly implementing the new technology into their industrial businesses.
4. Early 2107 - At 17 years old, Kenji finds that ordinary alloys fail under a Solaris field. He completes Kurogane-1 first, then the first stable Solaris prototype core in a Kurogane housing, and the Solaris-based Helios Technology design and prototypes.
   1. Kenji completes the development of the 'Kurogane Alloy Series', a hyper-dense atomic-lattice alloy ten to fifty times stronger than Grade 5 titanium. Tier 1 is ten times Grade 5, and the series caps at fifty times at Tier 5. At its strongest it dissipates nuclear-scale kinetic impacts by dispersing the shockwave across its crystalline matrix while vaporizing a sacrificial outer layer to shed lethal plasma heat.
   2. Mid 2107: Excavation of the Kii-Yamato Core-Forge begins beneath the Kii mountains. The first hall runs on prototype cores (decision).
   3. From this point each Solaris and Helios generation needs the Kurogane tier of its own number: Kurogane-1 for Solaris-1, Kurogane-2 for Solaris-3, Kurogane-3 for Solaris-5, and so on (author ruling). Kurogane-1, 2 and 3 share one production method (author ruling).
5. 2108 - At the age of 18, Kenji completes the first deterministic Quantum-Optical VI lattice prototype (decision).
6. 2110 - At the age of 20, Kenji completes development of Solaris-1, especially the 'Mote' for powering the Kurogane Yoroi armor series (the Silver Samurai armor, renamed). The first suits are Kurogane Yoroi-1.
   1. Himura Zaibatsu becomes one of Japan's biggest and most profitable Zaibatsu as the world's only Kurogane-1 Alloy supplier.
   2. Clan Himura begins planning for its Imperial Restoration ambition by approaching other ancient clans and obtaining their support via exclusive supply of Helios Technology.
   3. Rival Zaibatsu begin serious corporate espionage attempts on Himura Zaibatsu.
   4. The first Solaris-1I Early Forge core is installed in the Core-Forge, and Kōgyō-1 foundry automata begin building the later halls (decision).
   5. Himura Zaibatsu forms a counter-espionage section under Kurosawa Renji (decision).
7. 2111 - At the age of 21, a 40 m test frame lifts off from the Core-Forge on a cluster of Solaris-1 Motes. It is the first gravitic lifter, and Himura Zaibatsu presents it as a heavy-cargo logistics prototype (decision).
8. 2112 - At the age of 22, Kenji completes development of Helios-1, especially the 'Cube' for powering the Armored Exoskeletal Powered Suits (AEPS). The first suits are AEPS-1, plated in Kurogane-1I.
   1. At the age of 22, Kenji marries.
   2. The first Ashigaru-1A and Ashigaru-1B automata come off the Kii-Yamato lines on Helios-1 cubes (decision). Ashigaru is the vassal and export class, and it is all Clan Himura fields until late 2125.
9. 2113 - Clan Himura's 250 retainers fully adapt to the Kurogane Yoroi-1 armor and its associated weapons.
   1. Clans Date, Takeda, Mori, Chōsokabe, Otomo, Hojo and Shimazu secretly pledge vassalage in support of Clan Himura's bid for the Imperial throne. They receive the schematics for the Helios-1 Technology base, especially the AEPS. Every clan starts at Helios-1.
   2. Kenji's first child is born (Kenji is 23). He is named Kiyoshi (decision, from the files).
10. 2114 - Clan Himura's vassal clans fully adapt and equip their retainers with the AEPS-1.
    1. Himura Zaibatsu registers an orbital logistics subsidiary and a lunar ice-mining concession at a permanently shadowed polar crater (decision).
    2. The subsidiary builds a small low-orbit yard with one dock, declared as a freight-module works. It is the seed of the later low-orbit sovereign shipyard (decision).
11. 2115 - At the age of 25, Ashigaru-1 production scales up across the Core-Forge halls (decision).
    1. Kurosawa's section breaks several sabotage rings.
    2. Kōgyō-1 automata begin excavating a hidden base inside the leased lunar crater. It is the future lunar-shadow slipway (decision).
12. 2116 - At the age of 26, Kenji brings the proto-Chōju longevity protocol to its first stable result (decision).
    1. He and Kaguya receive the first Tier I immersions (decision).
    2. Masamune Himura declines treatment from the start (decision).
    3. A 60 m Solaris-1A test craft makes the first crewed flight to the Moon. Togo Kazuhiko, a former naval officer in Kenji's circle, trains the first crews (decision).
13. 2117 - At the age of 27, Solaris-1 Chrysalis core production reaches full output at the Kii-Yamato Core-Forge (decision).
14. 2118 - At the age of 28, Clan Himura is short of people to command its automata, so the vassal clans' AEPS-1 retainers are conscripted into the Automata Legions as officers and NCOs (decision).
    1. They train alongside Clan Himura's own Kurogane Yoroi retainers.
    2. The lunar-shadow slipway opens Docks 1 and 2 (one corvette bay and one frigate bay). The keel of the first 250 m corvette prototype is laid in Dock 1 (decision).
    3. Kenji completes Kurogane-2, twenty times Grade 5 titanium. Solaris-3 needs it (decision on the date).
15. 2119 - At the age of 29, a rival Zaibatsu's self-modifying logistics system corrupts its own supply data (decision).
    1. Kenji seals the Quantum-Optical VI lattice as deterministic and read-only.
    2. He issues the Anti-Smart-AI Edict across Himura Zaibatsu and all vassal domains.
    3. An uncrewed probe makes the first Sequential Spatial-Fold test jump, covering one light-year (decision).
    4. Kenji completes Solaris-3. It is the Empire's core for the Restoration War and for the fleet before the Reclamation War (decision).
16. 2120 - At the age of 30, the 25 Legions are raised, each of 5,000 men and automata, and begin staging near Kyoto.
    1. Each Legion has 1,000 human officers and NCOs and 4,000 Ashigaru-1 automata, the same 1:4 ratio the later fireteams use (decision). That is 25,000 humans and 100,000 automata in all.
    2. Command of the Legions is split by clan so that every Vassal clan commands at least 2 (decision on the final split): Clan Himura 5, Clans Date, Takeda, Mori, Hojo, Chōsokabe and Shimazu 3 each, and Clan Otomo 2. That totals 25.
    3. Each clan is assigned a region of the home islands to take over (decision):
       - Clan Himura, 5 Legions: the Kinki region (Kyoto, Osaka, the Kii Peninsula and Nara), plus the Tokyo-Yokohama ports and the Tokyo commercial districts, which are the main prizes.
       - Clan Date, 3 Legions: Tōhoku and Hokkaido.
       - Clan Hojo, 3 Legions: the Kantō hinterland around Tokyo.
       - Clan Takeda, 3 Legions: the Chūbu mountains, the Hokuriku coast and the Tōkai corridor between Tokyo and Kyoto.
       - Clan Mori, 3 Legions: the Chūgoku region of western Honshu.
       - Clan Chōsokabe, 3 Legions: Shikoku and the Inland Sea ports.
       - Clan Otomo, 2 Legions: northern Kyushu, including Hakata.
       - Clan Shimazu, 3 Legions: southern Kyushu and the Ryukyu islands.
    4. The counter-espionage section grows into Kinki-Kyoku, the arm for technical secrecy and counter-espionage inside Himura Zaibatsu (decision).
    5. Tachibana Haruka (18) joins the Himura household as a sworn retainer (decision).
    6. The corvette prototype completes trials in lunar shadow on a Solaris-1A core. The frigate prototype finishes fitting out on a Solaris-1B core and completes trials in early 2121. They are the only two hulls Clan Himura owns, and they stay hidden at the lunar base (decision).
    7. The Himura retainers move to Kurogane Yoroi-3, on Solaris-3M cores with Kurogane-2M plate (decision).
    8. Kenji completes the first gravitic-shearing mono-blades and relativistic coil-rifles. Only Himura's own retainers and Kinki-Kyoku carry them (decision).
17. Early 2121 - At the age of 31, Japan's coalition government is paralyzed by debt, population decline and Zaibatsu infighting, and the Restoration War begins (decision: early 2121).
    1. The Kii foundries have delivered the 100,000 Legion automata, all Ashigaru-1 (decision). Foundry output replaces battlefield losses throughout the war.
    2. Every clan moves into its assigned region at once. Rival Zaibatsu security forces and loyal government garrisons contest the cities, the major ports and the Zaibatsu strongholds in each region (decision).
    3. Kyoto and the Kinki region are secured in late 2121 (decision). The regional campaigns continue through the winter, and Otomo, with the smallest Legions, takes the longest to finish its region (decision).
    4. The two prototype hulls take no part in the war, because Kenji will not reveal them (decision).
    5. Zaibatsu infantry arms cannot harm Kurogane Yoroi or AEPS-1 suits, which use Kurogane-1 and 2. Only heavy anti-armor weapons can, and the Zaibatsu have few. The war runs a year because of the size of the cities and ports, not because of losses. The vassal retainers fight with conventional arms, and only Himura's retainers carry gravitic weapons (decision).
18. Spring 2122 - At the age of 32, the Restoration War ends after a little over a year. Kiyoshi is 8 or 9.
    1. Kurogane Yoroi retainers, vassal officers and NCOs in AEPS-1, and the 25 Legions complete the capture of the home islands. Each clan's Legions fight under its own commanders in its own region.
    2. The Chrysanthemum Throne is restored in the Kyoto-Heian Imperial Citadel, with Tokyo as the commercial and maritime metropolis.
    3. Kenji becomes Emperor and Clan Head (Tōshu), Kaguya becomes Empress, and the seven vassal clans become sworn Vassal Clans under Sankin-Kōtai.
    4. The Anti-Smart-AI Edict becomes national law. The Kansatsu-In and Kinki-Kyoku are formally founded.
    5. Kenji completes Helios-3 (it needs Kurogane-2). He holds it back and issues none to the vassal clans, who stay on Helios-1 and AEPS-1. Only Kenji's own household keeps Solaris cores (decision).
    6. The former national maritime and space-defense personnel, about 9,000 people, are absorbed into the new Imperial Navy (IJN-DNK-). They are the first trained hands for the coming fleet (decision).
    7. The Throne enrolls the hereditary Fudai retainer households, about 2,900 families, as the crew pool for the Imperial Guard (decision).
    8. The Imperial Guard is established at once. Its ground arm is the Hatamoto Guard, about 1,500 sworn retainers drawn from the 250 Kurogane Yoroi retainers and the Fudai households, and Tachibana Haruka (20) is appointed to command it. Its fleet is the Imperial Guard Fleet (IJN-KNE-). The Guard holds the Kyoto garrison and the close protection of the Emperor, the Empress and Kiyoshi (decision).
    9. Clan Himura's five Legions stay on Ashigaru-1, and about 20,000 spare frames go into depots (decision). The Imperial automata classes, Bushi-3, Kensei-3, Ō-Yoroi-3 and Onmitsu-3 on Solaris-3M cores, are ready but not yet issued to the Legions (decision).
    10. The Throne gifts all seven vassal clans the Kurogane-1, AEPS and Ashigaru schematics, so each clan can manufacture its own Kurogane-1I and build its own AEPS-1 and Ashigaru-1 from its own Helios-1 cubes (decision). Kurogane-1, 2 and 3 share one production method, so every clan, the future traitors included, can discover Kurogane-2 and 3 from the Kurogane-1 schematics (author ruling).
19. 2123 - At the age of 33, Taiwan and South Korea sign mutual-defense and maritime-technology treaties with the Throne.
    1. Helios-3 reactors and AEPS-3 Exo-Rigs are covertly supplied to their cadres. They may buy the Helios-1 and Kurogane-1 schematics (date open), and they design and produce their own only after the Reclamation War (author ruling).
    2. Kurogane exports end and steel exports are cut, because the Crown now needs all Kurogane for hull plating (decision). UNAS and EU observers read this as exhaustion. The yards draw domestic steel, Kurogane and ore from the lunar concession (decision).
    3. The keel of the 1,000m flagship IJN Amaterasu-no-Yaiba is laid in Capital Dock 1 of the lunar-shadow slipway (decision).
    4. The Crown starts the slipway expansion. The Lunar-Shadow Slipway grows from 2 docks to 36 by 2129. Kōgyō-3 automata dig and fit the docks, and Kinki-Kyoku guards every shaft (decision).
    5. The Imperial Naval Academy opens in Kyoto. Its first graduates join in 2125 (decision).
    6. Kinki-Kyoku finishes the Type-90 gravitic torpedo family (Kozuka, Hari, Kusari and Kage-Ya).
20. 2124-2125 - The first production hulls are commissioned from the lunar-shadow slipway (decision).
    1. In 2124 the fleet commissions its first production hulls. The two prototype hulls are commissioned among them as the first IJN-KNE-LE and IJN-KNE-EL, still on their Solaris-1A and 1B cores. They are the only hulls on Solaris-1. All hulls commissioned in 2124 and 2125 are Imperial Guard (decision).
    2. The low-orbit sovereign shipyard grows to three docks. They build hull sections and plate modules, and the sections are flown to the lunar-shadow slipway for final assembly (decision).
    3. A Solaris-3B frigate surveys candidate planetoids 25 light-years out with the fold drive proven in 2119. Seeding of the Proscribed Vault Sanctuaries waits for the 750m cruisers in 2127 (decision).
    4. Tier II Chōju cycles begin for Togo, Kurosawa, Yamashita and Tachibana, and each receives the Volitional Lock after his first immersion, and Hatamoto officers follow in batches as they reach command rank (decision).
    5. By 2124 every loyal clan has discovered Kurogane-2 from the shared method (decision).
    6. Late 2125: Kinki-Kyoku finds that Clans Shimazu, Chōsokabe and Otomo are plotting with foreign backers, and that Shimazu seeks support to replace the Himura as the Imperial Clan. The Throne takes no open action (decision). The three clans keep their Kurogane-1, AEPS and Ashigaru schematics and refuse to sell them, so that their backers cannot abandon them.
    7. The Throne divides Clan Himura's stock of about 20,000 Ashigaru-1 automata: 8,000 to South Korea, 4,000 to Taiwan, and 2,000 each to Clans Date, Takeda, Mori and Hojo (decision on the split). Himura stops Ashigaru production and moves its Legion lines to Bushi-3, Kensei-3, Ō-Yoroi-3 and Onmitsu-3. The five Legions re-equip over the next three years (author ruling, the span is a decision). From then on the vassal clans produce their own Ashigaru (author ruling).
    8. Right after the treachery is found, Helios-3 is issued to the four loyal clans only, in secret (author ruling). Kinki-Kyoku issues Helios-3 units and gives them the schematics. They begin manufacturing their own Helios-3 cores from those schematics and building AEPS-3, Ashigaru-3 and Helios-pattern mass accelerators in 2126 (decision). The traitor clans receive nothing.
21. 2126 - At the age of 36, Amaterasu-no-Yaiba is commissioned with her dual-redundant Solaris-3D core.
    1. Ayame is born (decision).
    2. Moscow and Beijing formalize the Eurasian-Continental Pact. UNAS and EU sanctions tighten over the closed Pacific sea lanes.
    3. Kenji completes Kurogane-3, thirty times Grade 5 titanium and the tier Solaris-5 will need. The Solaris-5 design work goes on, and the core is completed midway through the Reclamation War (author ruling).
22. 2127-2129 - The 750m Interceptor Cruisers enter service under Kinki-Kyoku and Shadow Squadron, and Line Cruisers follow from 2128 (decision).
    1. Seeding of the Proscribed Vault Sanctuaries begins with the first IJN-KNK-SP cruisers in 2127, using the fold drive proven in 2119 (decision).
    2. In 2128 the Imperial Guard completes its escorts. The Imperial Navy's own escorts begin to commission that year (decision).
    3. Clan Himura grows from 5 Legions of 5,000 to 25 Legions of 100,000 by 2132 (author ruling), each with 20,000 humans and 80,000 automata in the same 1:4 ratio (decision). The first five re-equip with Imperial automata, iteration 3, on Solaris-3M cores, and the 20 new Legions are Imperial-class from the start (decision). The vassal clans' 20 Legions stay at 5,000 each (decision).
    4. Pact orbital kinetic-rod platforms and fusion dreadnoughts take shape at Baikonur and Vladivostok.
    5. Jack Harper's UNAS intelligence work begins to touch Asian technology traffic (decision).
    6. Each crew drills six months in lunar shadow under Togo's staff before its hull is commissioned. The Imperial Guard hulls are crewed by the Fudai retainer households. The Navy and Shadow Squadron hulls are crewed by the absorbed maritime personnel and Academy graduates (decision).
    7. In 2127 the CPF steals the Kurogane-1, AEPS and Ashigaru schematics from the Otomo clan and sells them to the UNAS, the EU and the Russian Federation, keeping a copy (author ruling, the copy is a decision).
    8. In 2128 Kenji sees that Kurogane-1 to 3 cannot be kept secret, and begins a different production method for Kurogane-4 and 5 (decision on the date).
    9. The UNAS, the EU and the Russian Federation build their own Helios-1 versions in 2128 and their own Kurogane-2 by 2129. They discover Kurogane-3 by 2131 (decision on the dates). Their Helios-3 and Helios-5 versions wait for the war.
    10. By 2127 every loyal clan has discovered Kurogane-3 from the shared method (decision on the date).
23. 2130 - At the age of 40, Kenji's design-bay refinement holds the Solaris-3B core at the 30% mass-shedding cap under lower load.
    1. Kiyoshi, 17, asks to join the cadet company.
    2. Han Jae-won and Ri Hyok-chol fortify opposite sides of the DMZ. Ashigaru-1 automata from the 2125 gift and Imperial Legion advisors arrive in Korea.
24. 2131 - At the age of 41, the Hakata port seizure exposes a replaced customs seal and a late registry timestamp.
    1. Marchand and Williams cross-check optical tracks of Japanese hulls over Luna, and Brussels votes on sanctions.
    2. The last pre-war Tier II immersions take place. Masamune again declines.
    3. Sabotage is stopped at the low-orbit shipyard.
    4. Wolfpack drills run under Togo in lunar shadow.
25. Early 2132 - At the age of 41 to 42, the pre-war fleet totals 236 hulls (85 Imperial Guard, 15 Kinki-Kyoku and Shadow Squadron, 136 Imperial Navy), 234 on Solaris-3 cores and 2 on Solaris-1 cores, with escort packs at 1:4.
    1. Clan Himura fields 25 Legions of 100,000, 2,500,000 men and automata (author ruling), and the vassal clans field 20 Legions of 5,000 on top of them (decision). Each vassal clan's Legions remain garrisoned in the region it took in the Restoration War. Himura's Legions hold Kinki, the Tokyo ports and the Kyoto approaches (decision). The Imperial Guard, about 1,500 Hatamoto under Tachibana, holds the Kyoto garrison and guards the Imperial Family (decision).
       - Clan Himura, 25 Legions (2,500,000): Bushi-3, Kensei-3, Ō-Yoroi-3 and Onmitsu-3, with officers in Kurogane Yoroi-3. The first five finish re-equipping in 2128.
       - The four loyal clans, 12 Legions (60,000): Ashigaru-3 and the gifted Ashigaru-1, with officers in AEPS-3.
       - The three traitor clans, 8 Legions (40,000): Ashigaru-1, with officers in AEPS-1, which heavy anti-armor weapons can defeat. They stay nominally under the Throne and answer to their clans (decision).
    2. A Pact patrol meets the unfinished DMZ left flank and its waiting automata.
    3. The yards do not stop. All 36 docks and the low-orbit yard keep working at the 2131 rate, and a 2132 batch is on the slips: 12 corvettes, 3 frigates, 2 interceptor cruisers and 2 Line Cruisers. They are the first wartime replacements and reach the fleet from late 2132 (decision).
26. October 12-13, 2132 - At the age of 42, Kurosawa and Sano recover the Pact's encrypted launch codes at Yokohama, stamped with a seal from a Japanese port office. Mikhailovich confirms the launch, the Imperial picket takes station, and the Reclamation War begins on October 14. Kiyoshi is 19. The war will last a little over a decade (author ruling).
