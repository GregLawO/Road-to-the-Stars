1. STRATEGIC MANDATE: THE IRON AEGIS OF IMPERIAL TERRITORY
- Territorial Defense & Border Integrity: With the Systems Alliance (SSV-) policing wider human space after the 2149 Charon Relay activation using Imperial-built Helios-series infrastructure, the 1,500-vessel IJN- Starfleet concentrates its combat arms on defending the Japanese home islands, reclaimed East Asian and Western Pacific domains, Crown colony worlds, and Vassal Clan garden colonies, while dispatching dedicated Exploration Fleet (IJN-TNK-) squadrons far beyond human borders.
- Zero Technology Bleed (Fūin-Kōryō): Every IJN- warship is a blackboxed Solaris-9 and Quantum-Optical VI fortress. Captains are bound by blood-oath to execute Hearth-Quench reactor scrams and Hearth-Collapse self-destruction rather than allow a single core or hull fragment to fall into foreign or xeno hands.
- Human Command Supremacy: In strict enforcement of the Anti-Smart-AI Edict, deterministic Quantum-Optical VIs handle millisecond fire-control, point-defense tracking, and mass-modulation timing, while living Japanese officers retain sole authority over tactical maneuvering, target authorization, and strategic command.

2. GRAVIMETRIC FLIGHT, SHIELDING & WEAPONS DOCTRINE

A. Tactical Gravimetric Flight & Shield Protocols

- Iai-Pivot Maneuver (Iai-Senkai): Vessels shed apparent mass up to the 75% gravimetric mass-shedding hard limit (25% minimum apparent mass; 45% reduction on un-refitted Pre-War hulls) to execute high-G bow-pivots and maintain standoff range.
- Keystone Lock (Kaname-Jō) & Golden-Amber Shear Barriers: Ships snap back to 100% rest mass for firing recoil stability while projecting golden-amber External Shear Barriers that hug the sloped prow-wedge and bend kinetic projectiles and directed-energy beams around the hull.
- Phase-Gated Firing & Sensor Apertures: Fire-control VIs fold millisecond-scale firing tunnels through the shield, allowing outbound projectiles, torpedoes, beam volleys, and sensor pulses to pass unimpeded while imparting an outward gravitational boost to kinetic rounds.
- Single-Fold Tactical Micro-Jumps & Eclipse Stealth (Gesshoku): Post-war hulls execute instantaneous single-fold spatial jumps across engagement zones and use Eclipse (Gesshoku) stealth mode for thermal and gravimetric concealment.

B. Weapon Systems & Munition Doctrine

- Type-90 Kage-Ya Gravimetric Torpedoes (Primary Escort Armament): Solid-state capacitor-driven gravimetric torpedoes controlled by non-sentient Quantum-Optical VIs that shed up to the 75% gravimetric mass-shedding hard limit (25% apparent mass)—quadrupling effective gravimetric acceleration and evasive agility to weave through enemy point-defense grids before snapping back to 100% rest mass at terminal impact to crush hulls inside localized tidal-shear collapses (50 kg Kozuka, 120 kg Hari, 350 kg Kusari, 750 kg Kage-Ya, and 1,000 kg O-Yumi). Serve as the primary offensive armament of 250m Combat Corvettes (6 Bow Tubes) and 500m Frigates (12 Bow Tubes).
- Primary Axial & Heavy Turreted Macrocannons (Jū-Denjihō): High-velocity electromagnetic mass accelerators (550m–2,500m axial spinal barrels mounted on 750m+ cruiser and capital hulls plus multi-axis 10m–100m heavy turrets) firing sub-250 kg ferrous slugs across standard cruiser/escort spinal bores (120 kg to 220 kg) and up to 500 kg heavy penetrators on 2,100m–2,500m Grand Battleship and Imperial Flagship spinal bores at relativistic velocities.
- Energy Lances (Kōsō-Hō): Precision twin-barreled dorsal, ventral, and lateral coherent laser turrets delivering lightspeed thermal strikes at 2x Macrocannon range.
- Singularity Cannons (Tokuiten-Hō — Exclusive to Grand Battleships & Imperial Flagship): Short-range micro-singularity shear projectors mounted exclusively on 3,000m Grand Battleships (4 Prow) and the 10,000m Imperial Flagship (12 Axial), tearing apart heavy armor and shields regardless of material density.
- Point-Defense Grid (Taikū Bōgyo-Mō) & Protocol Kagami-Wari ("Mirror-Shatter"): Automated micro-turrets firing continuous-wave PD Energy Lances and Tungsten "Gravel-Curtain" flak, reinforced against hostile gravitic munitions by Sub-Harmonic Casimir Interferometry and Gravity-Well Decoy Drones.

3. FLEET FORMATIONS & COMMAND HIERARCHY

A. The 1:2:4 Line-of-Battle Division (Kantai Sentō-Gun — 7 Capital & Cruiser Hulls)  
1,500m Battleships and 3,000m Grand Battleships anchor all main lines of battle, supported directly by 1,000m Line Cruisers as their primary capital escorts:

- Line Flagship Anchor (1): 1x 3,000m Grand Battleship commands the line division.
- Subordinate Capital Anchors (2): 2x 1,500m Battleships operate under the Grand Battleship's command, forming the heavy artillery wall.
- Primary Capital Escorts — Line Cruisers (4 Total / 2 per Battleship): 1,000m Line Cruisers serve as the primary escorts of capital-class ships (2 Line Cruisers assigned to each Battleship, with 20 Line Cruisers dedicated to screening the 10,000m Imperial Flagship). Mounting 120 Point-Defense batteries and 32 main turrets, Line Cruisers erect an overlapping flak/laser dome around the capitals and intercept enemy cruisers and strike craft.
- Optional Outer Escort Assignment: While 500m Frigates and their 250m Combat Corvettes usually operate independently on patrol, scouting, or standoff torpedo ambush missions, Frigate-led 1:2 divisions may be attached to a battle line to provide outer-perimeter screening.

B. Exclusive Main Fleet Assault Carrier Task Group (Kyōshū Kidō-Butai)

- Exclusive IJN-TNU- Operation & Line Exclusion: 2,500m Assault Carriers (50 hulls) are operated exclusively by the main Imperial Japanese Starfleet (IJN-TNU-) and never join lines of battle. Dedicating 50% of internal volume to embarking Samurai-Form Automata Legions (1 full 100,000-strong Division + 1,000 Oni-Mecha per carrier, enabling 5 simultaneous Legion deployments) and extraction-only recovery shuttles, and 50% to Quantum-Optical VI drone hangars and Kage-Ya torpedo magazines, Assault Carriers operate strictly in rear-echelon standoff zones or orbital deployment corridors.
- Dedicated Carrier Escorts (2:1 Ratio) & Close-Support Wing: Every Assault Carrier is escorted by 2x 750m Interceptor Cruisers (100 total, 3,800 km/s)—reacting instantly to counter-ambush hostile raiders or interdictors while the carrier is deploying or recovering troops—and supported during planetary operations by 2x 250m Close-Support / Hospital Corvettes (IJN-TNU-LE(S), 100 total).
- Exclusive Main Fleet Close-Support / Hospital Corvettes (IJN-TNU-LE(S)): Operated exclusively by IJN-TNU- at 2 hulls per Assault Carrier (100 total). Never assigned to void wolfpacks or battle lines; once orbital superiority is established, these Corvettes descend into planetary atmosphere to hover above advancing Divisions—delivering precision Macrocannon, Energy Lance, and Kozuka micro-torpedo artillery support while running continuous medevac extractions for wounded Japanese officers and NCOs.

C. Escort, Patrol & Deep-Space Squadrons

1. The 1:2 Wolfpack / Patrol Division (Kage-Ōkami Buntai — 3 Vessels): 250m Combat Corvettes are always led by 500m Frigates in a strict 1:2 ratio (1 Frigate commanding 2 Corvettes = 24 bow Kage-Ya torpedo tubes per pack). Across the Starfleet, 270 such 1:2 divisions conduct standoff torpedo ambushes, sector patrols, and planetary defense over Crown worlds and Vassal Clan garden colonies.
2. Fast Troop Insertion & Diplomatic Envoys (750m Interceptor Cruisers): Fastest warships above 500m (3,800 km/s); used by the Imperial Shock Corps (IJN-TSG-), Compliance Directorate (IJN-KNS-), and Imperial Guard (IJN-KNE-) for rapid strike-team insertions, and by IJN-TNU- for sovereign diplomatic/envoy missions.
3. Deep-Space Exploration Squadrons (IJN-TNK- — 1:2 Ratio): 5x 500m Frigates (IJN-TNK-EL), each escorted by 2x 250m Combat Corvettes (IJN-TNK-LE), operating far beyond human borders to discover new phenomena and search for extraterrestrial life.

4. TACTICAL MANEUVER & STANDOFF ENGAGEMENT DOCTRINE

Imperial naval combat is governed by the Standoff Supremacy Principle (Enkyori Yūsei-Shugi): Imperial warships always maintain maximum safe distance to exploit the superior range of their Energy Lances, relativistic Macrocannons, Quantum-Optical VI drones, and Type-90 Kage-Ya gravitic torpedoes.

Phase I: Kōsō (Extreme-Range Beam, Drone & Standoff Torpedo Saturation — 100,000+ km)

- Twin-barreled Energy Lances (Kōsō-Hō) on the Grand Battleship, its 2 Battleships, and their 4 screening Line Cruisers open fire at lightspeed to strip hostile kinetic barriers and blind sensor arrays.
- Rear-echelon 2,500m Assault Carriers (IJN-TNU-VB)—screened by Interceptor Cruisers—launch Quantum-Optical VI drone swarms and long-range Type-90 Kage-Ya torpedo salvos.
- Detached 1:2 Frigate-Corvette wolfpacks remain hidden under Eclipse (Gesshoku) stealth at deep standoff range (50,000+ km), firing unspotted torpedo ripple volleys and micro-jumping to new positions without exposing themselves.

Phase II: Iai-Senkai & Kaname-Jō (Standoff Range-Keeping & Relativistic Bombardment — 25,000 – 100,000 km)

- The battle line uses Solaris-9 mass-shedding (up to the 75% hard limit, 25% apparent mass) and Iai-Pivots (Iai-Senkai) to match or exceed hostile closure speeds—keeping the enemy pinned inside the Imperial long-range gunnery zone while evading sluggish enemy slugs.
- At firing alignment, capitals trigger Keystone Lock (Kaname-Jō) (100% rest mass) and open millisecond Phase-Gated Firing Apertures through their golden-amber External Shear Barriers, hammering enemy hulls with 550m–2,500m axial spinal Macrocannons and turreted batteries while Line Cruisers shred incoming missiles and strike craft.

Phase III: Strict Conditions for Close-Range Engagement (< 20,000 km / < 5,000 km)  
Imperial warships never enter close-range combat by default. A Starfleet commander is authorized to close distance—bringing 3,000m Grand Battleships (4 Prow Singularity Cannons) and the 10,000m Imperial Flagship (12 Axial Singularity Cannons) into < 5,000 km execution range, or committing escorts to close-in gunnery—only when one of three strict tactical conditions is met:

1. All Torpedoes Expended (Gyorai Zen-Shōhi): Once the battle line, standoff Assault Carriers, and 1:2 wolfpacks have expended their Type-90 Kage-Ya torpedo magazines softening and crippling the enemy fleet, surviving capitals close in to finish broken hulls with Singularity Cannons (Tokuiten-Hō) and point-blank relativistic broadsides, while wolfpacks sweep in to mop up crippled stragglers with secondary turrets.
2. Last-Resort Blockade Break (Saishū Fūsa Toppa): When a hostile fleet holds an orbital blockade over a target world, Imperial forces always attempt to dislodge or lure away the defenders using long-range bombardment, VI drone swarms, and standoff torpedo salvos first. If time permits, the fleet refuses to close in and instead relies on clandestine orbital insertions via cloaked 750m Interceptor Cruisers to infiltrate ground teams past the blockade. A battle line is authorized to micro-jump or charge into close range to violently break the blockade only after all long-range attempts have failed AND ground forces urgently need to be dropped onto the surface immediately.
3. Dire Emergency / Total Commitment (Kinkyū Zenryoku-Sen): When an allied strategic asset, homeworld, or Throne-citadel faces imminent annihilation and the situation is so dire that every ship must commit everything it has—regardless of hull risk—to break the enemy immediately.

5. SPECIALIZED MISSION DOCTRINES

A. Shadow-Wolf Standoff Ambush & Redeployment Doctrine (Kage-Ōkami Sakusen)

- Zero-Exposure Standoff Launches: 1:2 Frigate-Corvette packs never expose themselves to close-range enemy fire to launch torpedoes. Operating at the 75% mass-shedding hard limit (25% apparent mass) under Eclipse (Gesshoku) stealth, packs hold deep standoff distances (50,000+ km) using passive targeting shared via tight-beam laser links, firing full 24-torpedo ripple volleys through millisecond Phase-Gated Firing Apertures before micro-jumping or sprinting (4,000–5,000 km/s) to a new vector.
- Secondary Weapons Restraint: Wolfpacks only close in to engage with their turreted secondary Macrocannons and Energy Lances once all torpedoes are expended to finish off stragglers after the battle is mostly won, during a last-resort blockade break, or in a dire emergency.

B. Autonomous Orbital Drop, Close-Support Hover & Recovery (Kōka-Sakusen)

- Zero Drop-Pod Orbital Insertion: Protected by their Solaris-9 gravitic cores and active golden-amber personal shear barriers, all soldiers, Samurai-Form Automata, and Oni-Mecha require zero drop-pods or descent craft—stepping directly from IJN-TNU- Assault Carrier ventral bays (or IJN-TSG-/IJN-KNS-/IJN-KNE- Interceptor Cruiser drop-locks) in low orbit, deflecting atmospheric entry heat via their shear barriers, and shedding apparent mass (up to the 75% hard limit) to land safely on target.
- Atmospheric Artillery & Medevac Overwatch: Once orbital superiority is achieved, 2x 250m Close-Support / Hospital Corvettes (IJN-TNU-LE(S)) per Assault Carrier descend into upper-to-mid atmosphere, shedding 75% apparent mass to hover above advancing Divisions—delivering overhead artillery support and running continuous medevac skiff extractions.
- Extraction-Only Recovery Shuttles & Interceptor Guard: Heavy gravitic recovery shuttles embarked aboard IJN-TNU- Assault Carriers are used exclusively to ferry the Legions back up to orbit once ground operations conclude. Throughout troop drops and shuttle recoveries, 2x 750m Interceptor Cruisers orbit each Assault Carrier at high readiness (3,800 km/s) to destroy any ambushing enemy vessels.

C. Deep-Space Exploration & First-Contact Protocol (IJN-TNK- Squadrons)

- Stealth Survey Mandate: Operating far beyond human borders, the 5x 1:2 Exploration Squadrons (IJN-TNK-) enter uncharted systems under Eclipse (Gesshoku) thermal/gravimetric masking.
- Non-Interference & Quarantine Alert: Upon discovering unknown cosmic phenomena, derelict technology, or extraterrestrial life, IJN-TNK- squadrons maintain strict observational silence, record full quantum-optical telemetry, and transmit encrypted burst alerts to Henkyō-Kyoku (Bureau of Frontier & Border Control / IJN-KNS-) and the Imperial Academy of Sciences before any overt contact is authorized.

D. Coalition & Transponder Rules of Engagement

1. Systems Alliance Deconfliction: Within recognized Systems Alliance space, IJN- vessels operate strictly as sovereign diplomatic envoys (750m Interceptor Cruisers) or invited reinforcement task forces, leaving routine colonial policing to Systems Alliance (SSV-) fleets.
2. 2149 Charon Relay Quarantine: Enforced by Henkyō-Kyoku (IJN-KNS-) Interceptor Cruisers; no unauthorized vessel may activate dormant primary relays bordering Imperial space.
3. Unconditional Yield to Throneside Transponders: All Starfleet arms immediately yield transit corridors and tactical command to IJN-TNS- (IJN-TNS-MC and the 4 Imperial Clan/Courier Corvettes IJN-TNS-LE) and IJN-KNE- Imperial Guard transponders.
