### POST-WAR FLEET REGISTRY (2136 to 2150+)

DOCUMENT REF: REG-774-HIMURA-IMPERIAL

The pre-war 236-hull fleet, hull classes and cores to 2132 are in the Technology and Timeline files.

---

I. COMMAND AND CEILING

- Operational command rests with Fleet Admiral Togo under the Combined Starfleet General Staff, serving the Throne in Kyoto.
- The 2136 Settlement fixes a permanent ceiling of exactly 1,500 active IJN- warships, built out by about 2142 with no growth afterward.
- Every IJN- warship is a blackboxed Solaris-9 and Quantum-Optical VI fortress. Captains are sworn to Hearth-Quench or Hearth-Collapse rather than let a core or hull fragment fall into foreign or xeno hands.
- Sole allies: Taiwan and South Korea. The Systems Alliance polices wider human space on licensed Helios hulls, while the Starfleet holds Japanese territory, Crown worlds, Vassal garden colonies and relay quarantine zones.

---

II. MID-WAR TRANSITION (Reclamation War)

- Wartime hulls bring conformal golden-amber barriers, which make podless "Golden Rain" drops possible, plus the Type-90D O-Yumi (late 2135), wartime 1,500m Battleships and prototype 3,000m Grand Battleships.
- A few prototype Close-Support / Hospital Corvettes (IJN-TNU-LE(S)) debut in 2135 and can hover only at 5 to 10 km altitude (decision). The full 5 to 25 km band comes with Solaris-9.

---

IV. EXPORT TIER

Licensed Helios hulls serve UNAS, EU and UN customers and later the Alliance. They carry spherical bubble shields, no fold drive, no Eclipse stealth, and the same Hearth-Quench and Hearth-Collapse anti-tamper seals, audited by IJN-KNS- cruisers.
